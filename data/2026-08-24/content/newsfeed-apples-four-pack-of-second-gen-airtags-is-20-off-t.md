## Some other great Verge-approved deals

* Thebiggest Stream Deck that Elgatomakes is down to its lowest price since Amazon’s Prime Day in late June. Originally $249.99 for the gadget with 32 programmable keys, it’s $50 off atAmazonandBest Buy, totaling $199.99. Each key can be customized within Elgato’s desktop software for Windows or macOS, letting you set not just the command that executes when you press it, but also the imagery that displays on the key. You can assign simple functions to keys, like muting a mic or turning off a webcam. The integrations run deep, though, letting you create multi-button macros that run with a single button press.
* If you own a Switch 2 but still need a way to keep your original Switch’s Joy-Con recharged, there’s a great accessory that can charge Joy-Con for both systems and is half off atAmazon.Fastsnail’s charging dockthat includes a USB-C-to-A cable and four Joy-Con charging sections is $6.99 (was already a great deal at $13.99) when you use the codeNMSV9HX3, which the community atSlickdealssurfaced.
* Backbone’s One controller is one of the best ways to play games on your phone. It offers a comfortable, complete set of controls, whether you’re playing native phone games, or streaming console or PC games. Normally about $100, theBackbone One PlayStation Editionthat works seamlessly on iOS with Sony’s Remote Play app is just $27 in used condition ateBay. The seller VIPOUTLET claims that the product is in “fair/good condition” and may have some blemishes, and it includes a 60-day warranty. You can purchase a one-year warranty via Allstate on eBay for an extra $4. It’s a good price if you’re curious but have been turned off by its retail price.
