# Citi scoops up card rewards firm

## Dive Brief
- Citi announced an agreement to acquire rewards platform **Kard Financial** to enhance its consumer cards business.  
- The acquisition aims to combine Kard’s technology, talent, and merchant relationships with Citi’s scale and payments capabilities, enabling more personalized rewards, offers, and stronger connections between customers and brands.  
- Deal terms were not disclosed; Citi stated the transaction is not material to its financial results.

## Dive Insight
- Kard, founded in 2015, provides rewards solutions to banks, fintechs, and marketers, leveraging predictive AI and first‑party transaction data.  
- Investors include Underscore Ventures, Fin Capital, and Tiger Global.  
- Citi’s head of value cards, lending and commerce, Abhinav Anand said the acquisition aligns with Citi’s vision for commerce and loyalty, allowing faster innovation and richer experiences for customers and merchants.  
- Kard’s founder and CEO, Ben Mackinnon, noted that joining Citi will let Kard’s commerce media network expand rapidly and serve Citi’s 70 million cardmembers in addition to its existing clientele.  

## Strategic Context
- Rewards and loyalty programs are becoming critical as banks vie for top‑of‑wallet positioning.  
- Other lenders such as Bank of America and PNC have recently upgraded or launched new rewards initiatives.  
- Citi’s U.S. consumer cards business serves roughly 70 million customers, generated about $18.3 billion in revenue and $177.5 billion in loans in 2025.  
- At its investor day, Citi’s consumer cards head, Pam Habner, outlined plans to accelerate growth investments and deepen loyalty through its commerce ecosystem.  

## Operational Details
- Citi did not disclose Kard’s employee count but confirmed no staff reductions are planned as part of the transaction.  
- The expected closing date for the deal was not provided.
