# Valve raises Steam Deck prices by more than $200

## Main details
- The 512 GB Steam Deck OLED price rose from **$549 to $789**; the 1 TB model went from **$649 to $949**, a $300 increase for the larger version.  
- Both models are currently in stock on Steam with an estimated delivery of 3‑5 business days.  
- Valve attributes the hikes to “rising memory and storage costs” and broader global logistical challenges; no hardware changes were made.  

## Additional offerings
- Refurbished Steam Deck OLED units are also for sale: 512 GB at $629 and 1 TB at $759.  

## Context and related impacts
- In February, Valve warned of intermittent out‑of‑stock periods in some regions because of memory and storage shortages.  
- The same shortages have delayed Valve’s planned launches of the Steam Machine and Steam Frame, now expected later in the year.  
- Valve released the Steam Controller on May 4, announced alongside the Machine and Frame.  

## Industry-wide “RAMageddon”
- Similar price spikes are affecting other gaming hardware:  
  - Lenovo’s Legion Go 2 saw a $650 increase.  
  - Sony and Nintendo have announced price hikes for the PlayStation and Switch 2.
