# American Airlines picks SpaceX's Starlink for in-flight Wi‑Fi

## Main announcement
- American Airlines will equip more than 500 narrow‑body Airbus aircraft, such as the A321neo, with SpaceX’s Starlink Wi‑Fi, starting early next year.  
- The carrier does not plan to change Wi‑Fi providers on its Boeing fleet, which currently uses a mix of Viasat and Panasonic.

## Context and competition
- Starlink already provides in‑flight connectivity to United, Southwest, Alaska (merged with Hawaiian) and other airlines.  
- American evaluated both Starlink and Amazon Leo as recently as March.  
- Delta announced it will adopt Amazon Leo for hundreds of jets beginning in 2028.  
- In January, American rolled out free in‑flight Wi‑Fi for members of its frequent‑flyer program.

## Industry trends
- Airlines are upgrading from slow, expensive in‑flight internet to higher‑speed services to attract higher‑spending customers.  
- Carriers are also exploring additional revenue streams, such as personalized advertising for travelers.

## SpaceX background
- SpaceX’s connectivity unit, which includes Starlink, posted $11.39 billion in revenue last year, representing 61 % of total sales.  
- The unit is preparing for an IPO, likely next month.
