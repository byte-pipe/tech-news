# AI coding startup Cognition raises $1B at $25B pre‑money valuation

## Funding round
- Cognition announced a financing of more than $1 billion, valuing the company at $25 billion pre‑money.  
- This follows a $400 million round in September that left the company at a $10.2 billion post‑money valuation.  
- The round was led by Lux Capital and General Catalyst.  
- Existing backers such as Founders Fund and 8VC participated, and new investors included Ribbit Capital, Atreides, and Layer Global.

## Market context
- The investment signals strong confidence from top‑tier VCs that independent AI coding startups can thrive despite major model makers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) targeting the same space.  
- Cognition previously acquired the remaining assets of Windsurf, a former Google acqui‑hire, strengthening its position.

## Customers and financial performance
- Enterprise customers listed include Mercedes‑Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, and Santander.  
- The company reports an annualized revenue run‑rate of $492 million.  
- Usage of its autonomous AI software engineer, Devin, has grown 50 % month‑over‑month for the past six months.

## Product highlight
- Devin is Cognition’s autonomous AI software engineer designed to automate coding tasks for large organizations.
