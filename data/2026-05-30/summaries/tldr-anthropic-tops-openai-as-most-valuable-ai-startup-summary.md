# Anthropic surpasses OpenAI as the most valuable AI startup

## Funding round
- Anthropic announced a Series H financing of $65 billion, valuing the company at $965 billion.  
- The round was led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital and includes $15 billion of previously pledged investments, among them $5 billion from Amazon.  
- This valuation nearly triples the $380 billion level recorded in February.

## Valuation relative to OpenAI
- OpenAI’s latest valuation was $852 billion after a $122 billion funding round in March.  
- Anthropic’s $965 billion valuation now places it above OpenAI, making it the most valuable AI firm in Silicon Valley.

## Revenue growth
- Anthropic reported a $47 billion revenue run rate, up from $30 billion earlier in the year and $10 billion in the prior year.  
- The surge is driven largely by its AI coding assistant, Claude Code.

## Product updates
- Released Claude Opus 4.8, the latest model in its Claude series.  
- Introduced Claude Mythos Preview, a cybersecurity‑focused model available to a limited set of companies.

## IPO outlook
- Both Anthropic and OpenAI are preparing for public listings.  
- OpenAI is expected to file a confidential IPO prospectus in the coming weeks, targeting a possible September debut.  
- Anthropic is also working on an IPO, though a timeline has not been set.

## Market context
- Other AI and space companies are in the news, including Elon Musk’s SpaceX AI filing for an IPO and Nvidia’s large investments in emerging AI technologies.
