# The UK Government’s Low Value Purchase System is a Waste of Time – Terence Eden’s Blog  

## Overview  
- Small businesses must report monthly to the Government Commercial Agency (GCA) whether they have made any sales through the RM6237 Low Value Purchase System.  
- Even when no sales occurred, suppliers must log in, complete multi‑factor authentication, and submit a “No Business” return.  
- The author argues this requirement wastes time and offers no benefit.

## Key criticisms  
- Only 59 small businesses reported actual sales via RM6237, yet more than a thousand are required to submit a nil return each month.  
- The reporting burden falls on suppliers, not on the buying departments that actually spend the money.  
- The GCA does not retain or publish individual user‑experience feedback; scores are anonymised and only available as aggregate service data.
