## ❤️ Thank you for the love!

Hey everyone 👋 It's Sunday again, and DEV Pet Corner is back for its third edition!

First of all,thank you so much to everyone who joined the previous edition ❤️I loved seeing your pictures, reading your stories, and getting to know more of your animal friends. It's so nice seeing familiar faces return with more pictures, and I love that we're making this little corner of DEV a place where we can share something we love.

Thank you for taking the time to share your pictures and a little piece of your lives with us. It really means a lot to me!

If you missed the previous edition, you can still meet all the cuties here 👉DEV Pet Corner #2

Now, let's do this again 😄

## 🐶 Show us your animal friends!

I'd love to see your pets, whether they're the ones waiting for you at home, the ones you grew up with, or the ones who have a special place in your heart.

And you don't need to have a pet of your own to join in! Maybe you've met a friendly dog on a walk, spotted a cat in your neighborhood, or come across an animal on campus that made you stop for a moment. Perhaps you follow an adorable animal account on Instagram or have a favorite animal video you keep going back to.

If it involves an animal you love or something that makes you smile, I'd love to see it 😃

Share pictures, funny moments, sweet memories, little stories, or links to your favorite animal posts. Share as many pictures as you'd like. There's no limit!

If you're sharing someone else's content, please link to the original post and respect the creator's wishes about sharing their work.

A little note for returning readers:You might notice this section looks familiar 😄 I'll keep it in future editions, too, so new readers can easily see what they can share without going back to previous posts. As always, feel free to join in with anything you'd like or simply enjoy all the cuteness in the comments!

As always, let's keep the comments friendly and welcoming to everyone.I'm looking forward to seeing your animal friends and enjoying all the cuteness with you!

Thank you for being here. See you next Sunday for DEV Pet Corner #4!
