# Anthropic IPO Live: 7 people will control voting power

Anthropic Filing on Nasdaq with Unique Voting Power Structure

Anthropic, a US-based company that develops artificial intelligence (AI) technology, has filed on Monday to go public on the Nasdaq stock exchange. The filing, which has garnered significant attention, reveals a unique voting power structure with seven individuals controlling voting power over Anthropic's Class F common stock. These seven individuals are:

- Dario Amodei, CEO and founder of Anthropic
- Daniela Amodei, Dario's sister
- Tom Brown
- Jack Clark
- Jared Kaplan
- Sam McCandlish
- Christopher Olah
