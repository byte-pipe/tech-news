# 5 Interesting Learnings from Anthropic’s S-1

by Jason Lemkin|5 Interesting Things, Blog Posts, Scale

 Anthropic filed its S-1 this morning, detailing its financial performance and strategy. Here are five key takeaways:

## 1. **Net Revenue Retention Exceeded 900%**

Anthropic reported a net revenue retention of approximately 900% as of September 30, 2026. This means that the company's revenue from qualified customers in the same quarter last year was roughly 13 times higher than it is currently.

## 2. **Usage Business Grew 9x Inside Existing Customers**

The usage business at Anthropic grew 9 times inside its existing customers in a year, according to the company. As of September 30, 2026, the company had more than 11,900 customers with revenue over $100,000, more than 1,900 with $1 million, and approximately 250 with $10 million.

## 3. **Costs Were Lowered and Compute Revenue Decreased**

Anthropic lowered its costs per task and decreased its compute revenue to 55% against 201% in the previous year. This decrease is attributed to lower costs and increased usage.

## 4. **Costs per Task Fell 40%**

Anthropic reduced customer costs per task by roughly 40% versus Claude Opus 5, and the filing says lower costs drive more usage, which encourages broader adoption.

## 5. **Revenue Growth Exceeded Expectations in the First Half of 2026**

Anthropic reported revenue growth in the first half of 2026, with a quarter of $16,286 million. This is roughly fourteen times the $1,163 million from the same period last year.
