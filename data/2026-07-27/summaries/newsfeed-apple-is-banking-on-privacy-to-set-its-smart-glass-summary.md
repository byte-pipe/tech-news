# Apple is banking on privacy to set its smart glasses apart

## Announcement and timeline
- Apple is expected to unveil its first smart glasses at WWDC in June 2026, with a planned market launch by the end of 2027.  
- The details come from Mark Gurman’s reporting.

## Privacy‑centric strategy
- Apple aims to differentiate its glasses from Meta’s AI glasses, which have drawn criticism for covert photo and video capture.  
- The device will likely use on‑device processing and omit controversial features such as facial recognition and always‑on recording (“super sensing”).  
- Recordings made by the glasses are expected not to be used for training Apple’s AI models.

## Potential hardware variations
- Rumors suggest Apple may release a version without a camera, or with a camera limited to sensing functions only, similar to the rumored AI‑enabled AirPods.  
- Such a design would prevent the glasses from capturing photos or video.

## Broader context
- Apple has spent more than a decade making privacy a defining product message; entering the smart‑glasses category could risk that reputation if privacy safeguards are not clearly communicated.
