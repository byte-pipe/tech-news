## The cross-examination: one dollar is not the other

The clause cuts both ways whileAnthropic'sdemand for compute outruns supply, and walking away would hurt the customer more than the landlord3. But the customer's own filing explains the split: pay-regardless, decade-long commitments went to the cloud giants, and the flexible one went to a newer landlord, the company Muskcalled "evil" on X in February↗13. A dollar of revenue that can vanish on 90 days' notice is not the same dollar as one locked for a decade, and a stock at 103 times sales needs the cancelable kind to behave like the locked kind.
