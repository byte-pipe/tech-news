##### for a year the new superintelligence lab stayed out of the news

Since his hiring, Wang remained largely out of public view. No one knew exactly what his team was working on. I was intrigued. But, with so much other news in the AI industry, it was easy to forget about Wang and the Superintelligence Lab.

But, then, on Sept. 23, 2026, at the Meta Connect 2026, both Zuckerberg and Wang revealed what they were cooking up: Muse, a personal AI agent, along with a gadget for accessing it without a phone. ##### call me surprised

Not that I ever doubted Wang, but, because Meta was playing catch up from the start and then pivoted away from LlaMa and tried to start all over, I didn’t expect that Meta could actually gain on the other AI companies during this period of transition. It has.

And it appears to have positioned itself in 2 ways distinguishing it from the other AI companies: consumer personal AI agents and open-weight frontier models.

Meta’s alliance with Apple shows that it’s a new day for Meta — and Mark Zuckerberg.
