# All the news about the iPhone Duo

Apple has finally announced its long-rumored foldable:the iPhone Duo.

The new phone has a 5.4-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner display, a “nano‑texture” finish on the inner screen, and barely any visible crease. Instead of Face ID, it uses Touch ID on the power button. It has two cameras, a main lens and a wide-angle lens. Applecompletely revampediOS 27’s controls and apps for the foldable, including putting the dock on the side of the phone. And it has aflashy opening animationwhen you unfold the phone.

The iPhone Duo a big new bet from Apple, though one that’s a little late to the game compared to competitors like Samsung and Google. The phone isn’t cheap: it starts at $1,999 for the base 256GB model, and the prices only go up from there. Still, Apple said thatpreorder demand was “extraordinary,”so it could have a hit on its hands.

Here’s everything unfolding about the iPhone Duo.
