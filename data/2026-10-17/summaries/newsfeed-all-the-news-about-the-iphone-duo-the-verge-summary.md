# All the news about the iPhone Duo

## Overview
- Apple officially unveiled its first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, during the “Surprise and Shine” event.
- The device is positioned as a high‑end, late‑entry competitor to Samsung and Google foldables.

## Specifications
- Outer display: 5.4‑inch screen.  
- Inner display: 7.6‑inch Super Retina XDR panel with a “nano‑texture” matte finish and minimal visible crease.  
- Unlock method: Touch ID integrated into the power button (Face ID removed).  
- Cameras: Dual‑lens system (main + wide‑angle) sharing the 48 MP sensor used in the iPhone 18 Pro, without variable aperture.  
- Build: IP68 rating, Ceramic Shield 2 on the back and front, A20 Pro chipset, supports Apple Pencil (later in 2026).  
- Software: iOS 27 redesigned for foldable use, including side‑dock placement and a custom opening animation.

## Pricing & Availability
- Base model: 256 GB for $1,999; higher storage and configuration options increase the price.  
- Preorders opened on Oct 16 2026, 8 AM ET.  
- Official launch in Apple Stores scheduled for Oct 23 2026; online delivery estimates pushed to early 2027.

## Preorder Demand & Delivery
- Apple described preorder demand as “extraordinary.”  
- Inventory limits were not disclosed; in‑store availability may be quicker than online shipments.  
- Delivery dates listed on Apple’s site show late‑January 2027 for all configurations.

## Software & User Experience
- iOS 27 introduces fold‑aware controls, side‑dock UI, and a smooth unfolding animation.  
- Early hands‑on impressions note a matte inner screen that reduces glare and a nearly invisible crease.  
- The folding mechanism is smooth, IP68‑rated, and feels premium despite the modest outer screen size.

## Repair, Warranty & Costs
- Without AppleCare Plus, repair estimates are high:  
  - Battery service: $229  
  - Inner display replacement: $999  
  - Other damage: up to $1,499  
- AppleCare Plus is recommended to mitigate these costs.
