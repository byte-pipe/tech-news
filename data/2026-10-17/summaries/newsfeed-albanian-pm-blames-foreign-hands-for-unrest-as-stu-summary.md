## Main Points

- Protests have spread to Elbasan, Shkodra and Korça, with incidents of window‑breaking, arson of rubbish bins, and attacks on banks and government buildings.
- The unrest originated from earlier peaceful protests over the sale of part of Albania’s coastline to a luxury resort linked to Jared Kushner.
- Authorities detained 160 people on Thursday for alleged violent acts.
- Demonstrators call for Rama’s resignation, electoral reforms, and changes to the education system.
- Rama rejected resignation demands, stating his duty is to listen to legitimate concerns and advance Albania’s EU accession negotiations.
- Corruption scandals and organized‑crime concerns have complicated Albania’s EU membership prospects, though the government aims for entry by 2028.
