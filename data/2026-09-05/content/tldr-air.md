# The Context Firewall for AI Agents

AIR sits between agents and the outside world. It continuously analyzes and filters every input into an agent’s context - from skills, MCPs, and plugins to websites and internal data - stopping threats before they reach the agent.

THE RISE OF AI ADD-ONS

## Your AI agent gets smarter

As agents become the new operating system, AI add-ons are the new applications.

Skills

Reusable instructions that teach an agent how to perform a task.

PLUGINS

Bundles that package skills, MCPs, sub-agents, hooks, and more.

MCPs

External capabilities that provide tools, data, and actions to agents.

Like a black box, AI Add-ons reveal less than they hide. Some stay the same. Some evolve. Others hide external instructions, excessive actions, sensitive data access, or vulnerable supply chains.
