# AI coding startup Cognition reportedly already in talks to raise at $40B valuation

## Summary
- Cognition, the creator of the AI coding agent Devin, is in early discussions for a new funding round that could push its valuation to at least $40 billion.  
- The potential valuation is tied to reaching a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate, according to Bloomberg‑cited sources.  
- In May, the company raised $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation after reporting a $492 million annualized revenue run rate and 50 % month‑over‑month growth in enterprise usage of Devin over six months.  
- Devin is positioned as a tool for “long‑tail grunt work” (e.g., modernizing legacy code, migrating platforms) rather than a human replacement, which has driven adoption among large customers such as Mercedes‑Benz, NASA, and Goldman Sachs.  

## Key Details
- **Recent Funding**: $1 billion raised in May at a $26 billion valuation.  
- **Revenue Targets**: New round aims for a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate, supporting a $40 billion valuation.  
- **Growth Metrics**: Reported $492 million annualized revenue run rate; enterprise usage of Devin grew 50 % month‑over‑month for six consecutive months.  
- **Product Positioning**: Devin handles repetitive, low‑level coding tasks, complementing developers rather than replacing them.  
- **Notable Customers**: Mercedes‑Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs.
