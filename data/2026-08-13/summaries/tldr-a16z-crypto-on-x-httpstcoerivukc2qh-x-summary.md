# Summary of a16z crypto post: How crypto cards are driving stablecoin spend

## Overview
- Crypto payment cards let users spend stablecoins anywhere traditional card networks are accepted, converting crypto to local currency at the point of sale.  
- Cardholders do not need a traditional bank account; they can deposit stablecoins with the issuer or hold them on‑chain via self‑custody.  

## Growth Metrics
- Monthly crypto card volume reached **$759 million** in July 2024, about **2.5×** the $306 million recorded in July 2023.  
- Number of purchases rose to **~9 million** in July, up from **~5.2 million** a year earlier.  
- Average spend per transaction is approximately **$86**.  

## Chain Distribution
- Settlement chains have diversified:  
  - Optimism handles about **29 %** of volume.  
  - Solana handles about **19 %** of volume.  
  - Base handles about **19 %** of volume.  
  - Gnosis dropped to roughly **2 %**.  
- Early 2024 spending was concentrated on Gnosis, home of the first Visa‑linked self‑custodial wallet (Gnosis Pay).  

## Stablecoin Composition
- Euro‑backed stablecoins fell from **~88 %** of volume (early 2024) to **~2 %** in July.  
- Dollar‑backed stablecoins now dominate:  
  - USDC accounts for about **58 %** of card spending.  
  - USDT accounts for about **26 %** of card spending.  
  - Both have increased from roughly 48 % (USDC) and 7 % (USDT) a year earlier.  

## Market Context
- Crypto cards remain a small segment compared with traditional card networks that process trillions of dollars monthly.  
- Growth is driven by stablecoins leveraging existing Visa rails, reflecting a broader acceleration of crypto adoption post‑GENIUS, including tokenized assets and stablecoin usage.
