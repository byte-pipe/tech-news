# Apple considers round screens and more radical designs for future Apple Watch revamp

 

Benjamin Mayo
 | Aug 9 2026 - 7:20 am PT																

Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman goesinto some detail today about the future for theApple Watch product line.

He suggests Apple is going back to the drawing board as the Watch faces stiffer competition from the likes of theOura Ringand other screenless smart fitness devices. It seems Apple is preparing to revamp the concept of the Apple Watch from the ground up …

Although it sounds like plans haven’t been finalized, Apple is considering all sorts of shapes and sizes, including watches with circular round displays, fitness accessories that have no screens at all, and more. This would represent a major breakpoint in the history of the Apple Watch, as every model up to now has basically revolved around the same basic design concept.

Bloomberg indicates Apple is looking at possibilities on the low-end and the high-end, weighing up the appeal of both more premium and more affordable models. The new wearable form factors would also intersect with a software revamp, in which Apple is looking to push the Health app in a more AI-centric direction.
