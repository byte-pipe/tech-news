## The Drop

* Resident Evil.Zach Cregger’s take on Capcom’s iconic survival-horror video game franchise sounds like a riot. My colleague Charles Pulliam-Moorecalled ita “comedy first and a thrilling nightmare second.” Say no more.
* Valve’s Steam Frame.Valve’s new VR headset, which was initially supposed to launch in early 2026, is finally here, and it starts at $1,059. Based onSean Hollister’s review, it seems like a really fun way to play VR and flatscreen games — when you’re not dealing with bugs and general instability. The Steam Deck was a similar story, and Valve righted the ship, so fingers crossed it can do the same for the Frame.
* Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave.I’ve been playing Nintendo’s latest entry in its strategy RPG series over the past several weeks, and it’s anexcellent evolutionof everything great aboutThree Houses. It’s easily my favorite game on the Switch 2. Seeing most of what it has to offer is a massive time commitment; I poured about 130 hours into the game. But if you’re intoFire Emblemor even just like RPGs, this one will keep you busy for a long while.
* Fujifilm’s Instax Pal 2.This $169.95 digital camera has an optical viewfinder and a 1.3-inch LCD screen, can take 10-megapixel photos, and is so dang small! Looks like a great pocketable camera to play with.
* Boox’s Picco.The newBoox Palma 3isn’t available to order just yet, but youcanpreorder the tiny Picco, which has a 3.97-inch E Ink screen and costs $99.99. I haven’t used any of these diminutive e-readers, but I love the idea of them.
* Marvel’s Wolverine.Insomniac’s latest superhero game isn’t getting the same rave reviews as the studio’sSpider-Mangames — my colleague Andrew Webster praisedWolverinefor being astraightforward action gamebut says the story ends up being “largely forgettable.” But if you just want to slice and dice as Wolverine, it seems like the game delivers.
* Hisense’s RoamView.Hisense now has its own version of the “screen on wheels” idea that LG tried with its StanbyME. It has a 32-inch 4K touchscreen and a 10-hour battery, and you can take it off the wheeled stand to use on your desk. It’s $1,199, so it’s not cheap, but is it bad that I really want to try it anyway?
