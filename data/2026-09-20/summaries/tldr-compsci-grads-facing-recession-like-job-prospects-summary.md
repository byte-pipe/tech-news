# Compsci grads facing recession-like job prospects thanks to AI

# AI and ML Job Market Impact on College Graduates

The article finds that computer science graduates are facing a tough job market, with a decline in job prospects and earnings.

**Main Points:**

* AI exposure has increased college undergraduate majors' job prospects, but so have lower-paying fields
* The most AI-exposed majors experienced a 5% decline in initial employment opportunities and a 13% decline in full-quarter initial earnings
* The effects are not evenly spread across industries, with more jobs in lower-wage sectors like restaurants and retail

**Key Findings:**

* The top 10% of AI-exposed majors had a 5% decline in initial employment opportunities and a 13% decline in full-quarter initial earnings
* This decline is comparable in magnitude to the earnings losses associated with a large recession
* Graduates from less AI-exposed fields, such as nursing and education, have not experienced the same decline in early-career employment

**Reasons for Decline:**

* Job prospects are worsening, with many graduates turning to lower-wage sectors like restaurants and retail
* The most AI-exposed fields, such as computer science, mathematics, and engineering, have not experienced significant declines in job prospects
* However, graduates in these fields have not experienced the same decline in earnings as those from less AI-exposed fields
