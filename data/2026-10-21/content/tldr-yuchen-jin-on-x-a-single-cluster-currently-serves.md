# Yuchen Jin on X: "“A single cluster currently serves 155 trillion tokens/day, generating $38 in revenue per GPU-hour (at off-peak pricing).”

Huge flex from DeepSeek. Not only did they open-source their inference engine, but they also open-sourced their revenue numbers.

They didn't disclose which GPUs they're using here, but DeepInfer supports both NVIDIA Blackwell and Huawei Ascend.

Even assuming they're renting B300s at $7 per GPU-hour, that's ~82% gross margin."
