# Alberta voters overwhelmingly reject plan to leave Canada in referendum

## Overview
- A referendum in Alberta asked voters whether to start a legal process toward independence.
- Unofficial results from Elections Alberta show about 76% voted to remain in Canada, 24% for separation.
- The vote was part of a ten‑question ballot covering immigration, health care, education and constitutional powers.

## Results
- Nearly 1.9 million votes counted at the time of reporting.
- Approximately 43 % of eligible voters had already cast ballots via advance voting or mail‑in, suggesting a higher turnout than the 2021 referendum.
- Delays at around 100 of the 1,400 polling stations postponed final results by more than two hours.
