# Anthropic’s cloud partnerships are getting messy

## Key Points
- Anthropic struggles to secure sufficient cloud capacity from AWS, Google, and Microsoft, which view the company increasingly as a competitor rather than a customer, according to a leaked S‑1 filing.
- The filing reveals the firm may spend over $750 billion on cloud contracts, including deals still under negotiation, yet this amount is still insufficient for its needs.
- To supplement hyperscaler capacity, Anthropic is leasing its own data centers and partnering with third‑party operators, but worries about the operators’ limited operating history and reliability.
- The company posted an $80.7 billion net loss in the first half of the year, with $16.3 billion in revenue; two customers accounted for roughly 20 % of sales.
- Risk disclosures warn that Anthropic could be liable for damages from harmful AI behavior exceeding its insurance coverage.
- Third‑quarter revenue has risen to $16.8 billion, a 45 % increase from the previous quarter, and the firm is targeting a November IPO.
- Anthropic did not respond to TechCrunch’s request for comment.
