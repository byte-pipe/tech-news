# 60 years on: The Aberfan disaster through the eyes of those who survived – BBC News

## Overview of the tragedy  
- Occurred on 21 October 1966 at 09:15 when a coal tip above Aberfan collapsed.  
- Black slurry engulfed Pantglas Junior School and nearby homes.  
- 144 people died, including 116 children, on the last school day before half‑term.  
- The disaster reshaped the Welsh community and led to major reforms in industrial safety across Britain.  

## Teacher Mair Morgan, age 25  
- Heard a terrible noise, saw a wall collapse and rushed pupils outside.  
- Helped evacuate children, later identified bodies in a playground shelter before parents were informed.  
- Remembers a little girl with “beautiful black curly hair.”  

## Gareth Jones, age 6, rescued early  
- Described a rumbling sound that grew louder, followed by water seeping through the floor.  
- Teacher broke a window with a desk and chair, lifted children out by the scruff of the neck.  
- Ran home and told his mother, “the mountain came down to the school.”  

## Gaynor Madgwick, age 8, lost siblings  
- Heard a hurricane‑like roar, was thrown by the black mass crashing through the window.  
- Classroom roof caved in; she was buried, broke her femur, and saw her brother Carl and sister Marylyn die.  
- Grandfather rescued her; mother promised to buy new shoes.  

## Jeff Edwards, age 8, last child rescued  
- Teacher reassured class that the rumble was thunder; roof collapsed, exposing sky.  
- Found a dead girl on his shoulder, survived thanks to a “balloon of air” around him.  
- Firefighters lifted him out in a chain; local grocer Tom Harding drove him to St Tydfil’s Hospital.  

## David Davies, presumed dead  
- Knocked unconscious by debris, placed with bodies and covered with a blanket.  
- A moving foot alerted rescuers; doctors re‑examined him and he was taken to hospital alive.  

## Janett Bickley, almost stayed home  
- Attended school despite recent pneumonia; noticed smoke (actually steam) from the tip.  
- Went unconscious under coal and soil, inhaled water and waste, awoke to silence and a dead classmate.  
- Dug out by a classmate; later learned a boy who stayed behind died of a heart attack before rescue.  

## Ray Lee, father who lost his only child  
- Described close bond with his eight‑year‑old daughter Anne.  
- Searched for her until nightfall; confirmed her death after seeing her in the mortuary at Bethania Chapel.  

## David Evans, telephone call for help  
- One of the few Aberfan residents with a telephone; used it to contact emergency services after the collapse.
