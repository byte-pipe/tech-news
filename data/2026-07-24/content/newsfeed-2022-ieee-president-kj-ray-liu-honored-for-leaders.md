# 2022 IEEE President K.J. Ray Liu Honored for His Leadership

## He led the effort for more equitable representation of members

Kathy Pretz
1h
6 min read

Kathy Pretz is the editor in chief of The Institute, IEEE's member publication

2022 IEEE President K.J. Ray Liu is the recipient of this year’s IEEE Haraden Pratt Award for his “transformative and impactful leadership.” He received the award from IEEE President Mary Ellen Randall on 24 April during a ceremony in New York City.
