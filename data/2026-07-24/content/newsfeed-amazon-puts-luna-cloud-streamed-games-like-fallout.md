# Amazon puts Luna cloud-streamed games like Fallout 4 inside Prime Video

﻿Now Prime Video has a games section, with Amazon’s cloud gaming platform Luna built in to let you start playing right away.

The new strategy that Amazon gaming exec Jeff Gattistalked toThe Vergeabout last monthis getting clearer now that Prime Video has added anew Games tab on Fire TV devices. With Prime Gaming, Amazon Game Studios, and Luna now combined into one organization, it’s aiming at the more casual audience of people already using Prime Video to stream movies, TV shows, and sports, instead of trying to compete directly with “core” gamers playing MMOs and shooters.

Luna was already included in Prime, but now users in the US and UK can access it directly within Prime Video to play titles likeDispatch,Fallout 4,Hogwarts Legacy,EA Sports FC 26, andClue. Amazon says that “details on additional devices, countries, and games” will be announced later.

The expansion is similar to Netflix’srecent additions of streaming games you can play on your TV, and in an interview withThe Hollywood Reporter,Gattis admitted Amazon has “never established” itself as a gaming platform so far, and emphasized the key role Amazon IP likeTomb RaiderandJames Bondwill have.

The Games tab is also a place for Amazon to promote tie-in games to people who watched its content on Prime Video, like theMasters of the Universe: Legends Uniteco-op card battler game, or its “AI-powered party game”Courtroom Chaos: Starring Arnold Schwarzeneggerahead of his new holiday movie for Amazon later this year.
