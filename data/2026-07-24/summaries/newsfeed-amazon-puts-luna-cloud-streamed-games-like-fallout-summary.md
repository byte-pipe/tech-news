# Amazon puts Luna cloud‑streamed games like Fallout 4 inside Prime Video

## Main Announcement
- Prime Video introduces a “Games” tab on Fire TV devices, embedding Amazon’s Luna cloud‑gaming platform.
- Users in the United States and United Kingdom can launch Luna titles directly within Prime Video.

## Available Games & Features
- Initial lineup includes *Dispatch*, *Fallout 4*, *Hogwarts Legacy*, *EA Sports FC 26*, and *Clue*.
- The Games tab also promotes tie‑in games linked to Prime Video content, such as the co‑op card battler *Masters of the Universe: Legends Unite* and the AI‑powered party game *Courtroom Chaos* starring Arnold Schwarzenegger.

## Strategic Context
- Amazon has merged Prime Gaming, Amazon Game Studios, and Luna into a single organization.
- The strategy targets casual viewers who already use Prime Video, rather than competing directly with “core” gamers of MMOs and shooters.
- Executive Jeff Gattis acknowledged that Amazon has “never established” itself as a major gaming platform and emphasized the importance of Amazon IPs like *Tomb Raider* and *James Bond*.

## Future Plans
