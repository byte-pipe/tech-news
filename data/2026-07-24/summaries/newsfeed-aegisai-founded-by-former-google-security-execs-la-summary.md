# AegisAI, founded by former Google security execs, lands $36M to stop AI-driven spear phishing | TechCrunch

# Summary of “AegisAI, founded by former Google security execs, lands $36M to stop AI‑driven spear phishing”

## Overview
- Hackers are leveraging AI to generate large‑scale, highly personalized spear‑phishing emails.
- Former Google security leaders Cy Khormaee and Ryan Luo founded AegisAI to combat these AI‑crafted attacks.
- Their experience showed rule‑based “if‑then” email filters are too slow and miss many AI‑generated threats.

## Technology
- AegisAI deploys AI agents that analyze each incoming message like a human, spotting subtle anomalies.
- The agents can detect malicious PDF attachments with passwords or CAPTCHAs that bypass traditional spam filters.
- The approach is positioned as “defend with AI against AI,” aiming to replace legacy email security tools.

## Funding
- Raised a $36 million Series A led by Battery Ventures, with participation from Accel and Foundation Capital.
- Total capital raised to date is $49 million.
- Battery Ventures’ partner Dharmesh Thakker highlighted the urgency of defending against faster AI‑driven email attacks.

## Customers
- Adopted by dozens of organizations within a year, including:
  - Mesh (crypto payments)
  - LangChain (AI startup)
  - Lokker (privacy‑compliance platform)

## Market Context
- Other startups, such as Oceanis (backed by Lightspeed), are also using AI to analyze email context and challenge incumbents like Proofpoint and Mimecast.
- AegisAI’s leadership, with direct experience securing Gmail, is seen as giving it a competitive edge.

## Future Plans
- While initially focused on email security, AegisAI intends to expand its AI‑agent technology to broader data‑security use cases.
- The company believes customizable, advanced agents will determine the next dominant security provider.
