# Summary of “Big banks' record Wall Street profits are increasingly tied to AI”

## Key Financial Results
- The five largest U.S. banks (JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citigroup, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley) generated **$114 billion** in capital‑markets revenue in H1 2026.  
- This represents a **31.5 % year‑over‑year increase**, with stock trading accounting for more than half of the growth.  
- Goldman Sachs contributed the most, adding **$7.1 billion** after advising on SpaceX’s IPO and Alphabet’s equity raise.

## Main Drivers
- **AI‑related activity** is identified as the top earnings driver for big banks in 2026.  
- Surge in AI‑focused IPOs, equity raises, and financing deals fuels higher trading volumes, underwriting fees, and wealth‑management inflows.  
- Wealth‑management assets: over 50 % of Morgan Stanley’s net new assets came from employees of recent AI IPO companies.  
- Anticipated AI build‑out spending is projected at **$10 trillion** over several years, gradually shifting from Big Tech cash flow to public debt, equity, private credit, and other financing channels.

## Executive Perspectives
- **Mike Mayo (Wells Fargo)**: AI is the “No. 1 earnings driver,” likening capital demand to a “100‑foot wave” that lifts Wall Street but could cause a fall if it crashes.  
- **Jeremy Barnum (JPMorgan CFO)**: The market is “extremely risk‑on,” and the bank is a “taker” of that risk; a repeat of the record trading haul is “hard to imagine.”

- **David Solomon (Goldman Sachs CEO)**: The AI cycle is early, with expected “bumps and recalibrations.”  
- **Ted Pick (Morgan Stanley CEO)**: The industry is “10 % to 15 % of the way through the investment cycle.”  

## Upcoming Deals & Market Outlook
- **Anthropic** is preparing an IPO in October, working with Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan.  
- **OpenAI** also plans a future public offering.  
- Potential slowdown in AI adoption could negatively impact banking revenues; technology investment may outpace actual adoption, leading to “poor allocated investment outcomes.”  

## Risks Highlighted

- Analysts warn that while the AI wave offers “the ride of your life,” it also carries the risk of “serious injury” to banks if the momentum falters.
