# Amazon is bringing games to Prime Video

## Overview
- Amazon announced the integration of its Luna cloud‑gaming service directly into Prime Video.
- The move aims to turn Prime Video into a single‑stop entertainment hub, similar to Netflix’s recent focus on party games.
- Games are now accessible from the “Games” tab on Prime Video for Prime members.

## Game lineup
- Launch titles include **Hogwarts Legacy**, **EA Sports FC 26**, **Indiana Jones and the Great Circle**, **Clue**, and **Taboo**.
- Amazon‑developed titles such as the “Courtroom Chaos” series and the co‑op card battler **Masters of the Universe: Legends Unite** are also featured.
- The library combines third‑party hits, cult classics, and Amazon’s own games.

## Integration details
- Previously, Luna games were only reachable via a standalone Luna app for Prime members.
- Now they sit alongside movies and TV shows within Prime Video, playable on Fire TV devices in the U.S. and U.K.
- Users can play using a controller or a phone as a controller.

## Vision and future plans
- Amazon’s goal is to remove barriers to gaming, making it as easy to access as streaming video, without the need for expensive consoles or PCs.
- Jeff Gattis, GM of gaming at Amazon, highlighted that embedding Luna simplifies discovery and reduces time spent searching for games.
- New games will be added monthly, with plans to expand to additional devices and countries in the coming months.
