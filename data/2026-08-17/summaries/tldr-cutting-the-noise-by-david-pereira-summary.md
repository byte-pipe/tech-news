# Cutting the Noise.

## What can you stop doing now?
- Adding more tools, skills, or AI agents makes product and tech work more complex.  
- Simplification comes from removing unnecessary practices, not from piling on new ones.  
- The author’s “adding by subtracting” approach means discarding habits that no longer add value.

## The Human Enemies in Disguise
- **Predictability**: Seeking control leads to endless planning, meetings, and discussion instead of actual work.  
- **Routine/Stability**: Over‑reliance on stable processes turns the process itself into the goal, killing innovation.  
- Both enemies limit potential and create a loop that hinders progress.

## Becoming the Noise Remover
- Adopt a “5‑year‑old” mindset: constantly ask “why?” about routine actions.  
- Examples of successful removals:
  - Deleted 800+ backlog items, forcing focus on what truly mattered.  
  - Eliminated the Definition of Ready (DoR), improving collaboration.  
  - Removed “PM Approval” step, increasing team accountability.  
  - Cancelled all recurring meetings, shifting effort to meaningful work.  
  - Simplified the tool stack, reducing friction and enhancing collaboration.  
- Resistance is common; critics often present hypothetical fears rather than real evidence.  
- Key lesson: “Backlog items age like milk, not wine”—outdated items disappear if they’re not needed.

## Stop Complicating, Start Simplifying
- Principle: **Never add before subtracting**.  
- Personal analogy: donate or discard old clothes before buying new ones.  
- Practical applications:
  - Limit weekly meetings; remove something else to stay within the limit.  
  - Cap the number of backlog items; delete excess.  
  - Restrict the number of tools; keep only those that truly fit needs.  
- Acknowledge imperfection; use mistakes as learning opportunities.

## Weekly Challenge
- Identify one routine element that no longer serves you.  
- Stop it, observe the outcome, and adjust accordingly.  
- The removal of old habits creates space for new, valuable practices.

## Moving from Idea to Value
- Shortcuts and over‑reliance on AI agents can undermine critical thinking.  
- A structured, collaborative, and transparent approach yields sustainable value.
