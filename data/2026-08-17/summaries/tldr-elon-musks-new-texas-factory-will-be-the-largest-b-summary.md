# Summary of “Elon Musk’s New Texas Factory Will Be the Largest Building on Earth, If All Goes According to Plan”

## Project Overview
- Elon Musk plans a massive “Terafab” factory in Grimes County, Texas.  
- Expected size: **over 100 million sq ft**, dwarfing Tesla’s 10 million sq ft Texas plant and the current world‑largest New Century Global Center (18.9 million sq ft).  
- Construction cost projected at **$16.8 billion**.

## Purpose and Production
- Joint supply‑chain hub for **SpaceX** and **Tesla**.  
- Vertically integrated chip facility aiming to combine logic, memory, and advanced packaging under one roof.  
- Will produce AI chips for:
  - Millions of Tesla Optimus robots  
  - Space projects (including a lunar factory)  
  - Solar‑generated power systems  

## Financial and Tax Incentives
- Musk seeks taxpayer support to offset part of the cost.  
- Eight tax‑incentive applications filed, requesting:
  - Freeze on maintenance taxes for two local school districts.  
  - Property tax set at ~48 % of appraised value for 10 years per phase.  
  - State coverage of maintenance & operations and interest & sinking taxes via the Texas JETI program.  

## Local Reaction and Government Support

- SpaceX already receives a **$30 million Texas Enterprise Fund** grant for expansion.
