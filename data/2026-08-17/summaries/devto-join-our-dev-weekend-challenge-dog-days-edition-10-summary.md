# DEV Weekend Challenge: Dog Days Edition – Summary

## Overview
- A short, weekend‑long coding challenge designed so participants have most of Saturday and Sunday to work.

- Submissions are accepted globally; timing is set to accommodate all time zones.

## Prompt
- Create any project related to dogs (e.g., training log, breed identifier, generative art, dog‑perspective game).  
- Projects honoring other pets that live with a dog are also acceptable.

## Prize Categories
- **Overall winner** plus four technology‑specific categories:
  - Best use of Snowflake  
  - Best use of Solana  
  - Best use of ElevenLabs  
  - Best use of Google AI  
- Participants may use multiple listed technologies, but can win only one prize per challenge.

## Judging Criteria
- Relevance to the dog theme  
- Creativity and originality  
- Technical execution (functionality, quality of build, usability)  
- Writing quality of the submission post  
- (Optional) Meaningful incorporation of the chosen prize‑category technology

## Prizes
- Five winners each receive:
  - $200 USD  
  - DEV++ Membership  
  - Exclusive DEV badge  
- Every participant with a valid submission earns a completion badge.

## How to Participate
1. Publish a DEV post using the provided submission template.  
2. Include an overview, motivation, and a demo of the project.  
3. Follow these rules:
   - One submission per person; the same project can qualify for multiple tech categories.  
   - The project must be built **within** the challenge window (no pre‑existing work).  
   - No work after the deadline counts; commit history will be reviewed.
