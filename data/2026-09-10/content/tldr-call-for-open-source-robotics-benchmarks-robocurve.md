# Call for Open-Source Robotics Benchmarks

We are funding academic teams to design open-source robotics benchmarks for dexterous manipulation and to run real-world evaluations.

## Build the benchmark. Keep the robots.

### All team requirements

1. 1Design the benchmarkBuild a benchmark for bimanual arms with at least 20 tasks.
3. 2Run real-world evaluationsCollect real-world evaluations of YAM arms on your benchmark across at least 3 publicly accessible models: VLAs such asMolmoAct2andIsaac 0.5, and LLMs such asGemini 3.7 Flash.We provide support for running evaluations onInspect Robots, the open-source physical AI evaluation harness.

### What each team receives

* A pair of YAM arms (ABC Research Kit), including 3× Intel RealSense D405 cameras.
* $20,000 cash
* $2,000 in compute credits
* $1,000 in evaluation bill of materials
* Co-authorship on a mega-author paper

Teams can keep all equipment, including the YAM arms and cameras, after the program.

### What happens next

Invitation emails will be sent to everyone who registers their expression of interest.
