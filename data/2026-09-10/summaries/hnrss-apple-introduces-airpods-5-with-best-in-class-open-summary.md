# Apple introduces AirPods 5, featuring best‑in‑class open‑ear Active Noise Cancellation

## Overview
- Launch date: press release September 9 2026, availability in stores September 18.  
- Two models: standard AirPods 5 ($129) and AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case ($149).  
- Open‑ear design with industry‑leading ANC, improved sound, Siri AI integration, Live Translation, and enhanced durability (dust, sweat, water resistance).  

## Best‑in‑Class Active Noise Cancellation
- New multi‑port acoustic architecture and computational audio algorithms remove up to 50 % more external noise than AirPods 4 with ANC.  
- Transparency mode sounds more natural, preserving voices and surrounding sounds.  
- Adaptive Audio blends ANC and Transparency automatically based on the environment.  
- Conversation Awareness lowers media volume when the user starts speaking.  

## Re‑Engineered Sound Quality
- Multi‑port acoustic architecture and next‑generation Adaptive EQ, inspired by AirPods Pro 3.  
- Delivers richer, more detailed audio across a wider range of ear geometries.  
- Enhances Personalized Spatial Audio for a more immersive listening experience.  

## Intelligent Features, Hands‑Free
- Siri AI (beta in iOS 27) offers deeper contextual understanding of messages, emails, photos, and app actions, with customizable expressive voices.  
- Users can respond to Siri with head gestures (nod for “yes,” shake for “no”).  
- Live Translation, powered by Apple Intelligence, enables real‑time multilingual conversations via stem press, voice command, or iPhone Action button.  
- Dictation accuracy improved; Siri continues to work with third‑party apps such as WhatsApp and Audible.  

## AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case
- Adds a force sensor with volume swipe on the stem—the first for an open‑ear form factor.  
- Battery life: up to 5 hours of playback with ANC (1 hour longer than AirPods 4), 22 hours total with the case.  
- Wireless charging case compatible with Apple Watch chargers, Qi chargers, and USB‑C.  

## Environmental Impact
- Constructed with 40 % recycled material overall; case contains 70 % recycled plastic and battery uses 100 % recycled cobalt.  
- 35 % of the supply‑chain manufacturing powered by renewable energy (wind, solar).  
- Packaging is 100 % fiber‑based and recyclable at home.  

## Pricing and Availability
- Pre‑order begins today in the U.S. and over 65 other countries.  
- Standard AirPods 5: $129 (U.S.); Wireless Charging Case version: $149 (U.S.).  
- Full feature set requires pairing with an Apple device running the latest OS; Apple Intelligence available with iOS 27 on September 14.  

## Executive Quote
- Dave Pakula, Apple Vice President of Hardware Engineering, highlighted bringing ANC to the most affordable AirPods, emphasizing the combination of world‑class listening, Live Translation, and hands‑free Siri AI as a game‑changing experience.
