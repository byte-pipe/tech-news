# Cosmos Update 7.0 Summary  

## New Gameplay Features  
- **Direct a Space Station** – Prove yourself to local authorities, become station director, style the central lobby, expand with ancillary rooms, and customize the exterior to welcome or deter travelers.  
- **Alliances** – Join existing galactic alliances or found your own, design a name and banner, and compete for the most expansive territory.  
- **Deep‑Space Outposts** – Visit alien outposts in inhabited systems to process rare minerals, trade with quartermasters, and sign up for deep‑space contracts.  
- **Orbital Construction** – Deploy a deep‑space base computer, claim a volume of space, and build free‑floating habitats or celestial cities.  
- **Derelict Hulks** – Explore colossal shipwrecks, navigate rusted chambers, salvage valuable technologies and cargo while monitoring hull integrity to avoid catastrophic collapse.  
- **Star System Map** – Use an interactive map to plot celestial bodies, points of interest, and unidentified signals; mark locations for starship navigation assistance.  
- **Extra‑Vehicular Activity** – Pilots can exit ships at any time, performing spacewalks with six degrees of freedom and advanced jetpack control.  
- **Tractor Beam & Gravitino Coil** – Upgrade corvette‑class ships with a tractor beam to pull and refine salvage; the coil stores large objects for transport.  
- **Asteroid Belts & Ice Fields** – Mine dense belts of rare asteroids and glittering ice fields for exotic minerals, encountering prospectors, traders, and pirates.  
- **Infested Outposts & Biohazard Cleanup** – Investigate outposts overrun by biological goop, extracting pathogen sacs, gelatinous fibers, and other valuable but hazardous materials.  
- **Reach The Sun** – Fly long‑haul missions to stars; visually spectacular but lethal if approached.  
- **Enhanced Terrain Details** – Overhauled planetary rendering with improved tessellation, higher detail, and richer textures.  

## Community & Commemorative Content  
- **10‑Year Anniversary Expedition “Our Journey Continues”** – Celebrate a decade of No Man’s Sky with milestones, developer commentary, and exclusive rewards.  
- **Framed Commemorative Art** – Earn a set of framed artworks featuring book‑cover images from major updates.  
- **Vintage Items** – Claim the Starbound v0.27 Multi‑Tool, a vintage Sentinel Interceptor, a Diplodocus companion, and an Astronaut Cockpit figurine by participating in the expedition.  
- **Alliance Leaderboards** – Rankings based on population, activity, and territorial expansion; visible from the station core.  
- **Hundreds of Decorative Station Parts** – Unlock monumental modules at the Space Anomaly to personalize station exteriors; rare pieces may drop from deep‑space anomalies.  
- **Developer Commentary** – Audio notes from Hello Games, read by Sean Murray, filed permanently in the Collected Knowledge catalogue.  

## Additional Gameplay Mechanics  
- **Deep‑Space Looting** – Use a Corvette equipped with a tractor beam to collect salvage, haul it to outposts for processing, and sell or use it for station customization.  
- **Hulk Meltdown** – Prioritize the most valuable loot first to escape before unstable hulks explode.  
- **Orbital Platforms** – Build bases on natural rock platforms in deep space for striking backdrops.  
- **Space Exploration Re‑Invented** – Fixed, revisitable points of interest such as outposts, abandoned freighters, asteroid belts, and anomalies make star systems more interesting and realistic.  
- **Outpost Missions** – Accept salvage and material recovery contracts at outposts, earning standing and rewards for assisting their survival.
