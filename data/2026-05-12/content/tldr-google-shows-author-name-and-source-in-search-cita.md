Anyone can generate content. Anyone can publish. So Google is doing something smart, by showing the person behind the information so searchers can make faster decisions about whether it's worth listening to. By showing the source and the name, they're reintroducing human credibility into search… it’s about time! 2.⁠ ⁠Having a point of view as an individual is about to become a serious differentiator. Now Google is focusing on
  identity, expertise and reputation. Original thinking is becoming a differentiator. 3.⁠ ⁠Search and social are becoming even more intertwined. You need to build visibility through search and social strategies being aligned. It makes so much sense for these social platforms to show the name, because anyone can write anything on these platforms - we need to know who to trust!
