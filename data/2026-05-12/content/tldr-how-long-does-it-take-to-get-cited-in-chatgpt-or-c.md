description: '"How long does it take to get cited in ChatGPT or Claude?" This is one of the biggest open questions in AEO. I dug through billions of logs and pulled ~900 newly published marketing pages to track time-to-citation, here''s what''s up: - Median time from publishing to citation: 6.81 days - 75th percentile (75% of pages are cited within): 18.68 days - 90th percentile (90% of pages are cited within): 37.10 days As far as I can tell, this is the first number on time-to-citation for either engine, especially Claude. Here''s what I think (and let me know what your thoughts below): AEO finally has a clock: up until now, a huge question every marketer has been quietly sitting with is "will my content get picked up by answer engines, and how long should it take?" I hear it constantly: "how long until I start seeing results?" and we''ve all kind of had to take a guess at it. "Usually we see citations in the first two weeks" or something similar. But now we have an answer: by 37 days,
  90% of pages have gotten their first citation. If you''re beyond that point w/o citations, the issue is most likely going to be on your end (either the page is buried, your robots.txt is blocking it, or the crawler can''t reach it, etc.) If you''re under 6 days to first citation, your performance is better than average (you''re doing something right and should keep doing it!) IMO the next question is, if your content is cited earlier and is theoretically more discoverable by ChatGPT and Claude, does that mean ChatGPT and Claude are going to cite it more often? 👀 | 98 comments on LinkedIn'
