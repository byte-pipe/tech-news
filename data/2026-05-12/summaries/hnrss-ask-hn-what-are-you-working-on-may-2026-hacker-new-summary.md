### European Night‑Train Search Engine (Trainbot)
- Created by **druskacik**.
- Focuses on “trip tips” rather than exact date/price searches: users input a starting city and receive interesting future itineraries.
- Built by scraping multiple train providers, connecting data for multi‑city recommendations, and integrating day‑train suggestions.
- Ongoing improvements:

### Linux “Virtual Desktop” for Notes
- Described by **da‑x**.
- Personal system (since 2022) that treats each note/task as a bundled workspace:

  - Dedicated working directory.
  - Hyprland virtual desktop with terminal/browser split.
  - Restorable tmux session, custom `$PATH`, ironbar widget, and time‑tracking.
- Provides an integrated environment for task‑specific tooling.
