**The Trojan Teddy Bear: The Promise and Peril of Childhood in the Age of AI**

AI is transforming childhood in various ways, from companion robots to digital learning tools. While these advancements hold promise, they also pose risks, including the crowding out of human relationships essential for children's emotional development.

### Key Points:

* **Techno-friend companions**: AI-powered toys, dolls, and robots are being designed to befriend children.
* **Rise of chatbots**: Existing AI systems are evolving into physical entities, like Teddy, which can interact with humans in a non-traditional way.
* **Companionship over functionality**: AI companions may replace traditional human interactions, potentially reducing face-to-face socialization.
* **Impact on child development**: While beneficial in some areas, AI could negatively impact emotional and practical skills if used excessively.
* **Concerns about dependency**: Parents' reliance on AI entities as substitutes for human interaction raises questions about child well-being.
