# 9 Tips to Get More Out of Google Chat | WIRED

**Getting More Out of Google Chat**

Google Chat is often overlooked despite its capabilities and seamless connections to other Google apps and services. Here are 9 tips to help you showcase what it can do from editing messages to assigning tasks:

### Create Spaces for Productivity
* **Create a new Space**: Click the "New chat" button, then "Create a space".
* **Add team members**: Give your Space a name and an optional emoji representing the group.
* Assign tasks: File sharing is elegantly handled in Spaces.

### Organize Conversations with Threads
* **Use threaded messages**: In one-to-one chats, group chats, or Spaces, threads allow you to spin off separate tangents.
* **Reply in new threads**: Hover over a message or long-press on a chat to reply in a new thread.
* Pin important messages: Click the three dots next to a chat and choose "Pin".

### Prioritize Conversations
* **Use Pinned Messages**: Pin messages from your favorite contacts to the top of the list.

### Organize Conferences with Sections
* **Create sections**: On the web or mobile, click the three dots next to a chat in the left-hand navigation pane.
* Option: Find "Move conversation" and select an existing section or create a new one.
* View sections: You can view sections on the web and in mobile apps.

By following these 9 tips, you'll be able to make the most of Google Chat's features.
