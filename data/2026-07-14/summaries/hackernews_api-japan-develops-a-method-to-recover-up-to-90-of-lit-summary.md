In a significant step forward for electric vehicles and battery recycling, Japanese scientists have developed a method to recover up to 90% of lithium from used EV batteries. This breakthrough could lead to major changes in the way EVs are made and reused.

## What is Lithium Recovery?

The innovative technique involves using a chemical tweak in the recycling process, which converts around 90% of battery waste into high-purity lithium that can be reused in new batteries. The process does not just recover materials but can cut carbon emissions by around 40% compared to conventional recycling techniques.

## Environmental Benefits

This method could also reduce Japan's reliance on imports and stabilize supply chains, as the country currently relies almost entirely on battery mineral imports. By domesticating lithium recycling, Japan could secure its economic security.

## Remaining Challenges and Future Plans

While this breakthrough is significant, there are still challenges to overcome, such as improving collection infrastructure for used lithium-ion batteries (about 14% of Japanese used lithium is currently recyclable). However, plans include increasing production by 2027 to extract tens of thousands of tons more lithium.
