# Anthropic names former Fed Chair Bernanke to its independent trust

**Anthropic Names Ben Bernanke to Independent Trust**
-----------------------------------------------

Anthropic, a company valued at $965 billion, has appointed former Federal Reserve Chair Ben Bernanke to its Long-Term Benefit Trust. This trust is an independent governance structure that advises Anthropic and appoints its board members.

**Key Points:**

* Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust is its external governance body, advising on AI's economic impact.
* Members do not hold any equity in Anthropic; instead, they receive compensation for their time and service.
* Ben Bernanke will contribute to the trust's mission, helping understand AI's benefits and risks.
* The trust has already brought in other experts, including Neil Buddy Shah, Richard Fontaine, and Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar.

**Bernanke's Role:**

* Assisted in advising Anthropic on its long-term vision for artificial intelligence.
* Will focus on ensuring AI benefits outpace risks to humanity.
* Bernanke will be part of the trust's board members, providing guidance as it grows and develops.
