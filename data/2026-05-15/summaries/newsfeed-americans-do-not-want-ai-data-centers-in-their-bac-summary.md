# Americans do not want AI data centers in their backyards

## Survey overview
- Gallup surveyed 1,000 random U.S. adults (March 2026) and 2,054 panel members (April 2026).  
- 71 % of respondents oppose the construction of AI data centers near where they live.  
- Only 7 % are “strongly” in favor.  

## Opposition compared to other facilities
- Americans would rather live near a nuclear power plant than a data center; peak opposition to nuclear plants was 63 %.  

## Main concerns driving opposition
- 50 % cite resource impacts—especially water and electricity consumption.  
- Additional worries include quality‑of‑life, rising cost of living, pollution, and negative perceptions of AI.  
- A Pew Research survey (May 2026) found 43 % see data centers as a major factor behind soaring power bills.  

## Political breakdown
- Democrats: 75 % oppose.  
- Independents: 74 % oppose.  
- Republicans: 63 % oppose.  

## Reasons for support
- Among the minority that favors new data centers, 55 % point to job creation as the primary benefit.  
- Maine Governor Janet Mills referenced jobs when vetoing an 18‑month moratorium on new data‑center construction in her state.
