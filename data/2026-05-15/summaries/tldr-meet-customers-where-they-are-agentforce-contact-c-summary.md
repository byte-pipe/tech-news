## Salesforce/Agentforce solution
- Salesforce’s native Agentforce Contact Center unifies voice, digital channels, CRM data, and AI agents on a single platform.

## WhatsApp Voice capabilities in Agentforce
- **Seamless inbound digital calls:** Customers tap the phone icon in WhatsApp to call the service team directly.

- **Unified Service Console experience:** Agents handle WhatsApp voice calls together with other channels in one screen, with full CRM context.  
- **Simple provisioning and management:** Admins enable and configure the WhatsApp voice channel from the same setup screen as other channels, without custom code.  
- **Targeted regional support:** Currently available for WhatsApp Business numbers in the U.S., Canada, Mexico, and Brazil; expansion planned for Europe, Australia, New Zealand, and APAC.
