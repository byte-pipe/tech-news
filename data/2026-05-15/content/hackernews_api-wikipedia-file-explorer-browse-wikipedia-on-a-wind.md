FEATURES
-------------

WIKIPEDIA
Explore all of Wikipedia categories as folders, articles should open up as documents. Everything on wikipedia should be accessible unless it's one of the 100 or so pages that don't have a category assigned.

MEDIA
Wikimedia Commons category explorer. Right-click on any image to set it as your desktop background.

GEOFILE EXPLORER
In-progress project I'm working on where you can explore the Earth as a folder on your computer. Drag and drop a image to upload it to the folder, or right click to write a text note.
