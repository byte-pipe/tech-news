# AI chip financing hits a wall

Nov 9, 2026, 8:08am EST
Nov 9, 2026, 8:08am EST
Post
Email
Whatsapp
Copy link
Share
The Stargate project in 2025. Daniel Cole/Reuters.

The AI bet is rapidly turning sideways. After rushing to lend gobs of money for the data-center buildout, now some investors can’t dump it fast enough. We’re hearing about whole portfolios of loans that are being shopped at steep discounts, with GPU financings in particular “getting smoked” over worries the life of those loans will outlive the useful life of the technology, one person told Semafor.

On Friday, Bloombergreportedthat Dutch bank ING was looking to dump $500 million of data-center loans, including a $25 million chunk backing Stargate, the OpenAI, SoftBank, and Oracle project. The next test for Wall Street will be whether Goldman Sachs and Apollo can convince enough investors to buy into a $40 billion chip financing deal for SpaceX, the largest of its kind, which the firms have started marketing.

Some big players have already put a stake in the ground against financing as a whole: “Without casting aspersions on GPU financing,” Ares Management CEO Michael Arougheti said on a recent earnings call, the firm was going to pass on such deals. “I, for one, personally do not know how to underwrite the decline and depreciation curves on that type of technology,” he said.
