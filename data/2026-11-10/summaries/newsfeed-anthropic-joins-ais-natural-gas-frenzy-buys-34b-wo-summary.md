# Anthropic joins AI’s natural gas frenzy, buys $3.4B worth of turbines | TechCrunch

# Summary of “Anthropic joins AI’s natural gas frenzy, buys $3.4B worth of turbines”

## Main points
- Anthropic is purchasing Caterpillar natural‑gas turbines worth $3.4 billion, capable of generating up to 1.7 GW of electricity.  
- The company, which has historically preferred renting compute power (e.g., $1.25 B/month from SpaceX’s xAI until 2029), is shifting to owning power infrastructure amid growing uncertainty in the power market.  
- The turbines were originally ordered by equipment supplier OnePWR, which could not secure financing; Anthropic bypassed OnePWR and bought directly from Caterpillar to retain the capacity.  
- The move reflects a broader trend: major AI players (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta) are building on‑site natural‑gas power plants for their data centers, reviving fracking and potentially driving U.S. data‑center gas consumption above that of Germany and Japan combined by 2035.  
- Dependence on natural gas exposes firms to price volatility; analysts warn prices could triple by decade’s end, raising the risk of buyer’s remorse for Anthropic.  

## Context
- Anthropic plans to go public this year and disclosed its compute‑rental arrangements in its S‑1 filing.  
- The natural‑gas turbine backlog extends into the early 2030s, and grid‑connection queues are similarly long, making the purchase a “last‑resort” solution for reliable power.
