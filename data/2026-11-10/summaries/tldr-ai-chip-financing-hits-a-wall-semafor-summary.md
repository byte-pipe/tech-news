# AI chip financing hits a wall

## Key developments
- After a rapid surge of capital for AI data‑center construction, investors are now scrambling to offload loan portfolios at steep discounts.  
- GPU‑specific financing is under particular pressure because loan maturities may outlast the useful life of the hardware, raising concerns about asset depreciation.  

## Recent actions
- **ING** (Dutch bank) intends to sell roughly **$500 million** of data‑center loans, including a **$25 million** tranche linked to the Stargate project (backed by OpenAI, SoftBank, and Oracle).  
- Wall Street’s next test is a **$40 billion** chip‑financing package for **SpaceX**, being promoted by Goldman Sachs and Apollo, which must attract sufficient investor interest.  

## Industry stance
- **Ares Management** announced it will steer clear of GPU financing. CEO Michael Arougheti said he cannot reliably underwrite the decline and depreciation curves of such technology, emphasizing the firm’s cautious approach.
