## Let’s Talk About Gears
- Gears multiply torque and change speed, essential for winding a stiff spring with modest child force.  
- Small axle gear provides mechanical advantage (high torque).  
- Larger gears increase wheel speed after torque is generated.  
- Meshed gears always rotate opposite directions, a fact used throughout the mechanism.

## Gear Train of the Car
- Six unique gears cooperate inside the toy:
  - Axle gear, spring gear, wind‑up gear (left and right sides).  
- In **drive mode** the left and right gear assemblies work independently.  
- In **wind mode** (car pressed down) the whole gear system links to wind the spring.  
- Wind‑up gears are permanently meshed and rotate together.

## Driving vs. Winding Modes
- **Drive mode:** gears on each side operate separately, allowing the car to roll.  
- **Wind mode:** gears connect, converting wheel rotation into spring winding.
