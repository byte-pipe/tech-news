# Apple acquires Swift development firm Rabbit 3 Times

## Background
- Rabbit 3 Times, Inc. was founded in 2021 in New York and incorporated in Delaware.  
- The company created a visual Swift development tool called **Play** (Play: Create Better Apps).  
- Play combined elements of Shortcuts and Xcode, allowing developers to prototype Swift iOS and macOS projects and export them to Xcode via a paid service.  
- The tool won an Apple Design Award for Innovation in June 2025.

## Acquisition details
- In February 2026 Apple reported a deal with Rabbit 3 Times to the European Union under the Digital Markets Act.  
- The arrangement is not a full takeover; Apple acquired specific assets and obtained an option to hire selected staff.  
- The transaction may serve as an acquihire or asset‑strip, enabling Apple to integrate talent and technology into its own projects.

## Post‑acquisition actions
- Shortly after the deal, Rabbit 3 Times announced it would stop supporting Play on iPhone and Mac as of 20 April 2026.  
- The paid “Play to Xcode” export service was made free to facilitate the transition.  
- The company’s website was taken down, leaving only a brief note: “We’re working on something new… It has been an incredible journey.”  
- It is possible Apple will incorporate Play’s capabilities into Apple Creator Studio, though no concrete plans have been disclosed.

## Regulatory context
- Acquisitions of a certain size affecting EU users must be reported under the Digital Markets Act.  
- The EU publishes these reports no earlier than four months after filing.  
- The EU’s listing describes Play/Rabbit 3 Times as providing “iOS and macOS tools for designing, prototyping, and generating SwiftUI code in real‑time.”
