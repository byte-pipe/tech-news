# Update from Amazon Engineering Leadership on the Role of the Developer in the Age of AI

## Overview
- AI is rapidly reshaping software development, but core SDE responsibilities remain unchanged.  
- Amazon introduces the concept of the **Renaissance Developer**, who blends AI tools with human judgment and domain expertise.  
- The memo is addressed to all Software Development Engineers (SDEs) and Software Development Managers (SDMs).

## Guideline Development Process
- Historically, SDE role guidelines avoided technology‑specific language to accommodate Amazon’s diverse businesses.  
- Senior Principal and Distinguished Engineers debated whether AI warrants a different approach because its impact is faster and more fundamental than past trends (cloud, microservices, serverless).  
- The decision: focus on immutable outcomes—impact on customers, businesses, and teams—rather than on specific tools or techniques.

## AI Adoption Expectations
- Engineers should **learn and apply AI now** to avoid falling behind.  
- Those already using AI should continue refining practices and measuring effectiveness.  
- Recognize that best practices will evolve; definitions written today may become outdated within a year.

## Metrics and Measurement
- Long‑term outcomes (customer impact, business value) are the preferred measurement, though they can be delayed and hard to isolate.  
- Input metrics (e.g., token usage, AI tool adoption, code throughput) are useful as proxies when applied with judgment.  
- Caution against using flawed metrics as targets, which can increase costs (token‑maxxing vs. frugality) and complexity (LOC vs. invent and simplify).  
- Recommend monitoring:
  - **10th percentile**: teams with low AI adoption to understand barriers.  
  - **90th percentile**: teams with high AI adoption to verify delivery gains.

## Leadership Responsibilities
- Leaders must develop deep, hands‑on understanding of AI tools and be able to articulate where they help or hinder production work.  
- Ownership includes connecting AI inputs to meaningful outputs and balancing exploration of new methods with investment in proven practices.  
- Emphasize “learn and be curious” while maintaining delivery results, highest standards, and customer focus.

## Recommendations for Individuals and Teams
- If AI tools do not benefit a particular workflow, document the finding, share with peers, and discuss solutions with managers.  
- Encourage ICs and SDMs to stay close to their products and customers to maximize AI‑driven innovation.  
- Prioritize curiosity‑driven learning combined with disciplined execution to achieve the greatest impact from AI adoption.
