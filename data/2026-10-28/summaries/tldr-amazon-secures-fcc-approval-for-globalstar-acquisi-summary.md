# Amazon Secures FCC Approval for Globalstar Acquisition – Via Satellite

## Transaction Overview
- The FCC approved Amazon’s $10.8 billion acquisition of Globalstar on Tuesday, citing public‑interest benefits.  
- Amazon will obtain 100 % of Globalstar and its subsidiaries; Globalstar will become a wholly‑owned affiliate of Kuiper Systems LLC (Amazon Leo) while keeping its corporate name.  
- The deal gives Amazon direct‑to‑device (D2D) satellite capabilities and access to Globalstar’s spectrum, infrastructure, and LEO constellation.  
- Amazon has not announced a revised closing date; the original expectation was a 2027 close.

## FCC Findings
- The FCC concluded the transaction will expand data coverage in unserved or underserved regions, enhance emergency communications, and improve industrial/enterprise data services.  
- Amazon’s substantial prior investment in satellite networks and its pending D2D license suggest intensive use of the MSS spectrum.  
- The agency noted the acquisition will enable a well‑financed operator to offer stronger D2D services, positioning Amazon to compete with SpaceX’s planned 15,000‑satellite D2D fleet.  
- No horizontal or vertical antitrust concerns were identified because Globalstar focuses on MSS connectivity and wholesale IoT capacity, whereas Amazon Leo provides fixed‑satellite high‑speed broadband.

## Competitive Landscape
- Only one objection was filed, from Yippy, which is in an arbitration dispute with Globalstar; the FCC dismissed the concern.  
- The FCC observed limited head‑to‑head competition: Globalstar’s MSS/IoT services differ from Amazon Leo’s fixed‑satellite broadband offerings.

## Regulatory Updates and Future Satellites
- In April, Amazon filed to launch an Amazon Leo D2D constellation of up to 5,105 satellites using L‑band and S‑band spectrum acquired from Globalstar.  
- The FCC recently authorized Globalstar’s C‑3 constellation (48 LEO satellites plus six spares) built by MDA Space, intended to boost capacity and performance for Globalstar’s D2D services.  
- Amazon plans for the new Leo constellation to operate alongside its first‑ and second‑generation broadband systems and Globalstar’s existing LEO fleet.

## Timeline and Next Steps
- The FCC approval moves the transaction a step closer to completion, but Amazon has not disclosed a new expected closing timeline.  
- Continued regulatory filings and satellite deployments are anticipated as Amazon integrates Globalstar’s assets and expands its D2D network.
