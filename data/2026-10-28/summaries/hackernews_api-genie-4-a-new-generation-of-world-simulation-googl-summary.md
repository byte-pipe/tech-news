# Genie 4: A new generation of world simulation  

## Overview  
- Genie 4 is DeepMind’s latest world‑model that delivers photorealistic visuals, fluid motion, and native audio in real‑time interactive simulations.  
- The model is natively multimodal, generating synchronized video, sound (music, ambient noises, dialogue) and physics‑based behavior.  
- Built on the Gemini family: Gemini Omni supplies realistic appearance and behavior; other Gemini models provide the reasoning needed for coherent, continuous worlds.  

## Core capabilities  

### Real‑time world shaping with natural language  
- Users can modify simulations on the fly (e.g., change weather, move the camera, add objects or characters) via text prompts.  
- Gemini interprets prompts in context, adapting the scene while autonomously steering it to maintain continuity.  

### Advanced realism in physics and human action  
- Incorporates an enhanced physical understanding (aerodynamics, collisions, material dynamics).  
- Human characters move naturally, react organically to their environment, and can be steered interactively.  

### Multimodal generation  
- Audio is generated directly by Genie 4, matching visual events (e.g., ambient lake sounds, saxophone timbre, wind).  
- No separate audio‑track stitching is required; sound and visuals are tightly coupled.  

## Applications  
- Safe, infinitely variable training environments for robots and AI agents.  
- Creation of new interactive experiences, virtual productions, and immersive storytelling.  
- Potential use in research, education, and consumer‑facing interactive media.  

## Release strategy  
- Currently available as a research preview for trusted testers.  
- Early access provided to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie, Gemini API partners, and Google AI Studio users.  
- General consumer and developer release planned after feedback collection; interested parties can join the waitlist via Project Genie.
