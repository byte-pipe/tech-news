# Announcing our $100M raise | Hex

# Summary of “Announcing our $100M raise | Hex”

## Background and Motivation
- After a record quarter, Hex decided to rebuild its product from a clean slate.
- The original platform (built 2019‑2021) was not truly “agent native” and faced:
  - Bloated agent harness with hundreds of tools.
  - Limited compatibility with external agents.
  - Compute engine not suited for high‑speed, high‑concurrency workflows.
- Data work was spreading across tools like Cursor, Codex, Claude, and custom agents, creating fragmentation and trust issues.
- The team concluded that a first‑principles, code‑centric approach was needed to support flexible, trusted data workflows.

## Hex 4.0 Overview
- Hex 4.0 is a complete rewrite designed for the era of AI agents.
- Core assumptions:
  - Everything is code; plain code (not proprietary YAML) provides flexibility.
  - Teams require context, controls, and guardrails for trust and scalability.
- The new architecture enables features that were impossible on the old platform.
- A detailed technical blog post is available for deeper reading.

## Funding Round
- Hex closed a $100 million financing round at a $1.2 billion valuation.
- Lead investor: Peak XV; participation from 137 Ventures, Amplify, Avra, a16z, Redpoint, Sequoia, and Zero Prime.
- Capital will be used to expand the team, accelerate go‑to‑market, and continue product development.

## Vision for Business Intelligence
- The traditional BI paradigm is considered obsolete; merely adding chatbots to legacy tools won’t revive it.
- Future BI will be rooted in code with built‑in context, governance, and guardrails.
- Hex aims to enable anyone to ask questions and build data products while restoring trust and control.
- The company anticipates a consolidation of software categories into new, agent‑native platforms.
