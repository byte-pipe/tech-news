For professional photographersand photography enthusiasts, Adorama has long been the go-to for new and used cameras, lenses, accessories, and services such as repairs, rentals, and photo printing. The retailer has since expanded into home electronics, audio, and musical instruments—even dropping “camera” from its name, though that remains the heart and soul of the company. With roots dating to the 1970s, this New York City institution is among the most recognized retailers for the latest photographic equipment, backed by knowledgeable staff. We often link to Adorama in ourcamera buying guides and reviewsbecause it is a trusted retailer.

If you’re shopping for a new camera or tripod, stocking up on Kodak film, or need a custom photo book made for gifting, check out these Adorama discounts before you check out. Adorama doesn’t require an Adorama Camera promo code or Adorama Camera coupon; instead, it prefers to drop prices from the get-go. Photography can be expensive, especially now that nearly all electronics have increased in price, so these Adorama promos and deals can help lessen the sting.

## Score Big Savings on Top Brands With Adorama’s Deals and Specials

The best place to find what’s on sale at Adorama is itsDeals and Specials section. Discounts are automatically applied, so there’s no code to enter. Save on cameras and lenses from Sony, Nikon, Canon, and Panasonic; accessories like speedlights, memory cards, and cases; computers, including MacBooks; home entertainment from the likes of Bose, Klipsch, and Onkyo; and much more. As with any sale, do some price comparison before you buy to make sure you can’t get it cheaper elsewhere.

## Earn Up to $200 in VIP Rewards When Shopping at Adorama

Nearly every retailer has a loyalty program, and Adorama’s is VIP Rewards. It’s free to join, and you earn points every time you shop, which you can redeem as a cash discount on future purchases. VIP Rewards is straightforward: 500 points knock $5 off, while 20,000 points save $200. You can earn points in many ways beyond shopping, including referrals, product reviews, and special promotions. If you’re a frequent shopper, you should get rewarded. Did we mention it’s free?

## Check Adorama’s Clearance Items for Potential Savings

Closeout items can be great deals if you don’t mind a discontinued item. Many are still supported and under warranty, but they may not be the latest and greatest or a big seller. Whatever the reason,Adorama’s clearance sectionis worth browsing. We spotted high-end hybrid tablet computers from Lenovo, Casio digital pianos, pro-grade flash media from SanDisk, Dell Thunderbolt displays, Polaroid instant film, and more. As with any sale item, make sure you can’t find it cheaper elsewhere and that it can be returned for a refund if you change your mind later.

## How to Get an Adorama Student Discount of Up to 20% on Camera Gear

Teachers and students can save up to 20 percent on new camera gear and up to 10 percent on used equipment when they sign up for Adorama’sClassroom Connect Program. It’s free to join, but you will need to verify that you are an educator or a student, regardless of discipline. Discounts range from 5 to 20 percent, depending on the product. Not all products qualify, but participating brands include Sony, Panasonic, BenQ, Fujifilm, and more. Even better, you can stack the education discount on top of a sale item (Black Friday is around the corner). To register, simply sign up on the Classroom Connect Program page.
