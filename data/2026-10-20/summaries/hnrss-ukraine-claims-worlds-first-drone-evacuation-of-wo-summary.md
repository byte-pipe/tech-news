# Ukraine claims world’s first drone evacuation of wounded soldier

## Overview of the mission
- Ukraine’s 92nd Separate Assault Brigade released a video showing an injured infantryman being evacuated by a heavy‑lift hexacopter drone.
- The soldier strapped himself into the drone, which flew at low altitude over blurred terrain and lowered him to the ground, with the operator greeting “Welcome back to the ground!”
- The drone, originally designed as a bomber, was adapted to carry a human without instability.

## Reasons for using a drone
- Intense enemy drone activity and extensive landmines made conventional unmanned ground vehicle evacuation impossible.
- The brigade’s specialists attempted a method “never done before” to rescue the wounded soldier.

## Details of the rescued soldier and operation
- The soldier is receiving treatment and rehabilitation.
- Squad leader/programmer “Napalm” praised the soldier as a “hero” for agreeing to the mission.
- Ukraine’s Ground Forces stated that its drone units are now conducting operations once considered science fiction.

## Broader context
- The war has become a technological arms race, with both sides relying heavily on drones to reduce casualties.
- Ukraine has advanced its drone capabilities, using them to disrupt Russian logistics and strike targets deep inside Russia, including oil refineries.
- Despite these innovations, Kyiv faces pressure from the United States to limit strikes on Russian oil sites and has reported restrictions on intelligence sharing and on Elon Musk’s Starlink service, which supports drone operations.

## Related developments
- Prime Minister Serhii Kuretskyi announced an “experimental project” to employ drones for medical logistics, allowing transport of medicines, blood samples, and donor organs between hospitals, especially in remote areas.
- The initiative requires coordination with the military and will give drones a special status and identifiable markings in the air.
