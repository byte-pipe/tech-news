# CERN and Mistral join forces to accelerate AI for science

## Partnership overview
- CERN and Mistral have signed a statement of intent to create a long‑term strategic partnership.
- The collaboration combines CERN’s expertise in fundamental physics, massive scientific data, and large‑scale computing with Mistral’s capabilities in training large‑scale AI models.
- Initial focus is on high‑energy physics, with the possibility to extend AI solutions to the wider scientific community.

## Large Physics Model
- Aim: develop a single pretrained “Large Physics Model,” the first foundation model for collider physics.
- Training data: billions of simulated proton‑proton collisions from the Large Hadron Collider (LHC).
- Intended functionalities:
  - Reconstruct particle collisions.
  - Identify particles.
  - Flag unexpected signals.
  - Evaluate future detector designs.
- The model seeks to replace the current chain of specialised reconstruction algorithms with a unified, multi‑task AI system.

## Motivation and context
- Current LHC data analysis relies on a sequence of specialised algorithms; machine learning already improves many individual steps.
- Foundation models can learn across the entire reconstruction chain, offering broader and more flexible capabilities.
- CERN stores roughly one exabyte (one million terabytes) of LHC experimental data; the upcoming High‑Luminosity LHC (HL‑LHC) will increase collision rates tenfold around 2030, providing unprecedented data volume for AI development.

## Statements from leaders
- Enrica Porcari, CERN Chief Information Officer: high‑energy physics offers a uniquely rigorous environment for AI development; the partnership will produce AI tools that can be shared across the physics community.
- Johannes Brandstetter, Vice‑President of AI for Engineering at Mistral: the collaboration will push the frontiers of high‑energy physics and may lead to new scientific discoveries through large‑scale AI.

## Broader impact
- By uniting European strengths in AI and fundamental science, the partnership aims to create a new generation of AI tools capable of handling the scale, precision, and complexity of modern scientific research across Europe and beyond.

## About Mistral
- Mistral is a global AI laboratory that builds state‑of‑the‑art open‑weight models, products, and infrastructure for enterprises, public institutions, and advanced industries.
- Headquarters are in France, with a strong international presence in Europe, North America, and Asia.
- More information: www.mistral.ai.
