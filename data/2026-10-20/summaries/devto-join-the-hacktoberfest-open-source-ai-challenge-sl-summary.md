# Hacktoberfest Open‑Source AI Challenge – Week 3 Summary  

## Overview  
- Week 3 runs from Oct 19 to Oct 25, 2026.  
- Focus: building new projects with open‑weight models or open‑source AI.  
- One overall winner plus winners in 20 partner categories (total 21 winners).  

## Theme – “Slow and Steady”  
- Aligns with International Sloth Day (Oct 20).  
- Goal: create tools that do the work for you, saving effort or helping relax.  
- Example project ideas:  
  - Overnight chat summarizer.  
  - Offline bedtime‑story or ambient‑sound generator.  
  - Fridge‑photo‑to‑grocery‑list converter.  
  - Automated file/inbox organizer.  
- Submissions must explain why the open‑source approach matters (offline use, privacy, cost‑free, etc.).  

## Prize Categories  
- **Overall winner:** $250 USD, DEV++ membership, exclusive DEV badge.  
- **Featured categories (6):** $200 USD + badge each (e.g., Best Use of Render, TabPFN, Tinker, Arduino, DigitalOcean, Gemma).  
- **Partner categories (14):** $100 USD + badge each (e.g., Auth0 for AI Agents, Browserbase, ElevenLabs, GitHub Copilot, MongoDB Atlas, Sentry Agent Tracing, etc.).  
- Projects may enter multiple categories but can win only once per challenge.  
- Partners provide free credits/promo codes (claim at hacktoberfest.com/my).  

## Judging Criteria  
- **Writing quality** (most weight): clear, engaging post describing the build, target users, and open‑innovation benefits.  
- Relevance to theme and open‑source AI core.  
- Creativity of the idea.  
- Technical execution and functionality.  
- Optional: meaningful use of a partner technology for that category.  

## How to Participate  
1. Publish a DEV post using the provided submission template.  
2. Describe the project, effort saved, demo (optional), and open‑innovation rationale.  
3. Include any partner tech usage if entering a partner category.  
4. Review judging criteria, rules, and FAQ before submitting.  

## Important Dates  
- **Oct 19:** Week 3 starts.  
- **Oct 20:** International Sloth Day.  
- **Oct 25, 11:59 PM PDT:** Submission deadline.  
- **Week of Oct 26:** Winners announced.  

## Looking Ahead  
- Week 4 launches Oct 26 with a new theme; registration is open for notifications.
