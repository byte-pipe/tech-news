# Amazon and AEP to explore deployment of up to 960MW of X‑energy SMRs in Indiana

## Main Highlights
- Amazon and American Electric Power (AEP) are studying the installation of up to 960 MW of small modular reactors (SMRs) in Indiana.  
- The plan calls for 12 X‑energy Xe‑100 reactors, each delivering 80 MW electric (200 MW thermal).  
- Amazon will finance the project and act as the first offtaker, using the power for its data centers and supplying excess to AEP’s grid.  
- Expected economic impact: >1,000 construction jobs and >100 permanent positions.  

## Context and Related Projects
- This is the second Amazon‑X‑energy deal with a regional utility; the first (2024) involved Energy Northwest in Washington for a similar 960 MW capacity at the Cascade Advanced Energy Facility.  
- Amazon’s broader goal: deploy >5 GW of nuclear capacity in the U.S. by 2039.  

## Technology Overview
- Xe‑100 is a pebble‑bed, high‑temperature gas‑cooled reactor using helium coolant and TRISO fuel pebbles.  
- Design claims inherent safety (no active cooling needed) and suitability for urban proximity.  

## Company Activities and Partnerships
- X‑energy also pursues:
  - 6 GW projects in the UK with Centrica.  
  - Up to 1.44 GW projects in Pennsylvania with PJM and Talen.  
  - A U.S. facility to produce TRISO‑X fuel.  
  - Collaboration with Doosan Enerbility and Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power to mobilize up to $50 bn for Xe‑100 deployment and supply‑chain expansion.  
- Funding: >$700 m raised, latest round led by Amazon; IPO valuation shifted from $11.9 bn to ~ $4.1 bn.  

## Industry Landscape
- Other hyperscalers backing SMRs include Google, Data4, Oracle, Switch, and Equinix.
