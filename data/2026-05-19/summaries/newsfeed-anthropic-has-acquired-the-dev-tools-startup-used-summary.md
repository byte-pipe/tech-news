# Anthropic has acquired the dev tools startup used by OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare | TechCrunch

# Anthropic acquires Stainless

## Deal overview
- Anthropic announced the acquisition of Stainless, a startup founded by former Stripe engineer Alex Rattray.  
- Financial terms were not disclosed; The Information reported talks of a purchase price above $300 million.  
- Stainless is backed by Sequoia Capital and Andreessen Horowitz.

## Changes to Stainless products
- Anthropic will wind down all hosted Stainless offerings, including the SDK generator.  
- Existing customers retain ownership of SDKs they have generated and may modify or extend them freely.  
- Future access to Stainless’s SDK tools will be limited to Anthropic, removing them from competitors.

## Technology and its relevance
- Stainless automates creation and maintenance of software development kits (SDKs) from API specifications.  
- Supports multiple languages: Python, TypeScript, Kotlin, Go, and Java.  
- Automatically updates SDKs as APIs evolve, eliminating manual maintenance.  
- Used by AI labs such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Replicate, Runway, and Cloudflare to connect AI agents with external software.

## Impact on Anthropic
- Anthropic states Stainless’s software has powered every official Anthropic SDK since the API’s inception.  
- The acquisition consolidates a key infrastructure supplier under Anthropic’s control, limiting competitor access.

## Founder’s perspective
- Rattray founded Stainless to give SDKs the same level of care as the APIs they wrap.  
- He highlighted Anthropic’s early support and expressed confidence that the combined teams can continue work on the platform where it matters most.
