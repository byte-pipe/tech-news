# Apple’s Siri revamp could include auto-deleting chats

## Key Points
- Apple is expected to showcase a revamped Siri at WWDC in June, emphasizing privacy as a core theme.  
- The new Siri will be delivered as a stand‑alone app powered by Google Gemini, offering a ChatGPT‑like chatbot experience.  
- Unlike other AI chatbots, the app will impose stricter limits on how long user data is retained, with a possible auto‑delete feature similar to iMessage that can remove conversations after 30 days, one year, or keep them indefinitely.  
- Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman suggests Apple may use privacy positioning to offset Siri’s functional gaps relative to competitors, while noting that Google will handle part of the security infrastructure.
