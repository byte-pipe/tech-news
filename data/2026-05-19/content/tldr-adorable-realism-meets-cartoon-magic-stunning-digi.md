# Adorable Realism Meets Cartoon Magic: Stunning Digital Paintings By Russian Artist Lera Kiryakova

Lera Kiryakova is a brilliant Russian sketch artist and illustrator renowned for her unique ability to transform real-life faces into enchanting, stylized masterpieces.

She has gained a massive international following by recreating famous celebrities and pop culture icons with a signature “big-eyed” aesthetic that perfectly balances anatomical resemblance with cartoon-like charm. With over a decade of professional experience in the gaming industry and freelance illustration, she meticulously crafts her work using Adobe Photoshop, focusing on expressive facial features and delicate textures. Beyond her viral portraits, she is the author of “Portrait with Markers” and continues to inspire aspiring artists through her collaborations with global creative brands like Wacom and Copic.
