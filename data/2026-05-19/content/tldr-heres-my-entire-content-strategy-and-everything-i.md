description: 'here''s my entire content strategy and everything i believe about building a brand on LinkedIn in one post: 1. your personal brand sits at the intersection of the problem your prospect has, the product/service/expertise you offer + your personal stories, passion + POV 2. your content is a funnel designed to to 3 things: build the right audience (top), command authority with that audience (middle), convert the 5% of that audience that is ready to buy (bottom) 3. people follow people on social media for 3 reasons and 3 reasons only: education, entertainment, encouragement. 4. if you want to build an audience, go broader. talk about industry pain, current events, market insights and relatable stories from your own life/work 5. if you want to build authority, give away all your best information for free. get stupid tactical. if someone can''t turn around and apply the post RIGHT NOW, it''s probably not a great playbook. 6. if you want to convert that audience and authority into
  pipeline, show the results. how have you done it for customers or yourself in the past? what did you achieve? can someone reading this relate to the problem and desire the outcome enough to reach out to you to ask about it? then tell them you''re taking on clients or offer to help. 7. the algo is just the audience 8. your profile should be about you, not your business 9. the company profile is a landing page, your people are the brand 10. hooks determine 80% of the posts success 11. video has excellent engagement but terrible reach (right now) 12. either go very lo-fi with images or incredibly detailed infographics (the middle does nothing for you or anyone) 13. you can build a whole brand without ever posting content (just show up in the comments and be helpful) 14. if a post does badly, rework the hook and try it again (no one saw it, they won''t care) 15. if a post does well, recycle it and post it again in a new format 16. you have 100 connection requests each week, you should be maxing
  them out (connect with peers, prospects + potential mentors, mentees or people you hope to work with/for someday) 17. study high performing content from people OUTSIDE of your niche (you want to stand out from everyone in your niche) 18. if you don''t know WHO you are talking to you''re basically just screaming into the ether 19. if you talk about more than 3 things, no one will ever know what you actually are an expert in 20. you have to be willing to take sh*t to the same degree that you''re willing to be vulnerable 21. post between 8-11a local time (for the market you SELL to) 22. if you post 5x a week for 3 months and follow the above list, it will be impossible not to grow your brand 23. some content gets great reach but little engagement, some content gets terrible reach but the right engagement - you need both to build your brand + your pipeline. ⬇️save this for later. ♻️repost this if you found it helpful. or don''t, idgaf. | 122 comments on LinkedIn'
