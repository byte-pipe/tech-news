### Go beyond the LinkedIn profile

There’s a difference between knowing who someone is and actually having a conversation with them. A 15-minute meeting can tell you more about a founder than weeks of following their company online. A conversation with an investor can help you understand what they are really looking for. Meeting another operator can surface an answer to a challenge you assumed was unique to your team.

And sometimes the useful conversation is the one you didn’t schedule. The founder sitting next to you before a session. The investor asking the question you were thinking about. The person you meet after a roundtable because you’re both trying to solve the same problem. That proximity matters.

### Make three days work for you
