# Summary of “‘I wish Apple would just stop that crap’: Apple has added persistent ‘ads’ to iOS, and it’s driving users crazy”

## Overview
- iOS now shows promotional banners in the Settings app for Apple services such as iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple TV, and AppleCare+.
- These promos often cannot be dismissed; they disappear only after the offer expires (sometimes weeks) or after the user purchases the service.
- Users have expressed strong frustration on social media, comparing the ads to Microsoft’s Start menu ads.

## User Reaction
- Many iOS users posted complaints on platforms like Reddit, Twitter, and Facebook, calling the ads “annoying” and urging Apple to stop them.
- Some users reported that the “dismiss” button, when present, does not work.
- The timing of the ads coincides with recent product launches (iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro), intensifying user irritation.

## Apple’s Services Push
- Apple is increasingly focusing on services revenue (iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple TV, AppleCare+) as growth in hardware slows.
- Embedding service promotions directly in iOS aligns with this strategic shift, despite contradicting Apple’s premium‑experience brand image.
- The company appears willing to accept user backlash to drive service subscriptions.

## Possible Bug
- Certain ads, like the iCloud+ promotion, appear for users who already have an active subscription, suggesting a potential bug.
- It is unclear whether Apple will treat these instances as errors to be fixed or as intentional persistent ads.

## Future Outlook
- Apple plans additional ads, potentially for its upcoming Visual Intelligence system, indicating the practice will likely continue.
- Users should not expect Apple to reverse this approach in the near term.
