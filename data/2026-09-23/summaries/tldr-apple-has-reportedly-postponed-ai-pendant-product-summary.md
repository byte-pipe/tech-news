# Apple has reportedly ‘postponed’ AI pendant product

## Main points

- Apple recently added “Audio Intelligence” features to the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, marking its first AI‑enabled wearables.  
- Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reports that the company’s separate AI pendant project—an always‑on camera and microphone accessory meant to feed visual data to Siri—has been put on hold.  
- The pendant was envisioned as a necklace‑style device that could also be attached to clothing or other body locations.  
- Apple’s current AI hardware focus has shifted to further enhancing the Apple Watch, introducing AirPods with cameras, and planning smart glasses slated for 2027.  
- Gurman also notes that Apple is exploring a new health‑oriented, screen‑less wearable aimed at competing with WHOOP.  
- No timeline has been provided for the postponed pendant, and the article invites reader opinions in the comments.

- The piece includes promotional links for iPhone discounts and accessories such as AirPods Pro 3, MagSafe car mounts, and AirTag accessories.
