# App Store ecosystem reaches record $1.4 trillion as developers continue to thrive globally

## Key Highlights
- 2025 App Store ecosystem facilitated over **$1.4 trillion** in developer billings and sales.  
- More than **90 %** of this value was generated without developers paying Apple a commission.  
- Consumer‑facing AI apps grew **4 ×** faster in billings than other top‑100 apps.  

## Economic Impact
- **Physical goods & services:** $1.1 trillion (grocery, food delivery, retail, travel).  
- **Digital goods & services:** $149 billion (games, enterprise, video streaming).  
- **In‑app advertising:** $151 billion.  
- Weekly average users: **850 million** across **175** countries/regions.  

## AI‑Driven Innovation
- AI integrated into health, fitness, photo/video editing, and productivity apps, delivering personalized and offline‑capable features.  
- Apple’s **Foundation Models** framework lets developers use on‑device large language models free of cost, preserving privacy.  
- **Xcode 26** introduces “agentic coding,” enabling more autonomous AI‑assisted development and faster iteration.  

## Global & Regional Growth
- Billings/sales doubled in **China** and more than tripled in the **U.S.** and **Europe** over the past six years.  
- **General retail** is the top category worldwide; secondary categories vary by region (e.g., travel in U.S./Europe, food delivery in Korea, grocery in China).  

## Commitment to Developers
- End‑to‑end support: Xcode, TestFlight, App Analytics, custom product pages, editorial placements.  
- Infrastructure investments for secure commerce, digital goods, and subscriptions.  
- **Developer Centers** hosted thousands in the U.S., China, India, Singapore; new center opening in Berlin.  
- Nearly **20 Developer Academies** and numerous Foundation Programs across Brazil, Indonesia, Italy, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, and the U.S.  
- Ongoing resources: Meet with Apple sessions, labs, multi‑language support, Pathways, Developer Forums.  

## Upcoming WWDC
- First look at new features, APIs, and technologies to further empower developers.  
- Over **100** video sessions, Group Labs, and community discussions.  
- Content available on the Apple Developer app, website, YouTube, LinkedIn, WeChat, and bilibili.
