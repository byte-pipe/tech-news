# AI Is Slowing Down – Summary

## Main Argument
- I contend that the hype‑driven AI boom is built on a financial house of cards that cannot sustain itself without massive new revenue streams.
- The narrative that generative AI can “slow down” is misleading; the industry actually needs to **accelerate revenue growth** to cover the colossal capital expenditures being undertaken.

## Financial Scale of AI Infrastructure
- Sightline Climate’s February data shows **190 GW** of data‑center capacity planned.
- Using NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang’s estimate of **$80‑100 billion per GW**, the build‑out costs range from **$9.5 trillion to $15 trillion**.
- To keep these facilities afloat, annual revenue of roughly **$1.75 trillion** (or at least **$875 billion** if only half is built) is required, assuming a PUE of 1.35 and a charge of $12.5 million per megawatt.

## Debt and Funding Challenges
- The Financial Times notes banks fear they will “choke” on data‑center debt; current issuance is about **$250 billion per year**, far below the **$500 billion‑$1 trillion** needed annually.
- Hyperscalers (Google, Meta) are resorting to large equity sales, a sign that **debt financing is becoming scarce**.

## Company‑Specific Pressures
- **NVIDIA** projects **$1 trillion** in revenue through 2027, but **54 %** of its income comes from three unnamed clients (likely ODMs for Microsoft, Google, Meta), making its outlook highly dependent on a few partners.
- **Anthropic** has secured **$95 billion** in funding but faces compute commitments of **$330 billion** and must generate **$174 billion** in annual revenue by 2029; it will need **another $200 billion** within a year.
- **OpenAI** anticipates burning **$852 billion** by 2030, with compute commitments of **$770 billion**; the recent **$122 billion** round is insufficient, and at least **$250 billion** more funding will be required by year‑end.

## Implications for the AI Bubble
- The entire generative‑AI ecosystem must produce **over $2 trillion** in annual revenue by 2030 to justify the data‑center capex and meet compute commitments.
- Outside of Anthropic and OpenAI, **very little demand** exists; the market is heavily concentrated in a few loss‑making giants.
- If the required revenue growth does not materialize, the bubble is likely to **collapse**, exposing the debt overhang and the fragile reliance on a handful of mega‑clients.
