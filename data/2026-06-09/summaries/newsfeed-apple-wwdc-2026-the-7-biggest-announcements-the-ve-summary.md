# Apple WWDC 2026: The 7 biggest announcements

## Siri’s AI revamp
- Siri upgraded to “Siri AI” powered by Apple Intelligence, co‑developed with Google.  
- Enables back‑and‑forth conversations with detailed, engaging answers.  
- Adds image editing and writing tools, and uses onscreen awareness to pull context from the current screen.  
- Standalone Siri AI app provides access to conversation history.  
- Rolls out to iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro.

## iOS 27 AI enhancements
- Access Siri AI by swiping down from the Dynamic Island; responses appear there.  
- New “Siri Mode” in the Camera app recognizes objects in viewfinder and provides information.  
- Introduces an opacity slider for the Liquid Glass design.  
- Supported on iPhone 11 and newer models.

## macOS 27 “Golden Gate”
- Siri integrated directly into Spotlight, allowing queries and file‑specific questions.  
- Liquid Glass design adjustable via a new slider; tighter window corner radius.  
- Restores colorful sidebar icons.

## Safari AI features
- AI‑driven tab management groups open webpages by topic.  
- “Notify Me” alerts when webpage content changes (e.g., product back in stock).  
- Automatic password updates for compromised credentials.  
- Users can create extensions by describing them in natural language.

## Apple Intelligence across core apps
- Messages suggests photos to share based on conversation context.  
- Phone app surfaces relevant info such as flight confirmation codes when a call starts.  
- Mail offers richer suggestions and lets users add calendar events via description.  
- Shortcuts can be created with natural‑language input.  
- Image Playground enables AI edits to photos, including composition changes and distraction removal.

## Home app 4K and AI video tools
- Generates textual descriptions of camera clips.  
- AI stitches footage from multiple cameras together.  
- Supports 4K playback on compatible cameras.  
- Natural‑language search through recorded clips.

## Parental controls and Screen Time redesign
- New child‑account system lets parents select allowed apps and content, and require permission for new website visits.  
- Redesigned Screen Time menu simplifies management of device usage for children.
