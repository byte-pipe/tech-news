# 44 things coming to your Apple devices that you might have missed

## Overview
- The WWDC 2026 keynote highlighted Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, but many smaller updates were also announced.  
- Developer betas for the new operating systems are available now; public betas start in July and the final releases are slated for fall 2026.  

## iOS 27
- Liquid Glass receives an opacity slider.  
- Apps launch up to 30 % faster; AirDrop transfers up to 80 % faster.  
- Seamless switching between Wi‑Fi and cellular on iPhone and iPad.  
- Mail shows a “most relevant” ranking at the top and loads messages faster.  
- Dual Capture mode for FaceTime on iPhone 17, 17 Air, 17 Pro.  
- Independent alarm‑volume setting.  
- Automatic synced captions for videos you watch.  
- Apple Maps FlyOver adds aerial imagery with visual‑intelligence detail.  
- Custom EQ presets for AirPods Max 2, AirPods Pro 3, AirPods 4.  
- Home app groups related activity notifications via Apple Intelligence.  
- Supported HomeKit Secure Video cameras stream and record in 4K.  
- Health app cycle‑tracking can notify of perimenopause‑suggestive patterns.  
- Camera powers up faster in Low Power mode.  
- Extra‑large widgets are now possible.  
- Failed messages are automatically retried.  
- Save a video frame as a photo.  
- Consolidated notifications for multiple tapbacks.  
- Smoother unlocking (details not specified).  
- Emoji and sticker keyboards load faster.  

## macOS 27 “Golden Gate”
- Refined design with enhanced Liquid Glass for better readability and consistency.  
- Safari adds tab groups and page‑change monitoring with notifications.  
- Improved Spotlight search suggestions.  
- Support for ultrawide displays up to 5K @ 120 Hz.  
- Swipe‑down refresh gesture in apps like Safari and Mail.  

## iPadOS 27
- File transfers to external drives up to 5 × faster.  
- Unread message count in Messages syncs more quickly after returning to the iPad.  
- Seamless Wi‑Fi ↔ cellular handoff similar to iOS.  

## watchOS 27
- New dynamic app grid.  
- Single‑tap opens a Smart Stack widget.  
- Workout Buddy works offline and is localized in Spanish.  
- More accurate indoor distance tracking.  
- Faster music playback start.  
- Step count in Fitness now matches the Health app.  
- Custom QR‑code passes can be created in Wallet for memberships or cards.  

## visionOS 27
- Siri AI appears as a virtual glassy orb in the real world.  
- Curved windows and extra‑small widget size.  
- Preview notifications by looking at them.  
- Convert panorama photos into “spatial scenes” for Personal Environments.  
- Redesigned Control Center.  
- Wi‑Fi connectivity up to three times faster.  
- Safari supports 360‑degree web experiences.
