# Apple announces iPadOS 27

## Overview
- Announced at WWDC 2026; public release expected in the fall of 2026.  
- Developer beta available immediately, public beta slated for next month.  

## Major Features
- **Performance upgrades**  
  - Apps launch up to 30 % faster via intelligent preloading.  
  - File transfers to USB‑C external drives and AirDrop up to five times faster.  

- **Siri AI integration**  
  - Accessible through Spotlight search, automatically distinguishing between search and assistant prompts.  
  - New dedicated Siri app offers ChatGPT‑style conversational experience and chat history.  
  - Powers natural‑language shortcut creation in the Shortcuts app.  

- **Screen Time and parental controls**  
  - Schedule app‑access windows and set usage limits per app/service.  
  - New APIs for parental controls and a Declared Age Range API for age‑appropriate app experiences.  
  - Child accounts can restrict iMessage contacts, require approval for website visits, with safety mode auto‑enabled for users under 13.  

- **Safari enhancements**  
  - “Organize Tabs” automatically groups open tabs by site content.  
  - “Notify Me” alerts for price drops, restocks, and other page changes.  

- **User interface refinements**  
  - Updated “Liquid Glass” visual language with improved readability, uniform refraction, and higher contrast.  
  - Sharper app icons and a customizable slider to adjust translucency from ultra‑clear to fully tinted.  

- **Accessibility improvements**  
  - VoiceOver receives better image‑description capabilities.  
  - Automatic caption generation for all videos, with on‑the‑fly translation to other languages.  

- **Photos app upgrades**  
  - Apple Intelligence‑driven editing tools fill empty areas, reframe shots, and enhance images.  
  - Simplified collaboration on iCloud Shared Albums via iCloud.com.  
  - Full‑resolution photo viewing and slideshow export to video.  

- **Multitasking and windowing**  
  - Menu bar can be set to “always show,” making the iPad experience closer to macOS.  

## Compatibility
Supported iPad models include:
- iPad Pro (M4 and later), 12.9‑inch (4th gen and later), 11‑inch (2nd gen and later)  
- iPad Air 13‑inch (M2 and later), 11‑inch (M2, M3, M4, and 4th gen and later)  
- iPad (A16) 9th gen and later  
- iPad mini (A17 Pro) 6th gen and later
