# Banks and FinTechs Are Sitting on the Most Powerful AI Dataset in Finance

## Overview
- Nvidia released a blueprint for banks to replace many separate AI systems with a single model trained on their own transaction data.  
- The model can handle fraud detection, credit scoring, risk assessment, and product recommendations simultaneously.  

## The Fragmentation Problem
- 65 % of financial institutions already use AI, but most have built dozens of disconnected models.  
- This sprawl slows innovation, forces repeated retraining, and prevents knowledge sharing across use cases.  

## Unified Model Approach
- Train one foundation model on the full history of a bank’s transactions.  
- The model learns patterns such as frequency, device, location, and timing, enabling it to interpret any payment in context.  
- New use cases can be deployed instantly without additional training cycles.  

## Real‑World Implementations
- **Revolut (PRAGMA)**: Trained on 40 billion transactions from 25 million customers in 111 countries; a single system now handles credit decisions, fraud detection, and recommendations, cutting setup time to “no time required.”  
- **Mastercard**: Developing a model on billions of anonymized card transactions, stripping personal identifiers and focusing on spending patterns.  
- **Stripe**: Payments foundation model trained on tens of billions of transactions; raised fraud detection for a common fraud type from 59 % to 97 %, blocked ~$112 billion in fraud and cut average fraud rates by 38 %.  

## Business Benefits of Consolidation
- Reduces compounding costs of maintaining multiple models.  
- Enables faster market entry and continuous improvement across product lines.  
- Even tiny gains in payment‑clearance rates (e.g., 0.1 %) translate into significant revenue for merchants, as shown by Adyen.  

## Industry Adoption and Support
- Nvidia’s blueprint is intended for smaller banks that lack Revolut’s data volume or Stripe’s engineering resources.  
- Consulting firms such as Infosys, EXL, and ThoughtWorks assist institutions in integrating the unified model into credit, servicing, and compliance workflows.  
- The unique asset for each institution is its own historical transaction data, which cannot be replicated by competitors.  

## Conclusion
- Consolidating AI into a single transaction‑data‑driven model offers operational efficiency, competitive advantage, and faster innovation for banks and FinTechs.  
- Nvidia’s framework, together with consulting support, provides a practical starting point for institutions of all sizes to harness the most powerful AI dataset in finance.
