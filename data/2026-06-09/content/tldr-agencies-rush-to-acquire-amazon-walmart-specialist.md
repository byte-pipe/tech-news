# Agencies rush to acquire Amazon, Walmart specialists amid retail media boom

## Agencies are acquiring Amazon and Walmart specialists to scale retail media capabilities, as rising ad spend on e-commerce platforms drives demand for talent, expertise and consolidation across the ad industry.

By

| 
June 8, 2026, 18:16:01 IST
Share
Follow Us
Rising demand for retail media expertise is driving agencies to acquire Amazon and Walmart-focused specialists, as brands increase spending on e-commerce advertising and seek platform-specific performance capabilities.

Advertising and commerce agencies are increasingly snapping up specialists focused on Amazon and Walmart as competition intensifies in the fast-growing retail media segment, according to an Adweek report.

A wave of acquisitions is underway as independent agencies look to build scale and deepen expertise in e-commerce and retail media. Firms such as Podean, Harvest Group and EVOQ Group are actively acquiring smaller specialist companies that focus on Amazon and Walmart marketplaces.

The trend reflects a shift in the advertising landscape, where retail media—advertising within e-commerce platforms—has become a major battleground for brands and agencies. Amazon and Walmart, which dominate the space, are drawing increased marketing budgets due to their ability to target consumers closer to the point of purchase using first-party data.

With larger holding companies having already acquired many established commerce agencies in recent years, independent agencies are now turning to smaller deals to build capabilities. These acquisitions are aimed at enhancing expertise in areas such as marketplace optimisation, retail media buying and performance marketing across platforms like Amazon Ads and Walmart Connect.

Private equity is also playing a key role in driving this consolidation. Investment firms backing agencies are enabling them to pursue multiple acquisitions and expand more aggressively in the retail media ecosystem.

The demand for specialised talent is rising as retail media continues to grow rapidly. Agencies are looking for professionals with deep knowledge of platform-specific tools, data analytics and campaign optimisation, as brands increasingly prioritise measurable, performance-driven marketing strategies.

This shift is part of a broader transformation in digital advertising, where retail media is emerging as one of the fastest-growing channels, reshaping how agencies structure teams and deliver services to clients.

As competition intensifies, agencies are focusing on building integrated capabilities across Amazon, Walmart and other commerce platforms to offer end-to-end solutions. The ongoing deal activity suggests that access to specialised talent and marketplace expertise is becoming a critical differentiator in the evolving adtech and commerce landscape.
