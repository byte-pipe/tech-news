# A $2,000 AI‑generated film will make its debut at Tribeca

## Overview
- **Film:** *Dreams of Violets* – a 75‑minute fictional dramatization of the Iranian government’s mass killing of protestors in January.  
- **Premiere:** Tribeca Festival, June 10, 2026.  
- **Cost:** Approximately $2,000 to produce.

## creators and company
- Created by brothers Ash and Pooya Koosha, who left Iran in 2009.  
- Pooya co‑founded Fountain 0, the production company; Ash serves as CEO.  
- Fountain 0 claims the film is the first full‑length, live‑action AI‑generated movie accepted into a major festival.

## AI tools used
- **Images:** Google’s Nano Banana.  
- **Video generation:** Kling AI.  
- **Language editing:** Anthropic’s Claude.

## Production background
- The story is built on journalistic reports, photographs, and eyewitness accounts.  
- The brothers note that without current AI capabilities the film would not have been possible.  
- They acknowledge industry concerns about AI’s impact on jobs and livelihoods.

## Industry context
- AI is increasingly adopted in Hollywood:  
  - Netflix launched an AI animation studio and acquired Ben Affleck’s AI startup.  
  - Amazon Prime Video ordered three AI‑generated animation series.  
  - Critterz, a film powered by OpenAI’s now‑shuttered Sora tool, is seeking a new AI partner.  
- A prior AI‑generated film, *Hell Grind*, screened at Cannes as a side event, not in the main program.

## Significance
- *Dreams of Violets* demonstrates that low‑budget, fully AI‑generated live‑action features can reach prestigious festival platforms, signaling a potential shift in film production economics and creative workflows.
