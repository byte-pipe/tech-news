description: '"First say it straight, then say it great" (Luke Sullivan) This principle completely changed how I write ad copy. Before that, I thought copywriters just sat down and conjured brilliant headlines. Now I understand that good lines are written in two steps: 1/ Say it straight: Say the message, plain and clear 2/ Say it great: Use a literary device to make that message stick Here are some examples I like: ALLITERATION • Straight: Framer helps you create beautiful websites. • Great: Ship sites with style. RHYME • Straight: Wise helps 1M+ users reduce transaction fees. • Great: Join millions saving billions. PUN • Straight: Vanta makes compliance and reporting easier. • Great: Compliance that doesn''t SOC 2 much. PARALLELISM • Straight: Lemonade insurance is trusted by both renters and landlords. • Great: Renter tested. Landlord approved. EPISTROPHE • Straight: v0 is an AI platform for brainstorming, building, and deploying apps. • Great: Think it. Build it. Ship it. ANTITHESIS
  • Straight: MyMind helps you save and organize notes and files. • Great: Remember everything. Organize nothing. PAIN QUESTION • Straight: Intercom replaces overpriced software your team hates. • Great: Tired of expensive, complicated software your team doesn''t want to use? REVEALS • Straight: Monday.com helps you build complex workflows quickly. • Great: Rome wasn''t built in a day... but your dream workflow can be. PLEONASM • Straight: Basecamp is an intuitive project management tool. • Great: Refreshingly simple project management. PERSONIFICATION • Straight: Ramp automatically organizes your receipts. • Great: Receipts now organize themselves.
