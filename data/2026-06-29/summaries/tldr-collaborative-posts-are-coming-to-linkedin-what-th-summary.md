# Collaborative Posts Are Coming to LinkedIn — What They Are and How to Make Them Work

## What is a LinkedIn Collaborative Post?
- A post that can be authored by two or more LinkedIn accounts (personal profiles and/or Pages).  
- All collaborators’ names appear at the top of the post.  
- The post is delivered to each collaborator’s network simultaneously.  
- Distinct from LinkedIn’s “Collaborative Articles” (AI‑prompted explainers) and from simple tagging/mentioning.

## How LinkedIn Collaborative Posts Work
- **Invite‑and‑accept flow** similar to Instagram collabs.  
- One account creates the post and invites other profiles or Pages.  
- Invited accounts must accept before their names appear and the post goes live to all networks.  
- For Pages, a super‑admin must approve the invitation and have collaboration notifications enabled.  
- Unknowns: maximum number of collaborators, edit/delete rights after publishing, and analytics sharing.

## How to Create a Collab Post (Beta)
1. Ensure the feature is enabled: Me → Settings → Notifications → Collaborations (both personal and super‑admin for Pages).  
2. When available, start a new post, add collaborators, and wait for their acceptance.  
3. Prepare before launching:  
   - Identify the people, brands, or Pages you truly want beside your name.  
   - Optimize your profile (headline, banner, recent posts) because the post will drive traffic there.  
   - Craft high‑quality content; the feature amplifies reach but does not replace good content.  
4. You can schedule surrounding content in Buffer while waiting for the feature to roll out.

## Why This Matters for Creators and Brands
- **Credibility trade:** creators bring authentic voice and engaged audience; brands provide a co‑sign that appears as a professional endorsement.  
- Shared authorship signals endorsement more strongly than a tag, adding “professional currency.”  
- Shifts the dynamic from a one‑sided sponsorship to a mutual partnership where both audiences and reputations are leveraged.  
- Aligns with LinkedIn’s Creator Marketplace (launched June) that connects brands and creators directly.

## Six Ways Creators and Brands Can Use Collab Posts
- **Brand partnerships:** co‑author a post with a brand Page for joint reach and authentic endorsement.  
- **Co‑marketing with a peer:** two creators or brands combine forces on launches, reports, or events.  
- **Team amplification:** employees co‑author with the company Page, adding a human face to corporate messages.  
- **Event and Live recaps:** hosts, co‑hosts, and guests share a single recap that spreads across all participant networks.  
- **Launches and announcements:** joint product or feature announcements show shared ownership.  
- **Customer and community spotlights:** feature the story’s subject as a co‑author for higher resonance.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Difference from Collaborative Articles?**  
  Articles are AI‑prompted explainers inviting perspectives; posts are co‑authored pieces with chosen collaborators.  
- **Launch timeline?**  
  Currently in beta with select creators and brands; broader rollout expected over the coming months.  
- **Can a Page collaborate with a creator?**  
  Yes—both profiles and Pages can be collaborators, provided a super admin approves the invitation.
