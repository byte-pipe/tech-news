- In a crisis of faith, Josaphat meets the Christian hermit Barlaam, converts to Christianity, and persists despite his father’s opposition.
- Eventually Abenner shares the throne, later converts himself, and after many years Josaphat retreats to the desert to live as a monk with his former teacher.

- Through successive retellings, the Buddhist narrative was reshaped into a Christian hagiography.
