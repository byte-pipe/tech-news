# Hacktoberfest Weekend Challenge Summary

## Overview
- First of five weekend‑sized challenges, timed so participants have most of Saturday and Sunday worldwide.  
- Focus on building a brand‑new project with open‑source AI at its core; no pull‑request count or repo hunting required.  
- Submission deadline: 5 October 2026, 6:59 AM UTC.  

## Prompt: Build for a Friend
- Create something that solves a real problem for a specific person you care about, using open‑source AI (open‑weight model, agent framework, local inference, etc.).  
- Example ideas:  
  - Meal planner aware of a roommate’s allergies.  
  - Language‑practice partner for a friend learning a new language.  
  - Tool that converts a grandparent’s voice memos into a family recipe book.  
- In the DEV post, explain why open innovation matters (offline operation, data privacy, fine‑tuning, cost‑free, etc.).  
- Bonus points for actually handing the project to the friend and sharing their feedback.  

## Prize Structure
- **Overall winner**: $250 USD, DEV++ membership, exclusive DEV badge.  
- **Featured category winners (6)**: $200 USD + exclusive badge each.  
- **Partner category winners (10)**: $100 USD + exclusive badge each.  
- All participants with a valid submission receive a completion badge.

## Judging Criteria
- **Writing Quality** (heaviest weight): clear, engaging post that explains the build, the intended user, and the value of open innovation.  
- **Relevance to Prompt & Theme**: open‑source AI must be central to the project.  
- **Creativity**: original idea or fresh take on a familiar problem.  
- **Technical Execution**: functional and well‑built solution.  
- **Use of Partner Technology** (optional): meaningful integration if entering a partner category.  

## How to Participate
1. Publish a DEV post using the provided submission template.
