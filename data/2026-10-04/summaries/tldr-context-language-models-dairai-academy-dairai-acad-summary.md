# Context Language Models

## Overview
- CLMs are language models that manage their own context by treating it as a mutable file.
- The model can edit this file; changes are synced for the next interaction.
- Multiple agent contexts can coexist as separate files, enabling swarms and sub‑agents.

## Zero‑shot Performance Gains
- On BrowseComp‑Plus: 11.4 % higher accuracy with 21.5 % fewer FLOPs.
- On a 12‑hour EdgeBench: 5 % higher scores with 59 % fewer FLOPs.
- On a 24‑hour multi‑repository agent‑swarm task: 65 % greater improvement at equal compute.

## Learning Strategies
- Natural‑language instructions generated by a skill‑optimization loop raise held‑out ContextBench accuracy by up to 35.9 points.
- Online reinforcement learning with a success‑gated efficiency advantage improves Qwen3.5‑9B on BrowseComp‑Plus by 47.6 % while using 12 % fewer FLOPs.

## Serving Cost Reduction
- Edits in the middle of the context break prefix caching.
- Introduced Suffix Cache Reuse, cutting server‑side compute by 35 % compared to standard SGLang at matched performance.

## Stated Risk
- A model‑editable context creates a new channel for injected or self‑written instructions that can persist across turns.
