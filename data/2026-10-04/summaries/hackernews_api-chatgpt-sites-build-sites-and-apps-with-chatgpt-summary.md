# ChatGPT Sites | Build sites and apps with ChatGPT

# ChatGPT Sites Overview

## How it works
- Describe the desired website or app to ChatGPT (or mention @Sites); you can attach files, visual references, or start from an existing Codex project.  
- ChatGPT builds the site and opens it in an in‑app browser for testing before publishing; you can request layout, content, or feature changes.  
- When ready, publish the site, choose the audience (selected people, workspace, or public), and share the link.  
- Return anytime to edit and republish; teammates in the same workspace can be invited to edit.

## Key features
- **No coding required** – plain‑language descriptions are turned into functional sites.  
- **Collaboration** – invite workspace members to edit and publish updates.  
- **Visitor persistence** – “Sign in with ChatGPT” lets users save progress; built‑in storage handles files and data.  
- **Tool integration** – in eligible workspaces, visitors can grant read‑only access to their connected tools; the site only sees data the visitor already can access.  
- **Custom URLs** – rename the hosted ChatGPT URL or attach a custom domain; old URLs redirect automatically.  
- **Privacy & security** – initial access limited to creator and workspace admins; sharing controls are managed via ChatGPT sign‑in.

## Frequently asked questions
- **What is ChatGPT Sites?**  
  A platform to create, host, and share websites, apps, and games through conversational interaction with ChatGPT.  

- **Do I need to code?**  
  No; describe what you want in plain language. Existing compatible projects can also be published.  

- **Who can use Sites and at what cost?**  
  Available in public beta for eligible ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans; limits vary by plan and region.  

- **Who can visit my Site?**  
  Initially only you and workspace admins; later you can invite specific people, share with the workspace, or make it public, depending on plan settings.  

- **Can teammates edit my Site?**  
  Yes; invited workspace members can edit and publish after the first publication.  

- **Can I change the Site address?**  
  Yes; rename the hosted URL or connect a custom domain, with automatic redirects from the old address.  

- **Can a Site use data from my connected tools?**  
  In eligible Business/Enterprise workspaces, visitors may grant read‑only access to their own connected tools; the Site never accesses the creator’s connections.  

- **Can I schedule updates?**  
  Yes; ask ChatGPT to set a recurring schedule, review it, and manage the task. Visitor tool data is only available during active sessions.
