## Make a Site that works for you

Control who can access your Site, let visitors save their work, connect tools, and choose its address.

#### Choose who can visit or edit

Share your Site with selected people or your workspace, or make it public. Invite teammates to edit and publish updates.

#### Let visitors save progress

Add “Sign in with ChatGPT” so visitors can pick up where they left off. Use built-in storage to save information and uploaded files, including documents and images.

#### Connect the tools you use

In eligible workspaces, people using your Site can approve read-only access to their own connected tools. The Site only sees information they already have permission to access.

#### Choose your Site address

Rename your ChatGPT-hosted URL or connect a custom domain you own. If you rename the hosted URL, the old address redirects automatically.

## Built with privacy, security, and control

When you create a site for the first time, only you and workspace admins have access to it. You can share with selected people, your workspace, or the public. Sites shared within a workspace use ChatGPT sign-in to control access. Public Sites can optionally offer “Sign in with ChatGPT” for personalized experiences.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is ChatGPT Sites?

ChatGPT Sites lets you create, host, and share websites, apps, and games with ChatGPT. Start with an idea, refine it through conversation, and return anytime to make changes.

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe what you want in plain language and ChatGPT builds the Site with you. If you already have a compatible project, you can ask ChatGPT whether it can be published with Sites.

### What can I build?

Create dashboards, team trackers, client portals, landing pages, calculators, games, and more. Sites can also save progress and uploaded files so people can come back and continue their work.

### Who can use Sites, and what does it cost?

Sites is available during public beta with eligible ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans. Availability,usage limits⁠, and workspace controls vary by plan and region. See theHelp Center⁠for current access and limits, orcontact support⁠.

### Who can visit my Site?

New Sites start limited to you and workspace admins. Depending on yourplan⁠andworkspace settings⁠, you can invite selected people, share with your workspace, or make the Site public. External viewer invitations are rolling out oneligible plans⁠and may require approval.

### Can teammates edit my Site?

Yes. Owners can invite active members of the same workspace to edit. After the owner publishes the Site once, editors can make changes and publish updates. Editors can also access data saved in the Site. External viewers can’t edit.

### Can I change my Site’s address?

You can rename your ChatGPT-hosted URL or connect a custom domain you own. If you rename the hosted URL, the old address redirects to the new one.

### Can a Site use information from my connected tools?

In eligible Business and Enterprise workspaces, people in the same workspace can choose to let a Site read information from supported tools they’ve connected to ChatGPT. The Site only sees what that person already has permission to access. It does not use the creator’s connections or make changes in those tools.

### Can I schedule updates to a Site?

Yes. Ask ChatGPT to suggest a schedule for recurring Site work, then review and manage the scheduled task. Information from a visitor’s connected tools is only available while they’re using the Site and isn’t available to scheduled tasks running in the background.
