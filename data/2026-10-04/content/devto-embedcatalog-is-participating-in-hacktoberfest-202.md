Hi everyone! A little late, perhaps, but in this article, I’d like to share that my new project is participating in Hacktoberfest.

I have been participating with projects for three years now, and this year was no exception. Today, I would like to talk about this in more detail.

Well, let's get started! 🏎

## 🔎 About Hacktoberfest

First of all, I would like to briefly explain what kind of festival this is. Hacktoberfest is a month-long celebration of open source throughout October. It’s free, and it’s for everyone, wherever you are in your open-source AI or software creating journey.

It is worth noting that this year, in-person events are also being held in many cities around the world.

## 💎 My new project

It is called EmbedCatalog, and its purpose is to create an embed that you can add to your website, README, and so on.

Over the course of a month, the core functionality required for the catalog was implemented. Features such as user registration, project statistics, custom embeds, and much more were added.
