# eMudhra Exits Global SSL/TLS Certificate Business

eMudhra Press Release

October 22, 2023

Share
eMudhra Limited, a global digital trust company, has decided to voluntarily withdraw from its global SSL/TLS certificate business after a recent incident.

The Board has made a decision to exit this part of its business due to increasing data sovereignty and compliance requirements, which are causing significant costs and complexity. This decision is not due to any issues with the SSL/TLS itself, but rather its diminishing role in eMudhra's overall business.

eMudhra plans to sharpen its focus on core enterprise solutions and trust services other than SSL/TLS. The company will continue to provide core services, including RSA roots, digital signatures, and identity solutions, while transitioning customers to its SSL/TLS certificate services. The transition is expected to be seamless, with affected customers replaced and certificate revocation and replacement services continuing until every issued certificate has expired or been replaced.

The decision was made by eMudhra's Board, which believes that concentrating on core enterprise solutions provides better return on investment (ROI) and allows the company to maintain its core services and business performance.
