# Don't Be a Proxy Between Me and Your AI - DEV Community

**Don't Be a Proxy Between Me and Your AI - DEV Community**

**The AI Question Problem**

Many people debate using AI as a communication shield, but it often devolves into a passive role where they're merely opening a browser to a AI assistant.

**The Pushback**

However, a common argument is that the AI is taking on the role of a decision-maker. The AI suggests a framework for navigating complex issues, but it often agrees too much with the AI's recommendations.

**The Reality Check**

A more realistic approach is to have a more nuanced conversation. A questioner should demand more from their AI assistant, encouraging them to debate which trade-offs are acceptable and which assumptions are challenged.

**The Guardrail**

The AI becomes a guardrail for the user, offering a final recommendation. But when the user has a disagreement with the AI's suggestion, it's not a disagreement at all – it's just a difference in opinion.

**The Test**

However, it's not as simple as tweaking a formatting change. To truly evaluate an AI's understanding, a person needs to demonstrate knowledge of their constraints, past experiences, or team preferences.

**In Real-Life Examples**

This issue is commonly seen in AI interactions, such as when someone is exploring different approaches to solving a problem and is seeking advice on what works and what doesn't.

**Key Takeaways**

To maintain a meaningful conversation with an AI, it's essential to demand more than just agreement. A genuine disagreement should be grounded in something the AI couldn't know, such as a specific constraint or incident.

**Encouraging a More Nuanced Approach**

Instead of relying on an AI as a proxy, users should strive to engage with the AI in a more nuanced and interactive way. This means being more explicit about their needs, constraints, and preferences, and encouraging the AI to respond with more depth and insight.

By taking a more active role in the conversation, users can ensure a more effective and engaging interaction with the AI.
