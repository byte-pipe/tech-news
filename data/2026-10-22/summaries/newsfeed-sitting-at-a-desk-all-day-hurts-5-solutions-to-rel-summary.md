# Sitting at a desk all day hurts. 5 solutions to relieve aches and pains : NPR

**5 Simple Ways to Relieve Aches and Pains from Prolonged Sitting at a Desk**

Sitting at a desk all day can be detrimental to our health, causing a range of discomforts including back pain, eye strain, and fatigue. However, there are several simple ways to alleviate these symptoms and even mitigate some health risks associated with prolonged sitting. Here are 5 effective strategies:

### 1. Take Walking Breaks

Take a 5-minute walk every 30 minutes to counteract the harm caused by prolonged sitting. This simple habit can lead to improved productivity and lower fatigue levels. A 2023 study by Columbia University Medical Center found that walking for 5 minutes every 30 minutes resulted in higher productivity and lower fatigue.

**Recommended: Walk for 5 minutes every 30 minutes.**

### 2. Rotate Your Spine

To alleviate back pain caused by slouching, try rotating your spine by mimicking the natural movements of the torso. In 2023, physical therapist Vinh Pham developed a 3D T-spine rotation exercise to loosen up the middle section of the spine and reduce stress.

**Modify this exercise:**

**Sit up straight** and stand up, then drop your right foot behind your left, about a foot apart.

### 3. Practice Ergonomic Solutions

Implement ergonomic solutions such as adjusting your chair height and typing position to reduce the strain on your muscles and joints.

### 4. Stretch Regularly

Perform regular stretches to relieve fatigue and promote flexibility. Try simple stretches like shoulder rolls, neck stretches, and wrist extensions.

### 5. Consider Ergonomic Accessories

Take advantage of ergonomic accessories like standing desks, document holders, and keyboards with adjustable heights to promote good posture and reduce the risk of discomfort.

Don't wait until it's too late; start taking small steps to alleviate discomfort and reduce the risks associated with prolonged sitting.
