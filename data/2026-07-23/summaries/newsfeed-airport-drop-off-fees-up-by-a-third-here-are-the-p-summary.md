# Airport drop-off fees up by a third - here are the priciest - BBC News

# Summary of “Airport drop‑off fees up by a third – here are the priciest”

## Overview
- Drop‑off fees at the UK’s 20 busiest airports have risen by roughly one‑third since summer 2025.  
- The RAC motoring group analysed charges for the closest “kiss‑and‑fly” points to terminals.  
- 16 of the airports increased their prices during the past year.

## Key Findings
- **Most expensive:** Gatwick Airport – £10 for 10 minutes.  
- Stansted Airport – £10 for 15 minutes at its “express set‑down”.  
- London City Airport – £8 for 5 minutes (£1.60 per minute), the highest per‑minute rate.  
- Birmingham Airport – £7 for 15 minutes (47 p per minute), the cheapest among the sample.  
- Average rise: £1.70 increase to a current average of £7 per drop‑off.  
- EU comparison: none of the top 10 EU airports charge for drop‑offs.  
- Free alternatives exist at many airports, such as longer‑stay car parks with shuttle buses; Blue Badge holders are often exempt.

## Stakeholder Reactions
- **RAC (Rod Dennis):** Calls fees “farewell fees” and argues they conflict with global airport practice; warns about “park now, pay later” zones that may trigger parking notices.  
- **Aviation Environment Federation (Celeste Hicks):** Supports fees as incentives to shift travelers to public transport, reducing congestion and pollution that can cause childhood illnesses.  
- **Airports UK:** States fees help manage congestion, local community traffic and air‑pollution concerns, and meet climate‑change objectives mandated by government.  
- **Business Travel Association (Clive Wratten):** Criticises fees for increasing business costs and questions the shifting justifications (environment, congestion, route funding, tax offsets).  
- **Gatwick spokesperson:** Claims charges deter “kiss‑and‑fly” trips and encourage use of 120 linked train stations, 24/7 bus routes, free long‑stay car‑park drop‑offs, and Blue Badge exemptions.

## How to Avoid Unexpected Charges
- Verify fee amounts and payment methods before travel; check for Blue Badge exemptions.  
- Use cheaper or free alternatives such as distant car parks with shuttle service.  
- Explore public‑transport options (train, bus) to the airport.  
- Keep drop‑off time short to stay within the free or paid limit.  
- Confirm whether taxi fares include the drop‑off charge.
