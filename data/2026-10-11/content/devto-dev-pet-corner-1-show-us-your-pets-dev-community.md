Every Sunday, let's share pictures, stories, and memories of our pets, animals we've met, and even the adorable animals we love following on Instagram or elsewhere online. Share as many pictures and links as you'd like! You don't need to own a pet to join in. If animals make you smile, you're welcome here ❤️

## ❤️ Why I'm starting this

I've wanted to start this series for the longest time, but somehow, I kept putting it off. Today, I'm finally doing it!

If there's one thing you should know about me, it's thatI LOVE PETS.

My family had a dog when I was very young, and although we haven't had a pet since, I've always had a soft spot for animals.

When I was doing my master's at the University of Oklahoma, I used to take pictures of dogs I came across on campus. Being away from home, those little encounters often made my day. Sometimes, a quick moment with a dog between classes was enough to brighten an otherwise ordinary day. I still have those pictures, and looking back at them brings back some lovely memories ❤️

If you've read my personal reflection posts, you probably know that I often talk about howour health and well-being come first.We're so busy studying, building careers, and working towards our goals that we sometimes forget to slow down and enjoy life along the way.

That's what inspired this series. We spend so much time talking about tech here on DEV, and I thought it would be lovely to have a little Sunday tradition where we can share something different, get to know each other beyond our work, and enjoy the simple happiness that animals bring us 💛

## 🐶 Show us your animal friends!

Now, let's get to the fun part!

I'd love to see your pets, whether they're the ones waiting for you at home, the ones you grew up with, or the ones who have a special place in your heart.

And you don't need to have a pet of your own to join in! Maybe you've met a friendly dog on a walk, spotted a cat in your neighborhood, or come across an animal on campus that made you stop for a moment. Perhaps you follow an adorable animal account on Instagram or have a favorite animal video you keep going back to.

If it involves an animal you love or something that makes you smile, I'd love to see it!

Share pictures, funny moments, sweet memories, little stories, or links to your favorite animal posts. Share as many pictures as you'd like. There's no limit!

If you're sharing someone else's content, please link to the original post and respect the creator's wishes about sharing their work.

Don't have anything to share this time? No worries! Just enjoy all the adorable animals in the comments, and join us whenever you'd like.

I'll share a picture of a very cute dog I came across at the University of Oklahoma in the comments. I'm happy to finally share a little memory from my time there!

## 🐾 See you next Sunday!

DEV Pet Corner will be here every Sunday 😃

I've wanted to start this series for a long time, and I'm so happy I've finally decided to give it a try.

I'm not doing this for views, likes, or comments. I simply love animals, and I thought it would be lovely to have something a little different to look forward to each week.

Even if nobody joins in this first edition, I'll still be back next Sunday, and the Sunday after that.Sometimes, an idea is worth trying simply because it means something to you.

If you have any ideas for future editions, I'd love to hear them!

One little note:I know you all are wonderful and kind, but since this is a little off-topic from our usual tech posts, I just wanted to mention that let's keep the comments friendly, positive, and welcoming to everyone.

I'll be checking the comments, and I'm really looking forward to seeing your animal friends, reading your stories, and enjoying all the cuteness with you!

Thank you for being here for the very first DEV Pet Corner! See you next Sunday!
