- Designers are pushing toward styles that are harder for AI to replicate, seeking a distinct “human” edge.

- The volume of AI‑generated content is rising rapidly, making it harder to identify what is truly human.  
- Example: the AI‑generated “actor” Tilly Norwood was criticized by the Screen Actors Guild for lacking lived experience and emotional depth.

## Cultural consumption in the algorithm age  
- Algorithms now curate music, books, and films, reducing the role of human recommendation.  
- Chayka warns that reliance on algorithmic taste‑shaping may diminish human influence on cultural discovery.  

## Takeaway  
- A growing segment of audiences values the human origin of creative works, creating a market “human premium.”  
- Simultaneously, the accelerating flood of AI‑generated content and algorithmic curation challenges our ability to recognize and preserve that premium.  
- The future will likely involve a tension between convenient AI‑driven production and the enduring desire for authentic human creativity.
