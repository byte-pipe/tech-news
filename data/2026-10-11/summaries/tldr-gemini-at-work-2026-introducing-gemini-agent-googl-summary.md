# Gemini at Work 2026: Introducing the Gemini agent  

## Overview  
- Announced by Thomas Kurian, CEO of Google Cloud, as the single, universal agent for work.  
- Handles questions, knowledge work, image/media creation, code writing and execution from one prompt.  
- Integrated inline across Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Slides, Sheets, Chat, Calendar) and available on web, iOS, Android, Windows, Mac, command line, Microsoft 365, Slack, and third‑party apps.  
- Operates with enterprise‑grade security, administration, and governance.  

## Core architectural principles  
- **Unified agent** – chat, autonomous task execution, and code generation through a single interface.  
- **Omnipresent access** – reachable from any device or channel, also usable as a headless agent.  
- **Persistent execution** – single cloud‑based memory and context that follows the user across sessions and devices.  
- **Multi‑agent orchestration** – creates temporary sub‑agents for multi‑step jobs and “coworker agents” with dedicated identities, email addresses, and storage.  
- **Deeply contextual** – learns from each interaction, leveraging tools, data, and work history; projects can refine context, skills, and tools.  
- **Model choice flexibility** – selects the most suitable model per task from the Gemini family, Claude models, and future private/open models, optimizing quality and cost.  

## Skills, tools, and context  
- **Tools registry** – secure connections to existing software (Confluence, Microsoft Office, Teams, Slack, Workspace, Git, Jira, Salesforce, ServiceNow, BigQuery, Databricks, Postgres, Snowflake, desktop files, MCP servers). Teams can publish tools for company‑wide use.  
- **Skills registry** – modular prompts that encode instructions, knowledge, or workflows; a global library is provided, and teams or individuals can add custom skills. Gemini automatically selects appropriate skills and improves them over time.  
- **Context management** – retains business‑specific knowledge (pricing, product portfolios, departmental norms) and remembers prior interactions to personalize output.  

## Industry‑specific specialization  
- Pre‑built tools, skills, connectors, and knowledge bases for financial services and legal teams.  

## Security and governance  
- Identity and policy management, authorization and permission controls, secure sandboxing, network gateways, and other enterprise safeguards.  

## Cost control mechanisms  
- Multi‑model orchestration and Smart Routing choose cost‑effective models.  
- Real‑time spend caps and other controls help limit expenses.  

## Customer impact and early adopters  
- Over 500 customers processed more than one trillion tokens in the past year; 80 % of Google Cloud customers use AI products, and 90 % of the Fortune 100 use Gemini Enterprise.  
- Early testers include a premium sportswear brand (dynamic model selection for faster market entry), Shopify (frontier‑model blending for merchant data), and PayPal (10 million multi‑model requests weekly).  

## Future outlook  
- Gemini is positioned as both a personal assistant and a team member, capable of acting on behalf of individuals, project groups, or specific organizational roles (e.g., finance analyst).  
- Ongoing expansion of skills, tools, and model integrations will keep the agent up‑to‑date as leading models evolve.
