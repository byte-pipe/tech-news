# UNIX® Certified Products – The Open Brand Register

## Overview
- Official register of UNIX Certified Products maintained by The Open Group.  
- UNIX® certification is a vendor‑neutral, globally recognized benchmark; only systems fully compliant with the Single UNIX Specification may use the UNIX® trademark.

## Advantages of UNIX Certification
- Provides the most stable, portable, and cost‑effective application‑development environment across a wide range of platforms, from mobile devices to mainframes.  
- Guarantees the highest level of availability, scalability, and maintainability for enterprises that want to focus on their business.  
- Enables buyers to specify UNIX conformance in procurements, supports Boundaryless Information Flow™, and reinforces the perception of UNIX as a consistently stable, flexible, and reliable operating system.

## Benefits for Application Developers
- Consistent services and behavior among UNIX® operating‑system implementations.  
- Improved portability and backward compatibility.  
- Faster development through a larger set of standard interfaces.  
- Greater innovation potential due to reduced time spent porting applications.

## Benefits for Users
- Evolutionary approach protects investment in existing systems, data, and applications.  
- Availability of UNIX systems from multiple suppliers gives users freedom of choice rather than vendor lock‑in.

## Certified Products (selected)

### IBM Corporation
- z/OS 3.1 or later with Security Server and C/C++ Compiler on IBM Series processors that support z/OS Version 3.1 or later.  
- AIX version 7.2 TL5 (or later) on systems using CHRP architecture with POWER™ processors.  
- AIX version 7.1 TL5 (or later) or 7.2 TL2 (or later) on systems using CHRP architecture with POWER™ processors.  
- AIX 6 Operating System V6.1.2 with SP1 or later on systems using CHRP architecture with POWER™ processors and 2, 8 or 128‑port async cards.  
- AIX 5L for POWER V5.3 dated July 2006 or later on systems using CHRP architecture with POWER™ processors.  
- z/OS V2R1 or later with Security Server and C/C++ Compiler on IBM zSeries processors that support z/OS Version 2 Release 1 or later.

### Hewlett Packard Enterprise
- HP‑UX 11i V3 Release B.11.31 or later on HP Integrity Servers.

### The SCO Group, Inc.
- UnixWare® 7.1.3 and later for single and multiprocessor systems based on IA‑32 and compatible processors conforming to PC architecture.  
- SCO OpenServer Release 5 and OpenServer Release 6 on single and multiprocessor Intel‑based platforms.
