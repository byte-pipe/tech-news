# ‘Bots have now passed human traffic online,’ Cloudflare boss laments — says agentic traffic wasn’t expected to eclipse real people until next year | Tom's Hardware

## Summary of Main Points
- Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince announced that AI‑driven “agentic” traffic has overtaken human traffic on the web, a crossover that occurred in 2026 instead of the predicted 2027.  
- Agentic traffic refers to sophisticated bots that browse and act like humans (e.g., personal assistants, price‑checkers), distinct from traditional crawlers or malicious bots.  
- Cloudflare’s measurements show AI agents now generate **57.5 %** of HTTP requests, while humans account for **42.5 %**.  
- The data reflects request volume, not user engagement; humans still dominate total time spent on apps, streaming, and scrolling feeds.  
- Typical activities of these AI agents include reading product pages, comparing flights, scraping content for AI models, ordering food, and handling customer‑service interactions.  
- Country‑level breakdown of bot‑heavy traffic: Gibraltar (92.1 %), Singapore (76.4 %), and Iran (76.4 %). High percentages are linked to data‑center density and extensive use of VPN‑based scraping tools.  

## Additional Context
- Cloudflare began classifying traffic into categories such as “signed agents” and “verified bots” only recently, so historical charts are limited.  
- Prince noted the data is “a bit messy,” but the overall trend clearly shows the web is now on the “other side” of the human‑bot split.  
- Despite the surge in automated requests, human users remain the primary consumers of content when measured by time spent.
