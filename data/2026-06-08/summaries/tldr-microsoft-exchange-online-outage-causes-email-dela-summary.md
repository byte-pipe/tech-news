# Microsoft Exchange Online outage causes email delays, failures

## Incident overview
- Microsoft is addressing a widespread service issue affecting the mail flow pipeline for Exchange Online customers in North America, APAC, and Europe.  
- The incident is tracked as EX1331830 and was first acknowledged at 10:33 EDT after reports appeared on social media.  

## Impact on users
- Users experience temporary SMTP deferral errors such as “The maximum number of concurrent connections per resource forest has exceeded a limit, closing transmission channel.”  
- Other error messages include “Connection was closed abruptly (SuspiciousRemoteServerError).”  
- Significant delays in sending and receiving emails, with some messages undelivered for over an hour.  
- The outage is classified as a critical incident due to noticeable user impact.  

## Timeline and response
- 10:33 EDT: Microsoft acknowledges the issue and begins investigation.  
- Ongoing: Engineers review reports to identify root cause, isolate error messages, and determine next troubleshooting steps.  
- 14:24 EDT (June 02): Scope expanded to include APAC and Europe regions; Microsoft analyzes mail‑queue backlog for these areas.
