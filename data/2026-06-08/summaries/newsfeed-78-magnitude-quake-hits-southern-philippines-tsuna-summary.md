# 7.8 magnitude quake hits southern Philippines; tsunami risk for some coasts

## Overview
- A magnitude 7.8 earthquake struck the southern Philippines early Monday morning.  
- Authorities warned of possible tsunami waves affecting several coastal areas.  

## Earthquake details
- Epicenter: 13 km (8 mi) southwest of General Santos City, Mindanao.  
- Depth: 10 km (6.2 mi).  
- Occurrence time: 7:37 a.m. local time.  
- Reported effects: power outages; tremors felt in Indonesia’s North Sulawesi and North Maluku provinces.  

## Tsunami threat
- Pacific Tsunami Warning Center forecast:
  - Up to 3 m (10 ft) waves on some Philippine coasts.  
  - Up to 1 m (3 ft) waves on parts of Indonesia and Malaysia.  
  - Smaller waves possible in Taiwan, Japan, Guam, Papua New Guinea, and other western Pacific islands.  
- Evacuation advice: residents in coastal zones urged to move to higher ground or further inland (statement by Teresito Bacolcol, head of the Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology).  

## Impact and response
- No immediate information on casualties or structural damage.  
- Authorities emphasized preparedness and evacuation measures.  

## Regional context
- The Philippines lies on the Pacific “Ring of Fire,” making it highly susceptible to earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, and about 20 typhoons or tropical storms each year.
