## How to Make the Most of ChatGPT Sites
1. **Select Sites mode** in ChatGPT.  
2. **Specify the goal** (landing page, dashboard, guide, app) and target audience.  
3. **Add context** – upload style guides, logos, mission statements, or other relevant files.  
4. **Provide visual guidance** – describe preferred style, colors, fonts, and include reference images.  
5. **Sketch mockups** – upload photos of hand‑drawn designs if available.  
6. **Give feedback** – use the annotate tool to mark specific changes after the first draft.  
7. **Publish or keep private** – share the link or retain it for personal use.

## Caveats
- Not available on free ChatGPT plans.  
- Heavy usage may hit a beta limit, temporarily blocking new or high‑traffic sites.  
- Site generation takes 10–15 minutes depending on complexity.  
- Limited help resources; OpenAI Academy workshops and a Paul Lipsky video are recommended.

## Alternatives
- **Tilda** – template‑driven, more manual control, modular editing.  
- **Lovable** and **Bolt** – AI‑first site builders, now less necessary for ChatGPT subscribers.  
- **Claude** (Artifacts & Claude Design) – professional interactive sites when deep context is already provided.
