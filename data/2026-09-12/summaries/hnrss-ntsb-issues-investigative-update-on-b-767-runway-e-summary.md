# NTSB Investigative Update on B-767 Runway Excursion Accident in Miami

## Investigation overview
- Date of accident: 6 September 2026; update issued 9 September 2026.  
- Aircraft: Boeing 767‑33A cargo, Flight 7598, operated by 21 Air.  
- Location: Runway 30, Miami International Airport.  
- The NTSB recovered the cockpit voice recorder (CVR) and flight data recorder (FDR) and read them at the Vehicle Recorder Laboratory in Washington, DC.  
- CVR model: Acron Aviation FA2100; FDR model: Ontic SSFDR.  
- CVR provided >2 hours of audio on four channels; FDR recorded ~54 hours of data covering >400 parameters.  
- A CVR analysis group will be convened at NTSB headquarters to produce a transcript.

## Cockpit voice recorder highlights (times relative to end of recording)
- 06:02 – Pilot requested flaps 1°.  
- 04:18 – Crew checked in with Miami tower, cleared to land runway 30.  
- 02:53 – Request for flaps 5°.  
- 02:37 – Landing gear lowered.  
- 02:14 – Request for flaps 15°.  
- 01:42 – Request for flaps 20°; other pilot warned of excessive speed, but no consistent response.  
- 01:23 – Sounds of autopilot disconnect.  
- 01:12 to 00:40 – Series of electronic callouts (altitude, sink rate, “too low terrain”, etc.).  
- 00:41 – Request for flaps 30°.  
- 00:15 – Pilot called for a go‑around.  
- 00:10 – Audio consistent with aircraft leaving the paved surface.  
- 00:00 – CVR recording ends.

## Flight data recorder highlights (times relative to end of recording)
- 00:30 – Nose and right main gear touchdown at 158 kt groundspeed.  
- 00:23 – Brakes applied at 146 kt.  
- 00:19 – Left main gear touchdown at 134 kt.  
- 00:15 – Brakes released; throttles increased to go‑around thrust at 120 kt.  
- 00:11 – Throttles reduced to idle; brakes reapplied; groundspeed 117 kt.  
- 00:07 – Acceleration changes; groundspeed 96 kt.  
- 00:00 – Final recorded groundspeed 65 kt; no deployment of speed brakes or thrust reversers.
