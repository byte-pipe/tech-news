# Amazon makes it easier to buy what you see on Prime Video

## New shopping features
- X‑Ray integration now includes a **Shop** tab that lists products related to the current Prime Video title.  
- “Shop the Scene” uses Amazon Lens visual‑search technology to suggest items inspired by characters’ outfits, home décor, and other on‑screen objects.  
- The second‑screen experience is streamlined: opening the Amazon Shopping app while watching automatically takes users to the title’s storefront, removing the need to type “shop the show.”

## Availability and coverage
- “Shop the Scene” is live on iOS and Android in the United States and works with more than 600 titles.  
- The “Shop the Show” feature expands from 1,300 to over 8,000 titles in the U.S., covering Prime Originals, selected licensed series, and live sports events.

## How it works
- On Fire TV, press the up button on the remote to launch X‑Ray, then select the new Shop tab to browse related products.  
- To purchase, open the Amazon Shopping app; it detects the current video and presents a synchronized feed of items.  
- Using Lens, users can tap an on‑screen image; Lens identifies similar products, though exact matches may not always be available.

## Industry context
- Other platforms are adding shoppable TV experiences: YouTube offers creator merch shelves and shoppable connected‑TV ads, Peacock introduced “Must Shop TV,” Roku enables remote‑based shopping, and Disney has tested shoppable ads on Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN.
