# Age assurance on Claude

## Overview
- Claude is restricted to users who are 18 years old or older.  
- During account setup you must confirm you are 18+.  
- If the system detects possible under‑18 activity, you will be asked to verify your age before you can continue using Claude.

## How it works
- Safety systems monitor for signals of minor usage and may disable accounts based on those indicators.  
- Verification is done through Yoti, a third‑party age‑verification platform.  
- A verification link is sent in a notification email; passing the process reinstates the account.

## Accepted age verification methods (Yoti)
- **Facial age estimation** – take a selfie; Yoti estimates your age without needing an ID document.  
- **ID verification** – upload a photo of a supported ID (passport, driving licence, national ID card, etc.).  
- **Yoti Digital ID app** – share a verified “over 18” attribute directly from the Yoti app.

## How we protect your data
- Yoti is independently audited for SOC2 compliance.  
- Your selfie, document images, and any personal data are deleted by Yoti once the age check is completed.  
- Anthropic receives only a pass/fail result; no personal data, ID, or images are processed or stored by us.
