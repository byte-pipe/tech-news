We call them summer projects, because it seems like only the pace of long days without distractions allows for us to dedicate time to them. These projects have very little extrinsic motivation, it's just what we default to when we're left to our own devices. For Rek, it's drawing comics. For Devine, that takes the shape of linguistic experiments, for weeks now Devine has been expanding on the work of Giuseppe Peano who was fascinated by the idea of encoding logic for preservation and communication. Giuseppe dedicated lengths of his life to auxiliary languages to make his mathematical ideas accessible across language barriers; he might have been inspired by today's difficulty of replicating software proofs and crumbling toolchains. Devine has been trying to formalize into BNF what Peano left behind aboutLatino Sine Flexione, and expanding on his experiments with theaxioms of natural numbers.

Devine has been haunted by a cursed idea we hinted at back in February. It has come together this month into a notation calledRejoice. Relatedly, Giuseppe Peano whose work has directly inspired this project, wrote some of his work in a language he called as Latino Sine Flexione.
