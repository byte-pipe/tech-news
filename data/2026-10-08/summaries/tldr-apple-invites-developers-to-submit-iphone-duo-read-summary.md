# Apple invites developers to submit iPhone Duo‑ready apps to the App Store

## Key announcements
- Xcode 27.1 Release Candidate 1 is now available and adds full support for building and testing iPhone Duo apps and games.  
- App Store Connect will require iPhone Duo screenshots for all submissions beginning April 2027.  
- Apps compiled with Xcode 27.1 will display a “Optimized for iPhone Duo” badge on their product pages.  
- Recompiling existing apps with Xcode 27.1 automatically adds Duo support; developers can use Device Hub to preview app behavior in all poses and orientations.  
- Apple provides several preparation resources: design guidance and templates, asset management tools, app preview specifications, and screenshot specifications.  
- In‑person iPhone Duo workshops will be held in the coming days; registration is open.  
- iPhone Duo pre‑orders start Friday, October 16, with deliveries and in‑store availability from October 23.  
- Customers can begin preparing for pre‑orders on October 12, as detailed in Apple’s blog post.
