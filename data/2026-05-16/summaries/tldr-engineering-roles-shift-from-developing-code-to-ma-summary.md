# Engineering roles shift from developing code to managing AI

## Dive Brief
- AI is taking over many coding tasks, moving engineers’ focus to overseeing AI‑generated output.  
- The State of Engineering Excellence 2026 report (Harness) surveyed 700 developers and engineering leaders at enterprise firms.  
- Adoption of AI in engineering workflows is now the default, yet teams struggle to quantify productivity gains and ROI.  
- Over half of respondents fear performance reviews will be tied to AI‑derived metrics and want a clear separation between improvement data and personal evaluations.  
- Trevor Stuart, SVP of Product at Harness, notes that leaders must make multi‑year AI investment decisions using outdated dashboards.

## Dive Insight
- AI accelerates code generation, shortening cycle times and improving output metrics, but 81 % of engineering leaders say the time saved is spent auditing AI‑produced code.  
- Approximately one‑third of a developer’s day is devoted to “invisible work” such as reviewing, fixing bugs, and context‑switching, which is not captured in traditional productivity metrics.  
- Pressure to deliver software faster has risen; a prior HackerRank study found more than two‑thirds of developers feel increased delivery expectations.  
- Engineers now must ensure code quality and security, assume responsibility for downstream outcomes, and decide when to trust or override AI suggestions.  
- Existing measurement tools do not adequately capture the true impact of AI on productivity.

## Recommendations for Tech Leaders
- Update evaluation frameworks to track metrics like code delivery rate and time spent reviewing AI output.  
- Audit current performance measurement systems to identify gaps introduced by AI adoption.  
- Implement stronger governance and security review processes.  
- Collaborate with developers to establish guardrails and measurement criteria for AI‑assisted work.

## Industry Perspective
- Unlike previous technological shifts (cloud, internet), AI is fundamentally reshaping the developer role and rendering legacy measurement frameworks insufficient.  
- Harness advises planning for increased governance, security oversight, and new productivity metrics to reflect the evolving nature of engineering work.
