# Apple event live: iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro & watches announced

## Event overview
- Apple’s annual fall event was held at its Cupertino headquarters on September 9‑10, 2026.  
- New CEO John Ternus (took over from Tim Cook on Sept 1) hosted the keynote.

## New hardware announcements
- **iPhone Duo** – Apple’s first foldable iPhone  
  - Passport‑like design that folds like a book.  
  - 5.4‑inch front display; 7.6‑inch inner display with nano‑texture matte finish.  
  - Marketed as the thinnest iPhone when unfolded.  
  - Starting price: $1,999.  

- **iPhone 18 Pro & 18 Pro Max**  
  - Similar silhouette to the iPhone 17 Pro/Pro Max.  
  - Powered by the A20 Pro processor (CPU, GPU, neural‑engine upgrades).  
  - Battery life: 24 hours (Pro) and 30 hours (Pro Max).  
  - Introduce a variable mechanical aperture for improved light and depth control.  

- **Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4** – latest smartwatch models unveiled.  

- **AirPods 5** – new generation earbuds announced.

## Pricing highlights
- iPhone 18 Pro: $1,199 (≈ $100 above the 17 Pro).  
- iPhone 18 Pro Max: $1,299.  
- AirPods 5 (ANC): $129 (down from $179 for AirPods 4).  
- iPhone Duo at $1,999 aligns with Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8 pricing.
