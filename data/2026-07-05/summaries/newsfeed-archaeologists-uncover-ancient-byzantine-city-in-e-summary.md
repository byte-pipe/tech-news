# Archaeologists uncover ancient Byzantine city in Egypt’s western desert | Egypt | The Guardian

**Ancient Byzantine City Uncovered in Egypt's Western Desert**

*   A well-preserved fourth-century quarter was discovered in Egypt's western desert, revealing details of daily life, urban development, and economic activities when the country was part of the Byzantine empire.
*   Archaeologists uncovered residential and religious structures, including a basilica-style church, coins, pottery fragments, tools, and ancient tombs at Marina el-Alamein near Alexandria.
*   The site features open squares, public spaces, north-south thoroughfares intersected by east-west streets, suggesting a planned urban design.
