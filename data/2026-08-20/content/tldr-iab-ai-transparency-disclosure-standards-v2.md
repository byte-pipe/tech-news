# AI Transparency & Disclosure Framework V2

 Aug. 18, 2026 

 Table of Contents 

As AI becomes more embedded in advertising and marketing, organizations need a clear and consistent approach to determining when AI involvement should be disclosed. Yet without shared standards, brands, agencies, publishers, and technology partners face uncertainty around what requires disclosure, what does not, and how to communicate AI use transparently with consumers.

IAB’sAI Transparency & Disclosure Framework V2translates theAI Transparency & Disclosure Framework’s risk-based, materiality-driven approach into practical protocols for when and how AI involvement should be disclosed across consumer-facing advertising and marketing content.

Inside the Framework:

* A risk-based approach to determining when AI disclosure is needed
* Practical guidance for AI-generated and AI-assisted text, imagery, video, audio, synthetic voices, digital twins, and AI-powered consumer interactions
* Common disclosure principles and use-case triggers to support consistent decision-making
* Guidance designed to balance consumer transparency with avoiding unnecessary labeling and disclosure fatigue
* Recommendations for teams navigating the evolving AI landscape

Whether you’re developing AI governance practices, evaluating disclosure requirements, or building consumer-facing AI experiences, these Standards provide a practical foundation for creating transparency and trust across the advertising ecosystem.
