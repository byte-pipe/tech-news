**Tina Peters to Join Shasta County Election Team**

- Peters was convicted in 2024 after allowing a former pro surfer to bypass security to access Mesa County election equipment.
- Colorado Governor Jared Polis commuted Peters' sentence in May due to pressure from President Donald Trump.
- Trump allegedly orchestrated a pressure campaign to free Peters from state charges.

- Shasta County Registrar Clint Curtis plans to hire Tina Peters, who may not have access to the county's voting system but could face difficult conditions.
- Peters' past role in promoting election-related conspiracy theories has raised concerns about her ability to carry out the duties of her new position.
- This appointment has sparked criticism, with Texas senator Alex Padilla calling it "a misstep" and arguing that Peters is not qualified for the role.
