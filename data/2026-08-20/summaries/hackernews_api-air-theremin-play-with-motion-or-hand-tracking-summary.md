# Air Theremin · Play With Motion Or Hand Tracking

## Overview
- An interactive virtual theremin that can be played in mid‑air using a webcam, phone gyro, or mouse.
- Designed for laptops, phones, or any device with a camera; the phone’s gyroscope offers an alternative control method.
- The interface includes visual cues for gestures, sound parameters, and effects.

## Control Methods
- **Hands (camera)** – Wave both hands in front of the camera.
- **Gyro (phone)** – Tilt the phone left/right and forward/backward.
- **Mouse** – Move the cursor when no camera or gyro is available.

## Hand Gestures (camera)
- **Spread hands** – Increases volume.  
- **Palms together** – Mutes (silence).  
- **Raise both hands** – Raises pitch.  
- **Tilt like a see‑saw** – Adds vibrato.  
- **Lean back** – Darkens tone, adds room.  
- **Tap** – Triggers a note.  
- Keep both hands within the camera frame; moving them outside cuts the sound.

## Phone Gyro Gestures
- **Tilt left/right** – Controls volume.  
- **Tilt forward/back** – Controls pitch.  
- The full range is confined to the on‑screen frame; tilting the marker outside silences the output.  

## Additional Controls
- **START** – Press to begin; on a phone, hold steady to calibrate to your grip.  
- **FX buttons** – Vibration, tremolo, echo, reverb, etc.  
- **REC / PAUSE** – Record and pause playback.  

## Calibration & Setup
- Calibration occurs automatically when START is pressed; the device aligns its sensor range to the user’s hand or grip position.
