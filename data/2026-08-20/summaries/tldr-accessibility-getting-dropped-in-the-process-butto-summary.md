# Accessibility getting dropped in the process • Buttondown

# August 5, 2026 - Accessibility getting dropped in the process

## In This Newsletter

This issue explores the topic of accessibility in product design, where it often fades away as the development process progresses.

### The State of Accessible Designs

Every now and then, I look at a design before development begins. Usually, it ends with a reassuring assertion: yes, this can be implemented accessibly.

It seems straightforward, but sometimes it isn't until accessibility crosses the finish line that the design's shortcomings are revealed.

### Understanding Accessible Design

To design accessible prototypes:

*   Use clear and concise language to describe how your design will be implemented.
*   Review the wording to ensure it encourages users with disabilities to interact with your site.
*   Review the interaction pattern to ensure it works well for assistive technologies.

### The Slippery Slope of Inaccessibility

It's not always an inevitable outcome. In some cases, this issue can be mitigated by:
*   Training developers to prioritize accessibility.
*   Providing more guidance on implementing accessibility, especially in areas that were already tested.

### Real Change Takes Time

It's easy to minimize this issue by blaming those responsible for the project. However, simply stating that developers didn't care about accessibility is oversimplification.

In some cases, this might be due to a lack of training, but more often than that, it's a matter of prioritizing different aspects of the design:

*   Sometimes, there's a misunderstanding about what constitutes an accessible product.

### More Than Just Accessible Design

While design is one aspect of accessibility, it is not the only one. To truly improve accessibility:

*   We need better documentation of implementation decisions.
*   We must consider the nuances of various accessibility standards and technologies.
*   More collaboration and communication between developers and designers would help mitigate inaccessibility.

This issue highlights the need for a more holistic approach to accessibility, one that considers the intricacies of accessibility at every stage of development.
