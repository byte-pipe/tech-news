# Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe to present Strictly Come Dancing

# BBC confirms new Strictly Come Dancing hosts

## Announcement overview
- Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe have been named the new permanent presenters of *Strictly Come Dancing*, replacing Tess Daly and Claudia Winkleman.  
- The BBC says the trio will share equal billing; exact presenting duties may differ from the previous format.  
- This is the first time the show will have three permanent hosts, although Sir Bruce Forsyth previously co‑presented with Winkleman.

## Reactions from the hosts
- **Emma Willis**: “I’m a huge fan… stepping into the Strictly ballroom is something I can’t quite comprehend.”  
- **Josh Widdicombe**: “I’m giddy with excitement, honoured and a little overawed to be given the chance to step into the biggest shoes in television.”  
- **Johannes Radebe**: “Returning in a new presenting role is beyond anything I ever imagined.”

## Executive comments
- Executive producer Sarah James called the combination “unexpected, but their magic was undeniable the moment they came together.”  
- BBC chief content officer Kate Phillips added that the trio’s chemistry is “undeniable” and thanked those who auditioned.

## Casting process & other contenders
- An extensive audition process featured several high‑profile personalities.  
- Zoe Ball disclosed she experienced “the seven stages of grief and rejection.”  
- Rylan Clark confirmed he also auditioned, offered congratulations, and posted a meme‑style video of his reaction.

## Impact on scheduling
- Willis may present prime‑time Saturday shows on both the BBC and ITV this autumn, having already filmed the upcoming series of *The Voice UK*.  
- She will step back from her regular BBC Radio 2 Saturday afternoon slot while *Strictly* is on air; a temporary replacement will be announced.

## Returning judges & dancers
- Judges Shirley Ballas, Craig Revel Horwood, Motsi Mabuse and Anton Du Beke will return for the new series.  
- Fifteen of the twenty professional dancers from last year are confirmed to return; five will not, and fresh international talent will join the roster.

## Background of the new hosts
- **Emma Willis**: former model, MTV presenter (2002), *Big Brother* host (Channel 5), *The Voice UK/Kids* presenter, host of *The Circle*, *Loose Women*, *This Morning*, married to musician Matt Willis, co‑hosted *Love Is Blind UK* (2024).  
- **Josh Widdicombe**: stand‑up comedian since 2008, appearances on *Taskmaster*, *Mock The Week*, *Have I Got News For You*, *8 Out of 10 Cats*, regular on Channel 4’s *The Last Leg*, co‑host of *Parenting Hell* podcast, former host of BBC Radio 5 Live’s *Fighting Talk*, creator of BBC sitcom *Josh*, contestant on the 2024 *Strictly* Christmas special.  
- **Johannes Radebe**: South African dancer, appeared on *Strictly South Africa* (2014), toured with *Burn The Floor*, joined the UK *Strictly* as a pro dancer in 2018, partnered with celebrities such as Caroline Quentin, Alex Kingston and John Whaite, performed in West End’s *Kinky Boots*.

## Quotes on the new chapter
- Willis: hopes to “steer the ship” into a new era and looks forward to “spending weekends with Josh and Jojo, the incredible dancers and the judges.”  
- Radebe: feels “deeply honoured to now help carry that magic forward” alongside Willis and Widdicombe.  
- Widdicombe: recalls dancing the Charleston in a penguin costume for the 2024 Christmas special as his career high point and says he will “dust off the penguin costume” for the new role.
