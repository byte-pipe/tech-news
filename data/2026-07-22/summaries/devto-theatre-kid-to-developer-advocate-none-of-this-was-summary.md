# Defying cultural norms for a solo global career  

## Early life and shift to tech  
- Grew up in Mumbai with a passion for acting, directing, and theatre; technology was not on the radar.  
- After her brother left his computer behind, she began tinkering and discovered a genuine love for IT.  
- Professors encouraged her to change her study path from business to a science degree in information technology.  

## Graduating into a recession  
- Graduated during a recession with virtually no job openings.  
- Managed to secure a position in an R&D group working on cloud computing in 2009, when the technology was still viewed skeptically in India.  

## Moving abroad against cultural expectations  
- In 2013, AWS was hiring in Cape Town, South Africa, and she was told to “get married first” because women in her culture were not expected to move abroad alone.  
- She chose to defy that advice, fought for the opportunity, and joined AWS as a Cloud Support Engineer with only two suitcases and the HR representative who hired her.  

## Building community on weekends  
- Spent weekends attending meetups, answering questions, writing blogs, creating videos, and helping grow AWS User Groups across South Asia.  
- Became a speaker at large regional events; these activities were driven by personal passion, not corporate assignment.  
- The community provided mentors, confidence, friendships, and unexpected career opportunities.  

## Transition to Developer Advocate  
- When a Developer Advocate role opened, she accepted it, recognizing that years of community work had already signaled the right fit.  

## Maternity leave and learning AI out loud  
- After maternity leave, she returned to a landscape dominated by AI and felt behind despite 15 years of experience.  
- Launched a series called “Learning AI Out Loud,” sharing her learning process, failures, and breakthroughs.  
- The community responded positively, offering support and reducing feelings of isolation.  

## Reflection and message  
- Emphasizes that a pause is not a full stop; personal timelines are unique and valid.  
- From a uncertain future in Mumbai to representing a major global tech company, she has lived and worked across continents, built communities, spoken on stages, created content, and helped thousands of developers.  
- Her career unfolded without a predetermined plan, yet it led to fulfilling and unexpected achievements.
