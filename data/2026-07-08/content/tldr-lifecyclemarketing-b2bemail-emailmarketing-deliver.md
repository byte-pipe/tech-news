description: 'i''ve got some lazy gal email tips that anyone can use 😘 (you don''t have to be a lifecycle marketer to use ''em, either) working with more b2b teams this year, i keep seeing a pattern where companies are concerned about things like deliverability, even though it usually gets treated as a technical thing. my take is that it''s a habits problem which, funnily enough, makes it a marketing challenge (not an IT one 😌). if you''re worried about similar email stuff, here are some of my lazy (aka low-lift), marketer-owned tips you can use right now (like, today): - send from a real person, real email address (real sender names signal intent and get better placement) - keep transactional and marketing emails on different sending subdomains (that way, if marketing tanks your sender rep, transactional doesn''t go down with it) - clean your database on a schedule, not when things start to look chopped (my rule of thumb is monthly, which sounds like a lot, but hey 🤷‍♀️) - tighten suppression
  logic ("no clicks in 90 days" isn''t a real signal in b2b, you want to get more granular; think no opens + no clicks + no replies + no site visits + no product usage over 4-6 months) - before any big send, suppress the chronically cold segments - if you''re plg, ask new signups a real question in your first email (ex. "what brought you here?" or "what are you hoping to solve?" reply signals are the strongest engagement signal there is) - if you''re changing something structural about your email designs (new template, cadence shift, new segment), ease your way into it - try your best to keep cadence predictable, because inconsistent patterns (2 emails one week, 8 the next, then nothing for a month) hurt placement more than steady higher volume - QA across more than one email client (like inbox placement testing) because you''d be surprised how jacked up emails can look across them 🙃 - if you have these insights, look at how your audiences engage with email (ex. do more folks read on mobile
  vs desktop?) and cater to their preferences and one last hot tip 🔥 if you can''t tackle more than a few of these, then start with prioritizing email hygiene. that means cleaning up your database, looking at list health, and tidying up suppression (this isn''t a month-long project). hope it helps 🤗 #lifecyclemarketing #b2bemail #emailmarketing #deliverability #b2blifecycle #lifecyclemarketingtips #emailtips #emailmarketingstrategy'
