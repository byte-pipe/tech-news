# AI law startup Norm raises $120M, hits unicorn valuation

## Funding round
- Raised $120 million in a Series C round  
- Led by Khosla Ventures  
- Valued at $1.2 billion, achieving unicorn status  
- Additional investors: Bain, Craft Ventures, Coatue, Vanguard, New York Life, TIAA, Tony James (former Blackstone president/COO), Jeff Hammes (former Kirkland & Ellis chairman), Fenwick LLP  

## Business model
- Operates an AI‑native law firm called **Norm Law**  
- Deploys proprietary AI agents that are supervised by human attorneys  
- Charges clients based on outcomes rather than hourly billing, unlike most of the legal industry  

## Product development
- Developing AI agents capable of supervising other AI agents in task execution  
- New capital will be used to expand the product suite and hire additional attorneys  

## Company background
- Founded roughly three years ago  
- Total funding raised to date exceeds $260 million  

## Market context
- Part of a growing wave of legal‑AI startups, alongside companies such as Harvey and Legora, aiming to automate repetitive legal work.
