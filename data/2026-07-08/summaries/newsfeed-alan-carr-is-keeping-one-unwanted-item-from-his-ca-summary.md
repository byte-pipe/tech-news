# Alan Carr is keeping one unwanted item from his castle auction – see what was sold - BBC News

# Summary of BBC News article: Alan Carr is keeping one unwanted item from his castle auction

## Background
- Comedian Alan Carr purchased Ayton Castle in the Scottish Borders earlier this year (revealed in February).
- The castle contained many items that were unwanted by both Carr and the former owners.

## Auction details
- An auction of the “residual” contents was held at Ayton Castle on Sunday, following viewings on Friday and Saturday.
- Items sold included furniture, paintings, textiles, and a five‑seat Bentley Mulsanne (top price £24,000).
- The highlight was the collection from the Branxton Cement Menagerie, created in the 1960s by a retired joiner for his disabled son and later relocated to Ayton Castle in 2021.

## Unsold item
- The only lot that failed to attract a bid was a concrete cow sculpture, part of the Branxton Menagerie.
- Auctioneer Jim Railton said the cow will remain with Carr as a memento of the Branxton animals.

## Financial outcome
- The Branxton animal pieces fetched about £28,000 in total.
- Overall, the auction is expected to generate roughly £100,000, which will go to the previous owners who sold the castle to Carr.

## Comments from the auctioneer
- Jim Railton described the day as “very busy,” with seven‑and‑a‑half hours of continuous selling and only one unsold item.
- He expressed satisfaction that the relatively unknown Branxton collection performed well at the auction.
