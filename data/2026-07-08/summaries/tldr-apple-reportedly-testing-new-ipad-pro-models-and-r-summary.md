# Apple reportedly testing new iPad Pro models and redesigned entry‑level MacBook Pro for 2027

## New iPad Pro models (spring 2027)

- Apple is testing **four** new iPad Pro models that keep the existing 11‑inch and 13‑inch screen sizes.  
- Launch is planned for **spring 2027**.  
- Improvements focus on internal hardware: faster chips and a vapor‑chamber cooling system to boost sustained performance and reduce overheating.  
- The iPad Pro line was last updated in October 2025 with the **M5** chip; recent price increases moved the 11‑inch model to **$1,199** (from $999) and the 13‑inch to **$1,499** (from $1,299).  
- Earlier rumors of a foldable 18.8‑inch iPad Pro are unlikely; the four models appear to be a conventional refresh of the current lineup.

## Redesigned entry‑level MacBook Pro (first half of 2027)

- Code‑named **K104**, a 14‑inch MacBook Pro adopting a new design that aligns with upcoming touchscreen MacBooks slated for later this year or early next year.  
- Expected release: **as early as the first half of 2027**.  
- Apple previously completed work on a refreshed entry‑level MacBook Pro (**J804**) for 2026, featuring the current design and a base **M6** chip.  
- The company plans to introduce the **M7** processor in the first half of 2027, accelerating the transition from the M6 generation to better handle demanding AI workloads.

## Potential challenges

- Persistent memory and chip shortages could disrupt Apple’s product roadmap and delay the planned releases.

## Overall outlook

- Assuming the schedule holds, the new iPad Pro models and the redesigned entry‑level MacBook Pro would contribute to what analysts anticipate as Apple’s **biggest product year ever in 2027**.
