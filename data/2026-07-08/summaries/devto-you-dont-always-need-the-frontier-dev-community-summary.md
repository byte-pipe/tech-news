# AI Engineer World's Fair Coverage Summary

## Key Observations
- Workshops at this year’s AI Engineer World’s Fair have shifted focus from RAG and prompt engineering toward evaluations and open models, highlighting a growing industry emphasis on measuring and trusting AI outputs.  
- Many engineers default to the newest, most powerful frontier models for everyday tasks, even trivial ones like checking the weather, despite the availability of capable fast models.  
- Current fast models match the performance of frontier models from six months ago (e.g., Sonnet 4.6 ≈ Opus 4.1, Gemini Flash 3.5 ≈ Gemini Pro 3.1, GPT‑5.4 Mini ≈ GPT‑5.1).  
- Fast models are significantly cheaper and faster, reducing latency and cost compared to waiting for max‑thinking responses.  

## Implications
- The industry’s focus on evaluation and trust may drive new standards for AI reliability.  
- Over‑optimizing for correctness when fast models already meet requirements can waste resources.  
- A cultural shift is needed for developers to trust and adopt fast models for routine tasks.  

## Suggested Actions
- When performing simple tasks (e.g., checking the weather), consider using a fast model instead of a frontier model.  
- Encourage teams to evaluate cost‑effectiveness and speed benefits of fast models, reducing unnecessary reliance on the latest frontier.
