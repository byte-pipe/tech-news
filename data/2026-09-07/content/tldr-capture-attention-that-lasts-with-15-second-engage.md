## TL;DR for marketers:

* Reddit’s new 15-second Engaged Video Views goal helps advertisers reach Reddit users most likely to watch a video to completion or for at least 15 seconds.
* It’s built for video creative that needs more time to tell the story, like trailers, product demos, feature explainers, testimonials, and consideration-driving campaigns.
* Advertisers only pay for meaningful views, with automated bidding options that help reduce manual campaign management.
* 15-second Engaged Video Views is now available in public beta for all advertisers globally.

Your video ads do more than capture attention. They build context, explain value, show the product in motion, and help people decide what to do next. But when your message needs more than a few seconds to land, you need an optimization goal built for deeper attention.

That’s why we’ve launched 15-second Engaged Video Views, a new optimization goal and billing option for video advertisers on Reddit.

This new goal is designed to show your video ads to Reddit users who are more likely to keep watching. For videos 15 seconds or shorter, a completed view counts as an engaged video view. For videos longer than 15 seconds, an engaged video view is counted once someone watches for at least 15 seconds.

## Why it matters

Reddit is where people go deep: into communities, conversations, recommendations, reviews, and entertainment they care about most. Video helps advertisers show up in those moments with richer storytelling and more engaging creative.

With 15-second Engaged Video Views, advertisers can optimize for users who are more likely to stay with the story, absorb the message, and engage with longer-form video creative. Alpha testers saw huge gains, including +51% 15 second view-through rate (VTR15) and +71% video completion rate (VCR) on average.1

## What 15-second Engaged Video Views can help you do

* Drive deeper attention:By optimizing for users likely to watch videos fully or for at least 15 seconds, this goal helps increase the chance that your message has enough time to land.
* Make video spend work harder:You only pay for meaningful views, helping your budget focus on people who show genuine interest in your content.
* Simplify campaign management:Automated bidding options, including Lowest cost and Cost cap, help remove the guesswork of constantly adjusting manual CPV bids.

## When to use 15-second views vs. 6-second views

A 6-second video view is a strong signal that someone noticed your ad. A 15-second view signals that someone stayed engaged long enough to absorb more of your story, product value, or brand message.

We recommend using 15-second Engaged Video Views for creative that benefits from longer watch time, including:

* Film, TV, gaming, and entertainment trailers
* Product demos
* Feature explainers
* Testimonials
* Brand storytelling
* Longer-form creative where the key message builds over time

If your message lands almost immediately, or you have a shorter creative, 6-second views may still be the better fit. If your campaign needs more room to persuade, 15-second Engaged Video Views gives you a goal built for that job.

In other words: Use 6-second views when you want to capture attention at scale. Use 15-second views when you want to capture attention that lasts.

## Test, learn, and scale what works

During the beta, advertisers can useReddit Split Testingto measure 15-second video views against the existing 6-second video views goal.

Split Testing helps advertisers run controlled experiments directly in Ads Manager, compare performance across variants, and understand which strategy is right for their campaign. For beta measurement, post-campaign reporting can include View-Through Rate (VTR) and Cost-Per-View (CPV).

For eligible split tests, campaigns should run at the ad group level for at least two weeks, with automated bidding. Lowest cost auto-bidding is preferred. Campaigns should not be paused during the test period.

Through split testing, The Times found that Reddit’s new 15-second engaged video views optimization drove improvements across the board for key video metrics. The video strategy drove higher video view and completion rates, increased CTR and resulted in reduced costs. Read the full case studyhere.
