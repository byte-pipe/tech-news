# Bullshit Management Didn't Die. It Got Automated.

## Overview
- The author coined “bullshit management” four years ago to describe work that adds no value but drains energy.  
- The original article went viral (over 1 million reads) and sparked global discussion.  
- Revisiting the concept in 2026 shows that the same unproductive practices persist, now amplified by AI tools.

## BS Management Mutation Map
- **Gathering requirements** – Previously relied on stakeholder input; now AI generates full PRDs from raw Slack or Zoom transcripts, often without any real customer insight.  
- **Extensive backlog** – Backlogs remain huge; AI can instantly create dozens of polished backlog items, increasing visual appeal but not relevance.  
- **Performance reports** – Manual reports have been replaced by AI‑generated updates that are exchanged between machines, creating the illusion of productivity.  
- **Striving for consensus** – Stakeholders now use AI‑crafted arguments, leading to richer‑looking but shallower discussions.  
- **Signing off deliverables** – The reviewer’s role shifted from human work to vetting AI‑produced specs, summaries, and code, becoming a bottleneck again.  
- **Meeting overload** – Meetings persist, with bots taking notes and AI summaries sent to absent participants, yet alignment remains poor.  
- **Fear of saying no** – “No” is harder because AI makes any task seem cheap; rejecting ideas now requires stronger justification.  
- **Bridging communication** – The human bridge has collapsed; everyone works with personal AI assistants, creating isolated silos.  
- **Prioritizing by opinion** – HiPPOs are replaced by LLMs that suggest priorities, but customer pain points are still ignored.  
- **Feature factory** – AI supercharges feature production; speed dominates metrics while real impact and value are neglected.

## What Actually Died
- The cost of delivery vanished, removing the natural constraint that forced teams to question “why” before building.  
- Cheap delivery eliminated the economic argument for saying no, widening the gap between low‑cost output and high‑cost value.

## What Emerged
- **Shiny sh*t** – AI now produces polished strategies, roadmaps, and decks that look impressive but may lack substance, reinforcing the same traps of bullshit management.
