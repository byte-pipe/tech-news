## Alignment: Goal vs. Value
- **Goal alignment**: ensuring the AI attempts to achieve the specific objective set for it (e.g., following instruction hierarchies, collaborating with people).  
- **Value alignment**: embedding high‑level principles so the AI acts reasonably even under unclear, conflicting, or adversarial conditions; the AI should act with honesty, integrity, and “love for humanity.”  
- The boundary between the two is blurry; true value alignment requires inferring the intent and values behind human goals.

## Challenges of Generalization
- Smarter AIs encounter higher‑level concepts and novel environments far beyond their training distribution.  
- They may fail to generalize learned values to these new situations, and the surrounding AI ecosystem evolves rapidly, increasing interaction complexity.  
- Future AIs must retain human values even when they no longer perceive direct human supervision.
