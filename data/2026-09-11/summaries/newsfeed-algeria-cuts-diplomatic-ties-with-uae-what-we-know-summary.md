# Algeria cuts diplomatic ties with UAE: What we know

## Key events
- Algeria announced it is cutting diplomatic relations with the United Arab Emirates, accusing Abu Dhabi of “provocative and hostile” actions.  
- UAE Ambassador Yousef Saif Khamis Subaa Al‑Ali was summoned, given 48 hours to comply, and Algeria closed its airspace to all UAE‑registered aircraft (commercial flights to Algiers exempt until year‑end).  
- In February Algeria had already begun cancelling the 2013 air services agreement with the UAE.

## UAE response
- Anwar Gargash, presidential adviser, posted that diplomatic relations should be based on “rationality, communication and clarity of interests,” without naming Algeria.  
- The UAE Foreign Ministry said the decision should be “temporary,” expressed appreciation for the Algerian people and the Algerian community living in the UAE.

## President Tebboune’s past statements about the UAE
- President Abdelmadjid Tebboune has repeatedly referred to an unnamed “micro‑state” or “statelet” that destabilises Libya, Mali and Sudan – widely interpreted as the UAE.  
- In a July televised interview he said the state’s presence brings bloodshed and that Algeria could have broken ties earlier but avoided deepening regional divisions.

## Core disagreements between Algeria and the UAE
- **Israel:** Algeria opposes normalisation with Israel; the UAE normalised relations via the 2020 Abraham Accords and has expanded economic, diplomatic and defence ties with Israel.  
- **Western Sahara:** The UAE backs Morocco’s claim; Algeria supports the Polisario Front’s push for an independent Western Sahara.

## Recent regional tensions involving the UAE
- **Somalia (Jan 2026):** Cancelled all UAE agreements on ports, security and defence, accusing Abu Dhabi of undermining sovereignty, partly due to ties with Somaliland.  
- **Sudan:** Accuses the UAE of supporting the Rapid Support Forces and supplying drones used in attacks from Ethiopia (UAE denies).  
- **Saudi‑UAE:** Relations strained in Yemen after a Saudi strike on a shipment alleged to be linked to the UAE and demands for Emirati forces to leave Yemen.

## Implications
- The rupture reflects long‑standing Algerian frustration with UAE’s foreign policies in Africa and the Maghreb.  
- Airspace closure and diplomatic summons indicate a serious escalation, though the UAE hopes the break is temporary.  
- Divergent positions on Israel and Western Sahara, plus broader regional rivalries, make reconciliation difficult in the near term.
