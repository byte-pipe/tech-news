# Everything Apple announced at its fall iPhone event, from the foldable iPhone Duo to an always-listening Apple Watch | TechCrunch

# Summary of Apple’s Fall “Surprise and Shine” Event  

## iPhone Duo  
- Apple’s first foldable phone, featuring a 7.6‑inch inner Retina display and a 5.4‑inch outer display.  
- Under‑display camera on the inner screen for an all‑screen experience.  
- Battery life: up to 31 hours video on the inner display, 44 hours on the outer display.  
- Camera system: 48 MP main sensor and 48 MP ultrawide sensor with 2× optical zoom; rear‑camera selfies possible.  
- Hinge built from Grade 5 aluminum with >100 engineered components for smooth operation.  
- Pricing: starts at $1,999 for 256 GB, available in two colors.  
- Pre‑orders open October 16; shipping begins October 23.  

## iPhone 18 Pro & iPhone 18 Pro Max  
- Design unchanged from previous generation; primary upgrade is the camera system.  
- New 48 MP sensor with variable aperture (six thin blades) improves low‑light performance and shooting speed.  
- Added Pro controls: white balance, shutter speed, aperture, histogram; new “Photographic Styles” with texture and grain options.  
- Video: 4K Dolby HDR Vision, plus post‑capture cinematic effects.  
- Pricing: iPhone 18 Pro $1,199; iPhone 18 Pro Max $1,299 ( $100 more than 2023 models).  
- Pre‑orders start Saturday; availability September 18.  

## Apple Watch Series 12 & Apple Watch Ultra 4  
- No major hardware redesign, but significant software enhancements.  
- “Sound Recognition” listens for sirens, alarms, doorbells, baby cries and alerts users with hearing impairments.  
- New “Audio Intelligence” suite:  
  - “Live Rewind” shows the previous 15 seconds of conversation as text.  
  - “Siri Recap” summarizes key points from daily talks (e.g., meetings, parent‑teacher conferences).  
- Pricing: Series 12 $399; Ultra 4 $499.  
- Pre‑orders begin today; in‑store availability September 18.  

## AirPods 5  
- Audio upgrades: better sound quality, 50 % stronger active noise cancellation, enhanced Transparency mode.  
- Volume control added to the stem (previously Pro‑only).  
- Battery: up to 5 hours with ANC on a single charge; 22 hours total with charging case.  
- Pricing: $129 for standard version, $149 with Wireless Charging Case.  
- Pre‑orders start today; shipping September 18.  

## Revamped Health App  
- Integrated Apple Intelligence to interpret health data more intelligently.  
- New “Insights” tab provides timely health summaries, personalized guidance, assessments, a “Readiness” score, and a “Health Age” metric.  
- Added “Longevity” tab aggregates sleep, movement, heart health, and other metrics from Apple Watch and iPhone.  
- Rollout later this year, beginning with U.S. English.
