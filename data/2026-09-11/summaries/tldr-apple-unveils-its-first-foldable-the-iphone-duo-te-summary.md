# Apple unveils its first foldable, the iPhone Duo | TechCrunch

## Event and leadership
- Introduced at the “Surprise and Shine” event in Cupertino.  
- First marquee device launched under new CEO John Ternus.

## Design and displays
- Wider form factor than early vertical‑slab foldables, improving aspect ratio for video.  
- Unfolded Retina display: 7.6 inches.  
- Outer display: 5.4 inches.  
- Under‑display camera provides an all‑screen feel.  
- Custom nanotexture finish hides the crease and reduces glare.  
- Hinge made from grade‑5 aluminum with over 100 special components for smooth operation and durability.

## Biometrics and unlocking
- Only Touch ID; Face ID omitted.  
- Unlock possible via Apple Watch.

## Hardware
- Powered by the A20 Pro chip and C2 modem; eSIM‑only.  
- Battery life: 31 hours video on inner display, 44 hours on outer display.  
- Camera system: 48 MP main sensor + 48 MP ultrawide sensor with 2× optical zoom; no telephoto lens.  
- Supports rear‑camera selfies, outer‑display animation for focusing, and a “smart‑take” AI feature that snaps when subjects are ready.

## Software
- iOS 27 adapted for foldable use: vertical UI adjustments, split‑screen, app pairing, and video pinning.  
- Can be used half‑folded or placed on a table.  
- Apple Pencil support slated for later in the year.

## Pricing and availability
- Priced at $1,999 with 256 GB storage; offered in two colors.  
- Pre‑orders begin October 16; shipping starts October 23.

## Market context
- Samsung and Xiaomi have already released wide foldables.  
- Counterpoint analytics: foldables account for <2 % of global shipments, but the iPhone Duo could capture up to 25 % market share by year‑end.
