# Anthropic expected to receive high ‘junk’ rating from credit agencies following IPO, FT report says

## Key points
- The Financial Times reports that Blackstone, the anchor investor in a $63 billion financing deal for Anthropic’s chips, told investors it expects the AI lab to receive a “high” junk rating after its IPO.  
- Blackstone now forecasts a double‑B range rating, the top tier of speculative “junk” grade.  
- This contrasts with an earlier Blackstone statement that Anthropic would achieve an investment‑grade rating (low risk) before 2027.  
- A junk rating would make it more difficult and more expensive for Anthropic to raise additional capital to fund its heavy compute‑spending.  

## Background
- Anthropic filed to go public earlier this week, sparking speculation about the impact of its large IPO on the AI sector.  
- Over the past 12 months the company has entered large compute‑power deals, leading to concerns among investors about its spending pace.  

## Reactions
- TechCrunch contacted both Anthropic and Blackstone for comment; neither responded.  
- Blackstone’s outlook is described as a prediction rather than a definitive rating decision.  

## Implications
- A high junk rating could increase borrowing costs and limit financing options for Anthropic’s ongoing operations and growth initiatives.  
- The rating outlook may influence broader market perception of AI‑related IPOs and the financing environment for fast‑growing AI firms.
