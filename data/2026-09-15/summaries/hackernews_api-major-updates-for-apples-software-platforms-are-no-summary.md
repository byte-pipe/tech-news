# Major updates for Apple’s software platforms are now available

## Siri AI and Apple Intelligence
- Introduces Siri AI, a next‑gen, more conversational assistant powered by Apple Intelligence; beta launch in English with French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish support arriving in October.  
- Siri AI understands personal context from messages, emails, photos, and can answer on‑screen questions or fetch up‑to‑date web information.  
- Visual Intelligence lets users ask Siri about what they see on iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Vision Pro screens.  
- “Write with Siri” enables dictation of drafts and refinement of text; a dedicated Siri app syncs conversation history privately across devices via iCloud.  
- Apple Intelligence adds new features to core apps:  
  - **Photos:** Spatial Reframing, Extend, upgraded Clean Up tool, and Image Playground for high‑quality AI‑generated images with metadata and upcoming SynthID support.  
  - **Safari:** Automatic tab grouping by topic, “Notify Me” for website changes (restocks, price drops), custom extensions created from descriptions, and Call Context that surfaces relevant info (e.g., confirmation codes) during calls.  

## Child Safety and Screen Time redesign
- New child‑account setup flow lets parents choose allowed apps from the start and add more over time.  
- “Ask to Browse” requires parental approval before a child can open a new website in Safari.  
- Communication Safety now detects and blocks gore or violent content in shared images and videos.  
- Redesigned Screen Time provides an at‑a‑glance view of average device usage and top apps, with daily time allowances for Entertainment, Games, and Social Media, plus schedule‑based app access guided by clinical experts.  
- Apple provides a dedicated website with resources for creating safer, more enriching digital experiences for kids.  

## Platform‑wide performance and connectivity improvements
- **Performance:**  
  - iPhone/iPad apps launch up to 30 % faster; photos load up to 70 % faster; AirDrop transfers up to 80 % faster.  
  - iPad file browsing and USB‑drive transfers up to 5 × faster; smoother multitasking window operations.  
  - Mac sees quicker AirDrop, faster iCloud Photos uploads, smoother space switching and Mission Control.  
  - Apple Watch music playback speed increased.  
- **Connectivity:**  
  - Seamless transitions between cellular and Wi‑Fi on iPhone and iPad.  
  - Messages can send large photos and videos in low‑bandwidth situations without blocking subsequent texts.  

## Additional notes
- Updates are available today for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, and other Apple platforms.
