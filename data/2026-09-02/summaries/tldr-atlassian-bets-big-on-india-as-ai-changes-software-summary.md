# Atlassian bets big on India as AI changes software development

## Overview
- Atlassian is positioning its India development centre as a strategic R&D hub.  
- The India site now employs more than 2,700 people, largely in engineering, product, and design, with an expanding sales workforce.

## New Bengaluru R&D Centre
- Launched in September 2025, covering over 200,000 sq ft—four times the size of the previous Bengaluru office.  
- Houses teams responsible for:
  - **Service Collection** – a billion‑dollar‑plus IT‑service management and DevOps vertical run entirely from India.  
  - Machine‑learning platform that powers Atlassian’s AI stack.  
  - Core search capabilities essential to Atlassian’s mission.

## Changing Role of Software Engineers
- Rapid AI advances are lowering the barrier to creating software, shifting the challenge to deciding *what* to build and how to manage resulting workflows.  
- Engineers are moving from manual code writing to orchestrating and prompting AI agents, while still providing architecture, design, and judgment expertise.

## Hiring Plans
- Ongoing expansion of engineering and sales hiring in India.  
- Strong focus on recruiting fresh graduates from Indian universities, described as “AI‑native” talent that can help adapt products and engineering practices.  
- Rao notes a “doubling down” on university hiring to tap this talent pool.

## Outlook
- The India centre is a revenue‑generating, strategic hub that will continue to drive Atlassian’s AI‑enabled product development and growth.
