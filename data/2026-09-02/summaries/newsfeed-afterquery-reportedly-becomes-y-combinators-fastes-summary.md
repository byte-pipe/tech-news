# AfterQuery reportedly becomes Y Combinator’s fastest-ever unicorn, now valued at $3.2B

## Key Highlights
- AfterQuery raised a new funding round that values the AI training‑data startup at **$3.2 billion**, more than a 10× increase from its $300 million valuation in April.
- Y Combinator partner Gustaf Alströmer says this is the fastest progression from launch to unicorn status in the accelerator’s history.
- The founders, aged 22 and 23, were part of YC’s Winter 2025 cohort, just 18 months ago.
- In April, the San Francisco‑based company reported an annualized revenue run rate of **$100 million** and listed major customers such as Nvidia, Legora, and Korean AI lab Motif Technologies.
- AfterQuery follows startups like Mercor and Scale, using knowledge professionals (doctors, lawyers, etc.) to train models and agents to replicate expert decision‑making and reasoning.
- Forbes first reported the round; AfterQuery could not be reached for comment.

## Context
- The latest round follows a $30 million Series A announced in April.
- The company is part of a new wave of AI firms that encode professional patterns into models rather than merely improving answer accuracy.
