description: 'The blog posts winning AI citations right now share one thing in common: how easy they are to extract from. Here are 11 tips for you to get your blogs cited: Tip 1: Pick topics with proven search demand, then run them through ChatGPT and AI Mode before you write. If the AI answer is thin, cites weak sources, or misses an angle, that''s your opening. Topics where AI already nails the answer are harder to win on. Tip 2: Match content format to intent. Informational and commercial queries reward blog posts. Transactional ones don''t. If the top 10 for your keyword are all product pages, a blog post won''t break in. Tip 3: Build a question map before you outline. Pull questions from People Also Ask, ChatGPT follow-ups, and Semrush''s Questions report. Map primary questions to H2s and follow-ups to H3s. Your structure should be driven by what readers actually ask. Tip 4: Structure for extraction. AirOps analyzed 12,000 URLs and found that pages with properly sequenced headings (one
  H1, then H2s and H3s in order) are 2.8x more likely to be cited in ChatGPT than pages with messy heading structures. Clean structure is the input AI tools use to decide what to quote. Tip 5: Lead with the answer in every section. Write the heading, then answer it in the very next sentence. Context, examples, and caveats come after. Posts that bury the answer three paragraphs deep get skipped by AI tools and skimmed past by humans. Tip 6: Show your work. Screenshots, walkthroughs, and original examples signal first-hand experience. Generic, unattributed content doesn''t earn citations from AI or trust from readers. Tip 7: Write clearly enough that a non-expert understands it. Short sentences, active voice, plain words. Read your draft out loud before publishing. Tip 8: Add visuals that teach, not visuals that decorate. Screenshots, diagrams, and comparison tables earn their spot. Aim for one visual break per screen of content, with descriptive alt text and keyword-relevant file names. Tip
  9: Get the on-page basics right every time. Target keyword in the title, H1, and naturally through the body. Compelling meta description that sells the click. Short descriptive URL. Internal links to related posts so you build topical clusters. Tip 10: Fix the technical foundation before scaling content. If Google can''t crawl and index your posts, nothing else matters, and AI tools that ground their answers in web search won''t find you either. Confirm indexing, compress images, add Article and FAQ schema, and run a quarterly site audit. Tip 11: Update, distribute, and track after publishing. Refresh stats older than two years, add sections that cover newly emerging questions, and cut what no longer fits intent. Track AI citations alongside rankings. Do the fundamentals better. Pick one post that''s underperforming relative to its potential. Apply five of these. Watch what happens over the next 90 days. | 17 comments on LinkedIn'
