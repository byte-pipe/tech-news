description: 'Another day in SEO, another Google update. Google officially killed FAQ rich results. Gone. As of May 7, they no longer appear in Search. Remember when we were all obsessing over FAQ schema? Adding perfectly structured questions to every page? Watching those beautiful dropdowns show up in SERPs? Yeah, that''s over. The funny part? I literally said on a podcast last week: "I was adding FAQs before LLMs were cool." Nathan, did we predict something???? And now Google said: cool, we don''t want them either. But before you go deleting all your FAQs - don''t. Here''s what to actually do: 1. Keep your FAQ content on the page. The schema won''t produce rich results anymore, but the content itself still matters. Well-structured Q&A sections improve dwell time and help users find answers faster. 2. FAQs still matter for LLMs. ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity - they all love pulling from clean Q&A content. Your FAQ section just moved from working for Google to working for AI search. 3. Shift
  your schema focus. FAQ schema is dead, but other structured data still works - article schema, author schema, breadcrumbs, how-to markup. Double down on what Google still rewards. 4. Turn your best FAQs into standalone content. If a question was driving clicks as a rich result, it probably deserves its own section or even its own page. Don''t let that intent go to waste. 5. Use FAQs as LLM prompt bait. Rewrite your FAQ questions to match how people actually ask LLMs. Not "What is our return policy?" but "What''s the best return policy for e-commerce in 2026?" That''s the prompt your buyer is typing into ChatGPT. SEO in 2026 is basically waking up every morning wondering what Google decided to remove overnight. At least it keeps us employed. | 116 comments on LinkedIn'
