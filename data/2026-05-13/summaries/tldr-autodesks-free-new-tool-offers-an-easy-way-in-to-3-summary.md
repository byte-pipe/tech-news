# Autodesk’s free new tool offers an easy way into 3D modelling

## Overview
- Autodesk has released **Project Falcon**, a free, browser‑based 3D modelling tool.
- Designed to lower the entry barrier for users with no prior experience or technical jargon.
- Currently available as a technology preview.

## Key Features
- **Guided workflow**: Interface walks users through the entire modelling process, making it intuitive.
- **Kitbashing approach**: Users assemble models from a large library of pre‑made parts rather than sculpting from scratch.
- **Extensive asset library**: Thousands of parts covering vehicles, spaceships, and many other objects.
- **Export options**: Models can be exported for further refinement in Autodesk Maya, 3ds Max, Blender, or for 3D printing.
- **Cloud‑based**: No installation required; each user receives dedicated cloud storage for assets.
- **Account requirement**: Access requires a free Autodesk account.

## Target Audience
- Ideal for newcomers who want to create 3D models quickly without learning complex software.
- Useful for hobbyists, educators, and anyone needing simple 3D assets for printing or further editing.

## Availability
- Project Falcon can be downloaded for free from the Autodesk website.
- As a preview, it does not offer the full manual control of traditional 3D modelling packages.
