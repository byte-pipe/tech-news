## Overall context
- Labour’s rule for removing a leader requires 81 MPs to back a single challenger. Over 80 MPs have called for Starmer’s resignation, but no unified challenger has emerged.  
- More than 100 backbenchers and junior ministers have signed a statement urging that “this is no time for a leadership contest.”
