# Android Auto is now one (screen) size fits all

## Major updates announced at Google I/O 2026
- Full‑bleed interface that adapts to any screen shape (curved, panoramic, circular, trapezoid).  
- YouTube video streaming in‑car (4K @ 60 fps, Dolby Atmos) available only while parked.  
- Material Three Expressive design language applied, pulling colors and themes from the driver’s phone.  
- Widget projection for sports scores, smart‑home controls, shortcuts, etc., with natural‑language access via Gemini.  
- “Magic Cue” that parses incoming messages and offers one‑tap replies to reduce distracted driving.  
- Gemini can act as an agent to place orders in apps like Starbucks and DoorDash without separate APIs.  
- Immersive Navigation in Google Maps with refreshed colors, 3D buildings, terrain, and greenery.  

## Screen‑size flexibility
- Previous Android Auto projected a rectangular UI with unused margins on non‑standard displays.  
- New “full bleed” design stretches apps to fill the entire display, matching unconventional shapes such as Lucid Air’s curved screen or MINI Cooper’s circular panel.  
- Patrick Brady (VP Android Automotive) likened the redesign to revisiting geometry classes.

## Video streaming via YouTube
- Enabled only when the vehicle is in park; the car signals the phone to unlock video playback.  
- Supports high‑resolution video and spatial audio, all streamed from the driver’s phone rather than an embedded system.  
- Addresses user demand for entertainment while charging EVs or waiting in parking lots.

## Material Three Expressive and personalization
- Brings the youthful, animation‑rich UI introduced to Android last year to the car.  
- UI adopts wallpaper‑derived color palettes and themes from the driver’s phone for a cohesive look.

## Widget support and Gemini AI
- Drivers can project personalized widgets (sports, smart‑home, garage door, lighting, contacts).  
- Gemini can retrieve widget data via voice commands, e.g., “What’s the score?” or “Show my calendar.”  

## Magic Cue and safety
- Analyzes incoming texts and surfaces relevant info (addresses, phone numbers) with one‑tap reply options.  
- Aims to cut down on unsafe phone interaction while driving.  

## Gemini as an app‑level assistant
- Performs actions in third‑party apps (Starbucks, DoorDash) in the background, eliminating the need for dedicated API integrations.  
- Reduces the impulse to manually use the phone during trips.

## Immersive Navigation in Google Maps
- Enhanced map view with detailed 3D structures, realistic terrain, and greener visuals.  
- Provides a richer navigation experience directly on the car’s display.

## Integration with Android Automotive and current limits
- Android Auto’s improvements narrow the gap between phone projection and native Android Automotive systems.  
- Some vehicle functions (HVAC, drive modes, driver‑assist settings, radio) remain unavailable via projection, though the gap is shrinking.  
- Google is working to let Maps on Android Auto trigger EV‑specific actions (charging stops, battery pre‑conditioning) that previously required deep integration.

## Outlook
- Brady predicts continued convergence as phones and cars become more capable and software integration deepens.  
- Ongoing driver‑distraction testing in simulators ensures safety as new features roll out.
