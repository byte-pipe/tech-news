# Authorities arrest arson suspect in connection with wildfires in Spokane

## Arrest details
- Spokane County Sheriff’s Office arrested 37‑year‑old Aaron Farinacci, a convicted felon, on first‑degree arson charges.  
- A citizen’s tip about a man “bending down near some grass” near the fire’s origin led investigators to Farinacci.  
- He was initially questioned and released, then re‑arrested by major‑crimes detectives after further evidence emerged.  
- Matches or a lighter were found on him at the time of arrest.  
- His home was searched; he will make his first court appearance on Tuesday.  

## Wildfire impact
- The Old Trails Fire is the most destructive of three blazes burning around Spokane, Washington.  
- Since the weekend, the fires have displaced about 65,000 residents, destroyed roughly 700 buildings, and burned 7,000 acres.  
- High winds, extreme temperatures, and dry conditions have fueled the flames.  
- No deaths or injuries have been reported so far.  

## Evacuations and sheltering
- Residents have been relocated to the Spokane Convention Center and a Red Cross shelter downtown.  
- Hundreds, including many elderly individuals, are staying at the shelter; nursing staff assist with basic needs.  
- Charlie Patrick, a 54‑year‑old disabled resident, evacuated with only personal belongings and is uncertain whether her apartment survived.  

## Official responses
- Mayor Lisa Brown described the situation as “unprecedented” for the city of 230,000 people.  
- Governor Bob Ferguson called Spokane a “war zone,” noting neighborhoods were “wiped out.”  
- Ferguson said he contacted President Trump and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin, requesting a federal emergency declaration for immediate housing, food, and search‑and‑rescue assistance.  

## Firefighting efforts
- Fire crews are using a brief lull in winds to contain the fires before an anticipated mid‑week heat wave.  
- Support staff from the California Complex Incident Management Team 7 are briefing firefighters at Mt. Spokane High School.
