# Summary of “Base Power raises $1B to roll out its giant new home battery”

## Product Overview
- **Core battery**: 39.2 kWh lithium‑iron‑phosphate (LFP) home battery.
- Provides up to 36 hours of whole‑home backup; two units give 78.4 kWh and up to 72 hours.
- Installation time under one hour; automatic switch to battery in 50 ms during outages.
- Operates in temperatures from –22 °F to 122 °F and has an IP67 rating (submersion up to 3 ft).
- Can be charged from solar panels or a portable generator; app shows real‑time backup time based on current or reduced load.

## Business Model
- Base Power designs, builds, installs, and maintains Core; ownership remains with the company.
- Homeowners subscribe to a plan (e.g., $695 installation fee + $19/month in Oncor territory) rather than buying the battery outright.
- Battery fleet supplies power to the grid when demand or wholesale prices spike; grid revenue subsidizes the homeowner’s backup service.
- Fixed electricity plan offered alongside the subscription.

## Production & Growth
- Core is produced at Base Factory 1 in Austin, with thousands of units shipped monthly.
- Battery capacity deployed grew from >100 MWh (Oct 2025) to >500 MWh.
- Geographic expansion from Texas into Illinois.
- Utility partnerships with El Paso Electric, Austin Energy, and CoServ total >200 MW of capacity.

## Funding
- New Series D round raised $1 billion, valuing Base Power at $13 billion post‑money.
- Lead investors: Ribbit, Addition, Valor Equity Partners, JPMorgan Chase Strategic Investment Group; plus existing investors.
- Total capital raised exceeds $2.5 billion.
- Funds earmarked for wider Core deployment, U.S. expansion, and hiring.

## Availability & Next Steps
- Core is currently available to homeowners in select parts of Texas and Illinois.
