# BMW’s in‑car Spider‑Man ad is villain behavior

## Key details of the campaign
- BMW is displaying a banner for *Spider‑Man: Brand New Day* on the Control Display of eligible vehicles.  
- The ad appears as a “festive animation” that can be tapped to launch a full‑screen Spider‑Man animation featuring the iX3 SUV, music, and optional ambient‑light effects.  
- Campaign period: July 27 – August 10, 2026.  
- Availability: more than 70 countries, on models equipped with optional equipment and BMW Operating System 7, 8, 8.5, 9 or Operating System X, produced after July 2020.  

## Owner and public reaction
- Many BMW owners are upset that a premium brand is using its infotainment system for movie advertising, especially after senior VP Stephan Durach said BMW would not do this.  
- The ads do not autoplay, but they are added as an app on the infotainment screen if not opened immediately.  
- Social‑media backlash highlights concerns about commercializing a paid‑for vehicle experience.  

## Industry context
- Similar in‑car advertising has appeared before, e.g., pop‑up ads in Jeep, Ram, and Chrysler displays.  
- BMW has previously released seasonal display animations for holidays, but labeling a movie ad as “festive” is seen as a stretch.  
- The controversy echoes earlier criticism of BMW’s attempt to charge a subscription for already‑installed heated seats.
