# Everything I Know – Buckminster Fuller Institute Summary

## Introduction
- Series of lectures delivered by Buckminster Fuller in late January 1975, totaling 42 hours.
- Covers Fuller’s major inventions and discoveries from the 1927 Dymaxion concepts to geodesic domes, tensegrity structures, and the ideas in *Synergetics*.
- Autobiographical elements link Fuller’s personal history with broader scientific and industrial developments.
- Topics span architecture, design, philosophy, education, mathematics, geometry, cartography, economics, history, structure, industry, housing, and engineering.
- Video recordings are available online at archive.org/details/buckminsterfuller.

## Un‑Edited, Un‑Cut Transcript
- Printed version is a verbatim transcript of the audio lectures, minimally edited.
- Contains Fuller’s distinctive language (“Bucky‑isms”), specialized terminology, and unconventional sentence structures.
