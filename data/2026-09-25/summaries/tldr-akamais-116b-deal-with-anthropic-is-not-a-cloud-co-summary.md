# Akamai’s $11.6B Deal With Anthropic Is Not a Cloud Contract – It Is an Equity Bet on the Model Layer – Forkast

## Defining the Cloud Infrastructure Deal Between Akamai and Anthropic

Akamai's $11.6 billion, seven-year cloud infrastructure deal with Anthropic is a significant milestone in the AI ecosystem, signaling a definitive shift in the way infrastructure providers interact with AI companies. The deal involves acquiring equity in Anthropic's model layer, making the providers "veentine landlords" of the intelligence age.

## Key Features of the Deal

*   A warrant for nonvoting convertible Series B Preferred Stock with approximately 5% of Akamai's common stock represented, tied to the performance of Anthropic's infrastructure.
*   A direct alignment between Akamai's infrastructure performance and Anthropic's long-term success, as driven by Anthropic's aggressive buying strategy.
*   A high capital expenditure commitment of $5.5 billion tied to the deal, forcing Akamai to raise its 2026 capex by $1.7 billion.

## Implications and Trends in the Cloud Infrastructure Market

This deal reflects the compute landlord thesis, where infrastructure providers embed themselves into the equity structure of AI companies, investing capital and expertise. The trend involves investments in power infrastructure, advanced computing, and AI model training at scale.

*   The high demand for compute resources, driven by AI projects like Anthropic's $517 billion ceiling, has accelerated the pace of these investments.
*   The deal presents a significant opportunity for Akamai to capitalize on the growth of these AI applications.
