# Amazon Blocks Meta's Shopping AI Agent

## Background

Amazon and Meta have been in a dispute over the use of artificial intelligence (AI) agents on its platform, specifically Muse AI, which is designed to run shopping errands on behalf of users. Meta has been trying to convince Amazon to block Muse, while Amazon has accused Meta of playing dumb.

## Key Points

### Amazon Blocks Muse AI

Amazon has decided to shut down Muse AI due to security and privacy concerns. Muse's AI agent is a security and privacy risk, and Amazon has asked Meta to remove it from its service.

### Security and Privacy Risks

Muse's AI agent is not properly identifying itself and is failing to prove its identity, which would require it to follow Amazon's TOS (Terms of Service). This is not good enough for Amazon, which is concerned about the potential risks to its security and privacy.

### Meta's Defense

Meta has argued that agentic AIs, like Muse, should follow the same rules and protocols as any other third-party application. This means that Meta is not violating any terms of service by having Muse run shopping tasks.

### Amazon's Concerns

Amazon has stated that it cannot allow agentic AIs to operate without proper consent and disclosure. Amazon's statements about Muse's capabilities have previously been unclear, but now it seems that Amazon is seeking clarification on the nature of Muse's authorization.

### Industry Response

Meta is trying to block other third-party agentic AIs, such as those from Google and OpenAI. Amazon's decision to block Muse AI has raised concerns about the role of AI in advertising and the potential risks to consumer data and security.
