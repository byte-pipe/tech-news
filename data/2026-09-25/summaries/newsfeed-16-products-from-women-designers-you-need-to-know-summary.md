# 16 Products From Women Designers You Need to Know | WIRED

**Slate Auto Fastback SUV**

The Slate Auto Fastback SUV is a pickup truck designed by women, for women. Founded by Tisha Johnson, an expert in automotive design, the vehicle started at under $25,000. The design team employed a modular, add-on-friendly approach to minimize compromise, focusing on durability and approachability.

**Away Softside Carry-on Aluminum Edition**

The Away Softside Carry-on is another product from the Away brand, which started in 2015 with $12 million in sales and over 100,000 customer units. The aluminum edition is made for those who value strength and size. Key features include a large 32-liter capacity, dual TSA locks, and a windproof design.

**Jelenew Mont Blanc Ultralight Long Windproof Jacket**

The Jelenew Mont Blanc Ultralight Long Windproof Jacket was designed by Marion Clignet, a six-time cycling world champion. The jacket combines features from women's clothing, ensuring a secure fit. Made from 94 percent recycled nylon and treated with a PFAS-free water-repellent treatment, UV protection, and mesh ventilation, the jacket is both durable and practical.

**TaylorMade Kalea Gold Driver**

The TaylorMade Kalea Gold Driver is a golf driver designed for women who seek performance beyond what men's equipment provides. Principal product-development engineer Michelle Penney contributed to the design, which prioritizes forgiveness, lightness, and ease of launch.
