# Universal UK Resort work leaves homeowner 'heartbroken' at move - BBC News

# Universal UK Resort Homeless Resident

## Summary

Ritchie Wood, a 55-year-old resident of Kempston Hardwick, Bedfordshire, is facing eviction due to the redevelopment of his neighborhood as part of the Universal UK Resort. Wood's home has been demolished along with other houses in the area, leaving him with few options. He hopes to be homeless within two months, as his landlord evicts him, likely due to the high cost of rent in the area. Wood has been a long-time resident of the village and is frustrated by the sudden change, describing the experience of seeing his neighbors' homes being demolished as "heartbreaking" and "very surreal."
