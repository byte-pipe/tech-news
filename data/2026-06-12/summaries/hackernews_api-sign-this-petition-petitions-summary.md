# Sign this Petition - Petitions

## Petition to the House of Commons: Oppose Bill C-22 and Withdraw Support for Bulk Metadata Retention

### What We Are Asking for:
- The government is considering Bill C-22, an act that authorizes regulations requiring designated "core providers" to collect and retain metadata on all Canadians without suspicion or investigation.
- We call upon the House of Commons to oppose Bill C-22 and vote it down at all stages.

### Current State of the Legislation
* Bill C-22 grants broad authority to compel electronic service providers to implement interception capabilities or technical assistance measures, which could weaken encrypted systems and create cybersecurity vulnerabilities for criminals and hostile foreign actors.
* Suspicionless metadata retention raises concerns about Canadians' rights under the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.

### Objectives of the Petition
- We aim to protect individuals from unnecessary bulk metadata retention and interception capabilities.
- We propose a more targeted approach to addressing national security concerns with less encroachment on citizens' privacy rights.

### Measures Proposing Reforms to Improve:
* Remove all suspicionless bulk metadata retention requirements for future lawful access legislation.
* Explicitly prohibit the weakening or breaking of encryption in any future lawful access legislation.

### Call to Action
- If you support these measures, please sign this petition and provide your personal information to express your opposition.
