description: 'You can literally 2-3x your income in the next 12 months if you obsess over mastering this skill: Copywriting. Here are 5 simple (but underrated) hacks to instantly make your writing more persuasive: 1/ Before writing anything, answer this question: what’s the emotion and the action you want to elicit with your copy? Action follows emotion. And it’s 10x easier to engineer an emotion if you make a conscious decision about it before you start writing anything. 2/ Spend a disproportionate amount of time in your first 1-3 lines. It doesn’t matter how good the rest of your copy is. If you don’t hook people immediately, they’re gone. 3/ Use vivd, hyper specific language when describing problems or pain points There’s a direct correlation between your ability to describe someone’s problems vividly & how likely they are to buy your things. Why? Well, if you show someone you understand their problems, they assume you must have the solution. 4/ Write like you talk. Please, for the love
  of god, don’t write like a marketer. Instead, write as if you were talking to a friend over coffee. Keep it casual. Skip all the jargon. The best marketing doesn’t feel like marketing. Pro tip: Use a transcription tool like WhisprFlow without having to “force” it. 5/ Read your copy aloud This makes it impossible to ignore clunky sentences and boring sections. If it sounds off, it reads off. And I know this is one of those things everyone says you should do. Yet, almost nobody does it. But if you try it, I promise your copy will instantly get so much better. And that’s it! I got dozens of other hacks & tips like these, but this is the 80/20. So give them a try and lemme know how you like them. PS - Actually, here’s another (bonus) hack for you: Do copywork -> find examples of great copy and transcribe it by hand. And yea, I know it sounds kinda crazy - but this is one of the fastest ways to get better at writing banger copy. In fact, it’s one of the first things I ask folks to do whenever
  we hire a new writer at my agency. So if you’d like to get a doc with 10 of my favorite pieces of writing to do copywork on, lemme below and I’ll send you a free copy. | 67 comments on LinkedIn'
