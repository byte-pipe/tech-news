# A Vinyl Bar in Shibuya is a startup offering fun music apps without any AI prompting | TechCrunch

**Music Generation Startups are Ramping Up**

A Japanese startup, **A Vinyl Bar in Shibuya**, has launched a music creation tool that mimics the style of any artist using generative AI prompts. The company has raised a $5.5m pre-seed round from notable investors.

**The Concept**

The startup is focused on providing a series of small apps that allow users to experiment with sounds and vocals in various ways, including using video clips and reworks of existing songs. The tools range from simple sound manipulation apps and website projects, to more complex instruments and effects-based applications like an iOS app called **bop**.

**Key Figures**

The startup has been acquired by Spotify's former head of innovation, Máuhan M Zonoozy, who founded a video clipping and remixing startup called **Bubbl** before leaving Spotify. The acquisition suggests that the company is looking to leverage its expertise in digital music and audio processing for its own development.

**Investment Insights**

The pre-seed funding raises significant funds for the startup, which has gained attention from major music industry players. The notable investors behind the round include Spotify's former CEO, **Dawn Ostroff**. Ostroff has served as an advisor to the company, offering guidance on content and advertising.

**The Future of Music**

The startup's focus on interactive, DIY approaches to music creation reflects the trend towards user-generated content in the digital music landscape. **A Vinyl Bar in Shibuya** is part of this shift, providing users with a range of tools and platforms to express themselves creatively.
