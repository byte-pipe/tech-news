## 5 Features I Wish Existed

### 1. An In-Thread Translate Toggle

* A tiny translation button that allows multilingual conversations to feel effortless.
* Current translations skip invisible posts when most non-speakers comment in a language other than English.

### 2. Visible Public Edit History

* A public revision history that makes it accessible to readers after they edit a published post, without increasing Writers' workload.
* Makes accountability on the platform more effective.

### 3. Real Series Discovery

* Navigation is fine, every post with a series widget linking to all other parts.
* Navigation problem lies in the initial stage, search function for series is currently not provided.

### 4. Opt-in Push Notifications

* DEV emails readers when they reply or mention someone, adding a new layer of engagement.
* Increases awareness of content and encourages readers to engage.
