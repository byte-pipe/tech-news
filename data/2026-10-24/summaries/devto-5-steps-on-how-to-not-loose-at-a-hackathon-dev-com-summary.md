# 5 Steps on How to not loose at a hackathon? - DEV Community

## Step 1: Talk to People to Make Connections
When participating in a hackathon, talking to people from diverse backgrounds and experiences will help you avoid losing. These conversations can lead to new connections, collaborations, and insights that might not come from just talking to teammates. Engage in discussions with others, especially organizers and support staff, to gain a deeper understanding of the event and its goals.

## Step 2: Observe to Gain New Insights
Observe the way other teams work, the tools they use, and what they discover along the way. This can help you avoid common pitfalls and develop your own creative solutions. Watch how different projects approach problems and what works well, even if it's not what you would do in a different context.

## Step 3: Explore the Space to Find Ideas
Hackathons can be a treasure trove of unique projects, innovative solutions, and forgotten ideas. Explore the space around you, ask questions, and seek out advice or inspiration from others. This can lead to unexpected breakthroughs and a more enriching experience than simply building something yourself.

## Step 4: Focus on the Journey, Not Just the Prize
While winning is not the only goal of a hackathon, it's essential to remember that. Focus on the process, learn from others, and enjoy the journey of innovation. The prize can often be taken with you, while the memories and connections made are truly unique.

## Step 5: Join a Community to Overcome Loss of Motivation
Lastly, consider joining a community dedicated to hackathons, such as Major League Hacking. Many organizers and participants find this support network invaluable in sticking with the experience even when motivation starts to wane. Being part of a community can make a significant difference in staying engaged and enjoying the experience.
