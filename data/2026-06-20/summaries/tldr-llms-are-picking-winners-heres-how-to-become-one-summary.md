# LLMs are picking winners. Here’s how to become one

## 1. Do LLMs even know you exist?
- Test visibility by prompting ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini with generic queries such as “best X tool” or “alternative to Y.”
- Use an AEO tool that runs hundreds of prompts daily to monitor presence across models over time.
- Expect low or missing visibility at first; models rely on older training data and search, so you must get your product into the sources they query.

## 2. SEO isn’t dead, it just got a new job
- Traditional SEO fundamentals still matter: clear structure, semantic HTML, fast load times, and direct answers.
- Content must be scannable and citable in small chunks, not just useful as a whole page.
- AEO checklist:
  * Make content scannable with subheadings, short paragraphs, and appropriate lists.  
  * Write in natural, conversational language.  
  * Answer the question directly in the first sentence; add context afterward.  
  * Structure for retrieval: use H2s that are actual questions, standalone definitions, summary tables, clear data, and FAQs.  
  * Be specific with concrete details (real numbers, named tools, precise scenarios) rather than vague statements.

## 3. Pick your battles
- Prioritize cleaning and improving owned content before chasing external channels.
- Optional side quests (when you have capacity): optimize Reddit presence, claim a Wikipedia page, build a YouTube strategy, earn traditional press mentions, target specific high‑ranking blog posts.
- Treat extra efforts as secondary; focus first on making your own assets genuinely great.

## 4. Call the bullshit, especially your own
- Be skeptical of inflated claims like “increased prompt visibility 50x” when prompt selection is self‑chosen.  
- Scrutinize vendor case studies: ask for baseline, measurement methodology, and whether results show correlation or causation.  
- Embrace honesty—if you don’t know the impact yet, say so. This prevents building strategies on false assumptions.

## 5. You can just… ask
- Add a conditional question to onboarding: “Did you hear about us through AI? Which prompt did you use?”  
- Collect first‑party prompt data; PostHog gathered over 9,200 real user prompts in three months.  
- Automate daily Slack reports with new prompts, feed insights back into AEO strategy. The setup costs near zero and becomes a highly valuable strategic input.

## 6. AEO reporting is a quilt, not a blanket
- No single data source tells the full story. Combine referrer traffic, self‑reported attribution, and other signals to build a comprehensive view of LLM‑driven visibility.
