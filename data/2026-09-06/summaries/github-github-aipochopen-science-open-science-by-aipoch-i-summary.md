## AI Research Workbench for Reproducible Science

### Overview

Open Science is an open-source, local-first, and model-agnostic AI research workbench that enables reproducible, inspectable research with scientific AI agents, Python, and R execution.

### Key Features

* Supports computational and data-intensive research across various disciplines (machine learning, statistics, life sciences, chemistry, materials science, physics, and environmental science)
* Supports the research process from literature review and hypothesis development to code execution, data analysis, simulation, visualization, and reporting
* Supports model-agnostic AI research, enabling reproducible experiments and improved reproducibility

### Development

* Developed by AIPOCH for scientists and researchers
* Supports cross-platform development, with support for macOS, Windows, and Linux

### Supporting Tools and Utilities

* Scientific AI agents
* Python and R execution
* Scientific data connectors
* Cross-platform support

### Example Use Cases
