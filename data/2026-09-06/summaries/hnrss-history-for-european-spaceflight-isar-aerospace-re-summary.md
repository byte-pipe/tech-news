# European Spaceflight: Isar Aerospace Reaches Orbit with V2 Mission

Isar Aerospace, the first European commercial space company, successfully delivered satellites into orbit on the second flight of its V2 mission, marking a historic turning point for the global space industry.

## Key Details

*   Was the launch of its **second flight**.
*   Launched from Isar Aerospace's dedicated **dedicated launch complex at Andøya Space** in Norway.
*   Deployed satellites from the **Microlauncher Competition** provided by the German Space Agency (DLR).
*   Successfully completed several milestones, including:
    *   Transiting MaxQ
    *   Completing MECO and stage separation
    *   Igniting the second stage as planned
    *   Crossing the **Kármán line** at an altitude of 100 kilometers
    *   Jettisoning the **payload fairing** before reaching orbital velocity
    *   Completing circularization burn after completing **launch**
*   Has **achieved significant milestones**, including breaking records in European commercial spaceflight.

## Achievements and Benefits

*   Isar Aerospace has now achieved the **mark** of having **sovereign access to space**, expanding Europe's capabilities for commercial and institutional customers.
*   **Faster-than-planned rapid scaling** of launch vehicle production, **delivering on order pipeline**, and **meeting surging global demand** have taken a significant step forward for the European space industry.
*   Demonstrates the capability to **deliver on a commercial and institutional order pipeline**, opening **a new era for the global space industry**.
