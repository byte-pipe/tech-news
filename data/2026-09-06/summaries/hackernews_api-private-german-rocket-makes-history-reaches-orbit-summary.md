# Private German rocket makes history, reaches orbit from European soil | Space

**Isar Aerospace's Spectrum Rocket Makes History**

* Launched from Andøya Space Center in Norway on Sept. 5, 2026
* Successful orbital launch after being the first Western European rocket to reach space
* Spectrum rocket's second shot at the apple in its mission to prove the capabilities of its new rocket design

**Key Facts and Achievements**

* The second stage of the Spectrum rocket reached orbit after a seven-minute flight
* An unintended opening of the vent valve and loss of attitude control led to a failure on its first attempted flight in March 2025
* Isar Aerospace retrofitted the design for its second attempt, "Onward and Upward", and achieved a successful orbital launch
* The rocket did not carry viable payloads on its debut flight but does on its current one
* The launch validated the engineering capabilities of the team and demonstrated the rocket's strength and reliability
