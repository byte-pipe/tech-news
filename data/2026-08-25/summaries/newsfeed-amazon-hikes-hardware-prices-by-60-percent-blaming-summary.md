# Summary of “Amazon hikes hardware prices by 60 percent, blaming memory shortage”

## Key Points
- Over the weekend Amazon increased prices on its hardware lineup—Fire TV, Echo, Kindle, and Eero—by as much as 60 %.

- Amazon attributes the hikes to “significant increases in memory and storage component costs” caused by a global memory shortage driven by the AI boom (“RAMmageddon”).
- The company says it absorbed the higher component costs for as long as possible and will continue to run occasional promotions throughout the next year.
- The shortage is expected to persist through 2027, with prices possibly stabilizing in 2028.
- Apple has faced a similar situation, raising device prices and introducing a leasing program to spread costs over time.
- The broader implication is that the current market environment makes purchasing consumer hardware more expensive for customers.
