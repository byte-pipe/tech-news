# Apple lays off 200+ people across Vision Pro and Siri teams

## Overview
- Apple has cut more than 200 jobs across teams working on Vision Pro, Siri, and software engineering, according to Bloomberg.  
- An Apple spokesperson said the changes are meant to “evolve our business to deliver the best experiences for our users” and that the company will support affected employees, including opportunities to apply for other roles.

## Vision Pro reductions
- Approximately 100 positions are being eliminated from the Vision Pro group.  
- The company is largely shutting down a Vision Pro team focused on gaming and scaling back the team that produces Immersive Video content.  
- Apple will continue to produce a smaller number of videos in‑house and will encourage third‑party creators to develop immersive content.  
- Vision Pro and its visionOS operating system are not being discontinued; the short‑term focus shifts to smart glasses that will not support immersive video or advanced gaming.  
- Bloomberg reports that Apple is considering a new Vision Pro model for release as early as the end of 2028.  
- An earlier report noted about 60 Vision Pro layoffs, indicating a continued reduction in that area.

## Siri and software engineering cuts
- Around 100 positions are being cut from Siri and related software teams.  
- The reorganization is linked to the launch of Siri AI, which uses a revamped technical architecture and requires different expertise.  
- Apple plans to eliminate some existing roles, reallocate resources, and create new positions that support the updated Siri platform.  
- The “Intelligent Systems Experience” team is also affected as Apple realigns AI‑powered capabilities across apps, features, and services, eliminating some roles while adding new ones.

## Additional information
- Apple emphasizes that new roles will be created alongside the cuts.  
- The company commits to assisting impacted employees throughout their transition.
