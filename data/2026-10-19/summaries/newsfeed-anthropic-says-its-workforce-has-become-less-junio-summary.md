# Anthropic says its workforce has become less junior and is spending more time in meetings | TechCrunch

## Key Findings
- Junior hires in technical departments fell from 21 % to 14 % overall and from 27 % to 9 % among technical hires since early 2025.  
- Technical staff now make up 56 % of the workforce, down from 61 %, a shift attributed to the expansion of business functions and low‑code tools that let non‑technical employees create software.  
- Employees feel “eternal pressure” to move quickly and use Claude for everything; a 2026 survey shows 27 % say Claude made them more productive, while 20 % say their role has shifted to “managing Claudes.”  
- Human attention and judgment are becoming a bottleneck because Claude generates a “deluge” of text that must be read and organized.  
- AI adoption has increased output: more code per person, more sales per rep, and more contracts processed, with no clear evidence of declining code quality.  
- Calendar data reveal meeting time per employee is up 20 % year‑over‑year (16 % for technical contributors), driven by the need for human trust, accountability, and face‑to‑face coordination.

## Employee Perspectives
- One engineer compared managing multiple Claude instances to babysitting, noting each demands equal attention.  
- Staff describe a constant push to move fast and rely heavily on Claude in daily work.

## Company Interpretation
- Anthropic presents its internal trends as a preview of how AI could reshape work organization, hiring practices, and labor demand across the broader economy.  
- While AI boosts productivity, it also creates new coordination challenges that increase meeting time and demand human oversight.
