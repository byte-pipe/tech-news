# Anthropic commits 5% of its shares to a ‘public benefit fund’

## Announcement overview
- Anthropic created a “public benefit fund” to give the company more flexibility and scale for societal‑good projects.  
- The fund is a wholly‑owned entity within Anthropic.

## Fund composition and potential growth
- Initial commitment: **5 % of Anthropic’s shares**.  
- Option to add an additional **2 % of shares for every 50 % increase** in the company’s valuation.  
- Intended uses include large donations to traditional nonprofits and direct distributions to specific populations.

## Current and future initiatives
- Already allocated **$200 million** to study AI’s impact on the job market.  
- The fund is positioned to support “bolder approaches” as AI progress accelerates.

## Industry context
- The move comes amid growing negative public sentiment toward AI, which CEO Dario Amodei describes as a “crisis of trust.”

## Governance and next steps
- Anthropic will release more details about the fund’s structure and decision‑making processes in the coming months.
