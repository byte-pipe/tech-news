# Investing in the public benefit

## Announcement overview
- Anthropic will allocate a portion of its shares to a new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund (APBF).  
- 5 % of Anthropic’s shares are committed today, pending stockholder approval.  
- An additional 2 % may be added for every 50 % increase in the company’s value above a baseline, at the board’s discretion.

## Purpose and activities
- As a public‑benefit corporation, Anthropic conducts AI‑safety, interpretability, and applied biomedical research internally, and offers Claude discounts to nonprofits and scientific institutions.  
- The APBF’s flexible structure will allow larger donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations.

## Example focus: labor‑market impacts
- AI can generate huge economic value but may destabilize many workers.  
- Anthropic has already pledged $200 million to study and address this issue.  
- The Fund will give Anthropic the versatility to pursue bolder, scalable interventions as AI progresses.

## Fund structure
- The APBF is created as a wholly‑owned LLC rather than a 501(c)(3) or similar entity, enabling adjustable forms of giving.  
- No equity held by the LLC will be used for lobbying or political contributions, domestically or abroad.

## Scaling with company growth
- The Fund’s share of Anthropic’s equity grows as the company’s valuation rises, directing equity that would otherwise fund executive compensation toward the public good.  

## Historical commitment context
- Founders originally pledged to donate 80 % of their equity holdings.  
- Anthropic runs an equity‑donation‑match program; roughly half of employees participate.  
- Combined with today’s announcement, about 20 % of Anthropic’s shares are now earmarked for the public good, with the potential for additional percentages if valuation milestones are met.

## Next steps
- In the coming months Anthropic will disclose more details on the APBF’s governance, valuation milestones, and how additional equity may be added.  
- A public‑benefit report will be released to track progress against the mission.
