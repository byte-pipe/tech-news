# Apple’s AirPods with cameras for AI are apparently close to production

## Development status
- Apple testers are “actively using” prototype AirPods that are in the design‑validation test stage, one step before production‑validation testing.  
- This information comes from Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman.

## Camera functionality
- The built‑in cameras do not capture high‑resolution photos or video.  
- They collect low‑resolution visual data that can be sent to Siri, allowing queries such as “what can I cook with these ingredients?” or providing assistance for turn‑by‑turn directions.  
- A small LED will indicate when visual data is being transmitted to the cloud.

## Design details
- The new model will resemble the AirPods Pro 3 but feature longer stems to accommodate the camera hardware.  
- The overall aesthetic remains similar to the AirPods Pro 3 released in September 2025.

## Timeline and launch expectations
- Apple originally aimed to launch the AI‑enabled AirPods in the first half of 2026, but the schedule slipped due to delays in the upgraded Siri.  
- The improved Siri is expected to roll out in September 2026, and the AirPods may launch around the same time.

## Competitive landscape and related Apple AI projects
- The AI‑infused AirPods would place Apple in direct competition with Meta’s smart glasses and could give it an edge over OpenAI, which is reportedly developing an AI‑powered phone.  
- Apple is also working on smart glasses and an AI pendant, with possible releases as early as 2027.
