# Businesses eager but unprepared for AI to transform their security strategies

## Dive Brief
- Companies are confident that AI will improve cybersecurity, yet they neglect fundamental tools such as identity management and zero‑trust networking, according to Zoho’s “State of Workforce Password Security” report.  
- There is a large gap between AI optimism and implementation readiness: 90 % of respondents believe AI can strengthen defenses, but only 8 % feel ready to deploy AI‑powered security tools.  
- The report also provides data on recent cyber‑attack experiences and future security‑spending plans.

## Dive Insight
- **Readiness gap:** Zoho describes an 82‑point difference between belief (90 %) and deployment readiness (8 %), marking a critical inflection point for workforce security.  
- **Identity visibility:** About 75 % of respondents lack full visibility into their identity ecosystem—36 % have partial visibility, 38 % limited visibility, and 14 % no visibility at all. Zoho warns this “identity visibility gap” is a central vulnerability for unauthorized access, insider threats, and compliance failures.  
- **Zero‑trust networking:** Roughly two‑thirds of businesses have no zero‑trust strategy; half of those plan to adopt one within three years, creating a “critical window of vulnerability” for credential‑based attacks. Over 50 % cite “unmanageable” growth in identity ecosystems as a barrier, and one‑third lack the necessary processes and tools.  
- **Attack prevalence:** One‑third of surveyed companies experienced a cyberattack in the past year, with an additional 7 % unsure whether they had been attacked.  
- **AI use cases:** The most desired AI capabilities are anomaly and threat detection (68 %), automatic policy enforcement (61 %), and employee‑behavior analysis (54 %).  
- **Adoption barriers:** Outdated technology, migration complexity, and budget constraints are the top reasons companies hesitate to adopt AI for security.

## Survey Details
- Sample: 3,300 cybersecurity professionals across nine regions and six industries worldwide.
