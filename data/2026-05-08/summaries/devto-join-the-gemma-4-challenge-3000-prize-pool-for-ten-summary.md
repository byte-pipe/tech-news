# Join the Gemma 4 Challenge – $3,000 prize pool

## Overview
- The Gemma 4 Challenge runs from May 6 to May 24, inviting developers to explore Google’s open‑source Gemma 4 models.
- Participants can either **build** a project using Gemma 4 or **write** a post about it.
- Total prize pool: $3,000 across ten winners plus badges and DEV++ memberships for all valid submissions.

## Prompt Options

### Build With Gemma 4
- Create any useful or creative application (IoT, multimodal tool, long‑context reasoning, etc.) with a Gemma 4 model at its core.
- Submission must explain why the chosen model was selected and what it enabled.

### Write About Gemma 4
- Publish an educational or inspirational piece (how‑to guide, comparison, personal essay, technical deep‑dive, etc.).
- Must provide genuine, useful content for the community.
- Projects belong in the “Build” track; each entry can compete in only one category.

## Model Choices
- **Small sizes**: 2B and 4B parameters – optimized for ultra‑mobile, edge, and browser environments.
- **Dense**: 31B parameters – balances server‑grade performance with local execution.
- **Mixture‑of‑Experts (MoE)**: 26B parameters – high‑throughput, advanced reasoning.

## Prizes
- **Build winners (5)**: $500 cash, DEV++ membership, exclusive DEV badge.
- **Write winners (5)**: $100 cash, DEV++ membership, exclusive DEV badge.
- All valid participants receive a completion badge on their DEV profile.

## Judging Criteria
- **Build submissions**: intentional model use, technical quality, creativity, usability.
- **Write submissions**: clarity, depth, originality, practical value, writing quality.

## Getting Started
- Access Gemma 4 for free via:
  - Gemini API in Google AI Studio.
  - Local download from Hugging Face or Kaggle (runs on phones, Raspberry Pi 5, etc.).
  - OpenRouter free tier (31B model, no credit card required).

## Important Dates
- May 6: Challenge begins.
- May 24, 11:59 PM PDT: Submission deadline.
- June 4: Winners announced.
