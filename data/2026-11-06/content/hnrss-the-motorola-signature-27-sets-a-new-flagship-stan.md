# Motorola sets a new flagship standard with motorola signature 27, featuring its most advanced camera system, next-generation agentic AI and the brightest display available in a smartphone

 
November 5, 2026

* The world’s best camera system launched in 202625, outranking all other Android and iOS devices, as assessed by DXOMARK.
* The brightest display available in a smartphone20, with an Extreme AMOLED Duo tandem display and up to 12,000 nits of peak brightness4.
* Among the first smartphones powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 Mobile Platform, delivering breakthrough performance, next generation connectivity, and advanced AI experiences.
* Designed for Gemini Intelligence⁷, the motorola signature 27 meets the high system standards required to run Google’s best Gemini experiences.
* The only smartphone in-market with Audio by Bang & Olufsen, providing richer, more immersive sound through a custom-tuned audio experience.
* A 7000mAh battery¹¹, 35% larger than the last generation.
* The first Motorola device to support GrapheneOS, an privacy and security-focused mobile operating system.

Motorola, a Lenovo Company, is designing to a higher standard with the new motorola signature 27, the company’s most advanced flagship smartphone to date, bringing together distinctive design, category-leading innovation and next-generation AI experiences. From its contoured edges, expressive finishes and refined craftsmanship to the breakthrough technologies within, every detail of the motorola signature 27 was purposefully built to deliver a premium experience without compromise. Featuring Motorola’s most advanced camera system yet,  recognized with a DXOMARK Gold Label for image quality performance, the brightest Extreme AMOLED Duo Tandem display¹ available in a smartphone, the Snapdragon® 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 Mobile Platform, making it among the first smartphones to unlock next-generation connectivity and AI experiences, and the only smartphone in-market with Audio by Bang & Olufsen, the motorola signature 27 represents the pinnacle of Motorola innovation.

### Motorola’s best camera system yet, recognized for imaging excellence

The motorola signature 27 includesthe world’s best camera sytsem launched in 202625, combining advanced hardware, cinematic video, and effortless photography. Awarded the coveted DXOMARK Gold Label in recognition of its industry-leading level of imaging quality performance and outranking all other Android and iOS devices, the motorola signature 27’s camera system delivers eye-catching photos and videos, with:

* A50MP Sony LYTIA L910 main cameracaptures exceptional clarity, rich color and deep contrast for vibrant, ready to share results. Combined withLOFIC technology,it records brighter, more balanced video with consistent detail across challenging lighting conditions, whether filming concert stages, neon-lit streets, candlelit dinners or nights out with friends.
* A 200MP periscope telephoto camera with 100x Super Zoomcaptures everything from distant landmarks and architectural details to wildlife and action unfolding far away with remarkable clarity. Powered by AI, 100x Super Zoom Pro helps preserve natural color, sharpness and details even at extreme distances. The 3x periscope telephoto lens also delivers flattering, natural-looking portraits, while ultra-resolution imaging provides greater flexibility for cropping, reframing, and editing.
* Thefirst ever dedicated EVS action sensor on a smartphone²introduces a new approach to smartphone motion photography, helping capture fast-moving subjects with greater clarity and precision. Working together with the Action Shot feature, it freezes motion while maximizing available light, producing crisp, vibrant images with richer detail and more natural color.
* The 50MP ultrawide camera³fits more into every frame, transforming tight spaces into expansive compositions and making it easier to capture sweeping landscapes, large landmarks and group photos without stepping back.
* And a50MP front cameradelivers sharp, ready-to-share selfies and supports 4K video recording, so selfie videos feature the same bold colors and clarity as still photos.

For a more cinematic video experience, the motorola signature 27 features a powerfulVideo Enhancement Enginethat uses AI to intelligently optimize color, contrast and dynamic range in real time. Support forDolby Vision®, the same technology used by leading filmmakers, helps enable richer, more immersive video. Combined with theQualcomm® Electronic Image Stabilizationand3.5° OIS hardwareon the main camera, panning, walking, and handheld recording looks more fluid and stable, so users can stay in the moment while the camera does the hard work.

### the brightest display available in a smartphone20

Engineered to unlock the full potential of the latest advances in display technology, the motorola signature 27 features a new Extreme AMOLED Duo display, the brightest display available, designed to provide a premium viewing experience in virtually any environment.

Built with breakthrough Tandem Technology, it combines two emissive layers into one, producing brighter visuals, richer color and greater efficiency. With up to 12,0004nits of peak brightness, users can comfortably view maps, messages, photos and videos virtually anywhere, whether navigating a sunny afternoon, checking directions on the go or capturing content outdoors, without needing to shield the screen or strain to see it. Plus, 1.5K Super HD resolution and Dolby Vision® bring entertainment to life with exceptional sharpness, contrast and precision.

This advanced display is also designed to be smarter and more durable, delivering up to 34% greater energy efficiency and up to a 121% longer lifespan than traditional AMOLED panels.24Pantone® Validated color calibration ensures colors and skin tones appear true to life5, while SGS-certified blue light technology helps reduce eye strain.

### powering next generation experiences

At the heart of the motorola signature 27 is the Snapdragon® 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 Mobile Platform, making it among the first smartphones to feature Qualcomm Technologies’ latest flagship mobile chipset. Engineered to power breakthrough gaming, advanced camera experiences, ultra-fast connectivity and AI, it delivers the performance needed for today’s most demanding mobile experiences and the next wave of AI innovation.

To sustain peak performance when it matters most, the motorola signature 27 pairs the platform with Motorola’s ArcticMesh cooling system that uses micro-diamond-infused thermal gel and a thermal chamber that is 40% larger than the previous generation⁶ to keep performance running at its best during extended gaming sessions, AI tasks, video recording and intensive multitasking.With a new 6‑antenna design delivering faster 5G downloads, up to 2x faster Wi‑Fi speeds6, Wi‑Fi 8 compatibility, the motorola signature 27 is built to stay fast, capable and ready for next-era AI. Andseven years of OS upgrades23help ensure the experience stays reliable long into the future.

### AI that goes beyond assistance

The motorola signature 27 is designed for Gemini Intelligence, which brings the best of Gemini to Android’s most advanced devices, alongside out-of-box Google Gemini experiences and Motorola Qira.

The motorola signature 27 can understand context, help people create, support decisions and move from intention to action across the apps and services they use every day. As Google rolls out these capabilities toGemini Intelligence⁷devices globally in the coming months, motorola signature 27 will be ready to bring them to users around the world:

* Whether booking a ride or preparing a grocery order from a shopping list on your screen, Gemini helps users go from intention to action with less effort.  Geminican handle complex, multi-step tasks on a user’s behalf, reducing app switching and helping them stay focused on what matters. Users remain in control throughout, with the ability to view progress, jump in, or stop a task.16
* Rambler17transforms natural speech into polished messages, letting users think out loud while it refines their words in the background. Rambler removes filler words, edits repetition, and organizes ideas into clear paragraphs or bullet points. Users can correct themselves mid-sentence, switch languages seamlessly, and review everything before sending, ensuring they share exactly what they intend. Afterwards, users can quickly ask Rambler to rewrite, rephrase, translate, or reformat their text.
* Create My Widget18enables users to create personalized widgets by simply describing what they want to see. Whether it’s a tracker to follow your monthly spending, daily recipe inspiration, or a trip countdown with your travel details, your homescreen can be as personalized as you want it to be.
* Sign-to-Text19translates American Sign Language (ASL) into written English in real time using the device’s front-facing camera. Integrated with Gboard and Live Transcribe, it opens new ways for users to communicate across digital and in-person interactions.

Right out of the box, the motorola signature 27 also includes powerfulGoogle Gemini⁸experiences designed to bring new levels of creativity, including Gemini Omni, which enables users to create and edit video through natural conversation, and Lyria which generates custom soundtracks from simple prompts.

In addition, the device featuresMotorola Qira⁹, a built-in AI system designed to connect and enhance experiences across supported Motorola and Lenovo devices, helping users get more from their broader ecosystem through a single, seamless hybrid AI experience.

### the only smartphone with “Audio by Bang & Olufsen”

As the only smartphone in-market to feature Audio by Bang & Olufsen, the motorola signature 27 unlocks an elevated audio experience built on Motorola’srecently announcedmulti-year partnership with Bang & Olufsen. Combining Motorola’s premium mobile innovation with Bang & Olufsen’s renowned expertise in sound and design, the device is engineered to deliver sound that is meant to be felt, not just heard. Featuring dedicated Sound Modes and an all-new speaker architecture, the device is tuned to preserve the character of the original recording, alongside deep bass, clear vocals and greater fidelity across music, movies and games.

### distinctive design, exceptional durability

With the motorola signature 27, Motorola continues the evolution of its signature family design philosophy, that balances aesthetic expression with everyday usability. The device features a sleek aluminum silhouette with contoured edges and a thin profile that feels comfortable in hand, while delivering a distinctly premium look and feel. Complementing the refined design is a seamlessly integrated camera plane, finished in smooth ceramic and brushed aluminum, creating a sophisticated focal point that feels both purposeful and modern.

Precisely crafted with a ceramic and glass construction, the motorola signature 27 is available in two unique, elevated finishes: PANTONE Coal Smoke, which features a tactical weave finish inspired by automotive design and performance materials, and PANTONE Capulet Olive, which features a textile-inspired finish influenced by premium fashion and interior design.

Premium materials and durability come together in the motorola signature 27, featuringCorning® Gorilla® Glass Ceramic 3, an aerospace-grade aluminum frame,military-grade durability (MIL-STD-810H)10,andIP68andIP69 ratingsfor enhanced protection against the elements.22

### power that gives the freedom to do more

To back up all this powerful innovation, the motorola signature 27 features a7000mAhbattery11, 35% larger than the last generation, so users can stay connected longer, stream and create without interruption, and move through the day without constantly thinking about battery life.

When battery does get low,90W TurboPower™ chargingquickly restores power for the day in just 12 minutes12,13, whether on a coffee break or while getting ready. For a more effortless experience, users can go cord-free with50W wireless charging, powering 0 to 100% in up to 80 minutes of wireless charging.12,14

### Motorola’s first GrapheneOS-supported device, built for privacy and peace of mind

Data privacy matters, and Motorola continues to build on its commitment to security with ThinkShield®, which helps protect users from the factory to the phone, and Moto Secure, a centralized destination for managing privacy and security features.

Expanding that commitment even further, the motorola signature 27 will kickstart a new era of smartphone privacy and security. It will be Motorola’s first device to support GrapheneOS, a hardened, open-source operating system based on the Android Open Source Project. Developed in partnership with the GrapheneOS Foundation, the leading nonprofit in advanced mobile security, the device reflects Motorola’s commitment to bringing cutting-edge security to everyday users across the globe.

That commitment runs deeper than software: GrapheneOS sets demanding requirements for the hardware it runs on and how it’s kept updated, and the motorola signature 27 is built to meet them. The device is Motorola’s first with a performant implementation of ARM Memory Tagging Extension, a new hardware caching mechanism for helping ensure system integrity and privacy. More details on it will be shared in the coming months.

Motorola signature 27 will be available in markets worldwide in the coming months, with a starting MSRP of $1,599. For local price information and availability, please contact your local PR team.
