# Summary of “I wish tech companies knew more about me”

## Main claim
- Although it is widely accepted that tech companies collect massive amounts of data on users, the author feels that this data is not being used effectively to provide truly personalized experiences.

## Examples of poor personalization
- **Ads that miss the mark**
  - Shown a purple gamepad ad right after purchasing one, yet also receives unrelated ads for industrial‑grade 3D‑printing resin, a smart‑home wellness system that doesn’t ship to Finland, and an asphalt‑cleaning tractor.
  - Clicking on a 3D‑printing ad leads to a flood of similar ads, despite having no 3D printer.
- **Music streaming quirks**
  - The author consistently skips vocal electronic tracks on Spotify and YouTube Music, but the recommendation algorithms never learn this preference.
- **Language and locale mismatches**
  - Google Chrome, logged into a Google account with English settings, redirects to a Finnish page after a search.
  - Services automatically switch to German when the author travels to Germany, despite the user’s English locale.
  - PlayStation’s online store reverts to Finnish after manual language changes.
- **Irrelevant product suggestions**
  - After searching for a specific oven model’s installation video, the homepage fills with generic oven and stove videos.
  - Bank offers car‑insurance promotions to a customer who already has that insurance.
  - Mobile provider sends children‑focused discounts to a childless user.
- **Content availability issues**
  - HBO recommends foreign movies without English subtitles, making them unwatchable.
  - Netflix on Apple TV forces a user selection screen every launch, even though only one profile exists and there is no option to disable it.

## Consequences and frustrations
- The author experiences a disconnect between the data that is presumably collected and the relevance of the content, ads, and settings presented.
- Repeated irrelevant or inaccurate suggestions feel wasteful and intrusive.
- The lack of nuanced understanding leads to annoyance rather than convenience.

## Desired outcome
- If companies already possess detailed user data, the author urges them to apply it more intelligently, delivering genuinely useful and personalized experiences instead of generic or mismatched content.
