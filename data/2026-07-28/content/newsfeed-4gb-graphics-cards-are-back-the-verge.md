# 4GB graphics cards are back

﻿ASRock’s Radeon RX 9050 is the first modern GPU in years to include only 4GB of VRAM.

The first modern GPU with 4GB of VRAM has appeared, asRAM prices and component shortagespush tech prices higher. AsDigital Foundryreports, a listing for an AMD Radeon RX 9050 with 4GB of RAM wasspotted on ASRock’s websiteby auser on X, the same day ASRock also launchedan 8GB versionof the RX 9050.

Does it make sense to buy a graphics card with just 4GB of VRAM? According to the specs on ASRock’s website, the 4GB version of the RX 9050 is built on AMD’s RDNA 4 architecture and has a 64-bit memory interface, 16 compute units, and a boost clock speed of up to 2,600MHz / 18GBps. That should be enough to enable new features like FSR 4, but is far below the suggested requirements of current AAA titles likeAssassin’s Creed Black Flag ResyncedandHalo: Campaign Evolved, even if you’re willing to run at 1080p and aren’t worried about future proofing.

While ASRock has not yet announced pricing for the 4GB RX 9050,Tom’s Hardwarespotted it ina Best Buy listingfor an $889 prebuilt gaming PC that also includes 8GB of DDR4 RAM, 500GB of SSD storage, and an AMD Ryzen 5 5500 CPU.

ASRock and AMD did not immediately respond to a request for on the record comment.
