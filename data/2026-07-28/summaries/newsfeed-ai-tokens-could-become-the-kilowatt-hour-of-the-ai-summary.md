# AI tokens could become the kilowatt-hour of the AI age : Planet Money : NPR

**AI Tokens Could Become Kilowatt-Hours of the AI Age**

* **Nerdy Tech and Economy Conversations Mainstream**
	+ AI tokens are a new metric to measure AI usage and pricing
	+ Companies in tech sector increasingly use AI in their work, leading to higher token bills for businesses
	+ Examples include Uber and Amazon
* **Tokens as Digital Meter Running Background**
	+ Every time an AI model performs task or writes prompt, it processes tokens
	+ Tokens represent tiny chunks of text data generated by AI models
* **Token-Based Pricing Trends**
	+ Businesses are now charged based on token usage, while consumers have traditionally paid flat rates
	+ Companies reducing soaring token bills through guardrails and using AIs in more ways
	+ "Tokenminimizing" has become a trend among tech companies and researchers
* **Tracking AI Economic Impact with Tokens**
	+ Growing number of economists studying token-based pricing trends, such as which companies' stock prices rise and fall with AI growth
	+ Researchers use tokens to analyze how the economic impact of AI changes over time

**Summary**

AI tokens, used to track usage and bill customers, could become a key metric for measuring AI's economic impact. As AI adoption grows, so does the demand for AI services. Companies in the tech sector are taking note, reducing soaring token bills by implementing guardrails and using AIs in various ways. Meanwhile, economists are beginning to study how token-based pricing trends affect companies' stock prices, leading some to dub it "tokenminimizing."
