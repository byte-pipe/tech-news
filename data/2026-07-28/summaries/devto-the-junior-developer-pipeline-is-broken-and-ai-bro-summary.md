# The Junior Developer Pipeline Is Broken... And AI Broke It - DEV Community

## The Junior Developer Pipeline: What Lies Ahead?
A debate in the tech industry revolves around a statement that AI has made senior engineers more valuable. However, there is an overlooked consequence: where does the next generation of seniors come from?

#### **AI and Senior Engineers' Role**
Several points highlight the importance of human developers:

*   The story that AI automates work is comforting yet lacks depth.
*   Seniors are not a renewable resource as they require years of experience accumulated through real-world challenges.
*   AI has quietly replaced work that starts with junior developers, having consumed its workload over time.

#### **Where Recent Senior Engineers Came From**
Ask recent senior engineers about their path to becoming seniors:

*   Systems and codebases were built through multiple unglamorous tasks: debugging, system maintenance, research for new technologies.
*   Junior developers faced challenges: writing bug fixes, wiring software interactions, tracing issues.
*   The work involved understanding the framework, identifying potential pitfalls, and building a mental model of complex systems.

#### **Apprenticeship and Learning Curve**
Before AI tooling improves significantly:

*   Junior developers were tasked with learning:
    *   Boilerplate code: structure, conventions, usage
    *   Bug fixes: addressing technical issues
    *   Glue coding: integrating components into larger applications
    *   Tracing error messages: understanding system behavior

The journey to becoming a senior developer involved countless small tasks that led to a deeper understanding of complex systems. The narrative framing the role of seniors as recipients of AI is fundamentally at odds with this reality.

Seniors are not some magical, magically acquired class. They require dedication, perseverance, and a willingness to tackle challenging issues head-on.
