# Our position on Open-Weights Models

Dario Amodei, CEO, Anthropic, responds to concerns about open-weights models, a type of AI model used by tech companies and governments worldwide.

## Main Points

### General Overview
Anthropic has stated that open-weights models are a public good, offering value without requiring significant resources. They do not pose a cost or significant security risk.

### Key Concerns
The company emphasizes the following:

1.  **Protecting National Security** 
    *   Prior to developing and using AI models like open-weights, there is concern that authoritarian governments could misuse them for military superiority, surveillance, or repression.
2.  **Risks from Misuse**
    *   The potential for powerful models to be used in cyberattacks or biological attacks raises concerns about alignment problems and the difficulty of applying guardrails.

## Response
Anthropic has not advocated for banning open-weights models but maintains that these are safe and beneficial public goods when used responsibly. Concerns about national security are valid, as authoritarian regimes pose a significant threat with their own advancements in AI technology. The company emphasizes its commitment to transparency and alignment, stating that once such weights are released, it becomes difficult to withdraw them.

Anthropic's stance is primarily focused on protecting business interests and national security risks rather than advocating for bans on open-weights models overall.
