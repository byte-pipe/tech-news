# Amazon Invests in Lean Focused Research Organization for Mathematical Proofs 

## **Amazon Providing Long-Term Financial Support**

By *Amazon*, we are investing in the **Lean Focused Research Organization (FRO)***, which aims to advance **Lean**, a programming language that enables mathematical proofs of software correctness. This investment will support the development and validation of proof tools for safety-critical AI applications.

## **Key Findings:**

*   **Amazon FRO**: Provides long-term financial support to **Lean FRO**
*   **Applications**:
    *   Bedrock AgentCore (agentic safety)
    *   SampCert (differential-privacy guarantees)
    *   AWS Neuron (AI chip compilation)
*   **Scientific Applications**: Utilize LLM-Lean combinations for proof-based distributed protocols
*   **Transparency and Trustworthiness**: The independent Lean FRO is committed to ensuring transparency and trustworthiness, allowing customers, auditors, and regulators to independently validate proof tools
