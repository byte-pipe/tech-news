# Anatomy's Campaign for the Horniman Museum
------------------------------------------

## Overview

Anatomy's campaign for the Horniman Museum has launched on a bespoke typeface that reflects the museum's eclectic nature and history. The project began with research in the garden, where teams fieldtripped to document diverse textures, architectural details, specimens, animals, patterns, and unexpected moments.

## Design Philosophy

- **Exploring Eclecticism**: The campaign aims to showcase the unique blend of nature and culture found at the Horniman Museum.
- **Incorporating Personal Touch**: Found letterforms uncovered from the museum's collections are integrated into a cohesive visual identity.

## Key Features

- **Bespoke Typeface**: An existing typeface is repurposed, allowing for flexibility in design decisions.
- **Found Letterforms**: Hundreds of photographs capture diverse found letterforms, which were then drawn out and arranged on the site.
- **Collaborative Process**: Team discussions, comparisons, and debriefing sessions help refine the letterset's character.

## Typography Guidelines

- **Playful and Curious First Impression**: Letters should convey a sense of curiosity while allowing readability.
- **Balanced Balance**: Ensure enough characteristic design elements to identify as Horniman while maintaining restraint for legibility.
