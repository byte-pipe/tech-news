SpotifyandSpotify For Artistscontinually tweak their platforms, and three recent updates change how artists, labels, venues and other music marketers should execute campaigns.

From enhanced playlist personalization to stricter data rules, here is a breakdown of the newest features and what they mean for yourmusic marketing strategy.

### Spotify User Notes: Enhancing Personalized Playlists

Listeners can now add personal captions to songs on their playlists. Discover how User Notes builds fan connection and deepens music discovery.

What are Spotify User Notes?

Spotify is giving listeners a new way to makeplaylistsmore personal withUser Notes,a feature that lets users add notes to tracks on playlists they create or manage. Think of it as adding a personal caption to songs: a place to share why a track made the playlist, when you discovered it, a hidden gem worth remembering, the story behind it and more.

Available to Spotify users 16+ on Free and Premium across most of the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, User Notes transforms playlists from a collection of songs into a more personal space for music discovery, bringing together personalization and added context.

How Spotify User Notes changes music marketing

* Curator Storytelling:Playlist curators and tastemakers can now add context to why they selected your track, essentially providing a written endorsement or "pitch" directly inside the playlist.
* Artist-Curated Playlists:Artists can use this feature on their own profiles to speak directly to fans, sharing behind-the-scenes trivia or inspirations for tracks included in their "Artist Picks" or curated mood playlists.

How to create Spotify User Notes

1. On mobile, in any playlist you've created or added tracks to, tap thethree-dot menunext to a track.
2. Select"Add note."
3. Type a note (a memory, extra context for why you chose this song for this playlist) and tapSave.
4. Your note is now visible to anyone who can see the playlist, with your name linking to your profile.

### Discovery Mode Adds New Campaign Lift Metric

The new Campaign Lift metric shows how Discovery Mode boosts your streams. See the impact of paid marketing compared to organic reach.

What is the Campaign Lift metric?

Spotify has updated its performance reports forDiscovery Modeadding a new metric calledCampaign Lift. It uses machine-learning to estimate the 'true' impact of a campaign. Opting into Discovery Mode means accepting a lower royalty rate, so this metric calculates the rate your streams and listeners grew in Discovery Mode compared to an estimate of what your songs would have generated organically without the program.

How Discovery Mode Campaign Lift changes music marketing

* Clearer ROI Validation:Marketers no longer have to guess if a spike in streams was due to their Discovery Mode enrollment or an outside viral trend. Campaign Lift isolates the algorithmic boost.
* Smarter Track Selection:By analyzing which tracks generate the highest "lift," marketing teams can make better data-driven decisions on which catalog tracks to leave in Discovery Mode and which to pull.

### Spotify Rule Change Impacts Forever Pre-Saves

Spotify now requires fans to renew third-party app permissions every six months, changing how lifetime pre-saves and fan data collection work.

What are the updates to forever pre-saves?

Historically, third-party marketing apps (likeHypeddit,Laylo,SymphonyOS, andFeature.fm) could retain permanent connection access to a fan's Spotify account, enabling "forever" or "lifetime" pre-saves for all future releases. Spotify has updated its API rules, and nowthat connection must be renewed every 6 months. If a fan doesn't re-authenticate after six months, the automated pre-saves will stop.

How Spotify Pre-Save updates impact music marketing

* Focus on Owned Channels:Automated pre-save lists can no longer be treated as permanent assets. Marketers must build email and SMS lists to re-engage fans whenever access tokens expire.
* Release Frequency Matters:Artists who maintain consistent track release schedules (every 4–6 weeks) are better positioned to keep fan authorizations active before the 6-month window closes.
* Audit Your Toolkit:"Different providers handle this new rule differently. For example,Hypeddit’s automated release emails include a built-in one-tap reconnect button," advices Marlen Hüllbrock ofMusic Ally. "OnLaylo, when a fan replies to a message or clicks a button within a message, Laylo immediately uses their cached data to trigger the pre-save again. Other tools rely on the artist team to remind fans to pre-save again."

### Hypebot's Bottom Line

These three updates collectively shift across Spotify's ecosystem away from passive growth hacks and toward intentional, active engagement.

WhileUser NotesandCampaign Liftgive artists richer storytelling options and actionable ROI data. But tighterPre-Savelimits mean that automated shortcuts won't build a sustainable career.

Winning on Spotify today requires a dual strategy.

Work to maximize native features that deepen listener connections while aggressively building direct, owned channels off-platform to keep the core audience locked in. If you capture email and SMS info and communicate regularly, you are far less dependent on the changes that Spotify and other platforms make that often get between you and your fans.
