# Amgen says cloud data breach exposed patient health, proprietary info

## Key Points
- Amgen detected unauthorized activity in its cloud environments in July 2026 and activated its cybersecurity response plan.  
- Attackers exfiltrated data from multiple third‑party cloud systems, including proprietary information, protected health information (PHI), and other patient data.  
- The company has not identified the specific cloud providers involved, the method of compromise, the number of affected individuals, or any link to known threat actors.  
- Amgen considers the breach material due to the volume of potentially impacted files but does not expect a material impact on its financial condition or operations.  
- Independent forensic experts and third‑party cybersecurity specialists are investigating the incident.  
- Legal and regulatory notification requirements are being evaluated, and affected patients will be notified where required.  
- A query to Amgen about a possible vishing attack, the specific cloud services affected, and extortion attempts by groups such as ShinyHunters received no immediate response.
