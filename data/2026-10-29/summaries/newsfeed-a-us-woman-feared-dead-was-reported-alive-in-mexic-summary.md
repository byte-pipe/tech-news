# Summary of “A US woman feared dead was reported alive in Mexico. But police now say that isn’t true”

## Background
- Shayna Feinman, 38, disappeared in March 2024 while walking to an acquaintance’s home in rural Yavapai County, Arizona.  
- Her car was found abandoned near the home she shared with boyfriend Cavett Richards; her wallet, ID and cellphone were recovered.  
- Yavapai County Sheriff’s Office initially charged Richards and William DalCerro with second‑degree murder, conspiracy and concealing a corpse, but those charges were later dismissed.  
- A $20,000 reward was offered for information about her whereabouts.

## False claim of discovery in Tijuana
- The secretariat for security and citizen protection in Tijuana announced that a woman matching Feinman’s description had been located in the Encino neighborhood.  
- The statement included a photo of the woman sitting at a desk with a missing‑person poster and said she was placed in protective custody pending contact with the family and U.S. consular officials.  
- The announcement was posted on a social‑media page belonging to Shayna’s sister, Kara Feinman.

## Family and U.S. authorities’ reaction
- Kara Feinman expressed cautious hope but said she needed confirmation from the Yavapai Sheriff’s Office before believing the report.  
- She noted that the woman in the photo had “similar physical attributes” but emphasized the need for realistic expectations.  
- Yavapai Sheriff’s Office spokesperson Paul Wick said the reports were unconfirmed and that investigators were actively reviewing the information.

## Confirmation that the woman was not Shayna Feinman
- On Tuesday, the Yavapai Sheriff’s Office announced that physical‑evidence analysis confirmed the individual in Tijuana was not Shayna Feinman.  
- The office criticized earlier media reports for publishing the claim without official verification and reiterated that detectives continue to work with the family.
