* ### Tandem OLED.PROLED XDR combines two self-illuminating pixel layers for a jaw-dropping visual experience. Each pixel turns on and off independently, producing deep blacks right beside brilliant highlights — without the blooming and halos typical of backlit displays. And innovations in the OLED layers ensure grayscale accuracy and uniform brightness for greater contrast and clarity.
* ### Exceptional brightness and contrast.With 2000 nits of peak brightness for HDR content, PROLED XDR delivers bright specular highlights with breathtaking intensity. And with a remarkable 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio, even the deepest shadows retain incredible detail, bringing HDR content stunningly close to reality without compromise.
* ### Display engine intelligence.The display engine precisely controls the color and luminance of every pixel at the sub-millisecond level, compensating for content, pixel use, temperature, and environment all at once. The result is exceptional brightness uniformity and shadow detail, even in fast-motion content. It also monitors each pixel’s usage to preserve stunning image quality over time.
* ### P3 + Adobe RGB.PROLED XDR offers you both P3 and Adobe RGB color spaces. P3 provides consistency for HDR video and color grading, while Adobe RGB is essential for designers and photographers when going to print. And cutting-edge calibration ensures that luminance, white point, and color gamut are precisely aligned with standard targets.
* ### Incredible clarity in bright light.With up to 2000 nits peak SDR brightness and a state-of-the-art antireflective coating, your screen is easy to read even in direct sunlight. And the nano-texture option further reduces reflections and glare to eliminate distractions while maintaining an excellent viewing experience.

## Security

No compromises.

Security starts with Apple silicon and extends to the macOS architecture. This deep integration of hardware and software, along with automatic updates and built-in antivirus protection, helps keep MacBook Pro stable and secure for the long term. At the heart of MacBook Pro is the Secure Enclave, a dedicated security subsystem that’s separate from the main processor. It keeps your most sensitive data, including encryption keys and biometric information, fully isolated from the rest of the system — the same protection that powers Touch ID and Find My.

* ### Touch ID.Unlock your Mac, sign in to apps, and make secure payments with your fingertip. The Secure Enclave keeps your fingerprint data safe.
* ### Passwords app.Store your passwords and passkeys with end-to-end encryption, manage them across devices with iCloud Keychain, and receive alerts if any are at risk.
* ### FileVault.Encrypt and protect your files and data without having to think about it.
* ### Find My.Locate your misplaced Mac and remotely lock or erase it if needed.
