## Three core challenges of online writing
1. **Quality (craft)**
   - Is the argument coherent and useful?
   - Does the article merit another person’s time?
2. **Audience fit (relevance)**
   - Does the target audience care about the subject?
   - Is the prerequisite knowledge appropriate for the readers?
3. **Distribution (discovery)**
   - How does the article reach its audience? (search, followers, newsletters, community links, etc.)

- An article must satisfy all three; failure in any one yields low numbers for different reasons.
- The author’s own data shows only about 4 % of traffic comes from search, indicating a distribution problem for evergreen reference material.
