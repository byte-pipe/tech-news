# Big Tech ruined the cloud, so we’re renaming ours - Home Assistant

# Summary of “Big Tech ruined the cloud, so we’re renaming ours – Home Assistant”

## Why the name change
- The term *cloud* now suggests lock‑in, data harvesting and price hikes, which conflicts with our values.
- “Home Assistant Cloud” never described the service accurately; it never runs Home Assistant in the cloud.
- Renaming to **Home Assistant Link** better reflects that the service is an optional, privacy‑focused connection layer.

## Origin and purpose of the service
- Founded in 2018 by Nabu Casa to prevent burnout of core developers and to make remote access easy for users.
- Provides a subscription that funds the Open Home Foundation, supporting both paid staff and volunteers.
- The service is optional; Home Assistant works fully locally without it.

## How Home Assistant Link differs from typical Big‑Tech clouds
- **Optional**: smart‑home functions continue without it.  
- **No lock‑in**: users can switch to another service at any time.  
- **No walled garden**: outages or price changes of a single provider won’t cripple the system.  
- **Data privacy**: data is encrypted end‑to‑end; neither Nabu Casa nor the foundation can read it.  
- **Profit model**: majority of subscription revenue goes to the nonprofit Open Home Foundation, not to shareholders.

## Key benefits and features
- Simple integration with Alexa, Google Home, and other voice assistants.  
- Faster, multilingual online speech‑to‑text compared with self‑hosted alternatives.  
- Private online backup of user data.  
- Real‑person support for paid subscribers.  
- Recent backend improvements: bare‑metal servers, automatic nearest‑server routing, enhanced security and account tools.  
- New UI elements: easier account page, speech‑to‑text Labs, setup wizard in Home Assistant settings.

## Community focus and future steps
- The service remains community‑driven; feedback is welcomed before the official name change in the December 2026.12 release.  
- Ongoing development will add more features while keeping the subscription privacy‑first, transparent, and non‑locking.  
- Users are encouraged to share opinions on the new name and any suggestions for improvement.
