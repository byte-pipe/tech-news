# Apple in talks to pay publishers to provide Siri with current news: report

## Key Points
- Apple is negotiating with publishers to use their content for the upcoming Siri AI, aiming to give the assistant access to current news and information.  
- The company proposes a variable compensation model that pays publishers only when their content is actually used, deviating from the industry’s typical fixed licensing fees.  
- A nine‑figure budget is being considered for these payments, according to The Wall Street Journal.  
- Apple has not responded to TechCrunch’s request for comment.  
- The talks occur as Apple works to significantly upgrade Siri, a project promised years ago.  
- The enhanced Siri AI is expected to launch later this year.  

## Context
- The report follows Apple’s recent outreach to publishers over the past few months.  
- The variable pay‑as‑you‑go approach contrasts with the standard practice of guaranteed, broad‑access fees.
