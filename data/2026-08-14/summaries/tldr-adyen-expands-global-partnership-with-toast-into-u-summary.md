# Adyen Expands Global Partnership with Toast into U.S.

## Key Announcement
- Adyen announced the expansion of its partnership with Toast into the United States.  
- The move supports Toast, which processed over $215 billion in gross payment volume across roughly 180 000 locations worldwide in the 12 months ending June 30 2026.

## Partnership Background
- The collaboration began in 2021 and already covers Ireland, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.  
- It provides a single, reliable solution that enables scaling across multiple markets.
