# Russian attack kills 17 in Kyiv as Ukraine keeps striking Moscow's oil sector : NPR

**Russian Invasion Kills 17 in Kyiv**

A recent Russian drone and missile attack on Kyiv, Ukraine, has resulted in at least 17 civilian deaths and numerous injuries.

*   The target of the assault was identified as oil refineries, which have caused disruptions to fuel supplies.
*   Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha described the evening's bombardment as a "night of horror" in Kyiv.
*   Emergency services were called upon to search for survivors among the rubble and debris of collapsed buildings.

### Damage Assessment

Reports indicate that widespread damage has been sustained across Ukraine, including residential buildings, civilian infrastructure, and essential services like schools and hospitals.

*   The exact number of casualties is still coming to light as rescue efforts continue.
*   Initial assessments suggest a long-term impact on Kyiv's residents, including the potential for housing shortages and disruptions to essential services.
