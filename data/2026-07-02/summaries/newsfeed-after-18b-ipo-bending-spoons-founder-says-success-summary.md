# Bending Spoons Raises $18B in IPO

AOL's owner, Bending Spoons, has gone public on the Nasdaq with an over $18 billion valuation. The company, founded in 2009 by co-founders Matteo Danieli and Luca Querella, has been quietly acquiring beloved but ailing internet brands to develop them through technology.

**Key Points:**

* Bending Spoons aims to transform acquired companies through tech and holding onto them.
* Co-founder Matteo Danieli emphasizes placing the company as an operator with a focus on beloved brand growth and user value creation.
* Despite controversy around layoffs, revenue growth has exceeded expectations, driven by AI technology.

**The Journey:**

* Bending Spoons' predecessor, Evertale, was a product that leveraged machine learning but ultimately failed due to lack of success factors outside of tech innovation.
* Co-founder Danieli learned from Evertale's failures and developed strategies for reducing luck's role in growth and success.
* This approach is reflected in company policies, such as pricing products for optimal user engagement.

**Operational Excellence:**

* Bending Spoons emphasizes operational excellence and uses data-driven insights to inform product development.
* Key features are released at no additional cost when word-of-mouth drives demand (e.g., releasing more features without pricing increases).
* Customer retention remains "remarkably stable" despite occasional price hikes for certain products.

**AI Integration:**

* Bending Spoons incorporates AI technology into its growth trajectory, driving revenue and improving user experience.
* The company believes a strong focus on operational excellence reduces luck's influence in achieving growth success.
