## Totally new to "Open-Source AI"? Here's what you need to know:

You don't need to be a developer, or to have ever touched open source, to take part in Hacktoberfest. A few quick definitions to get you going:

* Open sourcemeans the code is public. Anyone can look at how a project is made, learn from it, use it, and help improve it.
* Open-weight modelsare AI models whose "weights" (the learned knowledge that makes the model work) are publicly released, so anyone can download, run, study, and adapt them, rather than only accessing them through a company's app. Open-weight is not the same as open source.
* Open-source AI toolsare the building blocks around those models (agents, frameworks, and utilities) that are shared openly for anyone to use and improve.

Why does this matter? When AI is open, more people get to understand it, access it, and shape how it's used. That's what "AI belongs to everyone" is all about, and Hacktoberfest is a month-long invitation to come see for yourself.

## A quick note on what's changed

When DigitalOcean launched Hacktoberfest in 2014, the formula was simple: open four pull requests, earn a t-shirt. That idea helped bring a whole generation of people into open source. 💜

But AI tools now make low-effort pull requests trivial to generate, and open-source maintainers have been paying the price. So this year,pull requests and merge requests no longer count toward Hacktoberfest rewards.Instead, the focus is on learning and building together, and on open source as a philosophy of transparency and collective ownership, not a PR count.

You can read more about the thinking behind this change on theHacktoberfest Mission page.

## 🏙️ Join a Fest near you

This year there are300+ community-hosted, one-day Festshappening in 60+ countries around the world. We can't recommend going in person enough, especially if you're just getting started. There's nothing like learning alongside people who are figuring it out with you. Here's what you get when you show up:

* Hacktoberfest 2026 t-shirts, stickers, and swag, available in person while supplies last.
* An official participation certificateand an "Attend a Fest" virtual sticker for your sticker book.
* Your people.Meet others in your community who are curious about open-source AI.
* Hands-on building with real hardware.Select Hack Days haveArduinoson hand, so you can take an open-source AI project off the screen and into the physical world.

Fests come in two flavors:

* Meetups: talks, workshops, panels, and time to connect with others. No project or team required, which makes them a great first step if you're new.
* Hack Days: mini-hackathons where you build with open-weight models or open-source AI tools, demo at the end of the day, and compete for prizes. Come solo or bring a team.
