# Everything You Need To Know About Hacktoberfest 2026

## Overview
- Hacktoberfest 2026 starts on October 1 and is powered by Major League Hacking (MLH) and DEV, presented by DigitalOcean.  
- Theme: **AI belongs to everyone** – a month‑long invitation to discover, learn about, and build with open‑source AI.  
- Open to anyone, regardless of developer experience.

## Open‑Source AI Basics
- **Open source**: code is public, viewable, reusable, and improvable by anyone.  
- **Open‑weight models**: AI models whose learned weights are publicly released, allowing anyone to download, run, study, and adapt them. (Not the same as open source.)  
- **Open‑source AI tools**: frameworks, agents, and utilities that surround open‑weight models and are shared openly.  
- Goal: when AI is open, more people can understand, access, and shape its use.

## What’s Changed
- Traditional rule “four pull requests → t‑shirt” is replaced; pull requests no longer count toward rewards.  
- Emphasis shifts to learning, building, and the philosophy of transparent, collective ownership.  
- Details are on the Hacktoberfest Mission page.

## Sticker Book & Rewards
- Earn virtual stickers for activities; physical rewards unlock at milestones:  
  - 3 stickers → physical sticker pack mailed to you.  
  - 10 stickers → bonus holographic sticker.  
  - 17 stickers → “Hacktoberfest Completionist” status and automatic entry for a t‑shirt or Arduino Uno Q board.  
- Activities include: attending a Fest, checking into livestreams, joining Global Hack Week, completing DEV Challenges, participating in the MLH Discord, taking surveys, and using DevRelay.  
- Start by signing in with a free MyMLH account and connecting your DEV account.

## In‑Person Fests
- 300+ community‑hosted, one‑day Fests in 60+ countries.  
- Benefits of attending:  
  - On‑site Hacktoberfest 2026 t‑shirts, stickers, and swag (while supplies last).  
  - Official participation certificate and “Attend a Fest” virtual sticker.  
  - Community connection with others curious about open‑source AI.  
  - Hands‑on building with hardware; some Hack Days provide Arduinos.  
- Two formats:  
  - **Meetups** – talks, workshops, panels, and networking; no project required.  
  - **Hack Days** – mini‑hackathons with open‑weight models or AI tools, demos, and prizes.  
- Find a Fest via the provided link.

## Online Events

### Global Hack Week
- Dates: Friday Oct 9 (12 PM EDT) – Thursday Oct 15 (1 PM EDT).  
- Free virtual hacker festival offering:  
  - Daily livestream workshops (recordings on YouTube).  
  - Variety of challenges (social, design, technical) that earn points.  
  - Mini‑events and community hangouts in the MLH Discord.  
- Participation counts toward your sticker book.

### DEV Challenges
- Five cash‑prize challenges; each submission grants a virtual sticker.  
- **Hacktoberfest Weekend Challenge** – launches Oct 1, deadline Oct 5; launch times listed per timezone.  
- **Open‑Source AI Weekly Challenges** – themed weeks:  
  - Week 1 Oct 5‑11  
  - Week 2 Oct 12‑18  
  - Week 3 Oct 19‑25  
  - Week 4 Oct 26‑31  
- Follow the `#hf26challengetag` to see submissions; register early for each challenge.

### DevRelay
- New tool that links an AI coding agent to DEV and MLH.  
- Tracks which challenge or hackathon you’re in, assists building, enables in‑session submission, and provides partner coupons.  
- Install and log in to earn a sticker.
