# Ebola outbreak in DR Congo: Mourners learn how to grieve and safely bury a body

## **Ebola Outbreak in Bunia: Mourners Learn How to Grieve Safely**

In the Democratic Republic of Congo's Ituri province, a rare species of Ebola, Bundibugyo, is contributing to one of the country's deadliest outbreaks. A significant increase in the number of burials due to COVID-19 has resulted in a noticeable scarcity of crowds and rituals.

### **Key Points:**

*   The pandemic has led to an unusual number of funerals
*   Strict protocols are in place to stop Ebola transmission
*   Mourners must now wear suitably dressed bodies for burial, despite traditional practices that require a person's best attire
*   Body bags are used instead of coffins, allowing for immediate burial and transparent panels for viewing

### **Grief: A Sensitive Process**

While traditional funerals continue to take place with some modifications:

*   Women dress their deceased in wedding attire to honor the journey to the afterlife
*   They also incorporate makeup and celebrate before burial
