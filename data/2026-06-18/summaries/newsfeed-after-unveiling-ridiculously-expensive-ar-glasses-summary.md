# After unveiling ridiculously expensive AR glasses, Snap's stock takes a dive | TechCrunch

**After unveiling ridiculously expensive AR glasses, Snap's stock takes a dive**

* **Snap's new smart glasses, Specs, announced last week, have not gained much traction in the market.**
* * The company claims that the glasses will retail for nearly $2,200 per pair, which is extremely high compared to other affordable smart glasses and headsets on the market.*
* * Despite CEO Evan Spiegel's reassurance that specs are "highly wearable but also incredibly capable for immersive computing," investors remain skeptical about the product's profitability.**
* * The stock has dropped by 30% over the past year, with Snap's current price of around $4.83-$5.86 per share being a reflection of this investor concern.*
* * Analysts question whether Snap can justify the high cost to its core user demographic – teenagers who are unlikely to be able to afford such expensive glasses.*
