# Ideas on modernizing the open-source desktop [LWN.net]

**Modernizing Desktop User Interfaces**

* **Key Points:**
    + Scott Jenson, a UX expert working on various companies including Apple and Google, to convince open-source projects to experiment and revamp desktop design.
    + Jenson has been on the UX team at Google, but left to focus on other projects, leading to a shift in his approach.
    + He believes open-source projects need help in UX design, which is challenging due to staffing issues.
    + Jenson gave a talk at the KDE's annual developer conference (Akademy 2026), highlighting his ideas and sharing examples from his work.
* **Important Details:**
    + Jenson has experience working on open-source projects like Chrome, KDE, and Home Assistant.
    + He has noticed a cultural shift in the industry towards open-source projects being open-web focused, but at the cost of UX considerations.
    + Jenson's team has implemented features like ellipses in file names for better readability and suggested alternative designs for file searching and clicking drag-and-drop operations.
* **Innovative Ideas:**
    + Jenson's work emphasizes the importance of "attention-to-detail" in user interfaces, particularly for systems with large datasets.
    + He proposes using a middle-centered ellipsis in file names and reconsidering mouse click actions on multiple windows for a better user experience.
* **Context:**
    + Jenson's experience as a UX expert and his departure from Google to work on different projects have led to a new perspective on UX design in open-source projects.
    + The call for open-source projects to experiment and innovate on desktop design has sparked a conversation about the need for UX-driven solutions.
