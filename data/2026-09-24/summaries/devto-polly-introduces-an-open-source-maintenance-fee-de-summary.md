# Polly Introduces an Open Source Maintenance Fee - DEV Community

**Open Source Maintenance Fee Explained**

Polly, a popular .NET resilience library, is introducing an Open Source Maintenance Fee (OSMF) to fund open-source maintenance. The system works as follows:

* Companies earning at least $20,000 from a product or project using Polly must pay a $20/month fee per organization.
* This fee is used to fund maintenance of the Polly libraries.
* The source code remains open-source and available under its original license.
* Companies can collect the fee through the GitHub Sponsors platform, which allows them to define their own pricing or tiers.
* The OSMF aims to address the open-source sustainability problem by providing a revenue stream for long-term maintenance rather than switching to commercial licenses.

**Key Points**

* Polly is introducing an OSMF to fund open-source maintenance
* The fee is $20/month per organization, with a minimum of $20,000 in revenue
* The OSMF applies to maintained releases in revenue-generating products
* Companies can collect the fee through the GitHub Sponsors platform

**Maintaining Original Perspective**

The OSMF is an interesting approach to addressing the open-source sustainability problem. By charging a small maintenance fee for companies using Polly in revenue-generating products, maintainers can receive funding for long-term maintenance rather than switching to commercial licenses.
