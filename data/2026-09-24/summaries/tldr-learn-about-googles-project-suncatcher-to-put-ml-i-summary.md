# Project Suncatcher: Evaluating the Possibility of AI in Space

## Background and Mission

Project Suncatcher is a moonshot aimed at putting artificial intelligence (AI) hardware in space to test its scalability and performance in harsh space conditions.

## Objectives

1. **Assess AI in Space**: Evaluate how Google Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) handle extreme radiation, intense vibrations, and cooling in vacuums.
2. **Prototype Satellite Launch**: Launch a prototype satellite to demonstrate the feasibility of space-hosted AI infrastructure.
3. **Future Applications**: Explore the potential for linking satellite clusters using high-bandwidth lasers for massive AI workloads.

## Key Findings and Challenges

* **Radiation Resistance**: TPUs perform well against extreme radiation, allowing for safe deployment in space.
* **Vibration and Cooling**: The prototype satellite demonstrates the challenge of managing intense vibrations and ensuring reliable cooling in a vacuum.
* **Scalability**: The mission aims to demonstrate the feasibility of scaling up AI infrastructure for future missions.
* **Inter-connected Satellite Cluster**: Researchers are exploring the potential for inter-connected satellite clusters using high-bandwidth lasers.

## Future Scope and 2027 Leap

The initial launch marks the second step in Project Suncatcher's long-term plan to build powerful AI systems in orbit. As research progresses, the mission will help establish a foundation for future lunar and Mars missions.

## Technical Details and Resources

* **Launch Objectives**: Launch a prototype satellite to test AI hardware in space and evaluate the feasibility of scalable space infrastructure.
* **Resources**: Google AI, TPUs, and a prototype satellite will be used to achieve the mission objectives.
* **Collaboration**: Teams from Google, academia, and industry will contribute to the mission, bringing expertise in AI, aerospace engineering, and more.

## Overview of Project Suncatcher

Project Suncatcher is a pioneering effort to demonstrate the feasibility of AI in space and lay the groundwork for future lunar and Mars missions. By evaluating the performance of AI hardware in space and exploring the potential for inter-connected satellite clusters, the team aims to ensure AI's profound benefits in key areas.
