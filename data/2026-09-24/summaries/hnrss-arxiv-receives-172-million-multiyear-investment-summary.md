# arXiv receives 17.2 million multiyear investment

## arXiv Receives Multiyear Investment

arXiv, a trusted platform for scientists and researchers to share findings, has received multimillion-dollar support for its launch as an independent nonprofit. Three leading philanthropic organizations have made multiyear commitments to support arXiv's growth and development:
### Philanthropic Commitments

* Simons Foundation International: $17.2 million over 3-5 years
* XTX Markets: support for general operation and ongoing improvement of services
* Siegel Family Endowment: investment in governance and organizational operations

### Key Points

* New commitments will support platform development, technical infrastructure, and services for researchers
* Four-year support period will establish arXiv as an independent nonprofit
* Support will address core areas of work: general operation, technical development, and strengthening as an independent nonprofit
* Philanthropic organizations recognize arXiv's existing community-based donors and member organizations

### Impact

arXiv's new commitments will enable the platform to transition into independence, modernizing its systems to better serve the scientific community and secure its future.

### Organization Structure

* Simons Foundation International will review and finalize the new multiyear commitment
* XTX Markets will provide ongoing technical support
* Siegel Family Endowment will contribute to governance and organizational operations
* Contributions will be disbursed as needed, with a focus on ensuring a solid foundation for arXiv's establishment as an independent nonprofit.
