# A phone maker now has the world’s top open-weight AI model

## Credit: Xiaomi

### Overview

Xiaomi has released two open-weight models, Pro and Flash, under a MIT license. These models are built on Frontier intelligence and are now available for download.

### Key Features

* **Sparse mixture-of-experts model**: Pro has 1.02 trillion parameters, making it lightweight and inexpensive to run.
* **Handles multiple modalities**: Text, images, audio, and video input can be processed with one million tokens of context.
* **Live training**: Models are live and can be tested on a variety of tasks in Xiaomi's AI Studio and apps.
* **Available on OpenRouter**: Models can be pulled from Hugging Face's dataset or downloaded directly.

### Benchmarks

* **Intelligence Index**: Pro scored 46 out of 114 models, making it the top model in its class with the highest average score.
* **Cost**: Pro is priced at $0.13 per task, while Flash costs $0.14 and $0.28 per million tokens, making Flash the most cost-effective option.

### Adoption

* **Companies**: Pro will likely be the most adopted model, with Xiaomi noting that it is the cheapest and most efficient option.
* **Training models**: Flash is designed to fill the gap between training Pro and other models, with a focus on scalability and performance.

### Comparison to other models

* **Pro**: Top-performing model in its class, with a high average score of 46.
* **Flash**: Priced the lowest per million tokens, making it an attractive option for cost-sensitive companies.
