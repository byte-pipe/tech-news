## A Small Problem I Keep Noticing

There are many great series on DEV. Some are educational, some document a learning journey, some explore a specific technology, and others are simply fun to follow.

But finding these series consistently can be difficult. Unless we follow the author, discover one of their posts, or happen to see it in our feed, we might never know that the series exists.

And by the time we discover it, several parts may already be published. I know I have probably missed some really interesting series simply because I didn't know they were there.

## So, Let’s Share Them Here

If you're writing a series, feel free to share it in the comments. You can include the series title, a short introduction about what it covers, whether it is ongoing or completed, and a link to the series or its first article.

If you're reading a seriesthat you think others might enjoy, share that too. Tell us what makes it interesting or what you've learned from it.

If you're planning to start a series, you can bookmark this thread and come back once you've published the first part. That way, people can discover it early and follow along as you continue writing.

You can share as many series as you want. There are no limits here 😄

## Until DEV Has a Dedicated Series Section

I remember@dj29raising an issue in the Forem repo about a possible series discovery feature, so fingers crossed that we get something like that in the future 🤞

I know DEV already has so many great features, and I also understand that building something new takes time and effort. This is not meant as a complaint at all. I'm always grateful to the DEV team for creating and maintaining a space where we can learn, write, and connect with each other.

Until then, maybe we can use this thread as our own little discovery space. There are probably many series worth reading that we simply haven't found yet.

What series are you writing or reading right now? Share them below so we can discover something new together.
