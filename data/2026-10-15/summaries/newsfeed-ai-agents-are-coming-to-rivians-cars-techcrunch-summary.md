**Original Text Summary:**

Rivian, a leading electric vehicle manufacturer, has introduced an AI agent that enables drivers and passengers to interact with its cars using voice commands. The AI agent, referred to as "Aggent," can understand natural language and route instructions, allowing users to control various features of their vehicles, such as temperature, seat settings, and external apps like Google Calendar.

Rivian claims to have developed its own in-house agentic framework that enables the Aggent AI agent to operate seamlessly within its car's software and apps. The company aims to integrate Aggent with external AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI, although making it work will still require significant development and coordination.
