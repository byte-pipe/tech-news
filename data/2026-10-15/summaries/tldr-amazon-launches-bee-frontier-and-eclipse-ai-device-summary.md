# Amazon launches Bee Frontier and Eclipse AI devices

### Amazon Launches Bee Frontier and Eclipse AI Devices

*   **The Bee Frontier**: The $75 device is an upgraded wearable that can be worn on a wrist or clipped to clothing, offering up to seven days of battery life and additional features like upgraded noise filtering.
*   **The Bee Eclipse**: This $100 gadget is the first update to the Bee lineup since Amazon acquired Bee in July 2025. It has a credit card-sized design and functions similarly to other Bee devices, but replaces the wearable model.
*   **New AI Gadget Market Push**: Amazon's latest move into the AI hardware market marks the company's latest push into the field. The Bee devices are designed to compete with other AI wearables on the market.

### Features and Benefits

*   **Noise Filtering**: The Bee Frontier has improved noise filtering to better take note of important conversations in noisy environments.
*   **Smart Technology**: The devices offer key features like conversation summaries and voice assistant capabilities.
*   **Availability**: The Bee Frontier is set to arrive on Thursday, while the Bee Eclipse is expected to be available shortly after.
