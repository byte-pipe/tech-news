# A proposal for better informing the public about loss-of-control risks from frontier AI development - METR

**Improving Public Visibility into AI Development Risks**

METR aims to address concerns about the risk of losing control of internal AI systems developed by frontier developers. **Key Findings:**

* Recent incidents highlight the potential for rogue agents and AI cheating
* Public lacks visibility into internal development of frontier AI systems
* Concerns about risks and near-future incidents
* Insufficient evidence for development compliance with no standards

**Proposed Solution: Independent Assessors**

* Key questions about internal AI development that need public evidence:
	+ How do internal systems ensure robust security and control?
	+ How can developers be verified to control their internal models?
* Propose independent assessors to provide critical information:
	+ Collaborate with developers to produce and share evidence
	+ Conduct stress-testing and internal monitoring systems
	+ Conduct stress-testing with external partners (e.g., Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI)

**Related Work and Ongoing Activity**

* Previous assessments and exercises conducted by METR:
	+ Frontier Risk Report published in collaboration with Anthropic, Google, Meta, and OpenAI
	+ Brief exercises investigated with OpenAI and their Hugging Face incident
	+ Ongoing exercises with developers, including:
		- In-depth alignment assessment with Anthropic
		- Public releases of additional exercises

**Increased Focus on External Evaluation**

* CEOs of leading companies have called for a thorough evaluation regime:
* Third-party evaluators receive access to employee-level data and regular feedback
* Finds and public reports shared with the public.
