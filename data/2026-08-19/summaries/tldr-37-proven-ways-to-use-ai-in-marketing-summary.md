# 37 Proven Ways to Use AI in Marketing

## Overview
- Written by Ryan Law, Director of Content Marketing at Ahrefs (August 13 2026).  
- Presents 37 practical AI‑driven tactics organized by marketing discipline, from SEO to international marketing.  
- Emphasizes that AI augments—not replaces—human judgment; expertise remains essential.  

## SEO
- AI shines on repetitive, data‑heavy SEO work such as crawl exports, Search Console downloads, and complex spreadsheet analysis.  
- **Grade pages like a Google quality rater** – An AI agent reads Google’s 168‑page rater handbook, creates a checklist, searches the keyword, evaluates the target page, records a video, and produces a compliance report.  
- **Speedy keyword research** – Input a niche (e.g., “coffee”) and the AI expands seed keywords, pulls real volume, analyzes SERP intent and difficulty, clusters results, and labels each keyword Go/Maybe/Skip, delivering a hub‑and‑spoke map.  
- **Turn site audits into actionable pull requests** – A weekly job pulls the latest Site Audit, ranks issues by severity, and opens a GitHub PR containing a checklist and audit data for high‑severity problems, ready for developers to merge.  
- **Add AI‑generated FAQs to old posts** – Using ChatGPT to create FAQ sections for existing articles lifted traffic by an average of 32 % (≈16 k extra visits) and added thousands of keyword rankings; a control group declined, confirming the effect.  
- **Build an internal‑linking engine with embeddings** – The AI reads every article, creates vector embeddings, and returns the 20 most semantically similar pages for each URL, improving crawlability, authority flow, and anchor relevance.  

## AI Search Optimization
- Leverages AI to interpret search intent, generate structured data, and adapt content for AI‑driven SERPs.  

## Content Marketing
- AI assists in topic ideation, outlines, drafting, and repurposing content, enabling faster production without sacrificing quality.  

## Marketing Analytics
- Automates data aggregation, anomaly detection, and predictive modeling to surface actionable insights for campaigns.  

## Social Media and Community
- AI drafts posts, schedules publishing, monitors sentiment, and suggests real‑time engagement tactics.  

## PR and Outreach
- Generates personalized pitch letters, tracks media mentions, and identifies high‑value outreach targets.  

## Product Marketing
- Crafts product positioning statements, competitive battle cards, and launch plans using AI‑derived market insights.  

## International Marketing
- Provides translation, localization, regional keyword discovery, and culturally adapted messaging at scale.  

## Final Thoughts
- AI is a powerful assistant across every marketing function.  
- The real advantage comes from pairing AI output with human expertise, rigorous testing, and continuous refinement.
