# Join the June Solstice Game Jam: $1,000 in prizes!

## Overview
- Runs from **June 3** to **June 21**, ending on the solstice.
- Celebrates June themes: Pride, Juneteenth, Alan Turing, World Cup, International Sushi Day, National Flip‑Flop Day.
- Open to games that are meaningful, goofy, or anywhere in between.

## Prompt
- Build a game inspired by the solstice or any June celebration (e.g., light vs. darkness, time passage, Pride, Juneteenth, Turing).
- Broad interpretation encouraged: platformer using daylight as a resource, midnight‑reset puzzle, narrative turning point, etc.

## Additional Prize Categories
- **Best Ode to Alan Turing** – incorporate algorithms, code‑breaking, AI, or references to Turing’s legacy.
- **Best Google AI Usage** – showcase Google AI tools (Antigravity, AI Studio, Gemini CLI/API, Cloud Run, etc.) in the game.

## Judging Criteria
- Relevance to theme
- Creativity of concept
- Technical execution and playability
- Quality of submission writing
- (Optional) Effective use of the chosen prize category

## Prizes
- **Three overall winners** + **one winner per additional category** = **five winners total**.
- Each winner receives:
  - $200 USD
  - DEV++ Membership
  - Exclusive DEV Badge
- All valid participants earn a completion badge.

## How to Participate
1. Build a game fitting the prompt.
2. Publish a DEV post using the provided submission template.
3. Include a demo video and an overview of the development process.
4. Review judging criteria, rules, guidelines, and FAQ before submitting.

## Important Dates
- **June 3** – Jam begins
- **June 21, 11:59 PM PDT** – Submission deadline
- **July 9** – Winners announced
