# Broadcom, Samsung team for wireless SoC

## Overview
- Broadcom and Samsung Electronics are collaborating on a broadband‑optimized reference platform for the global Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) market.  
- The platform combines Broadcom’s Wi‑Fi 8 System‑on‑Chip (SoC) with Samsung’s 5G modem.  
- It unifies 3GPP Release 17 5G connectivity with the upcoming Wi‑Fi 8 (IEEE 802.11bn) standard.

## Wi‑Fi 8 Highlights
- Emphasizes ultra‑high reliability, lower latency, and better performance in crowded or interference‑heavy environments.  
- Target improvements over Wi‑Fi 7: about 25 % higher throughput in difficult conditions, 25 % lower latency, and 25 % fewer dropped packets.  
- Development expected to finish by 2028, with first products appearing in 2029.

## New SoCs (four processors)

- BCM68850  
  - 50 Gbps ITU‑PON home‑gateway SoC.  
  - Integrated Neural Processing Unit (NPU) and native Wi‑Fi 8 compatibility.  
  - Designed for intelligent‑edge applications across cable, PON, Wi‑Fi, and set‑top boxes.

- BCM6772  
  - Core for mass‑market Ethernet routers, extenders, and repeaters.  
  - Integrated 2×2 2.4 GHz and 2×2 5 GHz radios.  
  - Supports DDR4 and DDR5 memory; packaged in a 15 × 15 mm FCBGA.

- BCM6774  
  - Optimized for high‑volume Ethernet routers and extenders.  
  - Integrated 2×2 2.4 GHz and 4×4 5 GHz radios.  
  - Same memory flexibility and 15 × 15 mm package as BCM6772.

- BCM6776  
  - Targeted at premium tri‑band routers/extenders (paired with BCM6718).  
  - Integrated 2×2 2.4 GHz and 4×4 5 GHz radios.  
  - Dual PCIe Gen3 controllers; supports DDR4, DDR5, LPDDR4, and LPDDR5.  
  - Housed in a 19 × 19 mm FCBGA.

## Common Features Across the Four SoCs
- High‑performance quad‑core CPU complex.  
- Dedicated Network Processing Engine to offload intensive networking tasks.  
- On‑chip 2.4 GHz power amplifiers and third‑generation digital pre‑distortion (DPD) technology, reducing bill of materials and lowering 5 GHz power consumption.

## Market Impact
- Provides a scalable, cost‑competitive blueprint for mobile operators to deliver fiber‑level broadband via FWA.  
- Aims to accelerate service innovation and ecosystem growth in the mass‑market segment.
