# Amazon’s search bar will invent AI-generated products you can’t buy

## Overview
- Amazon’s updated search bar now generates AI‑created images of products as users describe them.  
- The feature currently works for clothing and home‑goods, letting users tap an image to search for similar real items.

## Intended Use
- Designed to help shoppers who can’t recall the exact name of a texture or style (e.g., “shirt with a draped collar” instead of “cowl neck”).  
- Offers limited benefit for straightforward searches like “blue t‑shirt.”

## Comparison with Competitors
- Similar to Google’s AI Mode launched last year, which also produces fake outfits and décor to guide users to real‑world equivalents.  
- Other online retailers are partnering with Gemini and ChatGPT as AI becomes more embedded in shopping experiences.

## Related Amazon Features
- The “shop by style” feature displays AI‑generated collages that include actual clothing items, allowing direct purchase.  
- Example: a carousel of outfits featuring denim shorts; the shorts shown are real products that can be bought.

## Availability
- The AI‑generated image search is rolling out in Amazon’s Android and iOS apps.
