### Event Overview

#### How marketers are using AI to build smarter, more useful customer journeys

Most AI marketing advice stops at “write better subject lines.” Let’s go further.Join Customer.io for a live session on how marketers are using AI to build smarter, more useful customer journeys.We’ll share real examples from Customer.io customers, plus workflows from our own team, to show what’s possible when AI moves beyond simple copy edits and into segmentation, personalization, automation, and decision-making.No vague trend talk. No “AI will change everything” hand-waving. Just practical use cases, honest lessons, and ideas you can bring back to your own team.
