description: There's one thing that almost everyone overlooks when it comes to positioning... And that's CATEGORY awareness. I'm not talking about the five stages of awareness concept. (I don't think that people move linearly through those, but that's a topic for another day) I am talking about whether people know the category exists, know what it does, know why they'd want it, etc. Those people need to be sold to fundamentally different than to people who do NOT know the category exists. Each of these strategies requires a completely different GTM. They BOTH have their own five stages of awareness starting with "problem unaware" — the people who have the category already may not be aware that that category has an issue at all. There have been plenty of companies who have primarily gone to market for either the category-aware side or the category-unaware side.
