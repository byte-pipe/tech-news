# 3D Artist Andres Rios Brings Horror Icons To Life Through Digital Art

Andres Rios is a digital artist who specializes in horror-themed illustration and creature/monster design, with work also hosted on ArtStation. Pieces like the “Pennywise fanart” for Welcome to Derry and “The Unwanted One” show a clear focus on dark, unsettling character art rooted in horror pop culture.

The style leans into monstrous, eerie figures rendered with strong texture and atmosphere, drawing on both original creature concepts and reinterpretations of well-known horror icons.
