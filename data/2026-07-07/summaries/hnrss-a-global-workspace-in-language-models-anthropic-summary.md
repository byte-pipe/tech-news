# A global workspace in language models \ Anthropic

**A Global Workspace in Language Models**

# Key Findings

- A novel "global workspace" ( JW) concept has been identified in modern language models like Claude.
- The JW is a collection of internal neural patterns linked to particular words.
- Unlike conventional activation-based patterns used in conventional language models, the JW does not directly represent its output but instead provides silent thought processes.
- The JW has several unique properties:
  * Can report on and modulate internal representations.
  * Modulates J-space representations upon request (e.g., thinking about a task).
  * Facilitates reasoning through mediating intermediate steps in complex problems.
* Represents information flexibly for various tasks, allowing for adaptable recall.

**Characteristics of the JW**

- Emerged during Claude's training process
- Unpredictably accessible and controllable
- Does not directly translate to output language representations

**Comparison with Conventional Language Models**

- More passive, whereas traditional models have active representation updates
- Provides internal thought processes, unlike explicit writing mechanisms in models like "scratchpads"
- Mediates reasoning in complex tasks without spoken statements
