With advancements in sequencing technology, individuals can now sequence their own genome at home. This article provides an overview of the process, its benefits and limitations, potential applications, and a protocol for getting started.

## Before You Begin

* Cheek cells are a convenient source for sequencing, as they are easily accessible and replenish quickly.
* Lab materials and consumables can be purchased online to perform genome sequencing at home.
* Costs may still be out of reach for the average person, but technology is decreasing in cost and becoming more affordable.

## What Can You Do with Your Own Genome?

1. **Access your genetic data**: Collecting DNA from cheek cells enables you to analyze your current state, identify variants, and determine which genes or pathways are affected.
2. **Identify potential medicines and risks**: Run VCF tools like VEP, ClinVar, gnomAD, PharmGKB, GeneInspector, and Claude to identify genetic variants that may indicate potential drug interactions or health concerns.
