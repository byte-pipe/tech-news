# Anthropic says Alibaba must be punished for largest Claude cloning attack - Ars Technica

**Antyhropic Accuses Alibaba of Launching Largest Claude Cloning Attack**

Anthropic, the organization representing leading AI model Mythos, has accused Chinese firm Alibaba of launching a massive cyber attack attempting to clone the world's most advanced AI model, Claude. The attacks allegedly occurred between April 22 and June 5 and targeted several critical capabilities of Claude.

**Key Points:**

* Anthropic alleges that Alibaba used obfuscation techniques and proxy networks to evade detection.
* Alibaba attempted to extract Claude's capabilities without incurring training and R&D costs required for their own model.
* The attack is part of a broader trend of illicit distillation attacks against US frontier models.
* Trump previously warned China about adopting similar tactics, which Anthropic cites as supporting its investigation.

**Recent Developments:**

* Alibaba allegedly ignored previous warnings to stop the attacks.
* OpenAI and Google have already published findings on similar attacks affecting their AI models.
* The European Commission has set up a new agency focused on monitoring Chinese activities related to AI research.

**Concerns Regarding Accountability:**

* Anthropic accuses Alibaba of ignoring Trump's warning not to interfere with US national security efforts in AI areas.
* Anthropic wants firms like Alibaba punished for their actions and risks being viewed as a threat to global stability through cyber manipulation.
