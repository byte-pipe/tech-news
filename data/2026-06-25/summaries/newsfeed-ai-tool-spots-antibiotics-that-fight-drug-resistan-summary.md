# AI tool spots antibiotics that fight drug‑resistant gonorrhoea

## Key points
- Researchers applied a machine‑learning (AI) strategy to screen for compounds that can kill *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* strains resistant to several existing antibiotics.  
- The AI identified a molecule that effectively eradicates the resistant bacteria; the compound is visually highlighted in the article (artificially coloured).  
- Two of the discovered antibiotics have chemical structures that do **not** resemble any currently used antimicrobial drugs, indicating novel mechanisms of action.  
- The study demonstrates that AI can accelerate the discovery of new antibiotics with fresh modes of attack, addressing the growing threat of drug‑resistant gonorrhoea.  
- Reference: Anahtar M.N. et al., *Sci. Transl. Med.* 18, eads4699 (2026).  

## Context
- Antibiotic resistance in *N. gonorrhoeae* is a major public‑health concern, limiting treatment options.  
- Traditional drug discovery is time‑consuming; AI‑driven approaches can rapidly explore vast chemical spaces to find effective candidates.  

## Implications
- The identified compounds could become leads for developing new therapies against multidrug‑resistant gonorrhoea.
