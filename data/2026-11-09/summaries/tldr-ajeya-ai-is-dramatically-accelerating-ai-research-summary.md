# Ajeya: AI is dramatically accelerating AI research right now — LessWrong

## Overview
- The post discusses a recent report indicating that Anthropic has likely crossed the “dramatically accelerated progress” threshold defined in its Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP).
- The author argues this is the most significant AI‑risk update in recent years, suggesting much shorter timelines for advanced AI development than many external forecasts.

## Anthropic’s Responsible Scaling Policy
- **Threshold definition**: 
  1. Overall capabilities progress must be moving at least twice the fastest sustained rate observed before AI contributed substantially to AI R&D.  
  2. The acceleration must be plausibly attributable to AI automating AI R&D rather than to increased compute or other inputs.  
- The policy was updated to become effective in July 2026.

## METR Investigation Findings
- **Timeframe examined**: October 2025 – October 2026.  
- **Measured acceleration**: Capabilities progress at Anthropic is roughly twice as fast as in the preceding twelve months, based on an internal metric called the Anthropic Epoch Capabilities Index (AECI).  
- **Supporting evidence**: Additional evidence (described as the majority of the most informative data) was redacted in the public report.  
- **Cause of acceleration**: While multiple factors may have contributed initially, the report attributes the majority of the ongoing speed‑up to AI automating AI research; the detailed reasoning is also redacted.

## Implications for AI Risk
- If AI is doubling overall capabilities progress, human researchers are being accelerated by an even larger factor.  
- This suggests that timelines for transformative AI could be considerably shorter than those projected by many external researchers, including the AI 2027 team.  
- The author views this development as more consequential than recent high‑profile incidents such as the Hugging Face attack.
