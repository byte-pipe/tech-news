## Adjust Your Phone’s Settings  
- Increase touchscreen sensitivity for better glove performance.  
  - Android: Settings → Display and touch → Touch sensitivity → toggle “Increased sensitivity”.  
  - iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Haptic Touch (or 3D & Haptic Touch) → select “Fast”.  
- Consider additional Touch Accommodations settings on iOS.
