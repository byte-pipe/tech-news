Over 115k participants and 500+ global meetups

Hacktoberfest 2026 wrapped up on October 31st! Thank you to everyone who built, hosted, mentored, judged, and tuned into Hacktoberfest this month. 🎉

This year, we focused on building with open-weight models and open source AI, at Fests around the world and online. Our hope was to bring the community together and WOW, so many of you showed up!

## 🎃 Hacktoberfest by the numbers

* 115,385 peopletook part, at in-person Fests and online, from Global Hack Week to DEV Challenges
* 500+ in-person Festsin 70+ countries and territories (our original goal was 300!)
* 50+ livestreamsfrom the community, partners, and Global Hack Week programming
