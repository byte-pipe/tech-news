### Gemini models

* Limits “refresh every five hours until you reach your weekly limit.”

* Google AI Ultranow starts at $100 per month with 5x higher Gemini app usage limits than AI Pro.The previous $250 plan is now $200 with the “exact same capabilities” as before.
* The previous $250 plan is now $200 with the “exact same capabilities” as before.
