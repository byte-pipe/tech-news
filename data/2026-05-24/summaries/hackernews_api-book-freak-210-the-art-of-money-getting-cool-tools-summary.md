## P.T. Barnum's Golden Rules for Making Money

- Barnum compiled 20 plain‑spoken rules from a lifetime of hustling, publishing the book in 1880 after a varied career that included building a famous museum, serving as mayor, experiencing bankruptcy, and co‑founding the circus that became Barnum & Bailey.

## Core Principles

- **Don’t Mistake Your Vocation** – Choose work that matches your natural abilities and strive to be the best at it; success comes from a knack, not from taking any paying job.
- **Avoid Debt Like the Plague** – Debt diminishes freedom and self‑respect; keep income consistently above expenses and eliminate existing obligations.
- **Whatever You Do, Do It With All Your Might** – Half‑hearted effort leads to poverty; full commitment separates the rich from the mediocre.
- **Preserve Your Integrity** – Trust is the true asset; dishonesty may bring short‑term gain but destroys long‑term reputation and sales.

## Try It Now (Action Steps)

- Review your current job: does it align with your natural talents? If not, outline a plan to transition toward a better fit.
- List all debts and devise a concrete payoff strategy, starting with the smallest; avoid taking on new debt this month.
- Identify one task you’ve been only partially completing; this week, execute it fully, leaving no stone unturned.
