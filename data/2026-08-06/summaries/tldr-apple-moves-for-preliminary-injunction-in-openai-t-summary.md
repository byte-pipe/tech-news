# Apple moves for preliminary injunction in OpenAI trade secrets lawsuit

## Key developments
- Apple filed a request for a preliminary injunction in its trade‑secrets lawsuit against OpenAI, asserting that without it the company will suffer irreparable harm.  
- Apple’s filing outlines five remedial actions it wants OpenAI to take:
  1. Stop accessing or using Apple’s confidential information.  
  2. Halt any ongoing use or disclosure.  
  3. Preserve all evidence.  
  4. Allow forensic inspections of devices, cloud storage, email, Slack, Teams, and other repositories.  
  5. Search OpenAI’s systems for any Apple confidential information.  
- OpenAI agreed to the first three items but did not agree to the forensic inspections and system‑search requests.  
- Apple therefore asks the court to:
  * Immediately prohibit OpenAI from using or obtaining any Apple trade secrets.  
  * Preserve all relevant evidence.  
  * Permit forensic inspections of OpenAI’s devices and accounts.  
  * Recover any Apple confidential information still in OpenAI’s possession.  

## Additional motions and timeline
- Apple filed a separate motion to expedite discovery, requesting early document production and depositions of key OpenAI employees and executives.  
- The hearing on the preliminary injunction is set for October 1, 2026.  

## OpenAI’s response
- OpenAI published its longest public reply to date, characterizing the lawsuit as “careless, aggressive and oddly personal” and stating it does not live up to Apple’s reputation as “one of the greatest companies of all time.”
