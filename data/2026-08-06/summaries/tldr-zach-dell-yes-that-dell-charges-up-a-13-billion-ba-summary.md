# Zach Dell (Yes, That Dell) Charges Up a $13 Billion Backyard Battery Startup

## Key Highlights
- Base Power raised **$1 billion** in a new funding round, pushing its valuation to **$13 billion**.  
- Investors include **Ribbit**, **Addition**, and **JPMorgan Chase’s Strategic Investment Group**; Michael Dell is not a backer.  
- The company has installed **more than 23,000 batteries** across Texas and Illinois and unveiled a battery capable of storing **over a day’s worth of energy**.  

## Business and Funding
- Fresh capital of $1 billion will support expansion and product development.  
- Total valuation now stands at $13 billion after three years of operation.  
- Funding sources are venture‑style investors rather than family ties to Michael Dell.  

## Operations and Technology
- Batteries are **rented** to electricity utilities, not just used as emergency backup.  
- They store excess energy during low‑demand periods and release it automatically when demand spikes (e.g., simultaneous A/C use).  
- Strategic deployment in:
  - **Texas** – a standalone grid still recovering from the 2021 winter freeze.  
  - **Illinois** – home to power‑intensive data centers.  
- El Paso Electric is covering installation costs and adding cash incentives for customers who adopt Base batteries.  

## Market Context
- Average U.S. household electricity bill for the summer is projected at **$800**, an 11 % rise from the previous year.  
- Rising demand from AI data centers strains grid capacity, creating a utilization gap where grids are over‑built for peak days but under‑used most of the time.  
- Home‑battery installations hit a record **gigawatt‑hour** volume in Q1, reflecting growing consumer interest in backup power.  

## Growth Plans
- Base currently completes about **100 installations per day**.  
- Target is to **double daily installations to 200** by the end of the year.  
- Expansion aims to further alleviate grid stress, lower repair costs for utilities, and make electricity more affordable and reliable for consumers.
