description: 'UTMs cause more arguments per quarter than any other tracking element. Always the same arguments. Almost always avoidable. The patterns are universal. Someone tagged "Facebook" as the source. Someone else tagged "facebook." Now there are two sources. Someone used "email" as the medium for both newsletters and transactional emails. Someone capitalised "Google" because they forgot the convention. The campaign report has 47 entries that should be 12. ⚠️ The cost shows up at the report stage, not the tagging stage. By the time the duplicates and inconsistencies appear in GA4, the original campaigns have run, the data is in, and the cleanup is retrospective. Looker Studio gets weighed down with regex rules to merge what shouldn''t have split. The fix is a UTM strategy document. Source list. Medium list. Campaign naming convention. Enforced consistently across the team. Reviewed quarterly. 📋 It''s the boring deliverable that pays back faster than almost any other tracking work. Implement
  it once, enforce it, and the quarterly UTM argument disappears. If your team has the same " is this Facebook or facebook" conversation every QBR, you don''t need more attribution - you need 60 minutes on UTM hygiene."
