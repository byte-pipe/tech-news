# Startup Ambrook, QuickBooks Killer For Farms, Raises $30M Series B

## Startup Ambrook Raises $30M Series B to Expand Across 'Real Economy'

### **Key Points**

*   Startup Ambrook, co-founded by Mackenzie Burnett, uses financial software to streamline bookkeeping and expenses for small businesses across various industries.
*   The company now handles more than 8,000 customers, including cattle feeders in Arizona, trucking operations, construction companies, and real estate ventures.
*   Burnett's vision is to help all businesses, not just agriculture, by providing comprehensive accounting solutions.
*   After launching QuickBooks for agriculture but replacing it with Ambrook, the platform has become a primary choice for multi-million dollar businesses.
