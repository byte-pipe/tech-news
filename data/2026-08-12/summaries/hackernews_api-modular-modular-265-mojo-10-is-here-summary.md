# Modular 26.5: Mojo 1.0 Released!

## **Overview**

Modular Team
----------------

### Major Milestone Reached with Mojo 1.0 Release

The team is pleased to announce that the Mojo language has officially reached a major milestone of version 1.0, building on its foundation established since its first release in 2023. This achievement solidifies Mojo's position as a stable and production-ready general-purpose language.

## **Background**

Modular has rapidly adopted and expanded Mojo through extensive internal use, but this pace of progress came at the cost of long-term support. To address this concern, the team made a crucial commitment to providing an enduring foundation for developers relying on Mojo. Today's release marks the final step towards achieving stability and productivity.

### Community Contributions

The path to this milestone was not dissimilar from what has been seen in earlier versions. A vibrant community took on a massive project consisting of nearly 200 contributors who contributed over 1,100 pull requests, with roughly 1,000 issue filings that shaped the language.

## **Key Improvements**

Much of the new release focuses on completing several critical enhancements and clarifications. These include:
* Consistent variable declaration to improve readability
* Unified closure syntax for easier development
* Simplified `Pointer` type for better performance
* Numerous code renames aimed at improving cohesion

This recent release brings Mojo up to date with industry standards, providing developers with a coherent foundation to build upon for years to come.

## **Additional Features and Improvements**

Further enhancements include:
* Enhanced support for Python-style "lambda" syntax in inline closures
* Improved structure of the Mojo Language Reference System (LSP)

By achieving these milestones, the team aims to provide a stable development environment that developers can rely on moving forward. This milestone is truly significant as a testament to the resilience and collaborative spirit of the Modular community.
