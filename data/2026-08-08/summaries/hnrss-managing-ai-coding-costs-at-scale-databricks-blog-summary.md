# Managing AI Coding Costs at Scale | Databricks Blog

**Managing AI Coding Costs at Scale**

At Databricks, we've seen two main challenges when deploying artificial intelligence (AI) tools at scale:

*   **Exponentially growing costs**: Companies have hit the limit where increasing their investment in AI tools leads to a sharp decline in revenue.
*   **The paradoxical situation**: Employees want better access and affordability of powerful AI tools, but maintaining cost profiles is essential for sustained efficiency gains.

**Key Findings**

 Studies by early adopters have identified some approaches that can help bridge this gap:

1.  **Providing broad access to AI tooling with minimal friction**: Ensure users feel comfortable exploring and using the tool without unnecessary barriers.
2.  **Keeping aggregate costs inside a roughly fixed envelope per user**: Adjust pricing models or utilize existing infrastructure components, like Open Source Meta-Harness (Omnigent) and AI Gateway (Unity AI Gateway), to optimize spending.

**The Efficiency Frontier"

The cost of AI tools can be viewed as two fronts: the **intelligence frontier**, which focuses on developing more efficient and accurate models, as well as the **efficiency frontier**, where the relative improvement in price-to-price tradeoff is becoming increasingly favorable. Current developments are driving these points towards convergence with unprecedented speed.

**Summary**

While initially, companies focused on high-end AI tools led to exponential growth costs that ultimately led to a mismatch between desired efficiency gains and feasibility through revenue. As our analysis showcases techniques that help bridge this gap in cost management at scale:

*   **Balancing access to tools**: Broadly expanding access without frustrating users.
*   **Reframing pricing perceptions**: Considering a more optimal model pricing strategy to keep costs under control.

**The Future of Code Management"

Our open-source solutions and partnerships with other digital-native companies have helped bridge this issue. As AI continues to evolve, focusing on creating cost-sensitive models while prioritizing efficiency will become increasingly important for enterprise leaders seeking success in driving business growth through technology adoption."
