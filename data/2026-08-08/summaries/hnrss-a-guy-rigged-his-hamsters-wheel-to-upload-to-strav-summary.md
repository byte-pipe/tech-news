# A Guy Rigged His Hamster’s Wheel to Upload to Strava. It Runs Surprisingly Far Every Night

**Hamster Tracks Distance and Speed with Automatic Uploads**

Thijs de Buck, a physics enthusiast from Utrecht, Netherlands, built a speed and distance tracker for his hamster wheels. The device automatically uploads data to Mollie, the 10-month-old hamster's Strava account.

**Initial Setup:**

* A magnet on the wheel detects rotations and sends data to an ESP32 computer overnight.
* A script on laptop collects data, turns it into a compatible .FIT file, and uploads through the Strava API.
* Mollie is displayed with a live speed OLED display, automated personal-best tracking, and numerous possible run titles.

**Challenges:**

* The auto-uploading feature requires an additional paid Strava Premium account.
* Hamster's performance data can attract attention from pet owners and other users.

**Outcome:**

* Thousands of likes, over 1,200 kudos, and notable recognition in the Running subreddit.
* Mollie's nighttime exercise routine remains private to prevent hamsters from being mocked or judged.
