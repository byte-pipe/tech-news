# AWS targets AI cost concerns with new Marketplace Insights tool | Network World

**AWS Offers AI Insights Tool to Simplify Pricing Evaluations for CIOs and Developers**

The AWS Marketplace has announced a new feature called AI Insights, aimed at helping customers better evaluate prices for AI-based products. This addition is designed to reduce complexity in pricing evaluations by providing clear explanations of each product's pricing.

**Key Features:**

* Simplifies understanding of pricing units
* Provides detailed explanations on how usage scales with price changes
* Offers multi-component pricing calculations and transparency
* Helps prevent "bill shock" when projects scale up

**Appreciation from Experts:**

* Analysts like Amit Jena, AI development manager at Kanerika, emphasize the value in being able to understand pricing in plain language for large-scale deployments.
* Principal analyst Pareekh Jain suggests that the feature is critical for securing CIO approval of AI-based investments.

**Implications for Procurement Process:**

* Streamlines procurement cycles through pre-purchase clarity
* Reduces risk of post-implementation bill shock
* Can help to ensure transparency and compliance with accounting regulations

**Future Benefits:**

* Allows for more efficient forecasting and budgeting processes
* Can improve decision-making among CIOs, developers, and CFOs
