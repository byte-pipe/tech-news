# After Rippling blew millions on AI in months, it built an employee ROI tool | TechCrunch

**Ripple's AI Spending Console: A Tool to Contain Excessive AI Costs**

After launching its anti-tokenmaxxing solution, Rippling unveiled an innovative product – AI Spend Console – which tracks and controls individual employees' AI spending. The tool provides insights to help organizations identify and optimize their AI usage.

**Key Features:**

* Maps employee AI spend patterns
* Identifies excessive AI expense, with particular attention to high-spending individuals
* Helps companies contain unnecessary AI costs

The company's AI Spend Console tool is rooted in its earlier tokenmaxxing approach but has evolved into a more sophisticated solution. By understanding which engineers have excessive spending habits and how peer feedback impacts productivity, Rippling aims to empower organizations to make informed decisions about their AI adoption.

**Challenges Identified:**

* Employees default to expensive models despite company rules
* Employees lack incentives for controlling costs; instead, they drive up spend

Rippling's founder and CEO Parker Conrad acknowledges that implementing the console took eight months but is pleased with its progress. With insights from internal benchmarks, companies now recognize the need for multiple AI models at various price points.

**Lessons Learned:**

* Multiple models are necessary to effectively manage AI usage
* Balancing innovation with cost optimization is critical
