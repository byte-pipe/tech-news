# Nixpkgs Core Team Disbands

## Announcement Details
August 7, 2023 (9:39pm)

1. The Nixpkgs core team is disbanding due to unsustainable circumstances.
2. Dissolution was necessary for individual's health.

### Summary of Key Points

* The Nixpkgs core team has been disbanded by its Steering Committee members
* Reasons:
  * Lack of native instinct and engagement with delegation in the Steering Committee institution
  * Inadequate clarity, micromanagement, communication issues with SC members
  * Insufficient response to concerns from relevant teams

### Additional Information

* This was a conclusion reached after repeated attempts to discuss and resolve these issues
* The team recognized that every member has limited time and energy, causing them to drop out (referred to as "attrition")
* Notable accomplishments included reforming the committer delegation process, onboarding 19 new members, empowering maintainers with the extend merge bot
* These efforts were hindered by GitHub integration issues and security concerns
