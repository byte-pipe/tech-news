# 4chan’s Misogynist ‘Wizards’ Are Nudifying Women by Request | WIRED

**4chan’s Misogynist “Wizards” Create Synthetic Depictions of Women by Request**

An anonymous 4chan user posted on the site's /r/ board, soliciting requests for synthetic images of women subjected to various forms of non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII). The image posted included a blonde woman in glasses, an open black jacket, white tank top, and ripped jeans with a sweeping view of an old-world city from behind her.

The request resulted in a second altered image, also depicting the same woman without clothing. A user responded positively to this change, praising the photographer.

A new research paper reveals that 4chan threads like these serve as an origin point for the dissemination of NCII across private platforms, such as Telegram and Discord. The paper also finds that theseNCII requests typically follow a familiar script, emphasizing humiliation, dehumanization, and explicit sex acts.
