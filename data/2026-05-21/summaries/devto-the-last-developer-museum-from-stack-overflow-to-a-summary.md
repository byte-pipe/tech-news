# Satirical Tour of Coding History: From Ancient Forum to AI Era

## Exhibit 01 - The Ancient Code (1998-2007)

Welcome to the Museum of Software Development. Please keep your hands away from some of our fragile and unstable exhibits.

We have here one of the earliest programmers, who spent their days typing away on outdated forums like phpBB and Usenet newsgroups. This was the golden age before Stack Overflow and Frameworks became dominant technologies. The environment was chaotic, with 37 open browser windows and animated GIFs galore.

* The ancient programmer's desktop wallpaper featured a dramatic image.
* Unexplained graphics were used to emphasize messages.
* Junior developers would often find their footing by reading through thousands of archived threads.

Some notable phrases the ancient programmer might have exclaimed include:

* "Please search before posting."
* "Duplicate thread. Locked."

These early developers relied on community spirit and sheer determination to survive. Some young programmers would have simply read through these threads for hours, learning valuable lessons along the way.

## Exhibit 02 - The Birth of a Collective Community (2008-2023)

Get ready to experience one of humanity's greatest achievements: the development of Stack Overflow as a platform for collective knowledge sharing.

Please approach carefully. Once you reach the exhibit, be prepared for a 5-minute existential crisis.

Around 2008, developers transformed programming help from fragmented discussions into a sprawling collective suffering project called Stack Overflow. The sacred four-step ritual was:

1. Google the error message and surrounding text.
2. Click on recently visited links...and then again...
3. Find the accepted solution (posted in 2012).
4. Whisper quietly: "Please still work."

Researchers were amazed by the experience. They recovered:

* 14 browser tabs stuck in an infinite loop of frustration.
* Three abandoned side projects with no explanation.
* A single, mysterious coffee mug.
* An unanswered existential question that had puzzled developers for years.

But wait, there's more! Stack Overflow went on to help over 50 billion users...with minimal bugs or errors. It was truly a miracle.

## Exhibit 03 - The Emergence of Frameworks (2013-2018)

And now, we come to the stage where developer toolkits became increasingly complex and efficient. Meet the modern era!

Some of our exhibits include frameworks like Spring Boot, Laravel, and Django, which allowed developers to build their own applications faster than ever before.

However, the real magic happened in 2020 when...a certain AI system came along and changed everything Forever. We're not sure what this is or why it is so important, but we do know that it seems to have disrupted our ancient coding rituals forevermore.

These exhibits remain unchanged since they were constructed over a century ago; the technology has evolved dramatically ever since.

Please feel free to roam around and explore our digital museum. But remember, your hands are not allowed within these hallowed halls...at all costs.
