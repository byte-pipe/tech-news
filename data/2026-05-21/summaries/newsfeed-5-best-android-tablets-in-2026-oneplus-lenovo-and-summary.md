# 5 Best Android Tablets in 2026: OnePlus, Lenovo, and Pixel Compared | WIRED

**The Best Android Tablets**

* **OnePlus Pad 3**: Features a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, a large 13.2-inch LCD screen with excellent 3.4K resolution, and decent battery life. It's affordable and easy on the eyes in bright sunlight.
* **Samsung Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra**: A luxury Android tablet with great performance, powerful specs, and an OLED display. However, it comes at a premium price point.
* **Lenovo Idea Tab Pro**: A budget-friendly option for beginners, offering decent battery life and a comfortable design. While not the best overall selection, it's still a good value.

**Comparison to iPads**

For those considering iPads as alternatives, here's how the OnePlus Pad 3 compares in key areas:

* **Screen resolution:** The iPad is currently set at 10-inch while the OnePlus Pad 3 offers an impressive 13.2-inch screen.
* **Battery life:** Both tablets are designed to be power-efficient, with the OnePlus Pad 3 offering up to a week-long standby mode and a recharge time of about 1.5 hours for full charge.
* **Software compatibility:** iOS is exclusive to iPads, while Android on non-Apple devices like the OnePlus Pad 3 offers Google's popular operating system.

Overall, the OnePlus Pad 3 appears to be an excellent option for those seeking a great all-around Android tablet experience without breaking the bank.
