# Apple unveils 30+ Apple Design Award app finalists

 

Ryan Christoffel
 | May 18 2026 - 10:38 am PT																

Applejust sentpress invites for the WWDC 2026 keynote, and the company has alsounveiledthe 30+ apps that are Apple Design Award finalists heading into this year’s conference. Here’s the full list.

## Apple Design Awards finalists for 2026 revealed

The Apple Design Awards recognize the best of the App Store each year. Winners will be announced in the coming weeks leading up to WWDC. But ahead of the conference, Apple has unveiled its finalists for this year.

There are 36 total finalists competing in six categories, with three apps and three games competing in each category. However, certain apps are finalists across multiple categories, so the total number of apps is a little shy of 36.

Here’s the full list,published here:

Delight and Fun: “Finalists in this category provide memorable, engaging, and satisfying experiences enhanced by Apple technologies.”

* Blippo+
* Metaballs
* grug
* PowerWash Simulator
* Is This Seat Taken?
* Ball x Pit

Inclusivity: “Finalists in this category provide a great experience for all by reflecting a variety of backgrounds, abilities, and languages.”

* Guitar Wiz
* Hearing Buddy
* Structured
* Sago Mini Jinja’s Garden
* Pine Hearts
* Civilization VII

Innovation: “Finalists in this category provide a state‑of‑the‑art experience through a novel use of Apple technologies that sets them apart in their genre.”

* Detail: AI Video Editor
* NBA: Live Games & Scores
* D-Day: The Camera Soldier
* TR-49
* Blue Prince
* Pickle Pro

Interaction: “Finalists in this category deliver intuitive interfaces and effortless controls that are perfectly tailored to their platform.”

* The Outsiders: Athlete Tracker
* Moonlitt: Moon Phase Tracker
* Tide Guide: Charts & Tables
* TR-49
* Sago Mini Jinja’s Garden
* Grand Mountain Adventure 2

Social Impact: “Finalists in this category improve lives in a meaningful way and shine a light on crucial issues.”

* Primary: News in Depth
* Katha Room
* Harvee
* Consume Me
* Despelote
* Spilled!

Visuals and Graphics: “Finalists in this category feature stunning imagery, skillfully drawn interfaces, and high-quality animations with a distinctive and cohesive theme.”

* Tide Guide: Charts & Tables
* Caradise
* (Not Boring) Camera
* Cyberpunk 2077 Ultimate Edition
* Arknights: Endfield
* SILT
