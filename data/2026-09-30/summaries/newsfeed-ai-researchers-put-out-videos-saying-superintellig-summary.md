# AI researchers put out videos saying superintelligence is ‘exactly as dangerous as it sounds’

## Summary
- A collection of video interviews with current and former AI researchers from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic and others was released on inside.ai, a project of the nonprofit Palisade Research.  
- The participants repeatedly warn that the development of super‑intelligent AI could pose existential risks, including the possibility of human extinction.  
- The videos do not present a unified mitigation plan; instead they highlight personal concerns, the difficulty of defining “AI safety,” and the tension between working on risky technology and wanting to reduce those risks.

## Key Statements
- **Geoffrey Irving (ex‑OpenAI, ex‑DeepMind)** – “The chance of human extinction is about a coin flip, in my view.”  
- **Neel Nanda (Google DeepMind research scientist)** – “There’s at least a 10 percent chance that it causes human extinction, and that is ridiculously high.”  
- **Daniel Kokotajlo (former OpenAI researcher)** – “Superintelligent AI would basically be god‑like powerful… we don’t know how to control them at all. This is exactly as dangerous as it sounds and must not be allowed to happen.”  
- **Mary Phuong (Google)** – “I think you absolutely should be suspicious of what I’m saying because I am being paid by the lab.”  
- **Neel Nanda (additional comment)** – “If I did not believe that my work was directly reducing these existential risks, I would quit… These companies are not going to stop making these systems just because I quit.”

## Context & Platform
- **inside.ai** aggregates the full interviews and extracts short segments on topics such as hype vs. genuine risk, motivations for continuing work, and the lack of clear control mechanisms.  
- Palisade Research describes itself as a nonprofit studying AI capabilities and motivations, aiming to make insider perspectives publicly accessible.

## Broader Discussion
- The videos echo recent “doomer” warnings and add to ongoing debates about AI safety, regulation, and the definition of existential risk.
