This is it: 3 days to get your startup in front of 10,000+ founders, investors, operators, and tech leaders atTechCrunch Disrupt 2026. Exhibit bookings closeFriday, October 2 at 11:59 p.m. PT, and this is your final opportunity to showcase at the heart of the global startup ecosystem.

Once the deadline passes, the Expo Hall moves on without you. Your competitors, potential investors, customers, and members of the press will be in San Francisco, discovering new companies, making connections, and deciding which startups are worth their attention. If you miss this window, you’ll be waiting until next year for another opportunity to put your product on this floor.

This is your final chanceto put your startup in front of 10,000+ founders, investors, operators, and tech leaders at Disrupt, taking place October 13-15 at Moscone West in San Francisco. They’re coming to find startups to back, products to use, companies to partner with, and technologies worth paying attention to.

Make sure your startup is one of the companies they see.

## Get your product in front of the right people

TheExpo Hallis where Disrupt’s highest traffic happens, giving your team three days to turn visibility into real conversations. Your exhibit table puts you directly in the path of customers, investors, partners, and other decision-makers who are there to discover what’s next.

Use your three days to:

* Demo your productdirectly to potential customers, investors, and partners.

* Meet VCsactively sourcing their next opportunities.

* Generate leadsthrough the Disrupt mobile app.

* Build customer and partnership relationshipsface-to-face.

* Put your brand in front of thousands of peopleexploring the Expo Hall.

Your $12,500 exhibit packageincludes a 6′ × 30″ table for all three days, 10 team passes, lead-generation tools, website and app branding, press-list access, Silver Tier sponsor branding, and more. Founders can also access opportunities, including Deal Flow Café and Investor-to-Founder Networking.

## Three days to make an impression—three days to create opportunities

The startups that show up get the chance to start conversations. The ones that get noticed can turn those conversations into leads, partnerships, customers, and potential investment.

Don’t wait until October to wish your startup had a place on the floor. Your competitors, customers, and potential investors will already be there.

You have 3 days. Get your startup in the room by October 2 at 11:59 p.m. P.T.
