Claude Opus 5.5 is available today acrossKiro IDE,CLI,Crew, andWeb. It isAnthropic's most capable Opus model yet, delivering Fable 5.1-level performance on most work at 40% lower cost than Opus 5.

## State-of-the-art on coding and knowledge work

For agentic coding, Opus 5.5 finds the root cause before it changes anything and checks its work as it goes, so its changes are easier to review and trust. Opus 5.5 also communicates more naturally than prior models and puts the most important information up front, which makes its writing clearer and easier to follow. In our internal benchmarking, Opus 5.5 outperformed Opus 5 while making ~40% fewer tool calls and using half the tokens.

"Every call an agent makes is time and cost a developer feels. On a public benchmark of real command-line tasks, Opus 5.5 solved more than Opus 5 while making about 40% fewer calls and using half the tokens. For developers building with Kiro, that means faster, more affordable agent sessions for routine tasks and complex challenges alike."

— Deepak Singh, VP of Agentic AI at AWS

## A note on safeguards

Opus 5.5 is comparable to Claude Mythos 5.1 in biology and cybersecurity, and has safeguards similar to those on Claude Fable 5.1. Everyday coding, health, and educational questions work as usual, and some higher-risk requests are blocked. If you hit a false positive on a benign task, switch to another model in Kiro.

## Availability

Claude Opus 5.5 is gradually rolling outwith experimental supportto Kiro Pro, Pro+, Pro Max, and Power customers in us-east-1 (IAD) and eu-central-1(FRA). It ships with afull 1M context window and a 2x credit multiplier.

Download Kiroor restart the Kiro IDE, CLI, or Crew to check for the latest available models.Web userscan refresh their browser to see Claude Opus 5.5 in the model selector.
