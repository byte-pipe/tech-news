* Blockspace@Blockspace_ETH# Blockspace: Connecting the World to EthereumWe are announcing Blockspace, an Ethereum Infrastructure Company.Ethereum is in the business of selling blockspace, and it produces the highest grade blockspace in the world.Today, more than 90% of that blockspace flows through out-of-protocol infrastructure that sits alongside the protocol. Gaps have emerged across the economics, robustness, performance, and services of this infrastructure, directly impacting Ethereum and its users. Because of this, last year teams came together to create an open-source effort, the Blockspace Forum.For an open-source effort like the Blockspace Forum to succeed, we believe it should be paired with commercial teams that have a direct economic tie to Ethereum's success and a mandate to ship. And with today's gaps combined with near-term changes such as ePBS, now is the perfect time to launch Blockspace. Blockspace is a for-profit company whose mission is to connect the world to Ethereum by helping build better out-of-protocol infrastructure.Our recent funding round was led by BlueYard with participation from ether.fi Ventures, Quasar Builder, Sharplink, Breed VC, Luganodes, Stakely, and other teams and individuals across Ethereum. Gattaca and ultra sound laid the foundations for Blockspace. Now, a full-time team that has spent years building open-source software for Ethereum carries that work forward.What Is BlockspaceAn Ethereum Infrastructure Company. First and foremost, we are an Ethereum Infrastructure Company. We research, spec, build, ship, and coordinate to help deliver Ethereum's core product, blockspace.How We Make Money. Our monetization is entirely in ETH and relies on Ethereum. We run and support Ethereum infrastructure, and part of that infrastructure is staking. We earn ETH from the transactions in the blocks our staked ETH produces. We then stake that earned ETH and repeat.Ethereum Wins, We Win. As our efforts contribute to the growth in Ethereum's blockspace usage, demand, and economic relevance, there is a flywheel effect on Blockspace. More demand for Ethereum blockspace leads to more transactions, which leads to more fees. Flywheels also come with risks. A single entity with substantial stake becomes a centralization problem on its own. To address this, we are self-capping our ETH holdings at 15% of total stake, a level the community has suggested as responsible.Commercially Focused. Ethereum's growth has always been dependent on mission-driven contributors, public goods, and open-source coordination. It also needs commercially thriving companies that can build durable businesses. This is Blockspace. We are structured as a for-profit whose success is directly tied to increased demand for Ethereum blockspace.Blockspace and the Blockspace ForumThe Blockspace Forum remains an independent, not-for-profit open-source coordination effort for teams working across Ethereum's transaction pipeline. Any team can participate, and so far it has gathered a significant part of that community. Blockspace is a for-profit company that will contribute a full-time team, resources, and commercial operating capacity to help advance that work alongside the Forum's other contributors.What's NextYou'll find us hard at work accelerating Ethereum's out-of-protocol infrastructure alongside the Blockspace Forum and the broader community, all the while stacking ETH.If your team works anywhere in the transaction pipeline, join the conversation at the Blockspace Forum.
