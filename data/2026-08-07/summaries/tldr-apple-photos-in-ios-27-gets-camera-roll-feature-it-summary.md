# Apple Photos in iOS 27 gets long‑awaited “Captured by Me” camera‑roll feature  

## Overview  
- iOS 27 brings a major update to the Photos app, including better slideshows, shared albums, and other enhancements.  
- The headline addition is a dedicated camera‑roll‑style collection called **“Captured by Me.”**  

## “Captured by Me” details  
- Shows only photos and videos taken with the iPhone’s Camera app.  
- Excludes screenshots, images received from others, shared album content, and other non‑camera media.  
- Available as a standalone collection in the Photos library.  
- Can also be applied as a filter within the main Photos view to isolate captured media.  

## Significance  
- Fills the gap left by the old “Camera Roll” (now “Recents”) which mixed captured and saved media.  
- Provides users a quick way to locate their own recordings without sifting through other content.
