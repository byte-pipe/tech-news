# Anthropic officially files for its public debut | TechCrunch

## Key Highlights
- Anthropic filed for an IPO on Monday, planning to list under the ticker “ANT.”
- The filing follows weeks of leaked S‑1 documents and is expected to be a highly watched U.S. debut.

## Financials
- Revenue for the first half of 2026: $16.3 billion (up from $1.2 billion in the same period of 2025).
- Operating loss for H1 2026: $693 million (down from $2.1 billion in H1 2025).
- Net loss reported: $80.7 billion, driven mainly by non‑cash accounting adjustments tied to convertible debt.
- The company cautions that it “may not achieve or sustain profitability in the future.”

## Customer Base
- Over 11,900 customers spend $100,000 + annually.
- About 1,900 customers spend $1 million + annually.
- Roughly 250 customers spend $10 million + annually (as of end‑September).

## Governance Structure
- The seven co‑founders (Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, Tom Brown, Jack Clark, Jared Kaplan, Sam McCandlish, Christopher Olah) form a “Founder LLC” that will control 50.1 % of voting power after the IPO.
- A Long‑Term Benefit Trust, created in 2023, will have authority to appoint most board members to safeguard the company’s public‑benefit mission.
- This structure is controversial because voting power is not proportional to economic ownership, limiting public shareholders’ influence over technology that the company acknowledges carries “catastrophic or existential risks to humanity.”

## IPO Details
- The company has not disclosed the amount it intends to raise or the proposed share price.
