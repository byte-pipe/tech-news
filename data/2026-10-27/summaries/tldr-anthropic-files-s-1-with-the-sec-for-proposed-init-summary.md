# Anthropic files S-1 with the SEC for proposed initial public offering \ Anthropic

# Summary of Anthropic’s Form S‑1 Filing

## Key Details
- Anthropic, PBC submitted a Form S‑1 registration statement to the SEC for a proposed IPO of its Class A common stock.  
- The company plans to list the shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker **ANT**.  
- The total number of shares to be offered and the price range have not been set.

## Prospectus Availability
- The offering will be conducted solely through a prospectus.  
- Preliminary prospectus copies (when ready) can be obtained from:  
  - **Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC** – Prospectus Department, 200 West Street, New York, NY 10282; phone (866) 471‑2526; email prospectus‑ny@ny.email.gs.com  
  - **Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC** – Prospectus Department, 180 Varick Street, 2nd Floor, New York, NY 10014  

## Regulatory Status
- The registration statement has been filed but is not yet effective.  
- No securities may be sold or offers to buy accepted until the statement becomes effective.  
- This announcement does not constitute an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy, and no sales will occur in jurisdictions where such actions would be unlawful prior to registration or qualification.
