## This week's theme: Ghost in the Machine

Build something spooky, or something that honors the past in the spirit of Día de los Muertos, with open-weight models or open-source AI.

This week brings usHalloween on October 31andDía de los Muertos on November 1 and 2, a stretch of the calendar when the line between past and present feels a little thinner. Whether that brings to mind things that go bump in the night, ofrendas glowing with candlelight and marigolds, or the memories and stories we carry with us, we want to see what it inspires you to build. The theme is intentionally broad, so interpret it however you'd like!

That can mean running an open-weight model, building on an open-source agent harness or framework, running inference locally, or all three. Whatever you pick, the open pieces should be what makes your project work.

A few ideas to get you going:

* A haunted terminal that "possesses" your shell and whispers back eerie commentary on the commands you run
* A virtual ofrenda builder that helps you write a remembrance for a loved one and gather their favorite foods, songs, and stories
* An offline séance chatbot that channels the spirit of a long-dead programming language
* A local oral history tool that records and transcribes family stories so they're never lost
* A ghost story generator that weaves your smart home's sensor logs into a creepy tale of what happened while you were asleep
* A project that brings an abandoned open-source tool or a forgotten corner of internet history back to life

In your post, tell uswhy open innovation matters for what you built. Does running your own model mean your project works with the lights (and the internet) off? Keep your family's photos and stories private and on your own machine? Make sure what you build isn't locked inside a service that might disappear someday? Tell us where your open-based approach worked better than a closed one.

## Judging Criteria

This is DEV, soyour write-up matters most.

* Writing Quality (weighted most heavily):Is the post clear and engaging? Does it explain what you built, who it's for, and why open innovation matters?
* Relevance to the Prompt and Theme:Is open-source AI or open-weight models at the core of the project? Does it capture the spirit of the theme in a thoughtful way?
* Creativity:Is it an original idea, or a fresh take on a familiar problem?
* Technical Execution:Does it work, and is it well built?
* Use of Partner Technology (optional):If you're entering a partner category, does the project use that technology in a meaningful way?

Show your work.We'd love to see how you built it. Save your agent session withDevRelayand embed it in your post, or link to it. It's optional, but it helps the judges understand your process.
