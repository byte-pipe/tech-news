Hi, I'm Cesar, a Senior Software Engineer with 10+ years of experience.

In 2023, I got sick and burned out.In 2024, I was laid off.

To recover, I went back to writing:I resurrected my blog and my dev.to account.It worked like free therapy by giving me a reason to get out of bed.

After hundreds of posts, I challenged myself to write a book.That's howStreet-Smart Codingwas born:A roadmap with 30 lessons I wish someone had told me when starting out.

Since then,Street-Smart Codingbecame book #2 of a 4-part series.I already wrote a prequel,Street-Smart Manifestoand I'm working in a sequel,Street-Smart Coding in the Age of AI.My goal is to shared everything I've learned the hard way in over 10 years.I have planned another book, by the way.
