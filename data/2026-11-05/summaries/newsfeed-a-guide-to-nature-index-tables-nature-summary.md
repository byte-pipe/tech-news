## Purpose and Scope
- Presents the 2026 Nature Spotlight: Neuroscience tables, which rank leading institutions in neuroscience research.

## Data Collection Methodology
- Uses a subset of 128 journals from the Nature Index, excluding those unrelated to neuroscience.  
- In 125 journals, neuroscience articles were identified via a combination of search terms and fields of interest in Digital Science’s Dimensions database.

## Metrics and Ranking
- Institutions are ranked by the metric **Share**, which reflects each institution’s contribution to the total neuroscience output in the selected period (2021‑2025).
