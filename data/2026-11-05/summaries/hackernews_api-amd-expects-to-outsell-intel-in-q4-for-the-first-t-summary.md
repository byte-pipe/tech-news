# AMD expects to outsell Intel in Q4 for the first time in history — $17 B guidance would exceed Intel's projections

## Q3 financial highlights
- Revenue: $13.377 billion, up from $9.25 billion YoY and $11.54 billion QoQ.  
- Gross margin: 54%.  
- Net income: $2.43 billion, a 95 % increase.  
- Diluted EPS: $1.46 vs. $0.75 a year earlier.

## Data center business
- Accounted for 64 % of total revenue in Q3.  
- Sales: $8.55 billion, +97 % YoY and +27 % QoQ.  
- Operating income: $2.88 billion, +168 % YoY; operating margin rose to 34 % from 25 %.  
- Growth driven by Epyc processors and Instinct MI455X AI accelerators used in Helios rack‑scale systems (customers include Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Oracle Cloud).  
- Guidance: Q4 data‑center revenue ~ $12.8 billion, driven by further MI455X and Helios shipments.  
- Outlook: server‑CPU revenue expected to double in 2027; AI‑accelerator and rack revenue to more than double, pushing data‑center contribution to >80 % of total sales by 2027.

## Client and Gaming business
- Revenue: $3.722 billion, –8 % YoY and –3 % QoQ.  
- Client CPUs: $2.95 billion (down from $3.06 billion QoQ, up ~ $200 million YoY).  
- Gaming products: $773 million, down from $1.298 billion YoY due to fewer console SoC orders and weak Radeon RX GPU sales.  
- Operating income: $573 million, –34 % YoY; operating margin fell to 15 %.  
- Projection: >20 % sequential revenue decline in Q4 2026; double‑digit PC‑shipment decline expected in 2027, but AMD aims to outperform by focusing on enterprise, premium consumer, and desktop processors.

## Embedded business
- Revenue: $1.11 billion, +29 % YoY.  
- Operating income: $453 million, +60 % YoY.  
- Growth sources: aerospace & defense, automotive, communications, industrial customers; increased demand from data‑center build‑outs for embedded CPUs and adaptive SoCs.  
- Guidance: double‑digit sequential growth in Q4 2026 and sustained double‑digit annual growth in coming years; target >$18 billion of design wins in 2026.

## Outlook and competition with Intel
- AMD Q4 revenue guidance: ~$17 billion ±3 % (≈66 % YoY increase, 27 % QoQ growth).  
- Intel Q4 guidance: $15 billion–$16 billion, constrained by PC demand weakness and manufacturing capacity limits.  
- If AMD meets guidance, it will surpass Intel’s quarterly sales for the first time, with an estimated $1.5 billion lead at the midpoint of forecasts.  
- Market shift: AMD’s rapid expansion in data‑center CPUs and AI accelerators contrasts with Intel’s reliance on PC and enterprise server processors facing headwinds from high memory prices and component shortages.

## Executive comments
- Lisa Su (CEO & Chair) highlighted expectations for server revenue to double YoY in 2027 and for data‑center AI revenue to grow faster than previously projected, emphasizing the data‑center’s future >80 % share of total revenue.  
- Su also noted plans to outperform a declining PC market by leveraging AMD’s roadmap and expanding its commercial footprint.
