## Overview
- The article examines a flood of short, AI‑generated “first‑person” melodramas that dominate users’ X feeds.

- Creators must be Premium X users with ≥ 500 verified followers and ≥ 5 million impressions in the past three months.
- The program promises payment for “authentic, high‑quality content” that drives engagement.
- Because payment is tied to virality, some participants exploit the system with sensational or bot‑amplified posts.
