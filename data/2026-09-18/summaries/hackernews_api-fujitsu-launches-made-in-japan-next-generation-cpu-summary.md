# Fujitsu launches made‑in‑Japan next‑generation CPU FUJITSU‑MONAKA and MONAKA Server

## Announcement Overview
- Fujitsu Limited will start global sales of the FUJITSU‑MONAKA CPU in November 2026.  
- The CPU will be sold as a standalone component to cloud providers, data‑center operators, and server vendors.  
- Simultaneously, Fujitsu will launch the MONAKA Server, built in Japan and equipped with the FUJITSU‑MONAKA CPU, targeting data‑center operators, enterprises, academia, HPC, and defense sectors in Japan and Europe.

## Technical Highlights of FUJITSU‑MONAKA CPU
- 3D‑stacked architecture using a 2 nm process for the core and 5 nm for cache/I‑O.  
- Maximum clock frequency: 3.8 GHz.  
- Memory transfer speed: 8 800 MT/s.  
- Hardware acceleration for matrix operations with dedicated instructions, combined with SVE2 vector support.  
- Delivers roughly twice the AI inference throughput and superior power efficiency compared to competing CPUs.  
- Includes confidential computing with hardware‑based memory encryption for secure multi‑tenant environments.

## Features of MONAKA Server
- Designed, developed, and manufactured entirely in Japan (Kasashima Plant) to ensure traceability and supply‑chain transparency.  
- Available in 2U (dual‑CPU) and 1U (single‑ or dual‑CPU) rack‑mount configurations.  
- 1U model supports CDI/CXL technology for flexible memory and accelerator allocation beyond the physical server limits.  
- Operates with air cooling up to 40 °C or water cooling up to 45 °C, eliminating the need for specialized cooling infrastructure.  
- Cooling power consumption can be reduced by up to 80 %, lowering operating costs and environmental impact.

## Strategic Impact
- Provides a sovereign AI infrastructure that is domestically sourced, enhancing national security and supply‑chain reliability.  
- Enables AI inference platforms to be built with fewer servers and lower power usage, addressing space and energy constraints in data centers.  
- Forms the core of Fujitsu’s vertically integrated AI model, combining the MONAKA platform with proprietary AI solutions such as Fujitsu Kozuchi and Takane generative AI, offering traceable, secure, and autonomous AI services.

## Market Context
- Growing demand for generative AI has created shortages in compute resources and increased power consumption.  
- Organizations seek low‑power, high‑performance CPUs for on‑premises AI workloads that can operate without water‑cooling.  
- Geopolitical risks are driving interest in domestically produced, transparent hardware solutions.
