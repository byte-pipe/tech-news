# Alibaba launches Wan3.0 AI video model after record $10 billion share sale

## Overview
- Alibaba Group introduced Wan3.0, an AI video generation model capable of creating 30‑second videos from text, spreadsheets, slides, and web pages.  
- The model entered public beta on August 6 and has been used for short films, ads, tourism marketing, and music videos.

## Funding and Share Placement
- The rollout follows a $10 billion share placement, the largest primary follow‑on equity offering by a Hong Kong‑listed company.  
- 710 million new shares were priced at an 8.4% discount, representing about 3.7% of existing capital; completion is set for August 26.  
- Alibaba shares fell 8.1% to HK$113 after the pricing.

## Financial Context
- Quarterly net profit dropped 75% to 10.44 billion yuan, while capital expenditure rose 75% to 67.68 billion yuan.  
- All net proceeds from the share sale will fund upgrades to the Qwen model infrastructure and expand full‑stack AI computing capabilities.  
- Management expects strong enterprise demand to offset higher infrastructure costs, citing a 45% rise in AI cloud revenue to 48.44 billion yuan.
