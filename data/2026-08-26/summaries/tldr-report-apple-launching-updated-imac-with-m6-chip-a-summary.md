# Report: Apple launching updated iMac with M6 chip and new colors later this year

## Main announcement
- Apple is developing a new iMac equipped with the upcoming M6 chipset.  
- The refreshed iMac is expected to ship “before the end of the year.”  
- The update will include “some new color options,” though specific shades have not been disclosed.

## iMac refresh history and positioning
- This will be the first iMac refresh in two years, skipping the M2 and M5 chip families.  
- Apple has not offered a larger iMac display; high‑end users are steered toward a Mac Studio or Mac mini paired with a Studio Display.  

## Broader fall Mac lineup (rumored)
- Overhauled MacBook Pro models featuring touchscreens and Tandem OLED displays.  
- A new base‑model 14‑inch MacBook Pro powered by the M6 chip.  
- M5 Max and M5 Ultra Mac Studio variants (subject to supply).  
- Potentially a refreshed Mac mini with M6 and M5 Pro chips, likely arriving later.  

## M6 chip specifics
- The M6 will be Apple’s first Mac silicon built on a 2 nm process.  
- No “Pro,” “Max,” or “Ultra” variants are planned for the M6 generation; Apple intends to accelerate the launch of the subsequent M7 family.
