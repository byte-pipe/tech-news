# Summary of “Dolly Parton: A life in pictures”

## Early life and beginnings
- Born in a one‑room cabin in the Smoky Mountains of Tennessee, fourth of 12 children.  
- Performed on local radio and TV as a child; recorded her first single at age 13.  
- Moved to Nashville the day after graduating from Sevier County High School (1965).

## Music career milestones
- First solo hit “Dumb Blonde” (1966).  
- Joined *The Porter Wagoner Show* in 1967, gaining national TV exposure.  
- Became a Grand Ole Opry member on 4 January 1969, calling the Opry “the church of my heart.”  
- Breakthrough album *Hello, I’m Dolly* (1967) and later crossover successes such as *Here You Come Again* (1977).  
- Sold over 100 million records, wrote more than 3,000 songs, and won 11 Grammy Awards, including a lifetime‑achievement honor.  
- Signature hits “Jolene” and “I Will Always Love You” written in a single 1973 session.  
- Notable collaborations, especially the duet “Islands in the Stream” with Kenny Rogers (1980s).

## Songwriting philosophy
- Describes songwriting as her primary form of self‑expression and therapy.  
- Emphasizes that, despite commercial success, she identifies first and foremost as a songwriter.

## Acting and screen work
- Breakout film role as Doralee Rhodes in *9 to 5* (1980).  
- Subsequent roles in *The Best Little Whorehouse in Texas* (1982), *Steel Magnolias* (1989), and other movies, showcasing a blend of humor and emotional depth.

## Business ventures and philanthropy
- Opened Dollywood theme park in Pigeon Forge (1985), which became a major tourist attraction and funded charitable activities.  
- Founded the Imagination Library, donating over 100 million free books to children worldwide; the 100 millionth book was her own *Coat of Many Colours*, placed in the U.S. Library of Congress.  
- Contributed millions to medical research, disaster relief, and created the My People Fund after the 2016 Great Smoky Mountains wildfires (≈ $10 million distributed).  
- Recent pledges include $2 million for Hurricane Helene relief (2024) and personal encouragement of COVID‑19 vaccination.

## Recent performances and media presence
- Iconic “Legends” slot at Glastonbury Festival (2014), drawing over 180,000 attendees.  
- Celebrated 50 years at the Grand Ole Opry with a televised special (2019).  
- Shifted from extensive touring to selective live shows, new recordings, and Netflix projects such as *Dolly Parton’s Heartstrings*, *Mountain Magic*, and *Christmas on the Square*.
