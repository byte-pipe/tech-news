# FDA Authorizes First Wearable Device That Continuously Monitors Both Ketone Levels and Blood Sugar

## Announcement
- Date: August 25 2026  
- The U.S. Food and Drug Administration authorized the **Libre Duo 10 Day Continuous Dual Glucose Ketone Monitoring System** for individuals aged 2 years and older living with diabetes.  
- First wearable in the United States to continuously monitor ketone levels and the first worldwide to monitor both ketones and glucose together in a single device.

## Significance
- Provides real‑time ketone monitoring, allowing early intervention that can prevent life‑threatening diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA).  
- Michelle Tarver, M.D., Ph.D., Director of the FDA’s Center for Devices and Radiological Health, called the authorization “a breakthrough for the safety of children and adults living with diabetes.”

## Diabetes Context
- Approximately 40.1 million Americans have diabetes; about 2.1 million have type 1 diabetes.  
- In type 1 diabetes, elevated ketone production (when the body uses fat instead of glucose) can quickly lead to DKA, a serious and potentially fatal complication.

## Device Features
- Continuously measures glucose and ketone levels in interstitial fluid every minute, 24 hours a day, for up to 10 days.  
- Wireless transmission of readings to a compatible smartphone, displaying current values and upward/downward trends.  
- Automatic alerts trigger when ketone levels reach a predefined concerning threshold, helping patients and caregivers act before DKA develops.  
- Ketone data should be interpreted alongside glucose readings and symptoms.

## Clinical Evidence
- FDA review included data from six clinical studies involving more than 600 participants aged 2 years and older.  
- Studies demonstrated accurate tracking of clinically meaningful ketone differences across the 10‑day wear period, including early identification of elevated ketones before DKA onset.

## Regulatory Pathway
- Authorization aligns with the FDA’s “Home as a Health Care Hub” initiative, promoting patient‑centered devices for home use.  
- The device previously received Breakthrough Device designation to expedite development and review.  
- Marketing authorization granted to Abbott Diabetes Care through the De Novo pre‑market review pathway, with special controls established for labeling and performance testing.
