# Don't Wordle

Don't Wordleis a free daily word game. Like Wordle, you get six tries—but here the goal is toavoidguessing the hidden word. Use the clues from each guess (green for correct letter and position, yellow for correct letter wrong place, gray for letters not in the word) to eliminate possibilities. Your job is to keep as many valid words in play as you can without accidentally guessing the answer.

Each day has one puzzle. You can use a limited number of undos to take back a guess if you get too close. Track your stats and share your results with friends.
