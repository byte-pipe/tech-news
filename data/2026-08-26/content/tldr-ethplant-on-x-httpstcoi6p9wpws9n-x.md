# $AERO: The Liquidity Layer for Tokenized Stocks

Coinbase is expected to release its own version of tokenized stocks on Base as early as this week, and the implications for the onchain economy, specifically Aero, are significant.

These tokenized equity offerings are backed 1:1 by shares, so holders own an actual piece of the company with the benefits of true ownership, such as dividend upside. This is not the first implementation of tokenized stocks onchain: Robinhood, xStocks, Ondo Finance, and Binance have all launched similar products that show impressive traction, despite US regulatory restrictions and the fact that these are debt/wrapper structures, unlike Coinbase's.

The Coinbase offering on Base has the potential to drive even more tokenized equity trading onchain, inevitably benefiting the largest, most competitive DEX currently on Base: Aero, a dynamic I model in my price forecast later in this report.

1) Tokenized Stocks Onchain Today

Tokenized equities spot DEX volume in July 2025 was a mere $115M; a year later, the onchain economy is doing over $6B in AMM tokenized equity volume, having grown over 50x year-over-year. Furthermore, tokenized assets as a percent of all spot DEX volume was at 1% in July 2025 but now represents 11%. While growth has been notably impressive, tokenized assets are still in their infancy relative to what they will become.

The annual spot DEX volume run rate of tokenized equities, based on July 2026 data, is $72B, while memecoins alone did over $775B in spot DEX volume in 2025. Given the current limited universe of investable assets onchain, it is remarkable how much trading value has occurred. I expect that tokenized equities will quickly surpass memecoin and some other crypto-native asset trading while more stocks are tokenized as investors take advantage of the composability of onchain assets.

Not only is spot DEX volume of tokenized equities small relative to other onchain primitives, but it is also negligible next to the trading volume in the US stock market today. In 2025, there was roughly $200T in trading volume in US equity markets. Using the $72B annual run rate of spot DEX AMM volume for tokenized stocks, less than 0.04% of all US stock trading happens onchain today.

So, despite the progress that has been made by Robinhood, Binance, xStocks, and now Coinbase to offer tokenized equities while regulation is still in the works (Clarity Act and/or SEC rulemaking), today tokenized assets are only in their beginning stages compared to their full market potential.

Now on to everybody’s favorite part of CT: speculation.

2) Tokenized Stocks Onchain Tomorrow

The entire crypto industry will benefit from tokenized equities, as growth in one segment tends to benefit the broader market. However, I believe one of the largest opportunities sits at the spot decentralized exchange layer, as the increase in investable tokens onchain will drive trading volumes with much more velocity than in the traditional financial system. In other words, assets onchain change hands much more frequently, driving more volume at the same market capitalization.

The US stock market had $200T in trading volume in 2025 against $69T of US equity market cap. This means the asset turnover was roughly 3x. Onchain assets will have a much higher turnover because of the unique benefits tokenization offers.

First, because the market is open 24/7, price discovery will increasingly occur onchain and at all times of the day. This means more opportunity to trade, generating volume.

Second, the composability of crypto encourages financial velocity, since assets can move freely across protocols for lending, liquidity provision, yield, and collateral. This is a sharp contrast to the fragmented traditional financial system, where shares are stuck inside a single brokerage.

Lastly, agentic finance will allow for automated trading at scale with bots doing orders of magnitude more transactions onchain than humans.

Brian Armstrong, CEO of Coinbase, said that “up to 10% of global GDP could be running on crypto rails by the end of the decade”. This is a bold thesis similar to his other prediction that bitcoin could hit $1M by 2030. Despite the recent downturn in the crypto market, these targets are still possible given the compounding growth the onchain economy has been experiencing.

It is hard to predict the future, especially in nascent industries with low market capitalizations, because even a small fraction of global assets entering the market could lead to extraordinary growth.

Even so, I will make my own predictions and provide an illustrative example of the impact of tokenized equities on onchain trading volumes and then what that means for the price of $AERO.

3) Tokenized Stocks 2030 Forecast

This model is built in two parts: 1) a forecast of global equities split between US and the rest of the world (ROW) using year-over-year growth rate assumptions, and 2) a forecast of US equities' onchain penetration and tokenized stock turnover, used to derive potential onchain trading volume.

Explanation of assumptions:

Equity market cap growth rates: Based on 10-year CAGRs of US and ROW equity. However, if AI continues to accelerate in productivity growth and becomes even more advanced, we could see higher growth rates, resulting in a figure substantially larger than $111T for US equities.

Initial turnover assumption: DEX volume of tokenized equities was $6B per month against only $2.5B in tokenized equity assets at the end of July 2026. With the annualized volume of $72B, current tokenized stock turnover could be as high as 30x per year.

Declining turnover: As the float or market capitalization of tokenized equities increases onchain, the turnover will decrease since a higher % of assets will likely sit idle over time. However, this will still be higher than the traditional financial market for the reasons listed earlier supporting onchain turnover.

% of US equity market cap: Given the recent acceleration in adoption of tokenized assets, I expect the growth rate of tokenized stocks to be high in 2027 and 2028 as regulatory clarity improves and TradFi companies integrate more with the onchain economy. It is also reasonable to expect explosive growth since tokenized stock market capitalization is initially so small relative to total equity market cap.

2026 Forecast: There is roughly $2.5B in tokenized equities today with about 4.5 months left in the year. I believe, as Robinhood continues to promote its chain and Coinbase also begins to roll out more tokenized equities, it would be reasonable to hit $7.5B in tokenized equity market cap by EOY. However, we may not see $190B in trading volume this year, but rather an annualized run rate by EOY, meaning $15B in volume in December 2026 alone. This is not far-fetched considering July was at $6B despite regulatory uncertainties.

2030 Forecast: My target for tokenized equities in 2030 is $8.3T, which could be 7.5% of US equities or roughly 3.5% of global equities. My base case is that crypto becomes an integral part of the financial system, so 3.5% of global stocks being tokenized in the next several years is probable, especially if the benefits of tokenized equities massively outweigh those of holding shares through traditional brokerages.

4) Why Aero

Alexander Cutler, CEO of Dromos Labs (leading contributor to Aero), said in an interview that Aero’s goal is to be “the number one onchain venue for tokenized stocks”. Aerodrome has dominated the Base chain in DEX spot volumes, holding 52% market share in Q2 2026, ahead of the next largest venues, Uniswap at 26% and PancakeSwap at 12%. If the success of Aero in attracting liquidity and trading volume continues given its track record, the scaling of tokenized stocks will be a significant catalyst for $AERO.

With the launch of tokenized stocks on Base, Coinbase will need to quickly spin up stock liquidity onchain to ensure trading pools have the necessary capital to offer a seamless trading experience. Aero's incentive structure is uniquely suited to channeling capital into these pools, because tokenholders can direct emissions there, attracting liquidity providers even before the pools generate meaningful fees.

A great example of this was Aero teaming up with Coinbase to drive deep liquidity to cbBTC on Base. The success of those efforts continues to persist, with Aerodrome having more BTC-USD DEX volume traded in July 2026 than all other EVM DEXs combined (54% Aerodrome vs 46% other DEXs).

Based on Coinbase's economic stake in Aerodrome, as well as its previous success in driving specific liquidity goals on Base, I believe we will see this dynamic repeat with tokenized equities, leading to market dominance similar to what Aerodrome achieved in BTC-USD DEX volumes.

Altogether, tokenized stocks will be a tremendous growth driver for Aero, and the valuation analysis below highlights a potential scenario given this acceleration in real world assets onchain.

6) Valuation Analysis v2

This is my bull case for Aero, assuming EVM dominance and rapid acceleration of tokenized assets over the next several years. For a more conservative view of the protocol, check out my first article “$AERO: Why the market is pricing this wrong”.

The 5-year forecast model is built in three parts: 1) a forecast of DEX volume broken out by pair category based on growth rate assumptions, 2) an Aero revenue forecast derived from Aero's projected % share of EVM DEX volume, applying revenue as a % of that volume, and 3) a valuation analysis based on a revenue multiple and the token count forecast shown previously.

Explanation of assumptions:

For the DEX volume analysis, the main driver is tokenized asset volume growth. To add some conservatism, this volume is derived solely from the tokenized equity forecast from earlier in the article. In reality, we can expect additional volume to come from other tokenized real-world assets such as tokenized commodities.

Overall, the model assumes DEX trading volume in 2030 to be roughly 17x that of 2025. Given the assumption that more regulatory clarity is coming, combined with financial institutions' growing interest in onchain offerings, I expect that we are in the early stages of a tokenization supercycle and that a 76% CAGR could be achievable.

EVM DEX market share is around 60% today, but given the preference of major institutions such as Coinbase and Robinhood to launch L2s on EVM, it is likely EVM will gain more share of the onchain economy.

If Aero truly does have superior incentive structure compared to other DEXs as discussed in my previous article, it is probable that it will have EVM dominance once it updates to METADEX03 and starts servicing all of EVM. BCG’s rule of three states that a stable, competitive market has roughly three main competitors with market shares at a ratio of 4:2:1, with the remaining 30% spread out among smaller participants. Applying this to Aero being the dominant DEX on EVM, we could see it own 40% of volumes by 2030. However, given low switching costs in web3 and Aero’s flywheel effect on deep liquidity pools, the protocol could potentially exceed this, having already captured a higher market share % on Base.

The valuation analysis assumes a 4-6x multiple on annual revenue for market capitalization estimates, then uses the circulating token forecast to derive an implied token price. July 2026 revenue annualized was $54.5M against a market cap of $423M, implying a revenue multiple of roughly 8x. As the industry matures, revenue multiples may begin to decline, consistent with what the model shows.

There is a saying in finance I like about long-range models: the only thing 100% guaranteed about a model is that it will be wrong. Financial forecasts cannot predict the future, but they can give you an idea of the direction of an investment based on a given set of assumptions.

Another important caveat is that this model does not take into account any yield generated from holding the token. Even with an 11-17% annual inflation rate, holders of circulating $AERO can still have over 4,000% returns by buying now and holding to 2030 (153% annually), assuming this scenario is accurate, of course. However, if holders were to lock $AERO over that time period, their return could be much higher.

7) Closing thoughts

According to Vlad Tenev, CEO of Robinhood, “we are in the early innings of a global tokenization supercycle”. With Coinbase, one of the most crypto-focused US companies, only now offering a tokenized stock on Base, it does feel like we are still in the beginning of a rapid transformation of the financial system.

Aerodrome’s expansion across the rest of EVM comes at a critical time when incentivizing deep liquidity onchain will become essential to compete with the traditional financial system. Tokenized stocks represent a major opportunity for DEX volume growth, potentially generating billions in annual fee revenue. Aero may currently be one of the most undervalued opportunities in the crypto market given its potential to own a significant share of a rapidly expanding industry.
