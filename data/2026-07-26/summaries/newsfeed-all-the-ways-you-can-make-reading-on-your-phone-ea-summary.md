# All the Ways You Can Make Reading on Your Phone Easier | WIRED

**Optimizing Phone Reading Experience with Tweaks**

Here is a concise summary of the article's main points:

### Enhance Display Quality on Android Phones

* Adjust font sizes and styles via Settings panel
	+ Use menu labels to customize settings from different models
* Enable Night Light mode on Android phones for reading comfort during nighttime usage
* Disabling blue light emission can also help reduce eye strain (iOS option)

### Utilize Web Browser's Reading Mode

* Access dedicated reading mode in most modern web browsers, such as:
	+ Google Chrome: Show Reading mode > switch to this view
	+ Safari (iOS): Reading mode icon appears on the left side of the address bar
	+ View settings to customize font and color options

By incorporating these tweaks and customizations, you can significantly enhance your phone's reading experience while minimizing eye strain.
