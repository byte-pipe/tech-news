# The risk and cost of missed ADHD treatments in women is striking : NPR

# ADHD in Women: Unconscious Health Risks and Complex Diagnostic Challenges

• Research has shown that Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) can manifest differently in women, making it more subtle and potentially harder to diagnose compared to boys.
• Girls tend to show distracted or disorganized behavior rather than hyperactive or impulsive symptoms.
• Due to differences in presentation, girls often wait years to seek diagnosis when they may need treatment for underlying ADHD issues. (Julia Schechter)
 
# Hidden Long-Term Costs

• Unaddressed undiagnosed ADHD can lead to significant mental health issues and compromised daily functioning, affecting overall quality of life.
• The cost of missed treatment extends beyond the individual affected, with economic burdens for families and society as a whole due to delayed interventions and associated medical complications.
