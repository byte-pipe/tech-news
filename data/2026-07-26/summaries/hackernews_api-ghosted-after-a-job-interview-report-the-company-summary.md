# Ghosted After a Job Interview? Report the Company

## Ghosted After a Job Interview?

* Ghosting after a job interview typically involves being suddenly and without further explanation removed or dropped from the application process or communication with potential employers.
* The term "ghosted" originates from folklore practices in which individuals would often go missing after receiving a phone call or message, only to reappear later but then disappear again, leaving behind unanswered questions and unfinished business.
* Ghosting can occur due to various reasons such as lack of preparation for the interview, inadequate research on the company, or simply miscommunication.
* The consequences of ghosted job applicants can be severe, including loss of skills, career stagnation, and even financial instability.
