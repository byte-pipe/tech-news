# GM Backs Sodium Ion Batteries for U.S. Grid Storage - IEEE Spectrum

## Sodium-Ion Batteries Get Another Shot at Success in the U.S.

General Motors has taken a interest in supporting the development of peak energy's sodium-ion battery technology, indicating the company is backing efforts to revamp the US grid storage market.

## Key Points

* Peak Energy, backed by General Motors, aims to contribute to improved US energy infrastructure with its sodium-ion battery system.
* Sodium-ion batteries have been gaining attention for their potential in various applications, including grid storage.
* However, initial commercial success has been elusive, with other US-based startups facing challenges.
