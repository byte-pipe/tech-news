# An opinionated guide to which AI to use to do stuff

**An Opinionated Guide to Choosing the Right AI Platform**

As an expert in using artificial intelligence (AI) for various tasks, I've put together this guide to help you choose the right AI platform for your needs.

### What Changed Since My Last Post?

Since my last post, there have been significant changes in the world of AI. Many models have become more powerful and capable of handling complex tasks on their own. Now, what it means "using AI to do stuff" includes not just simple chatbots but also agentic systems that can perform human-like work.

### Choosing an Agentic System

An agentic system is a computer-aided platform that combines the capabilities of AI models with tools for planning and acting on a task. The most effective way to get your hands on one is by signing up for either Claude or ChatGPT, both of which offer easy-to-use interfaces at a starting cost of $20/month.

### What's Missing?

Currently, two main options are available:

*   **Cloud Platforms**: Google has entered the AI landscape with cloud platforms such as Google Cloud AI Platform and Google Colab.
*   **Specialized Platforms**: Some companies provide specialized platforms for tasks like data analysis or writing articles.

### A Step-by-Step Guide to Choosing Your AGENT

1.  Identify your project type (e.g., creative work, research, productivity).
2.  Choose the right AI model by considering its capabilities and pricing.
3.  Select a suitable thinking level for your needs:
    *   High: Ideal for complex tasks like medical diagnosis or legal review
    *   Medium: Suitable for data analysis, writing articles, or general office work 
4.  Consider adding specialized tools to enhance your workflow

Keep in mind that choosing the right AI platform is not a one-size-fits-all decision; it requires consideration of specific use cases and needs.
