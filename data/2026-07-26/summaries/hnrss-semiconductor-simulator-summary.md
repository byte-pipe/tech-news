# Semiconductor Simulator Animations

## Introduction
This page showcases three sets of transistor animations created using semiconductor simulator software, illustrating the motion of charge carriers and currents through a transistor device.

## First Set: No Diffusion/Drift Simulation
- The blue dots (electrons) and red dots (holes) move horizontally with equal velocity.
- White flashes represent recombination events that occur when electron-hole pairs pair up.
- This simulation only assumes diffusion is negligible, focusing on drift currents and their combined effect.

## Second Set: Actual Diffusion/Drift Simulation
- Carriers undergo both diffusion and drift to accurately depict motion.
- Diffusion is not shown in these animations for a cleaner visualization.

## Third Set: Detailed Current/Capacitance Animations
- Simulations include currents and voltages at each terminal, providing deeper insights into the transistor's operation.
