# Andy Burnham rules out early election in exclusive BBC interview

In an exclusive interview with the BBC's Laura Kuenssberg, Andy Burnham explicitly dismissed the idea of an early general election, saying "I'm going to rule it out... I don't think people want it."

Explaining his decision, the new prime minister suggested the majority of the electorate "voted for a manifesto" in the last election, and vowed he would "honour that manifesto" with the support of parliamentary colleagues.

Burnham went on to say, "I just think what I most need to do is get Britain focused on getting the country back where it should be, getting it working."
