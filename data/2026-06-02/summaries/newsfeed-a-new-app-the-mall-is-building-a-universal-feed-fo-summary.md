# Summary of “A new app, The Mall, is building a universal feed for online shopping”

## Overview
- The Mall is a startup launching an app that lets users create a personalized virtual mall of their favorite brands and track sales, restocks, and drops in one place.  
- The concept addresses the fragmented online shopping experience that forces consumers to juggle many tabs, newsletters, and brand sites.

## Founders & Vision
- Co‑founder and COO Ellie Konsker (formerly at Tom Ford and Carla Otto) identified the pain of scattered shopping data.  
- Co‑founder and CEO Shreya Halder, a Stanford computer‑science graduate, envisioned a “Spotify for shopping,” a centralized database for fashion similar to platforms for music, movies, and books.  
- The company was founded in October 2025 with the goal of giving everyone access to all online brands.

## Product Features
- **Catalog scraping:** The app scrapes retail websites (instead of using brand APIs) to pull full product catalogs, pricing, and promotion data.  
- **Real‑time updates:** Frequent scraping enables push notifications for sales, restocks, and new drops.  
- **User‑generated malls:** Upon sign‑up, users add favorite brands; the database already contains >10,000 brands, and users can add more by sharing an Instagram or TikTok handle.  
- **LLM‑powered labeling:** Large language models and custom models tag scraped items, supporting precise search and discovery.  
- **In‑app browsing:** When purchasing, a browser view opens within the app and redirects to the brand’s checkout page; no affiliate links are used.  
- **Social discovery:** Collections can be public or private, allowing friends and followers to explore each other’s malls and discover new brands or lower‑priced alternatives.

## Business Model
- The consumer app is free.  
- Future revenue will come from a B2B data tool for brands, offering anonymized, aggregated insights on clicks, seasonal assortment trends, and advertising opportunities (e.g., sponsored billboards, subscription placements).  
- The B2B product is slated for launch later this summer.

## Launch & Availability
- Early beta involved 4,500 testers.  
- Current rollout is invite‑only via referral; there is no fixed invite limit per user.  
- Full public release is expected by the end of summer 2026.  
- The app is available as a free download on the App Store.
