# Amazon’s Echo speakers can now help kids wind down and fall asleep

## Feature overview
- Amazon introduced **Sleep Studio**, a bedtime‑focused add‑on for Echo and Echo Kids smart speakers.  
- The service combines bedtime stories, guided meditations, and relaxing soundscapes to make the transition to sleep easier for children and less stressful for parents.  
- A half‑hour wind‑down routine starts automatically 30 minutes before a child’s scheduled bedtime, but kids can also launch it anytime with “Alexa, play Sleep Studio.”  

## Availability and pricing
- Sleep Studio is included at no extra cost for Amazon Kids Plus subscribers ($5.99 / month).  
- New users receive a free one‑month trial; purchasing an Echo Kids device adds a complimentary six‑ or twelve‑month Kids Plus subscription.  

## Parental controls and scheduling
- Parents set bedtime schedules, sleep windows, and custom playlists through the Amazon Kids Parent Dashboard (available as a mobile app and web portal).  
- The Echo Glow night‑light syncs with Sleep Studio, using color‑coded illumination to signal when it’s time to stay in bed or get up.  

## Content partners
- The library draws from third‑party providers such as Headspace, Moshi, and Calm.  
- Calm’s VP of Partnerships, Fergal Walker, highlighted the breadth of meditations, lullabies, soundscapes, and Sleep Stories created for kids.  

## Summary
Sleep Studio extends the Echo ecosystem into bedtime routines, offering parents a controllable, subscription‑based tool that blends audio content and visual cues to help children wind down and fall asleep.
