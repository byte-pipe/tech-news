## Problem with the current keyboard
- Uses a wireless keyboard/trackpad combo for a living‑room media PC.  
- The manufacturer repurposed the F‑keys (F1‑F12) with extra “special function” actions, e.g., F4 = sleep.

- Only F4 has a severe side effect; other keys (e.g., F6) perform minor, tolerable actions.

## Why some keyboards get it right
- **WASD Code** keyboard: secondary functions are low‑impact and easily reversible; volume controls work well.  
- **Keychron K10**: double‑duty keys mirror macOS functions, stay in the locked “traditional F‑key” mode even after power loss, and are simple to switch.  
- The author also mentions a disliked MacBook keyboard with LCD‑screen keys that required looking at the keyboard to type.

## Principles for a good Fn implementation
1. **Low‑impact, quickly reversible secondary actions** – minimal cognitive load and easy correction.  
2. **Default state is the traditional function** (or clearly indicated), with a straightforward way to toggle modes without obscure shortcuts or proprietary drivers.  
3. **Mode persistence** – once switched, the keyboard should remain in that state regardless of power loss or disconnection.

- Many keyboards implement Fn keys poorly, causing frustration and wasted time.
