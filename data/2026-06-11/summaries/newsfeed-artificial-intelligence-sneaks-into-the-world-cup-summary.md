# Artificial Intelligence Sneaks Into the World Cup Thanks to Google Gemini | WIRED

## Overview
- Google has partnered with the Argentine Football Association (AFA) to make Gemini the main global sponsor of Argentina’s national team for the 2026 World Cup.  
- Gemini’s logo will appear on the Albiceleste training kit, and the AI tool will be used to analyze plays, form, performance, and statistics.

## Goals and Implementation
- **Team Use:** Players and coaching staff will access AI models to break down plays, study opponent data, and accelerate tactical decisions on the pitch.  
- **Fan Experience:** Google Search will be reconfigured to act like a fellow fan, providing AI‑generated answers to real‑time queries, play analysis, and detailed statistics. It will also let fans create songs, memes, cartoons, and other visual content for social media during and after matches.  
- **Testing Ground:** The World Cup serves as a high‑pressure “stress test” for Gemini’s capabilities in a live, global sports environment.

## Business Context
- The deal with Argentina was finalized in March 2024 but announced in May to keep negotiations open with other national teams.  
- Google has also secured partnerships with Brazil and France, two former World Cup champions, expanding its presence across top football markets.  
- For Google, the World Cup is the year’s most important cultural event, offering a platform to showcase AI to a worldwide audience.  
- For the AFA, the partnership injects modern technology into a traditional institution while providing new revenue and branding opportunities.

## Risks and Challenges
- Exposure to millions of simultaneous queries and diverse cultural contexts could amplify any AI errors (e.g., incorrect statistics, fabricated line‑ups, misplaced imagery).  
- Mistakes would receive global visibility, potentially harming both Google’s and the sport’s reputation.

## Historical Perspective
- Major sporting events have historically accelerated technology adoption (color TV, GPS training, VAR).  
- This partnership marks the first time a technology company places its AI brand on players’ kits and simultaneously on fans’ smartphones at such scale.
