# Apple refines Liquid Glass design and expands child safety tools at WWDC

## Design updates
- Apple introduced a refined version of its Liquid Glass design language, first launched in 2025.
- Changes focus on readability and control rather than a dramatic visual overhaul.
- Improvements include more uniform refraction, higher contrast, sharper icons, and a new slider that lets users adjust translucency from “ultraclear” to fully tinted.
- The updated look is applied across iPhone, Mac, and other Apple devices, aiming to smooth out rough edges of the original implementation.

## iOS 27 performance enhancements
- Faster app launches and quicker photo loading.
- Accelerated AirDrop transfers and smoother transitions between Wi‑Fi and cellular networks.
- Improved Mail search functionality.
- Lower‑level system upgrades such as an optimized CPU scheduler.
- These plumbing‑level updates are highlighted alongside Apple’s AI announcements, emphasizing everyday device responsiveness.

## Expanded child‑safety tools
- New and updated features for child‑safety accounts, setup flows, “Ask to Browse,” Time Allowances, Schedules, and a redesigned Screen Time experience.
- Parents can more precisely manage what children see, who they communicate with, and when apps can be accessed.
- Tools aim to help families negotiate digital usage, provide expert guidance, and give parents visibility into device activity.

## Implications and considerations
- Apple’s family‑safety suite deepens the company’s role as a gatekeeper in children’s digital lives.
- While the tools empower parents, they do not replace age‑appropriate digital education and can create a false sense of security.
- The article cautions that reliance on platform controls should complement, not substitute, parental judgment and broader digital‑literacy efforts.
