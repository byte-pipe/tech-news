# Upward, a Dating App Targeting Gen Z Singles

## Overview of the App

Upward, a dating app, relaunched in March, focusing on Gen Z singles. The app aims to appeal to those who value traditional values around family, faith, and community.

## Key Features and Promotions

- **Chivalry Mode**: When activated, men must message first. This feature was previously used on Bumble.
- **Marriage Timeline**: Users can share when they hope to get married, allowing potential partners to gauge compatibility.
- **Faith Statement**: Upon signing up, users are encouraged to include a statement explaining their relationship values.

## App's Shift in Focus

Upward's pivot away from a Christian-centric app reflects the changing preferences of Gen Z singles, who prioritize broader family values and social obligations. This shift acknowledges the growth of the dating market and the importance of humanizing apps.

## Implications for Traditional Dating Apps

The abandonment of a faith-centric focus may mark a turning point in the dating market, as some users may prefer a more inclusive and diverse approach. However, the success of Upward and the broader shift in the market's demographic may lead to further innovation in dating apps.
