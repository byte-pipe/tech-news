# 8 Things to Know About the iPhone Duo | WIRED

## iPhone Duo Overview

- Apple's first folding smartphone, the iPhone Duo, has finally arrived after 7 years since the Galaxy Fold was introduced by Samsung.
- The Duo builds upon the refinement of Apple's hardware compared to the Galaxy Fold, showing significant improvements in design.

## App Optimizations

- The iPhone Duo's unique designs cause many apps to not display optimally on both the outer and inner screens.
- Many third-party apps, such as Google Home, look great on iOS 27.1, but some apps, like Instagram, still have broken UI elements.
- Optimizations will take time to roll out to all apps in users' libraries, potentially leading to a richer folding experience.

## Face ID's Replacement

- Apple no longer features Face ID, but instead relies on Touch ID, an old biometric authentication method.
- Face ID's lack of a large presence in the iPhone Duo might cause inconvenience for some users.
- Users may prefer the traditional Touch ID method, which they used in older iPhones.
- Dynamic Island, also known as the notch, is still present on the iPhone Duo but has not disappeared from standard iPhones.

## Foldable Phone Price

- The iPhone Duo is priced at $1,999, making it unavailable to most customers due to its hefty price tag.
- Apple's decision to make the iPhone Duo available to back-ordered customers suggests that the company is aware that some users may struggle to afford the device at launch.
- The lack of a standard price for the iPhone Duo, at least during launch, may limit its appeal to some customers.
