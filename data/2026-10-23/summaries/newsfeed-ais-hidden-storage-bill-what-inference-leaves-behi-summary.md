# AI’s hidden storage bill: What inference leaves behind | MIT Technology Review

**Data Storage Costs Beyond the Cloud: Breaking Down the Hidden Infrastructure Expenditures**
=============================================================================

### Main Points

*   Data storage costs are often overlooked in initial AI infrastructure planning
*   Inference processes generate huge amounts of back-end data, far exceeding the output files
*   The associated data footprint grows exponentially, exceeding 100% of the original data size in a single inference request
*   The demand for data storage is expected to increase at a rapid pace, making up more than 40% of total data center demand by the end of the decade
*   Organizations need to optimize their storage to match the AI data life cycle, ensuring that GPUs are utilized efficiently

**Data Footprint Explosion**

*   A single AI-generated video clip can result in over 500MB of associated data being stored
*   For enterprises generating higher resolution videos, the back-end footprint can grow into hundreds of gigabytes per clip
*   The rapid growth of data storage demands will be difficult to keep pace with, as the volume of retained data does not decrease when compute demand slows

**Optimizing Storage for AI Data Life Cycle**

*   It is essential for organizations to match the AI data life cycle, ensuring that GPUs are utilized efficiently
*   Organizations need to plan their storage infrastructure to accommodate the exponential growth of data associated with inference processes
*   Optimizing storage will become increasingly important as AI demand continues to grow, with McKinsey projecting a 35% CAGR for AI inference demand through 2030
