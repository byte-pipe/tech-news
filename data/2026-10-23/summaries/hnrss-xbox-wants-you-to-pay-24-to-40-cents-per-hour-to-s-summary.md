# Xbox wants you to pay 24 to 40 cents per hour to stream games you already bought - Ars Technica

**Xbox Cloud Playtime Pricing: A Summary**

### Overview

* Microsoft has introduced a new pricing model for its Cloud Playtime service, allowing non-subscribers to purchase hours of cloud playable time for eligible games.
* The pricing structure includes options for 10 hours of playtime for $4, 25 hours for $8, and 50 hours for $12.

### Key Price Ranges

* 10 hours of playtime: $4
* 25 hours of playtime: $8
* 50 hours of playtime: $12

### Pricing Considerations

* The prices are significantly lower than what non-subscribers would be willing to pay for game streaming, evoking images of old arcades where players would buy quarters for gameplay.
* Cloud Playtime purchases do not come with the same benefits as Xbox Game Pass subscriptions, such as access to hundreds of games across the platform.
* The pricing model is based on the actual time spent playing games, so purchases will expire after 50 hours.

### How It Compares to Alternative Options

* Similar to old arcades, where players would pay a quarter per play, the Cloud Playtime pricing structure is a new and unconventional approach to game streaming.
* The model does not include features like free-to-play access, which was popular in arcades.

### Microsoft's Perspective

* The company is positioning the Cloud Playtime pricing structure as a new offering for Xbox Insiders, with introductory launch prices and ongoing support.
* The pricing model is designed to provide a new way for non-subscribers to access game streaming, despite the lower costs.
