# GNU Taler

# Overview of Taler in Germany

The NGI Taler project aims to introduce Taler, a widely-used open-source payment system, as a legally available online payment service in the European Union. Today, Taler Operations AG is being founded to take over the operation of Taler in Euro. This development marks a significant step forward in implementing Taler in Germany.

## Key Components of Taler in Germany

To facilitate the introduction of Taler, several key elements will be introduced in the future. These include:

### Licensing and Regulation

The process will require Taler Operations AG to obtain an e-money license from BaFin, the German financial supervisory authority, to operate Taler as a payment system in Euro.

### GLS Bank's Role

Although GLS Bank will no longer be responsible for deploying the Taler payment system in Germany, the company will maintain a supportive role in the project.

### New Company Structure

The newly founded Taler Operations AG will replace the current GLS Bank within the NGI Taler consortium, which includes Taler Systems SA (Luxembourg) as a subsidiary.

## Benefits of the New Arrangement

Establishing a Taler-only entity under the name Taler Operations AG will provide several benefits:

* **Focus**: The focus will be solely on promoting and offering the benefits of Taler for everyday payments in Germany.
* **Compliance**: The company will be able to avoid integration complexities and maximize its focus on the Taler system.
* **Single Market Access**: Once licensed, Taler Operations AG will be able to extend its services across the EU single market.

## Future Development and Timeline

A launch date will be announced when BaFin grants the necessary authorisation. It is worth noting that this development represents an important step forward for the implementation of Taler in Germany and marks the end of a three-year journey for the NGI Taler project.
