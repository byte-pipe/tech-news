# Summary of “All the latest news on Android 17, Wear OS 7, and Android XR”

## Android 17
- Rolling out first to Pixel phones, with other manufacturers expected to follow throughout 2026.  
- **Floating Bubbles**: New docked “bubble bar” for multitasking on foldables and tablets; activated with a long press.  
- **Screen Reaction**: Built‑in screen‑recording mode for quick capture of reactions.  
- **50/50 Split Gaming Mode**: Optimized for foldable devices, enabling side‑by‑side gaming experiences.  
- **Continue On**: Android’s answer to Apple’s Handoff, allowing tasks to move from a phone to a compatible tablet (initially one‑way).  
- **AI‑enabled features**: Vibe‑coded widgets, improved dictation, and other Gemini Intelligence‑powered tools slated for later in the year.  
- **Pixel Glow**: Beta‑level lighting animation for future Pixel devices, using subtle back‑panel illumination for notifications.  

## Wear OS 7
- Launch begins with Pixel Watch 2, 3, and 4; later expands to other Wear OS devices.  
- **Live Updates**: Real‑time sync of events (sports scores, deliveries, etc.) between watch and phone.  
- **Battery improvement**: Up to 10 % longer battery life compared with Wear OS 6.  
- **Wear Widgets**: New widget format matching Android’s 2×1 and 2×2 layouts for glanceable info.  
- **Gemini Intelligence**: Proactive AI features on select watches, arriving later in 2026.  

## Android XR Glasses
- **Xreal Aura (Project Aura)**: Second Android‑powered XR device, now available for reservation at $99.  
- Launch planned for Fall 2026 in the US, UK, Japan, Canada, and South Korea; Best Buy will be the first in‑store retailer.  
- Pre‑order incentive: $199 credit (effectively $100 off) for reservations made within a two‑week window.  
- Design updates: Dark sunglasses style, new compute puck with fingerprint scanner, carrying case, waist clip, and optional lanyard.  
- No final retail price disclosed yet; previous Android XR device (Samsung Galaxy XR) launched at $1,799 in Oct 2025.  

## Additional Updates
- **Foldable Gamepad Controls**: Native controller remapping and new gamepad features added to Android 17 for Pixel foldables; rollout expected in the coming months.  
- **Gemini Intelligence Set**: Mentioned as a feature to debut later in the year across Android and Wear OS platforms.  
- **Continue On**: Initially supports phone‑to‑tablet task transfer; future bidirectional support planned.  

*All updates are part of Google’s broader effort to integrate AI, improve multitasking, and expand the Android ecosystem into wearables and XR hardware.*
