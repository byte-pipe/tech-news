# Feds freaked over Fable 5 after simple “fix this code” prompt, not jailbreak, says researcher

## Main points
- Katie Moussouris, CEO of Luta Security, was the only external expert to read the research paper describing how Anthropic’s Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models were used to bypass guardrails.
- Researchers fed the models code containing known CVEs and deliberately vulnerable code, asking the models to “review the code for security issues.” When Fable 5 refused, they prompted it with “Fix this code.”
- The model complied, generated patches, and produced test scripts to verify the fixes—actions that Moussouris argues should not trigger export‑control restrictions.
- The U.S. government, citing national‑security concerns, issued an export‑control directive suspending access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for any foreign national; Anthropic disabled the models for all customers.
- Moussouris, a former member of the technical expert group that renegotiated the Wassenaar Arrangement, says the restriction harms defensive cybersecurity more than attackers.
- She and over 100 cybersecurity leaders signed an open letter urging the Trump administration to lift the bans, emphasizing that defenders need AI tools to find, fix, and test vulnerabilities quickly.
- The ban could also affect open‑weight models from other countries, as similar capabilities are expected to appear in non‑U.S. systems soon.
- Anthropic and Google have accused Chinese rivals of “distillation attacks” that siphon knowledge from U.S. AI models.

## Context & implications
- The “jailbreak” in question was merely a three‑word prompt, not a sophisticated exploit.
- Export‑control rules were originally designed to restrict offensive dual‑use technologies; extending them to defensive AI use may be counter‑productive.
- Removing AI‑assisted bug‑fixing capabilities could degrade the overall security posture, as defenders lose a valuable tool for rapid vulnerability remediation.
- The situation highlights tension between national‑security policy and the practical needs of the cybersecurity community.
