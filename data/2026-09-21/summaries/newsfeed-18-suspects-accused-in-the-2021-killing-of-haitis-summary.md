# 18 Suspects Accused in 2021 Killing of Haiti's President Being Extradited to U.S.

• Eighteen suspects involved in the July 2021 assassination of Haitian President Jovenel Moïse are being extradited to the United States to face trial in Florida, marking the end of a prolonged judicial process.

# Background

• The suspects, including 17 Colombian soldiers and former Haiti Ministry of Justice employee Joseph Badio, remain pending trial in Haiti, with some claiming they had planned a coup rather than an assassination.

# Extradition Process

• The United States Attorney for the Southern District of Florida, Jason Reding Quiñones, announced the extradition of the suspects, stating that they will be brought to account for their alleged involvement in the killing.
