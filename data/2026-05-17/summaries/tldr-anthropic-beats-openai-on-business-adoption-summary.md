# Anthropic beats OpenAI on business adoption

## Key findings
- In April, Anthropic’s business adoption rose 3.8 % to 34.4 % of U.S. companies, overtaking OpenAI, whose share fell 2.9 % to 32.3 %.
- Overall AI adoption among businesses increased slightly, reaching 50.6 % of firms.
- Over the past year Anthropic’s adoption quadrupled, while OpenAI’s grew only 0.3 %.

## Methodology
- The Ramp AI Index uses corporate card and invoiced payments recorded by Ramp to measure AI spend.
- Payments are counted at the transaction level to infer which AI providers businesses are using.
- Detailed research approach is linked in the original report.

## Implications and headwinds for Anthropic
- **Misaligned incentives:** Anthropic profits more from higher token usage, encouraging customers to run costlier models even when cheaper options would suffice; cost‑cutting pressures could erode its share.
- **Model performance issues:** Recent weeks have seen frequent outages, rate limits, and declining user satisfaction with Claude; the company responded by resetting usage limits and securing additional compute capacity via a SpaceX partnership.
- **Costly model updates:** The latest model version triples token costs for prompts that include images, raising concerns about rising expenses without proportional value.

## Outlook and next steps
- Fast‑growing vendors on Ramp’s platform are AI inference services offering cheap, open‑source models, suggesting a shift toward lower‑cost alternatives.
- OpenAI’s Codex is highlighted as a competitive, cheaper option with minimal switching friction.
- The author will monitor next month:
  1. OpenAI’s market share and subscription growth, especially among developers adopting Codex.
  2. Expansion of AI inference platforms that provide inexpensive models.
