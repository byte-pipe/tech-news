# Asteroid 2026 JH2 – Near‑Earth Flyby Summary

## Key Facts
- Diameter: about 20 m (≈ 66 ft), similar to Chicago’s Cloud Gate sculpture.  
- Closest approach: ≈ 57,000 mi (≈ 92,000 km) from Earth on May 18, nearer than the Moon (≈ four times farther).  
- It will be the closest of the tracked near‑Earth objects (NEOs) passing Earth in the next few months.  
- No impact risk: monitoring agencies do not list 2026 JH2 among objects with a measurable chance of hitting Earth.  

## Observation Opportunities
- Amateur astronomers can view the asteroid with a telescope.  
- A live broadcast is planned by Virtual Telescope, allowing broader public viewing.  

## Orbital Classification
- 2026 JH2 is an **Apollo‑type** NEO:  
  - Semi‑major axis > 1 AU (larger than Earth’s orbit).  
  - Perihelion < 1.017 AU (closest point to the Sun is inside Earth’s orbit).  
- All bodies with perihelion < 1.3 AU are classified as NEOs.  

## Context and Comparison
