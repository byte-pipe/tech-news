TheOriginal Articlewas published on 17 March 2021

Correction to:Naturehttps://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03341-5Published online 17 March 2021

In the version of this article initially published, due to mistakes during file revision and figure assembly, there was a duplication error in Extended Data Fig.10l, where the top-right 20% Control IL-1β image was a repeat of the lower-left 20%Jak2VFIgG image. In the initial submission, where current Extended Data Fig.10lwas originally presented as Fig.4d, the correct 20% Control IL-1β H&E stain was included. Due to the age of the article, Extended Data Fig.10lcannot be updated directly; the correct image, in submitted Fig.4d, can be accessed in the Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment.

Supplementary informationis available in the online version of this amendment.
