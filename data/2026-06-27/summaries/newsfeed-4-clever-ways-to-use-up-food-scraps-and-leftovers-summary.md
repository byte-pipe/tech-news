# 4 clever ways to use up food scraps and leftovers : NPR

**4 Clever Ways to Use Up Food Scraps and Leftovers**

Leftovers can be a daunting concept when they come to mind. With the increasing awareness of food waste, here are 4 clever ways to use up food scraps:

### **1. Eat Me First Box in Your Fridge**

* Store a container or box in your fridge with ingredients that can be repurposed as leftovers.
* When you're ready for a meal, simply head to this container and pick out the ingredient needed.

**2. Leftover Lattuce Leaves**

* Buy large lettuce leaves instead of individual heads.
* Use them as wraps for sandwiches or salads, and toss in other ingredients for added flavor.
* You can also grill or sauté lettuce for a crunchy snack.

### **3. Turn Leftovers into Soups**

* Boil down excess liquid from a pot of leftover chicken or vegetables to create a rich broth.
* Use the resulting soup as a base for future meals, such as added noodles, rice, or meat.
* Experiment with different flavor additions, like diced herbs or spices.

### **4. Make "Mystery Jars"**

* Fill empty mason jars with leftover ingredients and your choice of add-ins (e.g., frozen vegetables, nuts, seeds).
* Label each jar with the date and contents so you can easily identify them later.
* Use these versatile jars as a base for future meals or snack ideas.

These creative tips will help turn your leftovers into enjoyable meals without food waste.
