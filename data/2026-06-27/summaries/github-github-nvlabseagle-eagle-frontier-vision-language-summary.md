### Eagle: Frontier Vision-Language Models with Data-Centric Strategies

#### Overview
Eagle is a family of frontier vision-language models (VLMs) from NVIDIA that explore data-centric strategies across general-purpose multimodal understanding, long-context reasoning, and embodied applications.

#### Key Features

* Generalist grounding, detection, and pointing abilities
* Fast and high-quality operation with parallel box decoding
* Frontier VLM architecture with SOTA image and video performance
* Supports multiple flagship NVIDIA efforts in enterprise intelligence and Physical AI research

#### Main Components

* **Eagle**: A family of VLMs that represent the forefront of multimodal understanding, long-context reasoning, and embodied applications.
* **LocateAnything**: A generalist grounding, detection, and pointing model for fast and high-quality image and video processing.

#### Releases and Updates
The latest release:
* Eagle 2.5: A frontiers VLM with state-of-the-art image and video performance
**Avalanche**: Eagle's successor with expanded multimodal capabilities (coming soon)
