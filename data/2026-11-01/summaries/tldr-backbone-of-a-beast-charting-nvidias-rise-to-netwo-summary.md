# Backbone of a beast – charting Nvidia's rise to networking titan

## Overview
- Nvidia, traditionally known for GPUs and AI hardware, now leads datacenter Ethernet sales, surpassing Cisco in Q2 ($17.5 bn vs. $17.3 bn).  
- The transformation began with the $6.9 bn acquisition of Mellanox in 2019, shifting Nvidia toward a system‑builder model that combines compute and networking.

## Key Milestones
- **2019 – Mellanox acquisition**  
  - Added InfiniBand NICs and switches to Nvidia’s portfolio.  
  - Enabled Nvidia to offer tightly integrated clusters rather than separate GPUs and servers.  
- **2023 – AI gold rush**  
  - OpenAI’s ChatGPT launch drove a surge in demand for AI‑optimized networking.  
- **2024‑2025 – Blackwell generation**  
  - Introduction of Blackwell GPUs, NVLink switches, and Spectrum‑X Ethernet switches accelerated revenue growth.  
  - Large‑scale deployments (e.g., Elon Musk’s Colossus, Meta’s Prometheus) showcased the ecosystem.

## Integration Strategy
- **Co‑design philosophy** – hardware and software are developed together for specific AI workloads, not as isolated best‑of‑class components.  
- **Customer benefits** – simplified deployment, faster time‑to‑market, and unified troubleshooting when compute and networking come from a single vendor.  
- **Case study: Recursion**  
  - Switched from GTX‑based Ethernet clusters to Nvidia’s Quantum InfiniBand switches and ConnectX NICs for a 300‑GPU A100 cluster.  
  - Achieved easier optimization and support, handling >50 PB of data.

## Spectrum‑X Success Factors
- Combines high‑radix Ethernet switching with Nvidia’s superNICs and software optimizations, delivering InfiniBand‑like performance with broader port density.  
- More economical for very large GPU clusters because fewer switches are needed.  
- Competes with offerings from Broadcom, Cisco, AMD, but stands out due to deep compute‑network integration.

## Future Outlook
- **NVLink/NVSwitch evolution** – Blackwell’s GB200 NVL72 racks and associated NVSwitch trays create massive single‑GPU pools, guaranteeing ongoing networking revenue even without external switches.  
- Continued focus on end‑to‑end system solutions positions Nvidia to dominate both AI compute and datacenter networking markets.
