# Reading Anthropic's S-1 | Merge Conflict, by Tilo Mitra

# Reading Anthropic’s S‑1 – Key Takeaways

## Overview
- Anthropic filed its S‑1 on 26 Oct 2026, revealing its first public financials.  
- First‑half 2026 revenue: **$16.3 B**, a 14‑fold increase from the same period a year earlier.  
- Q2 2026 operating profit: **$974 M**, the company’s first profitable quarter.  
- Compute contracts signed total **≈ $637 B**, about 39 × the H1 2026 revenue.

## Revenue Growth
- Quarterly revenue rose from **$376 M (Q1 2025)** to **$11.6 B (Q2 2026)** – a 30.7× increase in five quarters.  
- Year‑over‑year revenue: $41 M (2023) → $386 M (2024) → $4.6 B (2025).  
- First half of 2026 generated more than three times the entire 2025 revenue.

## Profitability & Operating Leverage
- Until Q1 2026, compute costs exceeded revenue (sub‑$1 revenue per $1 compute).  
- From Q1 2026 onward, revenue per $1 of compute rose to **$1.40** and **$2.08** in Q2 2026, crossing the break‑even point.  
- Revenue grew 14.7× while compute spend grew only 4.0×, showing strong operating leverage.  
- Q2 2026 operating profit: **$974 M**.

## Cost Structure (H1 2026)
- Total costs: **$17.0 B** (slightly above revenue).  
- Compute & infrastructure: **$8.9 B** (53 % of costs).  
- Other expenses: S&M **$3.2 B**, Personnel **$3.2 B**, G&A & other **$1.7 B**.  
- Operating loss: **$693 M**, covered by cash reserves.

## Net Loss Accounting
- Reported net loss: **$80.7 B**, driven largely by a non‑cash “fair‑value remeasurement” of convertible notes/SAFEs (**$72.4 B**).  
- Cash‑flow‑positive operating cash flow: **≈ $3.9 B**.  
- Cash & investments on hand: **$131.7 B** (June 2026).

## Customer Base
- Customers spending ≥ $100 K annually grew 12.5× YoY, from ~600 (Jun 2025) to > 11,900 (Sep 2026).  
- Top tier: ~250 customers spend ≥ $10 M each year.  
- Net revenue retention ≈ 900 % (average business customer spent nine times more year‑over‑year).  
- Revenue composition: 80 % usage‑based (API), 48 % via hyperscaler marketplaces (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure), 20 % from the two largest customers.

## Compute Commitments
- Seven major contracts total **≈ $637 B** (Broadcom, SpaceX AI, Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Nscale, Lambda).  
- Minimum mandatory spend (non‑cancellable) as of June 2026: **$265.6 B**.  
- This mandatory portion is **2.0×** the cash balance, with commitments extending to 2033+.

## Bull vs. Bear Perspectives
- **Optimistic view:** Margins improve as compute costs fall faster than prices; market expands beyond the current ~12 k customers; cash and borrowing capacity can fund long‑term contracts.  
- **Cautious view:** Open‑source models could compress pricing; scaling risk lies in building enough compute capacity for AI agents; industry‑wide borrowing may pressure interest rates; contracts are largely fixed‑cost, while revenue is variable.

## Key Risks Highlighted in the Filing
- Dependence on chip, power, and data‑center supply chains.  
- Potential slowdown in customer demand, which would leave fixed compute commitments under‑utilized.  
- Competitive pressure from hyperscaler‑owned models.
