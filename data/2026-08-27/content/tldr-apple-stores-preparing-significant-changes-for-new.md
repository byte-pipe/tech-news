# Apple Stores preparing ‘significant’ changes for new Home product launches: report

 

Michael Burkhardt
 | Aug 23 2026 - 7:33 am PT																

According toBloomberg’s Mark Gurman, Apple is preparing significant changes at its retail stores in preparation for upcoming Home product launches. At the very least, Apple should debut a refreshed HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K this fall, though the changes would suggest a third, more significant product is also on its way.

Apple’s had along backlogof Home products pending release, largely because of all of the delays involving the company’s Siri revamp. Now that Siri has come together, we should see some of these products finally hit the market after years without a refresh.

That said, reports indicated that Apple would release a refreshedHomePod miniandApple TV 4Kfirst, and that the companies first smart home hub (otherwise known as HomePod with Display or HomePad) might debut a little bit later.

However, it’s now sounding like the smart home hub might release alongside the updated Apple Tv and HomePod mini, at least based on what retail stores are planning. From thePower On newsletter:

Apple is preparing significant changes to those sections this fall, including rearranging parts of the Avenues and adding accessory bays to showcase new products. The company is already planning an upgraded Apple TV set-top box and a new HomePod mini for this time frame. But the scale of the retail changes suggests something else is coming: the long-anticipated home hub with a display. The planned modifications appear too extensive to be solely about routine updates to existing products.

Of course, we’ll never know for certain until Apple holds its September keynote. Based on today’s reporting though, it sounds like we might get our first glimpse at Apple’s all-new smart home hub quite soon, alongsiderefreshed Apple TV 4KandHomePod minimodels that should support Siri AI.

## Wrap up

As a refresher,Apple’s smart home hubis rumored to sport a 7-inch square display and come in two versions: one with a built in speaker, and another with magnets on the rear that’ll support wall mounting. The product will run a new OS that’ll offerwidgets on the home screen, be useful for FaceTime calls, and of course serve as the hub for all of your smart home accessories and Siri. It’ll include an A18 chip as well.

Another new rumor for Apple’s Home lineup is that the new Apple TV 4K might offer arefreshed Siri Remote, though it isn’t clear what changes will arise.
