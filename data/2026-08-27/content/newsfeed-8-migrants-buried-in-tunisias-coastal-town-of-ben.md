8 migrants buried in Tunisia’s coastal town of Ben Guerdane

Residents of Tunisia’s coastal town of Ben Guerdane on Wednesday attended the funerals of eight young migrants who drowned after their boat sank. They were among 15 people aboard a small vessel attempting to reach Europe.
