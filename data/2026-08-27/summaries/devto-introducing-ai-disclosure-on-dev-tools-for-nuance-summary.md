# Introducing AI Disclosure on DEV: Tools for Nuance, Clarity, and Better Feeds

## Overview
- DEV is updating its AI guidelines to help authentic content rise to the top.  
- New features will give more weight to human curation by community leaders.  
- AI tools may be used, and bots may post, provided there is no deception and appropriate disclosure tools are available.

## AI Disclosure Feature
- Disclosure has been part of DEV’s AI policy since its inception in 2024.  
- Authors can now select one of three explicit disclosure tiers when creating or editing an article:  
  1. **Hand Written (No AI)** – entirely human‑written, no meaningful AI assistance.  
  2. **AI‑Assisted (Some AI)** – human author used AI for drafting, code generation, major edits, translations, etc.  
  3. **Fully Autonomous** – content generated primarily or entirely by automated agents or LLMs.

## Guiding Principles
- **Helpful assistance is normal:** Using an LLM for brainstorming, debugging, boilerplate, or outlining is accepted; disclose as AI‑Assisted and retain full accountability.  
- **Transparent bots add value:** Automated summaries of security alerts, release logs, or benchmarks are useful when clearly disclosed; undisclosed autonomy is considered deceptive.  
- Disclosure does **not** override core community standards; quality, insight, and human accountability remain essential.

## Authorship Rules
- Deception is prohibited: publishing low‑effort, undisclosed AI‑generated content or presenting synthetic output as personal experience may lead to account suspension.  
- The rollout is progressive: the tool introduces clear expectations first, with moderation enforcement evolving over time.

## Upcoming Reader Controls
- **Feed Preferences** will let users customize their reading experience, including options to reduce or filter AI‑Assisted or Fully Autonomous posts.  
- “AI Content in Feed” can be toggled in user settings; the implementation is open source via the Forem codebase.

## Call to Action
- Community members are encouraged to experiment with the new disclosure tiers, provide feedback, and help shape a transparent, high‑quality DEV feed.
