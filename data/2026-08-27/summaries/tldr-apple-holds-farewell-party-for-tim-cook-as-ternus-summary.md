## Key Event Details
- Tim Cook will step down as Apple’s CEO on **Tuesday, September 1, 2026**.

- In his new role, Cook will **spend less time in the spotlight**.  
- **John Ternus** – at Apple since 2001 and senior vice president of hardware engineering since 2021 – will assume the CEO role.  
- Ternus’s first major product launch as CEO will be the **September iPhone event**.
