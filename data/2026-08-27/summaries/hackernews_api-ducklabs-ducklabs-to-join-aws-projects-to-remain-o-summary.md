# DuckLabs – DuckLabs to Join AWS, Projects to Remain Open Source

# Summary of DuckLabs Joining AWS

## Announcement Overview
- DuckLabs will become part of Amazon Web Services (AWS) in early September.  
- The entire DuckLabs team stays together in Amsterdam.  
- Work on DuckDB, DuckLake, Quack, and the broader “Duck Stack” continues.  
- All open‑source components remain free under the MIT license, overseen by the nonprofit DuckDB Foundation.

## Journey and Motivation
- Founded a little over five years ago as a bootstrapped, founder‑owned company to give DuckDB a stable home.  
- Grew to more than 30 people while keeping focus on technology and community.  
- DuckDB now sees over one million daily downloads, used for data exploration, product powering, teaching, research, and unexpected systems.  
- The existing model risked becoming a bottleneck for scaling support, sales, and operations, potentially diverting attention from core technical work.  
- Reaching a broader, less technical user base requires larger infrastructure, industry‑specific solutions, and deeper resources.

## Why AWS
- DuckLabs and AWS have collaborated for over a year, establishing complementary strengths.  
- AWS provides infrastructure, scale, and customer reach that enable DuckLabs to focus on technical innovation at a larger scale.  
- AWS has pledged long‑term support for DuckDB and its community.  
- Quote from Andy Warfield (AWS VP) highlights the technical depth of the DuckLabs team and the anticipated broader impact.

## What Remains Unchanged
- DuckDB, DuckLake, Quack, and all Duck Stack projects stay open source under the MIT license.  
- The DuckDB Foundation continues to steward the projects and protect community trust.  
- The DuckLabs team remains in Amsterdam and will keep contributing to the open‑source ecosystem.  
- Community governance and openness stay central to the project’s future.

## Community and Governance
- Statements from Peter Boncz (CWI/DuckDB Foundation) and Torsten Grust (Universität Tübingen) reaffirm commitment to open‑source principles and community voice.  
- The DuckDB Foundation will maintain IP ownership and ensure community members are heard.

## Future Plans
- Leverage AWS resources to invest more in technology and community growth.  
- Establish a technical advisory board within the DuckDB Foundation for community input on direction.  
- Open the extension stack, allowing signed extensions from external developers and organizations to run in DuckDB.  
- Additional details will be shared as plans mature.
