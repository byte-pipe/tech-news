# Bitcoin miner MARA sold $1.5 billion of bitcoin as it shifts toward AI infrastructure

## Key takeaways
- First‑quarter revenue fell 18% to $174.6 million; net loss widened to $1.3 billion, mainly from unrealized losses on 38,689 bitcoin.
- MARA sold roughly $1.5 billion of bitcoin during the quarter, including a $1.1 billion sale to fund a convertible‑note repurchase, dropping from the second‑ to the fourth‑largest public bitcoin treasury holder.
- The company is pivoting from pure mining growth to leveraging its power assets for AI and high‑performance computing, aiming to allocate up to 90 % of non‑hosted mining capacity to such workloads.
- A planned $1.5 billion acquisition of the Long Ridge Energy & Power campus in Ohio would add a gas‑fired power plant and data‑center facilities capable of supporting more than 600 MW of AI load.
- Mining activity still grew: hash rate rose 33 % YoY to 72.2 EH/s and MARA mined 2,247 bitcoin in the quarter, up from 2,011 in the prior quarter.
