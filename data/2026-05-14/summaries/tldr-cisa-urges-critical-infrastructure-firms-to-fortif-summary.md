# CISA urges critical infrastructure firms to ‘fortify’ before it’s too late

## Background and Motivation
- CISA released new guidance as part of the international “CI Fortify” initiative.
- The guidance responds to intelligence warnings that China may sabotage Western critical infrastructure ahead of a possible invasion of Taiwan.
- Recent Chinese “Volt Typhoon” hacking activity has heightened concerns about operational‑technology (OT) interdependencies.

## Isolation Objectives
- Operators should assume third‑party connections (telecom, internet, vendors, upstream services) will become unreliable in a conflict.
- Identify “critical customers” (e.g., nearby military bases) and define service‑delivery expectations for them.
- Determine which OT assets are essential to support those customers.
- Maintain up‑to‑date business continuity plans and engineering processes that enable safe isolated operation for weeks to months.

## Recovery Objectives
- Document normal system operation and create detailed recovery procedures.
- Perform regular backups of critical data and system configurations.
- Practice replacing compromised systems or transitioning to manual processes if isolation fails.
- Ensure plans cover scenarios where components become inoperable.

## Recommendations for the Wider Ecosystem
- **Equipment vendors:** remove technical barriers that hinder isolation and recovery efforts.
- **Managed service providers and integrators:** assist operators with engineering, planning, and implementation of isolation/recovery measures.
- Operators are urged to discuss CISA’s advice with their vendors to map communications dependencies and identify workarounds.

## Direct Assistance from CISA
- CISA will conduct “targeted assessments” of participating operators to evaluate resilience, especially the ability to operate in isolation.
- A pilot phase of assessments has already begun.
- Regional CISA offices will lead the assessment work, though they have faced staffing reductions.

## Staffing and Implementation Outlook
- The agency has requested and received approval to hire 329 new employees to fill critical vacancies.
- Regional teams are a high priority in the hiring plan.
- Assessment focus will vary by sector: energy and transportation may prioritize selective customer supply, while water utilities have different operational constraints.

## Key Takeaway
CISA’s CI Fortify guidance emphasizes proactive isolation and recovery planning, collaboration with vendors, and direct agency support to ensure critical infrastructure can continue delivering essential services even under severe cyber‑attack or geopolitical crisis conditions.
