# Amazon launches Alexa for Shopping AI assistant

## Overview
- Amazon introduced “Alexa for Shopping,” a personalized AI shopping assistant powered by Alexa+.
- The new assistant replaces the 2024 generative AI tool Rufus.
- It is designed for voice and touch interactions on mobile, desktop, and Echo Show devices.

## Key Features
- Answers a wide range of queries, from product recommendations (“good skincare routine for men”) to personal order history (“when did I last order AA batteries?”).
- Users can type questions in the main search bar or a dedicated chat window.
- Provides tailored answers, custom shopping guides, and learns from habits, preferences, and purchase history.
- Can compare products, track prices, and schedule recurring orders for essentials.
- Supports conditional cart actions, e.g., “Add this sunscreen to my cart if the price drops to $10.”
- Includes a “Buy for Me” feature that can purchase items from Amazon and other online retailers on the user’s behalf.

## Comparison to Rufus
- Rufus focused on product discovery and comparison.
- Alexa for Shopping emphasizes deeper personalization and automation across multiple retailers.

## Availability & Integration
- Currently available to U.S. customers.
- Integrated with Amazon’s recent 30‑minute delivery service “Amazon Now” and a new AI‑generated real‑time audio response feature.

## Potential Concerns
- The “Buy for Me” capability raises questions about AI autonomy and privacy.
- Broader industry worries about how much control AI assistants have over purchasing decisions.
