# Amex ups marketing, tech spend

## Dive Brief
- American Express will raise marketing expenses by 10% in the second half of 2026 compared with 2025, and increase technology spending, though the exact amount was not disclosed.  
- CEO Steve Squeri said the company has a large backlog of technology projects and is accelerating their delivery.  
- The marketing increase follows a 6% year‑over‑year rise in the second quarter.

## Dive Insight
- In Q2, Amex added 3 million new card members (down slightly from 3.1 million in Q1), with 65% of them being Millennials or Gen Z.  
- About 60% of consumer deposit accounts are held by customers in those age groups.  
- The company targets growth in the U.S., Mexico, Japan, Canada, Australia and the U.K.  
- Marketing strategy relies on high‑profile sponsorships (NFL, Formula One, Wimbledon, Fanatics) and exclusive experiences for cardholders.  
- Recent acquisitions include three restaurant‑reservation firms; a $700 million purchase of TheFork for European expansion is pending.  
- Q2 net income rose 8% to $3.11 billion; revenue (excluding interest expense) grew 10% to $19.6 billion.  
- Consumer card volume in the U.S. grew 11% YoY, while commercial card volume grew only 5%; competition from Brex (now owned by Capital One), Ramp and Marqeta pressures the midsize‑business segment.  
- Amex launched a new expense‑management system for midsize businesses to improve commercial‑card retention.  
- Technology upgrades span multiple platforms; the company is pulling forward investments to the second half of the year.  
- Full‑year revenue outlook is now at the high end of the prior 9‑10% growth guidance, with profit outlook unchanged.
