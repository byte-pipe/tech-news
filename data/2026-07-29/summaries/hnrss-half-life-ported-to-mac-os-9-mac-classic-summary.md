# Half-Life ported to Mac OS 9

## Overview
- Half-Life, the story‑driven first‑person shooter starring Gordon Freeman, is now available for PowerPC‑based Macintosh computers, 28 years after its original launch.
- The game follows Freeman’s attempt to survive and escape the Black Mesa Research Facility after a disastrous experiment.

## Development and Release
- Originally slated for a Mac OS 9 release by Valve in 1999, the project was cancelled shortly before launch.
- Valve later brought Half‑Life to Mac OS X in 2013, during the Intel CPU era.
- The new Mac OS 9 port was created by GitHub user **doctashay** using a fork of Xash3D FWGS, a re‑implementation of the GoldSrc engine.
- The port is fully playable from start to finish, includes multiplayer, a demo of Uplink, and downloads for Blue Shift and Opposing Force.

## Features and Compatibility
- Supports G3 and G4 PowerPC Macs running Mac OS 9.0 or later.
- Performance varies with GPU; machines with less than 8 MB VRAM (e.g., some iMacs, iBooks) may experience struggles.
- Included titles:
  - Half‑Life
  - Half‑Life: Blue Shift
  - Half‑Life: Opposing Force
