We went looking for good posts by women on DEV. Not "inspirational," not "diverse" but just genuinely good, the kind you'd send to a friend with "okay you have to read this one" attached. Here are eight, and the honest reasons each one earns your attention.

This is what a good tangent looks like. Anna was seven hours into an unrelated Node.js tutorial when the thought hit her sideways: what if she scraped her own DEV followers, pulled their public GitHub data via GraphQL, and actually looked at who was reading her stuff?

There wasn't a plug-and-play tool for this. So she built one — a scraper, a JSON export, a chart at the end. What makes the post worth reading isn't the follower-count gimmick, it's that she's honest about the process being genuinely a little annoying to figure out. "There isn't a plug-and-play solution for this, you have to be a tiny bit creative," she writes. That's the whole game, most days.
