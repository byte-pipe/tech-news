# Join the Kaggle Benchmarking Challenge: $2,500 in Prizes for Five Winners!

## Overview
- Run from September 23 to October 11.  
- Participants build a custom benchmark on Kaggle, evaluate it on a suite of models, and share their findings.  
- The goal is to test real‑world capabilities of today’s multi‑step reasoning, code‑generation, tool‑use, and vision models.

## How to Participate
- Create a benchmark on Kaggle (tasks can cover reasoning, coding, image recognition, instruction following, or any failure mode you’re curious about).  
- Publish a DEV post using the provided submission template and include the tag **#kagglechallenge**.  
- Only one submission per participant is allowed.

## Submission Requirements
- Explain the task(s) you benchmarked and why they interest you.  
- List the models you evaluated and justify their selection.  
- Summarize the main insights, surprises, and next steps for measurement.  
- Provide a direct link to your benchmark on Kaggle.  
- The post should go beyond raw numbers and discuss the meaning of the results.

## Judging Criteria
- **Insights Shared** – Do the findings reveal real behavior of the models?  
- **Writing Quality** – Is the post clear, well‑structured, and engaging?  
- **Creativity in Approach** – Is the benchmark an original or clever way to measure capability?  
- A link to the Kaggle benchmark is mandatory for eligibility.

## Prizes
- Five winners receive:  
  - $500 USD cash prize each  
  - DEV++ Membership  
  - Exclusive DEV Badge  
- All participants with a valid submission earn a completion badge on their DEV profile.

## Getting Started with Kaggle Benchmarks
1. **Create a task** – Define a specific problem for AI models (e.g., multi‑step reasoning, code generation).  
2. **Build a benchmark** – Group tasks and run them across selected models.  
3. **Check the leaderboard** – Compare model performance and share results.  
- You can also create tasks locally using the Kaggle CLI.  
- Helpful resources: Kaggle Benchmarks documentation and the `kaggle-benchmarks` Python library on GitHub.

## Important Dates
- **September 23** – Challenge begins.  
- **October 11, 11:59 PM PDT** – Submission deadline.  
- **November 5** – Winners announced.
