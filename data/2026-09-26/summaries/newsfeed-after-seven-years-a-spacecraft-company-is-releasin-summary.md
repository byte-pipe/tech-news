# After seven years, a spacecraft company is releasing its Otters into the wild

## Background and founding
- Starfish Space was founded in October 2019 by former Blue Origin engineers Trevor Bennett and Austin Link.  
- The company aims to manage the growing congestion of satellites in low‑Earth orbit and to extend the life of assets in orbit.  
- Two primary business cases were identified: extending the life of large geostationary satellites and providing services for low‑Earth‑orbit constellations.  
- The vehicle designed to meet these needs is called “Otter.”

## Otter spacecraft and the upcoming NASA mission
- The first operational Otter will launch in the fall on a Falcon 9 Transporter mission.  
- Size: comparable to a large washing machine; mass: about 350 kg.  
- Mission name: SSPICY (Small Spacecraft Propulsion and Inspection Capability), funded by NASA with a $15 million contract.  
- Objectives: rendezvous with multiple US‑owned defunct satellites, approach within hundreds of meters, and gather data on spin rates and condition.  
- After the inspection phase, the remaining life of the Otter may be used for additional tasks.

## Company growth and previous demonstrations
- Starfish Space now employs roughly 120 people and has raised about $150 million.  
- The spacecraft bus is sourced from Astro Digital; the company focuses on software, guidance, navigation, and control systems for precise rendezvous and docking.  
- 2023: Otter Pup 1 launch experienced a failure, resulting in premature ejection and later thruster loss.  
- 2025: Otter Pup 2 achieved close proximity (within 1 km) to a target spacecraft, with a docking expected in the coming months.  
- Partnership with Impulse Space demonstrated autonomous rendezvous of the Mira spacecraft using Starfish’s lightweight camera and closed‑loop software.

## Future plans and outlook
- Five additional Otters are in production for missions showcasing docking, de‑orbit, and life‑extension capabilities.  
- Bennett describes a mix of nervousness and excitement, emphasizing the significance of introducing a new orbital capability and the collective effort of the team and customers.
