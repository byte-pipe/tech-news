## Lab Vision
- An independent research lab focused on tools that amplify human intelligence.
- Goal: a new computer that clarifies thinking, improves collaboration, and is universally accessible.

## Research Areas
- **Local‑first Software:** Architecture that returns data to users and enables collaboration in every tool.
- **Malleable Software:** Environments that let people customize tools on the fly.
- **Programmable Ink:** A dynamic medium where sketching behaves like programmable spreadsheets.
- **Universal Version Control:** Tools for exploring alternatives, tracking history, and collaborating across media.
