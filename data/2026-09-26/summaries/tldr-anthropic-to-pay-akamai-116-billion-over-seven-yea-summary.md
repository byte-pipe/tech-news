# Anthropic to pay Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years in cloud deal

## Deal overview
- Anthropic will commit $11.6 billion to use Akamai’s cloud infrastructure over a seven‑year period.  
- The amount is more than six times larger than the $1.8 billion deal reported in May.  
- The agreement is conditional: Akamai must meet delivery and service‑availability requirements, and either party can terminate under specified conditions.  

## Financial impact for Akamai
- Largest contract in Akamai’s history.  
- Expected revenue from the deal: $150 million–$300 million in 2027, rising to an annual pace of about $1.7 billion by the end of 2028.  
- Akamai plans to invest roughly $5.5 billion to build the required capacity and is adding $1.7 billion to this year’s capital spending for advance component purchases (e.g., memory).  

## AI infrastructure focus
- The deal highlights growing demand for CPUs, the general‑purpose chips used for running code and browsing, as AI agents take on more tasks.  
- Akamai did not disclose the specific workloads Anthropic will run on its CPUs.  

## Warrant component
- Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for non‑voting preferred stock convertible into 7.7 million common shares (about 5 % of Anthropic’s outstanding stock) at $111.33 per share.  
- Approximately 2 % of the warrant vests after Anthropic’s first payment; additional vesting is tied to spending milestones (each extra $3 billion of spend unlocks roughly another 1 %).  
- If Anthropic’s spending reaches the maximum, the deal could expand to roughly $20 billion in total value.  

## Market reaction and context
- Akamai’s shares rose as much as 17 % in after‑hours trading following the announcement.  
- This is the first Akamai cloud deal to include a warrant, mirroring similar structures used by AMD with OpenAI.  
- Anthropic has previously received investments or agreements from Amazon, Google, Microsoft, and AMD, though it notes its participation is not at the same scale as some other AI players.
