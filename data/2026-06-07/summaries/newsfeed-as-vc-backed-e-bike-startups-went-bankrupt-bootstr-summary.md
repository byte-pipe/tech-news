# Summary of “As VC‑backed e‑bike startups went bankrupt, bootstrapped Lectric grew”

## Market context
- The e‑bike sector has seen a wave of bankruptcies, including high‑profile failures such as Rad Power Bikes, which filed Chapter 11 after raising $330 M in VC funding.
- Many competitors have folded or been acquired, leaving a perceived gap in the market for strong, affordable players.

## Lectric’s overall strategy
- Lectric eBikes, a Phoenix‑based bootstrapped company, has invested about $10 M in expanding its portfolio despite the downturn.
- CEO Levi Conlow emphasizes that the market is not saturated; Lectric recorded its biggest sales month, moving nearly 30,000 bikes.
- The company follows a “bootstrap, stay profitable, let better‑funded rivals implode, then expand” playbook.

## Brand separation to avoid dilution
- Lectric sells ~90 % of its products direct‑to‑consumer via its website, which draws 2–4 M monthly visitors.
- To prevent brand dilution, each new line operates under a distinct brand with its own engineering, marketing, and customer‑service teams.
- Conlow wants the brands to compete with one another rather than appear identical.

## New brands launched in 2025‑2026
- **Juiced Bikes** – acquired in 2025 and relaunched; targets mainstream e‑bike buyers.
- **Juiced Powersports** – will ship its first e‑moto in August 2026.
- **Monarc** – spun out as a premium adventure brand based in Minnesota, led by Julia Moran and Ryan Callahan.
  - First product, the “Marker” all‑terrain trail bike, ships July 2026.
  - Features include two LG 48 V 15 Ah batteries (720 Wh each), Bafang motor, Shimano drivetrain, 3.5‑inch touchscreen, and a five‑year warranty with live phone support (no AI).

## Operational details
- Monarc and each Juiced brand have small teams (10 and 8 employees respectively) but share Lectric’s supply‑chain, purchasing power, and back‑end support.
- Lectric overall employs about 170 people.

## Outlook
- Conlow says the company will continue scouting new opportunities but is not in a rush, preferring to focus on the current portfolio before adding more independent brands.
