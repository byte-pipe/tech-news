# CompTIA debuts AutoOps+ certification  

## Overview  
- CompTIA introduced AutoOps+, a vendor‑neutral certification and training program.  
- Designed to validate automation, scripting, infrastructure‑as‑code, and DevOps skills required in modern IT operations environments.  

## Target Audience and Prerequisites  
- Intended for systems administrators, cloud administrators, infrastructure engineers, DevOps professionals, site reliability engineers, and other IT operations specialists.  
- Recommended: foundational IT knowledge, 2–3 years of experience in a core IT operations role, and hands‑on experience with systems administration, cloud technologies, networking, and scripting.  

## Core Topics Covered  
- Automation fundamentals and scripting.  
- Version control and infrastructure‑as‑code.  
- CI/CD pipeline creation and management.  
- Observability and broader DevOps practices.  
- Secure, compliant delivery pipelines for rapid and reliable deployments.  

## Skills Gained  
- Use scripting and automation to streamline IT workflows and manage enterprise systems efficiently.  
- Automate configuration, provisioning, and maintenance with modern toolchains and IaC.  
- Build and manage CI pipelines to automate testing, ensure quality, and improve team collaboration.  
- Implement secure, compliant delivery pipelines that enable rapid, reliable deployment and operational efficiency.  

## Availability  
- The AutoOps+ exam is available now.  

## Context and Rationale  
- Developed in response to growing demand for automation expertise as organizations adopt infrastructure‑as‑code, continuous integration, continuous delivery, and other automated operational processes.  
- Katie Hoenicke, Chief Product Officer at CompTIA, emphasized that the ability to lead digital transformation, simplify complex environments, and operationalize automation is essential for today’s technology workforce.
