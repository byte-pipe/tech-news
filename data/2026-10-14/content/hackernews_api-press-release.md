# Press Release

## An Important Update About Grace Hopper Celebration

It is with deep regret that we are announcing the cancellation of this year’s Grace Hopper Celebration in Anaheim, CA.

This is not a decision we made lightly; we understand it leaves many of our community disappointed. It is a function of not having the funds required to move forward with the Celebration, unlike in years past. Unlike past events, this year, both individual registrations and sponsorship commitments came in significantly below our expectations and, ultimately, below the level needed to cover critical operating expenses.

We know this decision affects far more than an event. Many of you have made travel arrangements, booked hotels, received visas, secured time away from work, organized teams, and planned important moments around being together. We are deeply sorry for the disruption this creates.

If you have plans to attend GHC, please take the following steps now:

* Cancel or modify your airline, train, or other travel reservations directly with your travel provider.
* Cancel hotel reservations made independently directly with the hotel or booking provider, and any other reservations or commitments connected to your trip.
* Information regarding GHC registration, sponsorships, exhibitor commitments, and other financial matters will be communicated directly to the individuals and organizations affected. AnitaB.org will contact registrants about registration fees by October 26. We recognize that each person’s circumstances may be different.
* For questions or assistance specifically related to the cancellation, please contact [GHC@AnitaB.org].We ask that you use this address so we can centralize inquiries and respond as effectively as possible.

To our attendees, sponsors, speakers, volunteers, partners, and everyone who has invested in making Grace Hopper Celebration possible:we are profoundly grateful, and profoundly sorry.

GHC has always been about more than a place or a moment on the calendar. It is about a community that has spent decades opening doors, expanding opportunity, and making sure women technologists have a voice in shaping the future of technology.

GHC has been central to our mission, and the circumstances leading to its cancellation have adversely affected our finances. We are evaluating strategic alternatives and the type of programming we can offer in the future. I anticipate further announcements will be made in the coming weeks as we gain greater clarity on key variables.

Brenda Darden Wilkerson

President & CEO

AnitaB.org

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why was this year’s Grace Hopper Celebration cancelled?

AnitaB.org has determined that it will not have the funds required to pay the costs of producing the Celebration. This year, both individual registrations and sponsorship commitments came in significantly below prior years and, ultimately, below the level required to cover basic operating expenses.

### What happens to my in-person or virtual registration fee?

Information regarding GHC registration and other financial matters will be communicated to you promptly once we obtain a clearer understanding of AnitaB.org ’s financial condition.

### Do I need to do anything right now?

We encourage you to review cancellation and refund eligibility with your travel and accommodation provider. Many providers offer more flexibility the earlier a change is requested.

### My visa was approved for this trip. What does cancellation mean for me?

AnitaB.org is unable to advise on immigration matters. Please consult a qualified immigration attorney or your sponsoring institution.

### I had interviews scheduled through the career fair. What now?

Employers who had scheduled interviews are being notified, and we are asking them to continue those conversations directly where possible.

### We are a sponsor or exhibitor. Who should we contact?

Your AnitaB.orgcontact is reaching out directly. Information regarding sponsorships, exhibitor commitments, and related financial matters will be communicated to the organizations affected. For anything urgent, please useGHC@AnitaB.org.

### How will I hear about updates, and when is the next one?

Updates will be posted on the GHC website, the most recent updates will appear at the top of the Press Release page.

### Who do I contact?

Please useGHC@AnitaB.org, for anything related to the cancellation. Using one address allows us to centralize inquiries and respond as effectively as possible.

### Is Grace Hopper Celebration India still taking place?

Yes, Grace Hopper Celebration India (GHCI) is still taking place in Bangalore, India, January 20-22, 2027. Learn more here:GHCI 27 - AnitaB.org India.

We also encourage corporate sponsors to consider GHCI sponsorship inclusive of bulk registration opportunities to send your teams. Emailcorporate@anitabindia.orgto learn more.

### I uploaded my resume through the GHC 26 platform. Will participating companies still have access to it?

Yes. If you uploaded your resume through the GHC 26 Resume Hub, participating companies will continue to have access to your submitted resume. This service remains available to help connect attendees with potential career opportunities.
