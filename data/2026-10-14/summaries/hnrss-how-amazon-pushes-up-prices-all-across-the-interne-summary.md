## The Story Behind the Screens

Amazon's pricing strategy is built into its business model, with fees that sellers must pay to reach Amazon's online marketplace. The basic commission in most categories is around 19% of sales, but for some sellers, it's as high as 50% or more. To understand how this works, let's follow the money behind each sale.

## Amazon's Fees: A Delicate Dance

*   Referral fee: the commission Amazon takes on every sale, which is mandatory for sellers.
*   Fulfillment fee: the cost of packing and shipping items from Amazon's warehouses.
*   Advertising fee: the sponsored spots in the top row seen in the search results.

## Sellers' Real Choice: Price or Profit

When sellers choose to work with Amazon, they're forced to accept the terms and conditions set by Amazon, including the fees. By raising prices, sellers must make up for the costs of sales, shipping, and other expenses. The FTC's Fair Pricing policy allows Amazon to remove the Buy Box and suspend sellers who try to undercut prices elsewhere.

## In conclusion, Amazon's pricing strategy is built into its business model, with fees that sellers must pay to reach Amazon's online marketplace. Sellers' real choice is between making sales and raising prices, which forces them to pass the costs on to customers.
