# Apple Unveils HomeView: A Smart Home Display Powered by Siri AI - MacStories

**Apple Unveils HomeView: A Smart Home Display Powered by Siri AI**

**Key Points:**

* Apple has unveiled the HomeView, a new smart home display that integrates with Siri AI and HomeKit devices.
* The HomeView comes in two configurations: a flatSurface model, priced at $299, and a wallMounted model, priced at $79.95, which lacks the base and woofer of the flatSurface model.
* The HomeView features a 7-inch Liquid Retina True Tone display, a downward-firing woofer, and a force-cancelling passive radiator in both models.
* The device supports Spatial Audio and integrates with various Apple apps and services, including Apple Music, calendar, Reminders, Apple Podcasts, and Photos.
* HomeView is powered by an A18 chip with Secure Exclaves for audio and video processing, and requires a paired iPhone or iPad to function.

**Hardware Configuration:**

* FlatSurface model: domed aluminum base, 7-inch Liquid Retina True Tone display, 2 drivers, and 2 force-cancelling passive radiators.
* WallMounted model: base, 7-inch Liquid Retina True Tone display, and 2 drivers.

**Features:**

* Customizable faces with swipe gestures, including photos, weather, clock, and widgets
* Integration with Apple apps and services
* Support for HomeKit-compatible devices
* Siri AI navigation and interaction
* Integration with audio and video apps from third-party developers

**Availability:**

* FlatSurface model: preorder available now in Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore, and the UK.
* WallMounted model: preorder available now in the US and UK; Power Mount option available for US consumers in early 2027.

**Technical Details:**

* HomeView comes with a USB-C Wall Connector and AC Wired option for wall mounting.
* The HomeView features two Force Touch Trackpads for gestures; the flatSurface model has a single Force Touch Trackpad.
* The device is powered by an A18 chip with Secure Exclaves for audio and video processing; Messages and Calendar content are only available when a user is recognized and their paired iPhone or iPad is present and unlocked.
