# Bumble agrees to a $1.3B acquisition deal as online dating faces a reset | TechCrunch

**Bumble to Become Private after $1.3 Billion Acquisition**

Bumble, the online dating app founded by Whitney Wolfe Herd in 2014, has agreed to a $1.3 billion acquisition deal with Ithaca Holdings, marking a significant shift for the company.

**Key points:**

- Bumble will cease to be a publicly traded company after the acquisition, giving it more flexibility to experiment with new features and make changes.
- The company plans to explore AI-driven features and offline connections, reflecting a broader shift in how users approach dating.
- Ithaca Holdings, a private investment firm, will become the sole owner of Bumble, with Ithaca Holding's Ravi Gupta and John Hegeman leading the business.

**Major shift for Bumble:**

- The acquisition deal is part of Bumble's efforts to adapt to a changing market, as the company faces declining subscriber numbers, weaker revenue, and mounting pressure to rethink its online dating experience.

**Impact on Bumble's future:**

- The deal may give Bumble a chance to work on its next chapter, away from the pressure of public markets.
- This could provide Bumble with the opportunity to focus on its core mission of creating a platform where women are in control and have the freedom to make the first move.
