# Announcing the Databricks storage ecosystem: Governing the enterprise data estate, wherever it lives | Databricks Blog

**Databricks Storage Ecosystem: Natively Connecting Hybrid Environments**

The Databricks Storage Ecosystem connects hybrid and on-premises storage platforms with Databricks using the OpenSharing protocol. This enables centralized data governance, allowing enterprises to scale GenAI across their entire hybrid infrastructure.

**Key Achievements:**

* Zero-copy architecture allows for instant data activation
* Enterprise can run Databricks Serverless Compute, Genie, and LLMs directly on on-premises datasets without copying a single file

## Data Management Challenges

Some of the world's most sophisticated enterprises face unique challenges when managing their diverse data:

* Difficulty in migrating datasets to cloud due to regulatory requirements
* Economic constraints of large-scale egress fees and storage costs
* Struggle with low-latency access to low-level edge workloads

Meanwhile, industry leaders seek a more structured approach towards managing their large-scale datasets, often adopting strategies such as:

* Hybrid Forever: Merging on-premises and hybrid environments
* "Govern Everything": Managing all data stakeholders' requirements in-house
* Tier-specific models for optimized cloud-to-onpremises traffic
