### Trump Privacy Restrictions May Reduce Census Bureau Data

The Trump administration has issued an order limiting the ways in which the Census Bureau can protect people's privacy when releasing statistics. This may result in a reduction of data from the bureau, including key statistics used for redistricting and policymaking.

**Impact on Key Statistics:**

* Redistricting statistics
* Policy-making statistics
* Research statistics

**New Policy Options:**

* Coarsened statistics with fewer details or no release at all
* Releases some statistics without coarsening

**Concerns from Data Experts:**

* Neighborhood-level data may not be publishable, especially in rural areas
* Data for very small counties may also be unpublished

**Consequences for Census Bureau and Other Agencies:**

* Reduction of data availability for key purposes (e.g., redistricting)
* Potential loss of access to useful statistics for research and policymakers
