# All about Electric Motors with No Rare Earths by Renault Group

* **Introduction**: The article provides an overview of electric motors developed by Renault Group that do not rely on rare earth elements (REE) like magnets.
 
## Overview of Electric Motor Technologies

### Permanent-Magnet Synchronous Motors
- Requires no pistons or cylinders
- Uses electrical energy to transfer motion through a rotating stator generating a magnetic field

### Asynchronous Motors/Inverted Synchronous Motor (ISM)
- Used on front axles for secondary motors

### Electrically Excited Synchronous Motors (EESM)
- Delivers high efficiency without rare earths
- Uses electrical excitation to generate the magnetic field in these motor designs

## Renault Group's Range of All-Electric Motors

Renault Group, a pioneer in electric car technology since 2011, has invested heavily in developing EESM technology.

* **First Generation**: Found under the bonnet of vehicles like Renault Kangoo Z.E and Renault Zoe
