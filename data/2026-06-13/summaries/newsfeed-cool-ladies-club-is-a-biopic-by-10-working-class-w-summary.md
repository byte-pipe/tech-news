# 'Cool Ladies Club' is a biopic by 10 working-class women : NPR

# Summary

* A group of 10 working-class women from India's Mumbai region came together to make a documentary about their unseen lives.
* They were given smartphones as part of an initiative, which helped them film their experiences as domestic workers, community health workers, toilet operators, and home caregivers.
* The women, who had little experience with filmmaking, learned by watching Shilpi Gulati's filmmaking style and instructions on how to use a camera.
* They were encouraged not to worry about filming "correctly" but rather just enjoy the process of creating a documentary.
* The initial goal was to showcase their lives, while also celebrating their creativity and sense of humor.
