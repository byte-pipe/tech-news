# An AI agent startup just let its agent run its $100 million fundraise

## Overview
- Lyzr, a three‑year‑old AI‑agent platform based in Jersey City, used its own AI agent, **SivaClaw**, to lead its $100 million Series B round.
- The round valued the company at roughly $500 million and attracted about $400 million in investor interest.

## How the AI agent handled the raise
- SivaClaw fielded questions from more than 130 investors, drafted investment memoranda, and tracked which presentation slides captured the most attention.
- The AI acted as the primary point of contact, eliminating the need for founders to travel for traditional “coffee meetings” and warm introductions.

## Investor mix
- Capital came from Silicon Valley, Middle‑East, and financial‑sector investors.
- The process required minimal manual legwork from Lyzr’s founders.

## Significance
- Demonstrates that Lyzr’s product works in a real‑world, high‑stakes scenario.
- Highlights the abundance of capital chasing AI opportunities, allowing founders with traction to raise large sums without extensive outbound effort.
