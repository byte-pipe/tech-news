# Amseatec Criss Cross Office Chair Review: Just Right | WIRED  

## Overview  
- The author needed a chair that accommodates cross‑legged, side‑sitting, and tucked‑leg positions due to a past hamstring injury and chronic sciatica.  
- After trying meditation and kneeling chairs, the Amseatec Criss‑Cross model was tested as a potential “unicorn” desk chair.  

## Key Features  
- Seat width: ~25 inches (vs. typical 18‑20 inches).  
- Five‑position fold‑down armrests that click into place.  
- Memory‑foam top layer over a firm base; tall backrest.  
- Faux‑leather upholstery marketed as pet‑scratch‑resistant; easy to clean.  
- Swivel base with casters that roll on hardwood and carpet.  

## Pros  
- Extra‑wide seat provides ample room for cross‑legged, sideways, or curled‑up sitting without needing to fold armrests each time.  
- Fold‑down armrests allow multiple postures and can be collapsed for a lounge‑like feel.  
- Memory‑foam cushion remains supportive after weeks of daily use.  
- Sturdy frame; no wobble or creaking after a month of varied use.  
- Easy assembly (≈20 minutes) and smooth rolling on different floor types.  
- Upholstery held up against toddler and pet contact; stains wipe away with a damp cloth.  

## Cons  
- Faux leather can become warm, especially in short‑sleeved attire or warm rooms.  
- Armrest clicks are loud, which may be distracting on important calls.  
- Armrests are set wider apart than many users prefer and lack height adjustment.  
- When fully extended, armrests may not fit under fixed‑height desks; lowering the seat or collapsing the arms can mitigate but may reduce armrest usability.  

## Personal Experience  
- The author, 5’2”, could sit cross‑legged, tuck a leg under, sit sideways, or curl up with knees bent, all comfortably.  
- Frequently switched between upright typing, cross‑legged meetings, and relaxed reading by adjusting armrests.  
- The chair’s comfort encouraged occasional lounging after work, indicating high satisfaction.  

## Verdict  
- Not a flawless chair, but its generous seat width, adaptable armrests, and supportive cushioning make it a strong option for users who need flexible sitting positions.  
- Best suited for desks with enough clearance for the extended armrests or for users with adjustable‑height desks.
