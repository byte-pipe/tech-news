# Arne Slot sacking completes a remarkable fall from grace – BBC Sport

## Context and comparison with other title‑winning managers
- Slot was dismissed a year after leading Liverpool to the Premier League title, joining a short list of champions such as Carlo Ancelotti, José Mourinho, Antonio Conte and Claudio Ranieri who were later sacked.
- Unlike those cases, Liverpool had been seen as a “bastion of stability,” making Slot’s fall appear especially striking.

## Slot’s achievements in his first season
- Took over from Jurgen Klopp with only a £10 million addition (Federico Chiesa) and won the league by 10 points, losing just two games.
- Introduced tactical tweaks that reduced Klopp’s “thrilling chaos” but created a more organised, cohesive side.
- Promoted Ryan Gravenberch to replace the missed target Martin Zubimendi, resulting in a strong midfield platform.
- Re‑positioned Luis Diaz as a striker, yielding several match‑winning performances.
- Secured Mohamed Salah’s best season statistically, although the relationship later soured and Salah announced his final year at Liverpool.

## Summer of celebration turned tragedy
- The title parade was marred by a car‑ramming incident that injured over 130 people; the driver was sentenced to 21 years.
- Striker Diogo Jota and his brother Andre Silva died in a separate car crash on 3 July, leading to profound grief within the club and fanbase.

## The £450 million transfer spree and its impact
- Liverpool spent heavily on new players: Alexander Isak (£125 m), Florian Wirtz (£116 m), Hugo Ekitike (£70 m), Milos Kerkez (£70 m) and Jeremie Frimpong (£70 m).
- Generated over £250 m from sales of Darwin Núñez, Luis Diaz, Jarell Quansah and the departure of Trent Alexander‑Arnold to Real Madrid.
- Despite the outlay, many signings underperformed or suffered injuries, leaving the squad “mediocre and unbalanced.”
- The failure of the recruitment strategy is attributed both to Slot’s decisions and to the club’s sporting hierarchy (Fenway Sports Group, Michael Edwards, Richard Hughes).

## Possible reasons for Slot’s dismissal
- Perception that the title was lost due to an ineffective spending spree and squad imbalance.
- Deteriorating relationship with key players, notably Salah.
- Overall decline in performances compared with the previous season despite the financial investment.
