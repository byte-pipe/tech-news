# Ferrari Luce EV debut – Jony Ive‑designed cockpit

## Overview
- Ferrari revealed the complete design of the Luce, its first electric supercar.  
- The interior was created by LoveFrom, the design studio founded by Jony Ive and Marc Newson.  
- Emphasis is placed on physical controls rather than touchscreens, with a minimalist cockpit built from glass, aluminum, and Ive’s signature clean lines.

## Design details
- In February, Ferrari announced the Luce’s name and released early interior mockups, hinting at a feel similar to the long‑rumored Apple Car.  
- The new reveal adds the exterior design and expands on interior features beyond the initial mockups.  
- A Ferrari‑provided video shows:
  - A steering wheel equipped with on‑hand physical controls.  
  - A dashboard and side panel containing several tactile buttons and a mechanical clock.
