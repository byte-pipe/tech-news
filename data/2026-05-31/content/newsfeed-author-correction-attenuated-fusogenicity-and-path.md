Correction to:Naturehttps://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04462-1Published online 1 February 2022

In the version of this article initially published, there were presentation errors in Extended Data Fig. 7, where the first and third “Delta” images were swapped, while the first Omicron image was an inadvertent duplicate of the original first Delta image. The figure has been replaced in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
