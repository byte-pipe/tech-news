Correction to:Naturehttps://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08954-8Published online 3 April 2025

In the originally published version of this article, several figure captions in the Extended Data contained incorrect descriptions of the applied fields. In Extended Data Fig. 2, the caption “There is no external magnetic field (B = 0) or displacement field (D = 0)” should have read “with an 80 mT magnetic field and no electric field”. In Extended Data Figs. 4 and 5, the caption “with no electric or magnetic field” should have read “with an 80 mT magnetic field and no electric field”. An 80 mT magnetic field was used to suppress magnetic fluctuations in the QAH state1(see also Extended Data Fig. 1d). These errors have now been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
