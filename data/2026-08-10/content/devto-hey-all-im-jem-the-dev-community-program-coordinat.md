Jem steps up as part-time coordinator

It feels good to finally be writing this.

I've been working part-time behind the scenes at DEV for almost two years managing social accounts, handling support, and helping with moderation. Earlier this year I stepped into a more curatorial role as the Community Program Coordinator where I work alongside@jess, helping with editorial stuff andDEV Challenges. The past few months we’ve been interviewing for a full-time Community Program Manager, and I’m super excited to have someone new on the team to grow our community programs. Moving forward, I'll be taking on the weeklyTop 7and Challenge Announcement posts, so expect to be hearing from me more frequently :)

When I’m not working for DEV, I'm pursuing a graduate degree, learning how to build interactive experiences at the intersection ofmusic,video games, media theory, and poetry. I also teach design principles and introductory HTML/CSS to undergraduates at CUNY Hunter, which means I spend a lot of time thinking about how people encounter new ideas for the first time.

The reason I find DEV such an exciting place to be is summed up well by@kernelpryanicina post from April:

I don't think all hand-written code is art, just as not every handmade object is. But I do think the category is emerging.

Code has always been both tool and art form, and this community feels like a place where both can grow and thrive. I’ve always thought the overlap of technical expertise and creative curiosity is where the real exciting stuff happens, and I feel lucky to be in a position to help elevate the voices that live in that space.
