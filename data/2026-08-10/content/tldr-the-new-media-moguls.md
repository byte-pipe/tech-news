## THE BIGGEST NAMES IN THE CREATOR ECONOMY ARE HIRING CEOS, LAUNCHING PRODUCT LINES, AND COURTING WALL STREET. BUT SCALING A FACE IS HARDER THAN IT LOOKS.

On a Friday morningin June, Jimmy Donaldson—better known as MrBeast—notched a digital media milestone, becoming the first person to surpass 500 million subscribers on YouTube, making his viewership larger than the combined populations of the U.S. and Canada.

But inside his North Carolina studio hangs a plaque that has little to do with subscriber counts. It reads “YouTube first,” a motto that still governs the Beast Industries operation, according to its chief executive Jeff Housenbold, even if the company it anchors no longer looks much like a YouTube channel.
