## Counter‑Surveillance Measures

- **Noise‑suppression research**: Projects like Microsoft’s Deep Noise Suppression Challenge aim to improve hearing aids, phone calls, and podcasts, inadvertently strengthening AI’s ability to recover speech.  
- **Data‑poisoning approaches**:

## The Ongoing Arms Race
- Countermeasures like Spectre I aim to detect nearby microphones, but their effectiveness is still under development.  
- As AI models improve, they may adopt new listening tricks, such as lip‑reading from video, bypassing audio capture entirely (illustrated by the HAL 9000 scene in *2001: A Space Odyssey*).
