# An artist's “I.C.E. pop” exhibition was shut down within days at a Texas university

## Background
- Victor “Marka27” Quiñonez is a Mexican‑born, Texas‑raised visual artist whose work addresses U.S. Latino experiences.  
- The exhibition “Ni De Aquí, Ni De Allá — Neither from Here Nor from There” previously toured Boston University successfully.  
- It opened at the University of North Texas (UNT) in Denton on Feb. 3, 2026.

## Exhibition details
- Part of the show featured the “I.C.E. Scream” series: large, brightly colored resin sculptures shaped like Mexican paletas (popsicles).  
- Each pop contains an object such as handcuffs, replica firearms, or rosaries, and the stick bears a parody of the ICE seal reading “U.S. Inhumane and Cruelty Enforcement.”  
- The pieces comment on immigration enforcement, immigrant labor, and cultural identity.

## Shutdown decision
- Within a few days, students alerted the artist that the show had been closed.  
- UNT did not comment publicly, but a public‑records request revealed text messages between President Harrison Keller and former Provost Michael McPherson.  
- McPherson, who later became a senior advisor, recommended removing the entire exhibition, writing: “I think it’ll be easier to manage any barking from our friends in Austin.”  
- The decision was driven by concern over potential backlash from Texas state legislators, who have been targeting DEI programs.

## Reactions on campus
- Student Jenny Yanez described a “walking on eggshells” atmosphere for artists and noted a lack of transparency from administration.  
- Faculty in the arts department issued an anonymous open letter protesting the closure.  
- Graduating seniors abandoned the traditional on‑campus graduation show, opting for venues in Denton and Dallas instead.  

## Broader context
- The shutdown reflects a national trend of universities scaling back diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives after political pressure.  
- Similar actions include the University of North Carolina board repealing its DEI policy (2024) and Ohio lawmakers proposing bans on DEI at public universities.  
- Texas began dismantling DEI programs at state‑funded institutions in 2023, and federal funding has been withheld from schools whose policies conflict with former Trump administration directives.

## Future of the work
- Quiñonez’s “I.C.E. Scream” series will be exhibited at the University of California, Santa Cruz in September 2026.
