# Summary of “Finance teams spend 13 hours a week verifying AI outputs: Trial Balance”

## Part 1 – Findings from the IDC‑Sage report  

- Finance teams are still spending significant time checking AI‑generated results, averaging **13 hours per week** per senior finance professional.  
- At an “all‑in” compensation of $250,000, this verification effort translates to roughly **$78,000 per year** per senior finance employee.  
- Survey of 2,000+ senior finance leaders (832 in the U.S.) shows:  
  - 48 % spend more than 15 hours weekly on verification.  
  - 19 % spend over 30 hours weekly, a point where AI creates more work than it saves.  
- The report calls this the **“verification tax,”** estimating it consumes about **26 % of projected productivity gains**, leaving a net **74 % return**.  
- Human roles are shifting from “doers” to “reviewers” as AI adoption grows.  
- Only **4 %** of respondents run largely autonomous finance operations; **62 %** remain primarily manual or rule‑based.  
- Finance leaders demand **explainability**:  
  - 70 % would limit AI autonomy without real‑time visibility into logic or a robust audit trail.  
  - 71 % would reject a 99 % accurate AI tool lacking a human‑readable reasoning trace.  
- Concerns about fully autonomous tasks (e.g., paying invoices) persist due to risk and compliance requirements.
