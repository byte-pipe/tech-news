## Before You Begin
### Popular Books on Physics
- Popular books help maintain motivation and provide a big‑picture perspective.  
- Caution against overly speculative works; prefer books that discuss established physics (e.g., authors Frank Close or Richard Feynman).  
- Recommended titles, ordered by difficulty:  
  * *The First Three Minutes* by Steven Weinberg – Easy  
  * *The Character of Physical Law* by Richard Feynman – Easy  
  * *The Particle Odyssey* by Frank Close – Easy (hard to find online)  
  * *Black Holes and Time Warps* by Kip Thorne – Easy/Medium  
  * *The Theoretical Minimum* by Leonard Susskind and George Hrabovsky – Medium (aligns with undergraduate level 5)  
  * *The Feynman Lectures on Physics* (Boxed Set or Kindle) – Medium  

These resources are suggested to complement the textbook‑heavy curriculum and keep the learner inspired.
