# Apple One and Apple TV subscription prices increase by up to 20 percent - Ars Technica

# Summary of “Apple One and Apple TV subscription prices increase by up to 20 percent – Ars Technica”

## Price Changes
- Apple TV monthly subscription rises by $2, from $13 to $15.
- Apple TV annual plan goes up 20 %, from $99 to $119; existing subscribers get one month’s notice before the new rate applies.
- Apple One individual plan (bundling Apple TV+, Arcade, Fitness+, Music, News+, iCloud+) increases from $20 to $22.
- Apple One Family plan moves from $26 to $28; Premier plan moves from $38 to $40.
- Recent prior hikes: Apple TV monthly price went from $10 to $13 in August 2025, with earlier increases in October 2023 and October 2022. Apple TV+ launched in 2019 at $5/month.

## Market Context
- Apple TV+ remains ad‑free and cheaper than major rivals (Netflix ad‑free $20/mo, Disney+ ad‑free $19/mo) but offers a smaller content library.
- Apple’s services now account for 28.1 % of total revenue (quarter ending 27 June), while hardware still dominates at 71.9 %.
- Services have a lower cost of sales (13.7 % vs. 86.3 % for hardware), making them attractive for profitability.

## Business Implications
- After heavy spending on content, Apple appears to be pushing Apple TV toward profitability.
- A 2025 report by The Information claimed Apple TV was the only unprofitable Apple service, losing over $1 billion annually and not expected to turn a profit for at least ten years.
- Small price hikes without ads may boost revenue but could deter potential new subscribers.
