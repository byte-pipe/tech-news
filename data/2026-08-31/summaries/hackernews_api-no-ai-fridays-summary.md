# No AI Fridays – Summary

## Why?
- Repeated use of large language models (LLMs) can create cognitive debt, reduce engagement, weaken critical thinking, and impede skill development.  
- Offloading decisions to AI creates blind spots and hides trade‑offs, making it hard to verify that AI‑driven direction matches personal preferences.  
- A single AI‑free day each week can offset these downsides while still preserving overall productivity gains.  
- Reducing token usage one day per week can lead to significant long‑term savings.  
- The day aims to recapture the enjoyment of coding, flow states, and personal pride in work.

## How?

- On the designated day, disable AI assistants, write code manually, read documentation, and think through problems yourself.
