description: 'Got a hot take for my #marketing friends who send out cold email campaigns to hot, fresh, dope business owners like myself: Drop the personalization line. I know it''s conventional wisdom to open a cold email with something to connect with the recipient to make the exchange warmer, but that was in an era when people did things like "research" and "typing things with your fingers." Every time I get an email that opens with: - Something scraped off my website or LI profile ("I noticed you develop user-centered software with a collaborative approach! I really respect how you offer custom solutions.") - Some reference to a local site you''re clearly unfamiliar with ("Have you ever eaten at Dave''s Crabatorium LLC? I''m in India, but it''s definitely on my list!") - A company or vertical that has nothing to do with us ("We just got Ajax Plumbing 500 new calls a day. Worth a chat?") - Something about me that doesn''t relate to your pitch at all ("Noticed you help companies reorient
  their software development processes in an age of AI! I help companies just like yours clean their carpets to FDA food grade surface requirements.") - Some over the top effort that is still patently artificial ("I can''t wait to get back to Kansas City and stroll through the West Bottoms with its average surface temperature of 77 degrees or hit up the nightlife in Raytown with its 8% Asian or islander population.") It''s utterly alienating. It makes you sound lazy, like you don''t even care to spend a minimal amount of effort reaching out to me, but you want me to give you 15-30 minutes of my time for free. Your emails would be much more effective without any of that off-putting automation. "But Phillis," you say, because your CRM enriched my first name wrongly, "to do real personalization takes time. I can''t do that at scale or automate it in a pipeline." Yes, exactly. That''s why I recommend you leave it out completely. It''s terrible, people hate it, and it''ll make your campaign outbound
  bound out more quickly. I do know there''s a way (in this "AI age") to do a much better job with automated personalization, but in order for that to happen, an actual software engineer would have to work with the people who automate your campaigns, and unfortunately, AI-driven CRM / outbound management software companies are populated with work ethics very similar to your own - how can we get as much to the masses as quickly as possible even if it''s half-assed garbage. That''s unlikely to happen anytime soon, because most of you are perfectly happy with the half-assed garbage, so why should anyone spend any money to change that? So until then, I think you should just remove it. I''m serious. I would find all your cold emails a lot less off-putting without it. Just send me the boilerplate pitch. At least you''re not insulting my intelligence when you do that. | 77 comments on LinkedIn'
