# 30 Best Tech Books to Gift in 2026: Biographies, Startup Histories, Exposés | WIRED

**The Best Tech Books to Gift in 2026: Profiles of Companies and Leaders**

The rise and influence of technology companies have become a topic of discussion in recent years. A series of books provides in-depth profiles of companies and leaders in the tech industry, making them great gifts for the tech-savvy individuals in our lives.

Here are four of the best tech books to gift in 2026, along with their respective publishing information:

*   **The Thinking Machine: Jensen Huang, Nvidia, and the World's Most Coveted Microchip** by Stephen Witt ($30) - This biography of Nvidia's cofounder and CEO provides a comprehensive look at the company's history and the role of Jensen Huang in its rise to becoming one of the most valuable companies in the world.
*   **The Early Days of the Computer** by Tracy Kidder (available on Amazon) - This Pulitzer Prize-winning book is a nostalgic look at the early days of the computer industry, focusing on Data General's efforts to design and release a successful minicomputer.
*   **The Nvidia Way: Jensen Huang and the Making of a Tech Giant** by Tae Kim (available on Back Bay Books) - For a slightly different perspective on Nvidia's history, this book offers a more nuanced look at the company's development and growth.
*   **The Soul of a New Machine** by Tracy Kidder (available on Bookshop.org) - This classic book is a must-read for anyone interested in understanding the struggles and triumphs of engineers in the 1970s, as they sought to develop and release innovative technology.

These books offer a fascinating glimpse into the world of technology and its many players, making great gifts for anyone interested in learning more about the industry.
