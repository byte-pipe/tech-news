# AI bubble could burst and take global economy with it, IMF warns

Here is a concise and informative summary of the article:

**AI Boom and Its Consequences: IMF Warns**

• The AI spending boom could burst and take the global economy with it, according to the International Monetary Fund (IMF).
• Financial markets are increasingly exposed to an AI-driven correction, making them susceptible to a selloff.
• Millions in AI funding may not deliver the promised profits, leading to a correction.
• Circular financing arrangements, involving AI firms as investors, financiers, and customers, pose a risk to financial stability.
• The IMF fears AI spending could lead to "massive capital destruction" and disrupt financial markets.
• Increasing government bond yields and cyberattacks also pose threats to financial stability.

**Key Risks and Concerns:**

• **Rising government bond yields**: Higher borrowing costs could increase debt burden for governments struggling with debt.
• **Cyberattacks**: Threats to critical financial services could bring entire financial systems to a grinding halt.
• **Circular financing arrangements**: AI firms acting as multiple roles can create risks for individual companies and financial institutions.
