For those companies dealing with sensitive data and strict compliance requirements, Firefox Enterprise is a solid choice. Its features include:

*   **Centralized policy control**: Ensure consistent browser behavior across endpoints while managing security-sensitive data.

*   **Native Data Loss Prevention (DLP)**: Warn or block sensitive data actions by domain to prevent data breaches.
