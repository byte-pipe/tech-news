# 39-Year-Old Unvaccinated Woman Becomes 6th Measles Death in Pennsylvania - Pennsylvania Health Department

*   A 39-year-old unvaccinated woman in Pennsylvania has died of measles, bringing the total death toll to 6 in the state.
*   She had underlying health conditions and was recently diagnosed with measles.
*   The woman's death is a result of probable acute cardiac event due to possible complications from the measles.
*   This is the sixth measles death in Pennsylvania, and the state's measles outbreak is still ongoing.
*   The exact cause of the recent deaths is still being investigated, with the coroner's office noting that the primary cause of death was listed as "probable acute cardiac event due to possible complications of the measles."

# Pennsylvania's Measles Outbreak

*   Over 1,136 cases of measles have been reported in Pennsylvania since the start of the year.
*   As of October 9, there have been 1,124 reported cases, including the recent deaths.
*   The outbreak has now spread to 41 of the state's 67 counties.
*   Hospitalizations for measles have increased significantly, with a total of 218 people hospitalized.
*   The state's measles case count is expected to break the previous record for highest total since 1991.

# Measles Outbreak in the US

*   The pandemic is seeing a resurgence, with measles reported in all 50 states and a projected loss of its measles elimination status by the US.
*   The number of measles cases has hit its highest total since 1991, with nearly 4,080 reported cases for the year alone.
*   The Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) will review US measles data in November to assess the country's progress toward eliminating the disease.
