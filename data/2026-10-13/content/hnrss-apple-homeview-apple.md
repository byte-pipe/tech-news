## Your data isprivate property.

Apple HomeView comes with privacy protections built in at every step. Since multiple people can use HomeView, it’s designed to keep everyone’s personal content separate.

### Multiple users.

HomeView can recognize up to six people and seamlessly switch between them. It syncs with each user’s iPhone or iPad and is designed to show their content only when they’re using HomeView.12

### Secure Exclaves.

The A18 chip inside HomeView contains Secure Exclaves that process the camera and microphone feeds in a secure compartment that cannot be accessed by the operating system or by Apple. This enables HomeView to recognize the face and voice of each user without exposing the audio and visual data used for recognition to the operating system.

### Personal content.

Users can access their personal content on HomeView when their paired iPhone or iPad is connected to the same Wi-Fi network and has been recently unlocked, and HomeView recognizes the person by face.
