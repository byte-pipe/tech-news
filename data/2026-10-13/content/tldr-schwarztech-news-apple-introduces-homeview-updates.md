This type of device isn’t surprising, especially with Apple playing with large-display widgets on StandBy mode on iPhones and CarPlay. Sadly, the user-specific content requires a device with Apple Intelligence, rather than switching profiles like the Apple TV—this would have been a perfect use for Face ID supporting multiple faces? I dig the industrial design—the square display sets this apart from phones (portrait) and computers (landscape), emphasizing that this isn’t something you’d sit down at for a long use session (save for FaceTime.) It feels like a hybrid of an iPad, Apple Watch, HomePod, and nods to both the23-year-old iSight cameraand the24-year-old iMac G4. If there’s one complaint, aside from rose pink, I wish that Apple would branch out from silver, gray, andwarm-silverstarlight as choices. These should match most decor, though.

Using the A18 makes sense, although I’m wondering if this will be a product that sees infrequent updates, much like the Apple TV and HomePod.

The extra horsepower also adds more audio trickery with computational audio, so it should sound better than its predecessor:

### Apple TV 4K

Finally, theApple TV 4K got a refresh. Shockingly (not) it has Siri AI, as it is now powered by an A19 chip. While I hate that the Apple TV has gotten to be a $299 product, I still have some 11-year-old Apple TV HDs around and kicking, so this product should be usable for a long time. I really wish Apple would make an “Apple TV 4K mini” (neo?) that covers the basics at a lower price point. The unit itself gets three far-field microphones and it appears the power light has been centered:
