# A Lebanon town's grief in the aftermath of a deadly Israeli airstrike : NPR

**A Lebanon town's grief in the aftermath of a deadly Israeli airstrike**

The war between Israel and Hezbollah has resulted in over 3,700 deaths in Lebanon. A recent airstrike in a southern Lebanese village killed 14 people, including 10 women and children.

*   More than 3,700 Lebnonese victims have died
*   One airstrike last month was the deadliest attack on civilians since March 2022
*   Israel has targeted Hezbollah fighters after an airstrike
*   The conflict has resulted in widespread displacement of people

**The human toll of violence**

*   The village where the airstrike occurred is currently cleaning up debris and searching for body parts
*   Families are still being relocated from their homes due to safety concerns
*   Many residents remain wary of their surroundings, citing fears of further attacks
