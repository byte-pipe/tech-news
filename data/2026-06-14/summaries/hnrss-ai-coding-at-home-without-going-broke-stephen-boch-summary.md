# AI Coding at Home Without Going Broke | Stephen Bochinski

Here is a concise and informative summary of the article:

**Three Ways to DIY AI Coding at Home**

The options to do AI coding at home without breaking the bank are as follows:
- Self-hosting: Buy a machine, locally run open-source models on it, and pay nothing per token after installation, but consider the steep upfront cost and weaker local performance.
- Renting from providers: Utilize APIs for models at a lower cost while avoiding hardware investments, skipping long-running tasks, and switching between cheaper alternatives.

**The Most Effective Approach**

A hybrid approach combining both is recommended:
- Set aside funds for limited high-end development work (2-3 months)
- Leverage free or low-cost API access for smaller mechanical tasks
- Use expensive models as "fillers" in less demanding projects, while cheap ones do the heavy lifting
