# CIOs and CTOs are making high-stakes decisions with incomplete information, IBM survey reveals | IT Pro

**CIOs and CTOs Make High-Stakes Decisions with Incomplete Information**

In a recent IBM survey, high-level executives (CIO and CTO) discussed the challenges they face when making critical decisions. However, the vast majority of respondents highlighted that their organizations lack the necessary information to make informed choices.

*   **Lack of data**: Many reported relying on intuition or incomplete information, leading to suboptimal decisions.
*   **Complexity over data analysis**: Some executives noted that their roles often focus more on complex system management rather than data-driven decision-making.
*   **Inefficient processes**: The ability to automate tasks helped improve efficiency but only marginally mitigated information overload.
