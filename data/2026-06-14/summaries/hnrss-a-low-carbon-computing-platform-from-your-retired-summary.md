# A low-carbon computing platform from your retired phones

## Low-Carbon Computing Platform from Retired Phones

### Introduction

Researchers at the University of California San Diego are exploring a new way to reduce computing's environmental impact by recovering and utilizing the functional components of retired smartphones.

### Key Components

*   **Phantomboards**: The motherboard core of a smartphone, which retains its original performance and capabilities.
*   **Datacenters**: Created from thousands of Pixel smartphones, these will serve as low-cost cloud computing platforms for researchers and students, reducing the need for newly-manufactured hardware and associated emissions.

### Benefits

*   **Reduced Raw Material Extraction**
    People replace their phones every four years, driven by people's desire for new devices.
*   **Minimized Emissions**: By reusing existing smartphone components in datacenters, the process will significantly reduce computing's carbon footprint.

### Innovative Approach

While modern smartphones' performance processor cores are comparable to those of servers, there lies a significant difference between their size. Smartphones have fewer processor cores and less memory compared to dedicated server hardware.

## The Next Generation of Computing?

The project aims to create a platform that makes efficient use of existing smartphone components for computing purposes, thereby addressing the growing sustainability challenge posed by computing's carbon footprint.
