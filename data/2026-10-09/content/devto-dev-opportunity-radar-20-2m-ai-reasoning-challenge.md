### 📌 PayPal AI Hackathon

Who it's for:Developers, designers, founders, students, and anyone interested in building with AI and payment technologies.

What it's about:

The PayPal AI Hackathon is a global online hackathon where participants build an application that meaningfully integrates PayPal's developer platform and an AI tool, model, or platform of their choice.

There are no fixed problem statements or required tracks. Projects can include AI agents, payment experiences, business tools, applications, or automations. Participants can work individually or in teams, and they can start a new project or make meaningful progress on an existing one during the hackathon.

A working prototype or proof of concept is required. Submissions must include a public code repository with a license, documentation, a functional demo, details of the AI tools used, and a demo video under three minutes. Static mockups and non-functional demos do not meet the requirements.

The total prize pool is listed as$67,500+, with awards for the overall winners and categories such as creativity, impact, agentic commerce, and the use of PayPal and sponsor tools. Some awards include cash, while others offer credits or subscriptions.

Format:Online, worldwideDates:Deadline November 13, 2026, at 1:30 AM ISTTeam Size:Individual or teamPrize Pool:$67,500+ in cash and other prizesRequirement:Meaningful use of both PayPal and AI

### 📌 ARC Prize 2026

Who it's for:AI researchers, developers, and anyone interested in reasoning systems, AI agents, and open-source AI research.

What it's about:

ARC Prize 2026 is a competition focused on advancing AI reasoning, with$2 million in total prizes across three tracks. Participants can work on static reasoning tasks, interactive AI agents, or research papers exploring approaches to improving performance on the ARC-AGI benchmark.

There are three tracks:

* ARC-AGI-2:Build systems that solve static reasoning tasks by predicting the correct outputs for visual grid-based problems.
* ARC-AGI-3:Build AI agents that explore unfamiliar environments, learn how they work, set goals, and plan actions without being given explicit instructions.
* Paper Prize:Submit a research paper presenting an approach to improving ARC-AGI performance, supported by a corresponding Kaggle code submission.

The prize pools differ by track. ARC-AGI-2 has $700,000 available, ARC-AGI-3 has $850,000, and the Paper Prize has $450,000. Awards include prizes for high-scoring solutions, research contributions, and progress toward specific benchmarks.

One important requirement is that participants must open-source their solutions to be eligible for prizes. Submissions are made through the designated Kaggle competitions, and internet access is unavailable during evaluation, so API-based systems such as GPT or Claude cannot be used during that stage.

### 📌 Coinbase Strategy, Execution and Analytics Bounty Challenge

Who it's for:People interested in analytics engineering, data modeling, SQL, Python, and data quality.

What it's about:

Coinbase's Strategy, Execution & Analytics team is running a bounty challenge built around a fictional scenario involving an Analytics Engineer intern supporting the Growth team.

Participants must complete four exercises covering data modeling for an onboarding funnel, investigating inconsistencies across signup reports using Python, reviewing an inherited SQL pipeline, and communicating a metric discrepancy to a product manager.

The challenge focuses on technical skills as well as decision-making, data validation, and explaining data issues to stakeholders. No live dataset is provided, so participants are expected to explain their assumptions and describe their solutions using code, schemas, or written responses where appropriate.

Submissions must include a current resume in PDF format, responses to all four exercises within a combined limit of 1,500 words (excluding code blocks), and a brief disclosure explaining how AI tools were used, if applicable.

The top 20 participants are eligible to receive $250 in USDC and an exploratory call with a Coinbase Emerging Talent recruiter. Participation does not count as a job application or guarantee an interview or internship.

### Build Your Own X

Who it's for:Developers and learners who want to understand how programming concepts, tools, and technologies work by building them from scratch.

What it's about:

Build Your Own Xis a GitHub repository maintained by CodeCrafters that collects step-by-step tutorials for recreating different technologies. Topics include databases, neural networks, programming languages, operating systems, web servers, Git, Docker, AI models, and more.

The repository links to tutorials in different programming languages, covering projects ranging from building a simple neural network to implementing a database or creating a programming language. Readers can browse the categories and choose a project based on what they want to learn.

It's a collection of external tutorials rather than a single structured course, so the languages, prerequisites, and difficulty levels vary between projects.

Format:Free, open-source collection of tutorialsTopics:Software Engineering, Systems Programming, AI/ML, Databases, Networking, and moreLanguages:Varies by tutorialCost:Free
