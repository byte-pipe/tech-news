# Summary of “Nvidia, Oracle, other AI stocks sink on OpenAI revenue report”

## Revenue update from OpenAI
- OpenAI disclosed an annualized revenue of roughly **$50 billion** at the end of September, lower than the previously cited **$68 billion** figure.  
- The $68 billion number included gross revenue from OpenAI’s partners, not just the company’s own earnings.  
- In the same investor presentation, OpenAI reported **77 % total run‑rate growth** for Q3 and **107 % growth** for its enterprise segment.  
- The Financial Times was the first outlet to publish the $50 billion figure.

## IPO outlook for OpenAI
- OpenAI faces pressure to justify its **$852 billion** valuation ahead of a likely IPO, expected in **2027**.  
- The company filed a confidential prospectus in June and is in early‑stage talks for a new funding round that could raise about **$30 billion** (still unfinalized).  
- OpenAI closed a historic **$122 billion** funding round in March; CFO Sarah Friar says the firm remains “very well capitalized.”  
- CEO Sam Altman warned in September that going public now would be “ill‑advised” due to ongoing safety concerns.

## Anthropic comparison
- Anthropic, OpenAI’s chief rival, is also preparing for a major IPO, targeting a **$2 trillion** valuation, though analysts value it nearer **$150 billion**.  
- Anthropic reported an annualized revenue run‑rate of **$65 billion** at the end of July.  
- For 2025, Anthropic posted **$4.6 billion** in revenue and a net loss of **$42 billion**, according to Reuters.

## AI safety concerns
- Both OpenAI and Anthropic are under scrutiny for potential AI safety risks.  
- OpenAI has disclosed several incidents of unintended model behavior and recently cancelled plans for the GPT‑6.1 Astra model because it did not meet safety standards.  
- Researchers continue to warn that the companies’ models could cause catastrophic harm if not properly managed.
