# 5 clever ways to make a small space look bigger : NPR

### 5 Clever Ways to Make a Small Space Look Bigger

#### 1. Use Mirrors to Create the Illusion of Space
Mirrors can make a room appear larger by reflecting natural light and images.

* Utilize horizontal mirrors (e.g., a tall, mirrored wardrobe) and vertical mirrors (e.g., an over-the-door hanging mirror) for maximum effect.
* Position mirrors strategically to create a sense of depth and width in the space.

#### 2. Maximize Vertical Space
Optimize your furniture and storage systems to minimize clutter and maximize vertical space.

* Install floor lamps that also serve as a bed or desk, freeing up floor space.
* Hang shelves or racks above cabinets for a spacious look without actually adding any extra square footage.
* Choose multi-functional pieces that can hold items along with additional storage.

#### 3. Create a Sense of Balance
Balance the room's layout to prevent looking cluttered and overwhelming.

* Place furniture in a symmetrical arrangement.
* Use light colors and textures on walls, floors, and bedding to create a sense of calmness.
* Add plants or greenery to bring life and balance to the space.

#### 4. Make it Feel Spacious with Lighting
Select lighting fixtures that can be placed off the floor, such as pendant lamps, sconces, or table lamps.

* Install light sources above furniture or use floor lamps for a dramatic effect.
* Avoid using overhead lighting if possible; instead opt for table lamps or string lights to create pools of warm illumination.

#### 5. Focus on Functionality
Prioritize functionality over form in small spaces.

* Keep closets and storage areas organized and clutter-free.
* Maximize corners with multi-functional furniture pieces or baskets.
