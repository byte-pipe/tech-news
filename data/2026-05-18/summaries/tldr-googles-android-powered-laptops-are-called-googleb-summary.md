# Google's Android-powered laptops are called Googlebooks, and they're coming this year - Ars Technica

# Googlebooks: Android‑powered laptops arriving later this year

## Overview
- Google’s next laptop line is called Googlebooks and is slated to ship later in 2024.  
- The devices run Android natively rather than Chrome OS and are built around Gemini‑based AI features.

## AI‑driven interaction
- **Magic Pointer**: moving the cursor back and forth launches a full‑screen Gemini experience that sees the screen and offers contextual suggestions (e.g., combine images, create calendar events).  
- **Magic Cue**: similar to the feature on Pixel phones, it recommends actions based on email, messages, and other context.  
- AI‑generated widgets provide a personalized dashboard on the home screen, pulling data from the web and Google apps.

## Software and app ecosystem
- Googlebooks include the Google Play Store, allowing native Android apps to run without the Chrome OS compatibility layer used on Chromebooks.  
- Google is still defining policies for third‑party app stores and sideloaded APKs; more details will be shared closer to launch.  
- Deep integration with a paired Android phone: a task‑bar button lists phone apps, which can be streamed to the laptop in floating windows; files can be transferred seamlessly from the phone.

## Hardware and OEM participation
- No Google‑branded hardware; existing Chromebook OEMs (Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, Lenovo) will produce Googlebooks with varied price points and specifications.  
- Devices are identified by a “Glowbar” on the lid, a light bar reminiscent of older Google devices; its exact function has not been explained.

## Outlook
- Discoverability of AI features remains uncertain, as earlier implementations like Magic Cue on phones have seen limited use.  
- Google has not commented on many specifics, promising more information about “app ecosystem partners” nearer to launch.
