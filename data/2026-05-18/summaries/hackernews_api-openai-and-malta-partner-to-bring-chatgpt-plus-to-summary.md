# OpenAI and Malta partner to bring ChatGPT Plus to all citizens

## Overview
- OpenAI and the Government of Malta announce a world‑first partnership to provide every Maltese citizen with free access to ChatGPT Plus for one year.  
- The initiative combines AI literacy training with the subscription, aiming to turn intelligence into a utility comparable to electricity.

## Programme details
- **AI for All initiative**: a course created by the University of Malta that teaches:
  - What AI is and its capabilities/limitations.  
  - Responsible use of AI at home and work.  
- After completing the course, participants receive a one‑year, no‑cost ChatGPT Plus subscription.  
- **Launch timeline**: first phase begins in May 2026, managed by the Malta Digital Innovation Authority; the program will expand as more residents and citizens abroad finish the course.  

## Statements from leaders
- **Silvio Schembri**, Maltese Minister for Economy, Enterprise and Strategic Projects: emphasizes equal opportunity for digital skills, practical assistance for families, students, and workers, and Malta’s pioneering role.  
- **George Osborne**, Head of OpenAI for Countries: describes AI as a national utility, praises Malta’s vision, and hopes other nations will follow the model.

## Broader context
- The partnership exemplifies a growing trend of governments seeking practical AI adoption pathways that blend education, tool access, and national priorities.  
- It is part of OpenAI’s “OpenAI for Countries” program, which tailors support to each nation’s needs (e.g., education, workforce training, public services).  
- OpenAI is already collaborating with Estonia, Greece, and other countries on similar national AI initiatives.
