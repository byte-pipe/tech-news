# An update on AI’s most important number

## Revenue growth of OpenAI and Anthropic
- OpenAI’s annualized revenue run‑rate jumped from **$13 billion** (August 2025) to **over $40 billion** (August 2026), roughly a **3×** increase in one year.  
- Anthropic grew from **$1 billion** to **$9 billion** in 2025 and then **more than tripled** its run‑rate in Q1 2026, reaching **$65 billion** by the end of July 2026.  
- Combined, the two labs **tripled** in 2024, **more than quadrupled** in 2025, and have already **grown 3.5×** in 2026 (from $30 billion to $105 billion by August).  

## Interpreting the hyper‑growth
- Such **100 %+ annual growth** at multi‑billion‑dollar scales is historically rare; it is expected to **slow** as low‑hanging technical gains fade and markets saturate.  
- The recent surge may be a **temporary acceleration** driven by coding agents reaching a critical threshold with the release of **Opus 4.5**, analogous to the ChatGPT‑GPT‑4 explosion that took OpenAI from tens of millions to $1 billion in 2023.  
- Future “critical thresholds” (e.g., large‑scale project automation, broader white‑collar work) could generate **stacked S‑curves**, but the number and impact of such steps remain uncertain.  

## AI revenue in the context of the world economy
- Together, OpenAI and Anthropic now generate **≈ $100 billion** per year; the entire generative‑AI market is estimated at **≈ $200 billion** annualized, about **0.1 %** of global GDP.  
- If revenue continued to grow **3× per year**, a naïve extrapolation suggests AI could match the current world economy in **≈ six years**—an implausible long‑run forecast but useful for framing the current pace.  
- More realistic scenarios envision a **slowdown** to growth rates typical of mature tech giants, yet even with moderated growth AI could become a **trillion‑dollar** industry.  

## Why the numbers matter
- Revenue reflects **usefulness** and fuels the **compute feedback loop** (better AI → more revenue → more compute → better AI).  
- Monitoring whether the growth curve **bends** will reveal if frontier AI follows a fundamentally different trajectory from past technologies or eventually conforms to typical maturation patterns.
