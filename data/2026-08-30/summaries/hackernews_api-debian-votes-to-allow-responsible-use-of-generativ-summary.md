# Debian votes to allow "responsible use of generative AI"

## Decision summary
- The general‑resolution vote on large language model usage concluded with choice 5 winning: **Responsible Use of Generative AI**.  
- Debian does not endorse nor prohibit AI tools in development, packaging, documentation, or other project media.  
- Contributors may use AI to improve productivity, but must still ensure that all contributions meet Debian’s standards for quality, correctness, maintainability, and legal compliance.  
- Responsibility for AI‑generated output remains with the human author, who must review, test, and modify the output as needed before submission.
