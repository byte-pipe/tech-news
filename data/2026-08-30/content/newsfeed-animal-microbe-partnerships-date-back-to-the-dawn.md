Conotubusspp. was an enigmatic, tube-shaped creature that lived during the Ediacaran period, more than 500 million years ago. Credit: Jihao Li (Chinese Acad. Sci.) and James Schiffbauer (Univ. Missouri)

Many animals live in close contact with microorganisms – think of the teeming bacteria in the human gut. Direct evidence of such inter-species partnerships, known as symbioses, in the early fossil record is lacking. Now Zhenfei Wang at Nanjing University in China and colleagues report the earliest known example of this behaviour.
