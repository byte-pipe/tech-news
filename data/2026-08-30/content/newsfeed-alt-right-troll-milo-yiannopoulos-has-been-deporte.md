# Alt-right troll Milo Yiannopoulos has been deported

The controversial Gamergate figure has been sent back to the UK after overstaying his visa.

Alt-right troll Milo Yiannopoulos wasarrested by ICEon Thursday, and today the Department of Homeland Security confirmed toReutersand theWashington Postthat he had been deported to the UK. Yiannopoulos came to the US legally in 2019, but overstayed his visa. After he failed to appear in court for an immigration hearing, a final order of removal was issued ​on July 22.

The British national made a name for himself as an editor at Steve Bannon’sBreitbartand an early supporter of Donald Trump’s. He has had a long history of controversial statements and associations, including reportedlyhelping arrangeTrump’s dinner with Kanye West and white nationalist Nick Fuentes, and he was a key figure in the online harassment campaignGamergate.

In recent years, however, his antics have led to afalling outwith most significant figures on the right, including MAGA influencer Laura Loomer. He has denied condoning statutory rape orpedophilia, but he has suggested it’sacceptable for an adult to have a sexual relationship with a 13-year-old.

There’s no word if the44 interns in a trenchcoatwho also constitute the entity known as Milo Yiannopoulos have also been deported.
