## What It Catches
----------------

- **Banned Phrases**:
  - Throat-clearing openers
  - Emphasis crutches
  - Business jargon
  - Adverbs and vague declaratives
- **Structural Clichés**:
  - Binary contrasts
  - Negative listings
  - Dramatic fragmentation
  - Rhetorical setups and false agency
- **Sentence-Level Rules**:
  - No Wh-word starters
  - No em dashes
  - No staccato fragmentation
  - Active voice required
