# A Probe Took Incredible Pictures of Mars on Its Way to a Far-Off Asteroid | WIRED

**Psyche Probe Takes Stunning Images of Mars**

The Psyche probe, launched on October 2023, recently performed a flyby of Mars, utilizing its gravitational pull to continue its trajectory toward the asteroid it studies. During this maneuver, the spacecraft obtained new images of the red planet.

During the approach, NASA activated onboard cameras, magnetometers, and gamma ray and neutron spectrometers to calibrate each instrument using the planet's atmosphere and terrain. The rugged Martian surface can be seen in detail, featuring traces of solar wind around craters and a highly active south polar cap rich in water ice.

**Mission Objectives:**

* Obtain valuable data on the asteroid Psyche
* Map its surface and determine whether it contains a partial core of a primitive planet

The space agency aims to reach Psyche in August 2029, where it will orbit the asteroid. This historic mission promises to provide insights into Earth's interior by studying one of the few intact asteroid cores.

**Key Images:**

* A flyby of Mars revealed new details on its surface
* Traces of solar wind can be seen around craters and the south polar cap
* Water ice is present at the planet's poles

This mission has far-reaching implications for understanding Earth's interior and potentially informing technological innovations.
