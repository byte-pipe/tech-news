# Trauma Survivor Elizabeth Smart Finds Strength in Bodybuilding

**Summary:**
Trauma survivor Elizabeth Smart is speaking out about how bodybuilding has helped her rebuild self-confidence and overcome shame surrounding her body.

However, through consistent practice and determination, Smart has discovered confidence in her own body. She attributes this newfound self-esteem to the support of her friends, family, and coaches, including bodybuilding coach Robyn Maher.
