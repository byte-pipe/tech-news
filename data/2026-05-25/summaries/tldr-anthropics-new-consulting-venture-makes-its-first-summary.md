# Anthropic's New Consulting Venture Makes Its First Acquisition

Preeti Singh
Thu, May 21, 2026 at 06:00 AM MDT

• **New Consulting Venture**: Anthropic PBC has acquired Fractional AI, a consulting firm focused on artificial intelligence enterprise services, to expand its presence and capabilities.

• **Joining Forces**: This is the first acquisition by Anthropic, founded to encourage midsize companies to use generative AI like Claude. Apollo Global Management Inc, General Atlantic, Leonard Green & Partners, GIC, and Sequoia Capital are also investors.

• **Joint Venture Strategy**: Anthropic plans to promote its AI tools across thousands of portfolio companies with private asset managers through joint ventures.

• **Goal for Fractional AI**: The firm aims to "close the multitrillion-dollar gap" in AI innovation by providing services and tools to midsize companies not owned by private asset managers, according to CEO Chris Taylor. 

• **Expansion and Partnerships**: After its founding by Taylor, Siegel, and May, Fractional AI has worked with several large firms including Datasite, acquired by CapVest Partners.

• **Google's Investment**: This acquisition marks the first time Google has been involved in an investment of this scale, indicating optimism about artificial intelligence infrastructure.
