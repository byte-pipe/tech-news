# Airbnb gets into hotels, expands AI for host onboarding and customer support

## New hotel offering
- Airbnb is adding boutique hotels to its platform, initially in 20 cities such as New York, Paris, London, Madrid, Rome, and Singapore.  
- A new filter lets users search specifically for hotels; a pop‑up appears for stays of one or two nights.  
- The company offers a price‑match guarantee, refunding the difference in app credits if a lower rate is found elsewhere.  
- This move gives Airbnb access to markets where short‑term rentals are restricted, expanding its addressable market in high‑value cities.

## Expansion of services
- Beyond stays, Airbnb is growing its ancillary services: grocery delivery, airport pickup, luggage storage at over 15,000 locations, and a summer launch of car rentals.  
- Within the Experiences category, the platform adds local‑guide tours at 3,000 landmarks and more than 2,500 food experiences, aiming to compete with Viator and GetYourGuide.  
- No formal loyalty program is announced, but users can earn Airbnb credits (e.g., for a first car‑rental booking or up to 15 % credit on hotel bookings).

## App redesign and user flow
- The home screen will surface suggestions across stays, experiences, and services, while still allowing users to switch to dedicated tabs for each category.  
- The redesign supports the broader goal of a single app that owns the traveler’s entire journey, mirroring moves by Uber into hotel and trip booking.

## AI integration
- **Host onboarding:** AI can auto‑populate address details and other listing information to simplify creation.  
- **Guest side:** New category tags (location, amenities, family‑friendliness) let travelers filter reviews; an AI‑generated summary compares properties in a user’s wishlist.  
- **Customer support:** The AI chatbot, launched in the U.S. last year, now handles about 40 % of queries, supports 11 languages, and includes interactive cards for actions like trip updates.  
- **Future plans:** Later this year Airbnb will introduce a voice‑based AI assistant for phone interactions, developed with multiple undisclosed partners.

## Industry context
- Airbnb’s expansion mirrors a broader convergence in travel tech, where platforms aim to control the full travel experience—from accommodation to transportation—within a single app.
