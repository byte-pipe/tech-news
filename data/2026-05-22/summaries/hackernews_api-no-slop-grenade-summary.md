# no slop grenade

## Definition
- A “slop grenade” is a massive AI‑generated response pasted into a chat or email where a human would normally write a brief reply.
- It overwhelms the medium, turning a quick exchange into a lengthy essay.
- Example: answering “Should we use Redis or Memcached?” with a multi‑paragraph technical comparison instead of a concise recommendation.

## Why it’s problematic
- Recipients have to spend time extracting the single sentence they need, wasting minutes.
- Even if technically correct, the format is hostile to human communication habits.
- It kills conversation: the long wall of text leaves little room for follow‑up, clarification, or dialogue.
- It replaces the asker’s need for human judgment with an AI‑generated monologue.

## Recommended behavior
- Use AI to clarify thoughts, not to lengthen messages.
- Provide concise, human‑focused answers (e.g., “Redis, because we need pub/sub for notifications”).
