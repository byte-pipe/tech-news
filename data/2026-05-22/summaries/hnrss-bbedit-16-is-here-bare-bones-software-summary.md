# BBEdit 16 is here!

## What’s new in BBEdit 16?

- More than a hundred new feature additions, changes, and refinements, including foundational work that delivers order‑of‑magnitude performance improvements in several areas.  
- Expanded Shortcuts support powered by App Intents, adding a whole new collection of actions that make BBEdit’s text transformations available to external workflows.  
- Search for text in images, with multi‑file and grep‑compatible capabilities, allowing you to locate text embedded in pictures.  
- Color customizations for Projects and Notebooks: choose from factory‑supplied schemes or create your own to keep things visually organized.  
- AI Chat Worksheets improvements: faster response times and streaming of results.  

## Additional notable additions and changes

- W3C HTML5 syntax checker integrated for more accurate HTML5 validation.  
- Support for vi‑keyboard emulation, enabling basic navigation and editing with vi‑style keys.  
- Numerous quality‑of‑life enhancements to the built‑in Git integration.  
- New configuration options in website projects, including separate settings for “production” and “testing” deployment locations.  
- Significant throughput improvements in the built‑in SFTP file‑transfer engine.  
- Wide‑range reworked internals, fine‑tuning, and optimizations that produce palpable performance gains across many features.  

## Upgrade Information

- BBEdit 16 is a free upgrade for all licensed BBEdit 15 customers who purchased licenses on or after November 1 2025.  
- Paid upgrade pricing:  
  - US $29.99 for BBEdit 15 customers (if purchased before November 1 2025).  
  - US $39.99 for customers with BBEdit 14.6.9 or earlier.
