# 8BitDo’s FlipPad is the most pocketable way to turn your phone into a Game Boy

## Overview
- 8BitDo’s FlipPad is a $30 USB‑C‑connected gamepad that turns a smartphone into a Game Boy‑style handheld.  
- Announced alongside GameSir’s Pocket Taco at CES 2026, it targets casual gamers who want physical controls without an emulator’s touchscreen.

## Design & Build
- One of 8BitDo’s smallest controllers; thin enough to stay attached to the phone in a pocket.  
- Underside covered with a thin rubber layer for grip and to protect the phone’s screen.  
- Hinged USB‑C connector lets the control panel flip down, exposing the full touchscreen.  
- Available in black and a retro Game Boy‑color scheme.

## Connectivity
- Connects directly to the phone’s USB‑C charging port; no Bluetooth pairing or internal battery required.  
- Works with phones that have a standard USB‑C port; cannot connect to iPhone 16 Pro when inside a thick folio case.  
- Slight wobble (≈¼ inch) during play due to the non‑adjustable connector.

## Controls
- Buttons feature etched labels; D‑pad and ABXY buttons use stiffer silicone membranes with a clicky feel.  
- Two pairs of shoulder buttons are placed on the front edge in a single row of four round buttons, which can feel awkward for some users.  
- Button travel is short but responsive; overall feel differs from 8BitDo’s larger controllers.

## Compatibility & Gameplay
- Works with any smartphone game that can be played in portrait orientation.  
- Tested with Game Boy, GBA, SNES, and Sega Genesis titles via Delta and RetroArch on iPhone.  
- Controls cover nearly half the screen; games may need vertical centering adjustments or a skin to avoid obstruction.

## Comparison with Pocket Taco
- **Size & Weight:** FlipPad is thinner, lighter, and cheaper; Pocket Taco is bulkier with a clamping mechanism.  
- **Power:** FlipPad draws power from the phone; Pocket Taco has a rechargeable battery and can operate as a standalone wireless controller.  
- **Button Feel:** Pocket Taco offers larger, softer buttons and back‑mounted shoulder buttons, which many find more ergonomic.  
- **Case Compatibility:** Pocket Taco’s clamp accommodates thick cases; FlipPad’s fixed USB‑C connector may not fit phones in bulky cases.  
- **Price:** FlipPad $29.99 vs. Pocket Taco $34.99.

## Pricing & Availability
- Pre‑orders open on 8BitDo’s website and Amazon.  
- Release date set for July 30, 2026.  
- Pricing: $29.99 for both black and Game Boy‑color editions.

## Verdict
- The FlipPad delivers a minimalist, pocket‑friendly solution for vertical‑orientation retro gaming, eliminating the need for charging or Bluetooth setup.  
- Its unconventional shoulder‑button placement and limited case compatibility may deter some users, who might prefer the more robust Pocket Taco despite the higher price.
