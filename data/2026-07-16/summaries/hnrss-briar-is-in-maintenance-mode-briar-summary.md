# Briar is in maintenance mode – Summary

## Overview
- The Briar project remains active but is now operating in **maintenance mode**.
- Our focus is limited to essential security updates and bug fixes, with hopes for incremental improvements on long‑standing issues over time.

## Reasons for the shift
- Persistent challenges: high battery usage, unreliable background operation on Android, missing features (account backup, file attachments), and a cumbersome offline contact‑adding experience.
- Attempts to rebuild or split the app were hindered by lack of funding and the absence of a clear long‑term plan, restricting work to spare‑time contributions.
- In the previous year we concluded that fully solving these problems was unrealistic and announced a shutdown, releasing a final update for Android and desktop.
- Community feedback and continued user growth persuaded us to keep the project alive in a reduced, maintenance‑only capacity.

## Current status
- We are **not shutting down**; rumors to that effect are outdated.
- Ongoing work consists of:
  - Critical security patches.
  - Important bug fixes.
- Future plans include modest, incremental work on the major issues once resources allow.
