# Amazon opens up its global logistics network to all businesses

## Key Announcement
- Amazon launched **Amazon Supply Chain Services**, extending its freight, distribution, fulfillment, and parcel shipping capabilities to businesses of all sizes.
- The service directly competes with traditional logistics providers such as UPS and FedEx.

## Target Audience & Industries
- Open to any business, from small startups to large enterprises.
- Initially highlighted sectors: healthcare, automotive, manufacturing, and retail.

## Strategic Significance
- Turns a service previously used mainly by third‑party sellers on Amazon Marketplace into a broader B2B logistics offering.
- Mirrors the impact of Amazon Web Services by leveraging Amazon’s infrastructure, intelligence, and scale for supply‑chain needs.

## Early Adopters
- Companies that have already signed up include:
  - Procter & Gamble
  - 3M
  - Lands’ End
  - American Eagle Outfitters

## Quote from Amazon
- Peter Larsen, VP of Amazon Supply Chain Services, stated the service brings “the infrastructure, intelligence, and scale of its supply chain services—proven over decades—to businesses everywhere, much like Amazon Web Services did for cloud computing.”
