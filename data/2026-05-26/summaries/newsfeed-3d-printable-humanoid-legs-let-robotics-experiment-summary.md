# 3D-printable humanoid legs let robotics experiments run wild - Ars Technica

**3D-Printable Humanoid Legs for Robotics: A New Accessible Option**

The LeRobot Humanoid project is a new release on the Hugging Face machine-learning and AI development platform that offers humanoid robot legs built from 3D-printed parts and off-the-shelf components. These legs are not designed to be marathons-ready, but they can enable researchers to test and train AI-powered robotics software in real-world experiments.

**Key Features:**

* Designed for ease of assembly and modification
* Affordable and priced at $2,500
* Access to a full-stack release including bill of materials, 3D-printable parts, wiring documentation, and physical assembly instructions

**Benefits:**

* Enables researchers to conduct real-world experiments with AI-powered robotics software in a physical body
* Offers a flexible platform for training robots in various behaviors and interactions
* Aimed at building a more reproducible "full-robot design loop"

**Future Development:**

* Integration with an upper body and advanced behaviors planned
* A bigger roadmap with plans to include 3D-printable robotic arms

**Push for Affordable Robots:**

* Helping to mitigate industry dominance by large corporate interests through open-source robotics projects
