# 7-Eleven data breach affects over 185,000 people's personal data | TechCrunch

**7-Eleven Data Breach Affects Over 185,000 People's Personal Data**

On May 26, 2026, a data breach was reported at convenience store chain 7-Eleven. The breach revealed the sensitive information of over 185,000 people, including their names, dates of birth, physical addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses.

**Data Breach Details:**

* **Affected Individuals:** 185,000 or more
* **Types of Data Stolen:** Names, dates of birth, physical addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses
* **Information Stored on Internal Server:** Franchisee documents

**Attacks Credit a Hackers Group Known as ShinyHunters**

The attack is attributed to the hackers' demand for payment, according to Have I Been Pwned, which collects data breaches. The specific details of the hacking are not publicly known, but it's reported that ShinyHunters took credit for the breach.

**Consequences:**

* **Personal Data Breach Notice:** People affected by the breach have received a notification from the data breach notification service Have I Been Pwned.
* **Security Concerns:** The incident raises security concerns and potentially jeopardizes personal information, especially if the stolen data falls into the wrong hands.
