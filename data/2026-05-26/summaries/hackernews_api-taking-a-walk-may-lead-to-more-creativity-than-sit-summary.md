# Taking a walk may lead to more creativity than sitting, study finds

**Taking a Walk May Lead to More Creativity than Sitting**

*   A study published by the American Psychological Association has found that taking a walk outdoors may lead to more creative thinking and problem-solving abilities compared to sitting or being pushed in a wheelchair.
*   The research, conducted by Marily Oppezzo PhD of Santa Clara University, involved 176 participants who were mostly college students.
*   Walking resulted in higher levels of creativity for three out of four studies mentioned, with some estimates showing that as many as 100% and 95% of walkers provided more creative ideas compared to the control group.

**Key Findings:**

1.  **Walking increases free-flowing thought:** Participants under study showed greater creativity while walking outdoors than when they were sitting or pushed in a wheelchair.
2.  **Similar results across studies:** The researchers found similar levels of creativity among walkers and controls for three out of four experiments
3.  **Variability in creative responses:** When asked to come up with ideas, some participants had more unique but less appropriate ideas than others did under the control group condition
