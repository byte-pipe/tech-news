# 3 Top Takeaways From Dropbox’s Former Most Senior Engineer | James Cowling

Here are the 3 top takeaways from Dropbox's former most senior engineer, James Cowling:

* **Career advice in the AI era**: Software is not just about syntax or algorithms, but rather conceptualizing problems and coming up with clean solutions for them. With experience, you don't just need to exercise your muscle; focus on building things that matter.
* **Fixing broken team incentives**: A problem occurs when a team's identity, mission, and name are closely tied to the system they own. To fix this, James changed the organization's name from "AWS Team" to "Storage Team". He believes teams with built-in biases (i.e. self interest) may unintentionally hinder their own effectiveness.
* **Simple systems simplify operations**: Simple systems may seem obvious, but designing them is actually more challenging. With simpler designs, teams face fewer operational burdens and are easier to debug when problems arise.
