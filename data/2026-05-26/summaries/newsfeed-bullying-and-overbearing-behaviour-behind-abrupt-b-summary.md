# 'Bullying' and 'overbearing' behaviour behind abrupt BP chairman removal

• Concerns about "bullying" and "overbearing" behavior led to the immediate removal of BP chairman Albert Manifold.
• The decision is part of a broader governance oversight and conduct issue, affecting several high-level directors in the company.
• Manifold joined BP as an executive director in September 2023 and was appointed chair in October.
• His departure follows concerns over his role in renewable energy, particularly after he declined to include climate activism resolution at AGM.
• Critics argue that Manifold's handling of governance issues, including refusal to report on climate obligations, is unacceptable.

 

• The company has stated its dissatisfaction with the situation and declined to comment further on whether bullying or overbearing behavior was a contributing factor in his dismissal.
