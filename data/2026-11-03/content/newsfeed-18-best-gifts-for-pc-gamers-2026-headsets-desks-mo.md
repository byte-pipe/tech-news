Shopping for aPC gamer is a perilous endeavor. What might look like a great sale on a GPU or gaming keyboard can turn out to be a bad deal because of an arcane spec only the most die-hard forum-dwellers understand. If you're shopping for the gamer in your life who lives that nerd life, we have a simple, accessible guide to what they might like.

Be sure to check out ourother buying guides, includingBest Gaming Laptops,Best Gaming Headsets,Best Keyboards,Best Gaming Mice, andBest Game Controllers. If your giftee prefers console gaming, read ourBest PS5 AccessoriesandBest Switch 2 Accessories guides.

Updated November 2026: We've added the Razer Viper V4 Pro, SteelSeries Rival 3 Gen 2, Smartish Cord Holders, Razer Kraken Kitty V3 Pro, Branch Ergonomic Chair SEGA Edition, and Creative Pebble V3 Speakers.

The best advice I can give when shopping for a PC gaming enthusiast is simple: If you're unsure, just ask. Find out if they have a favorite mouse, keyboard, or headset brand they've been eyeing, or anything else they've been thinking about picking up for themselves. If you're still not sure and don't want to get them the wrong thing, another one-size-fits-all gift any PC gamer will like is aSteam gift card. This lets them pick a game from a vast online store. Most popular titles are around $60, so use that as a baseline for how much to add to the card.
