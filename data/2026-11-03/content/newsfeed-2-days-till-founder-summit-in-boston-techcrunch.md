## Practical scaling insights to move the needle in your startup

A Boston company that became much bigger than Boston.

Steve Fredette, President, Co-founder, and Director ofToast, brings one of the city’s most recognizable founder stories to the stage. Toast’s journey offers a look at finding customers, developing product-market fit, scaling a company, and continuing to evolve after the original idea succeeds. It is proof that founders don’t have to leave Boston to build a company with national and global reach.

Chase Garbarino, CEO and Co-founder ofHqO, will tackle the geography question directly.
