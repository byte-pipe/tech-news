# All this food is now useless: Piles of rubbish after shop freezers hit by cyber‑attack

## Overview
- A cyber‑attack disabled refrigerators and freezers in dozens of supermarkets across several European countries, forcing the disposal of large quantities of perishable food.

## Affected regions and stores
- Belgium and Luxembourg suffered the most severe damage, with 40 Delhaize supermarkets impacted.
- Additional incidents were reported in Sweden, Ireland, the Netherlands, Estonia and Finland.

## Consequences for retailers
- In a Delhaize branch in Hoeilaart, near Brussels, frozen pizzas, ice cream and other items were left to rot in a “mountain of food.”
- The branch estimated the direct cost of the loss at over €100,000 (£83,500).

## Broader implications
- The attack highlights vulnerabilities in supermarket supply‑chain infrastructure.
- Large‑scale food waste raises concerns about food security and the environmental impact of discarded perishables.
