# Anthropic IPO – International Retail Brokers and Selling Agents

## Retail Offering Overview
- Anthropic plans a Class A common stock IPO with retail participation in:
  - Australia, Canada, selected EEA member states (Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden), Japan, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.  
- No intention to list the shares on any exchange within these jurisdictions; the primary listing target is Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker **ANT**.

## Disclaimers by Jurisdiction  

### Australia
- Information is provided solely to alert potential investors; it is **not** an offer or solicitation.  
- The Australian retail offer will be made under an Australian prospectus filed with ASIC.  
- Anthropic will **not** seek a listing on the Australian Securities Exchange.  

### Canada
- An amended and restated preliminary base PREP prospectus is filed with provincial regulators and available via SEDAR+.  
- No sale or acceptance of offers will occur until the final base PREP prospectus is issued.  
- Investors must review all prospectus documents (pre‑, final, supplemented) for full disclosure and risk factors.  

### European Economic Area (EEA) & Switzerland
- The communication is an advertisement under the EU Prospectus Regulation, not a prospectus itself.  
- The European Public Offering will rely on a European Prospectus, to be approved (e.g., by BaFin) and made available on Anthropic’s website.  
- Only **eligible retail investors** in the Covered EEA Countries may subscribe, and they must do so based on the European Prospectus.  
- In other EEA states, the communication targets **qualified investors** only; certain offers may proceed without a prospectus under Article 1(4) of the Prospectus Regulation.  

### Japan & United Kingdom
- No specific disclaimer text provided in the excerpt; standard IPO prospectus requirements and local regulatory filings will apply.
