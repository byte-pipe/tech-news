# ABC’s livestreamed news show is powered by trending topics

## Overview
- ABC News introduced “Searched,” a livestreamed program that highlights stories people are discussing on social media and searching on the web.  
- The show began airing on the ABC News Live streaming channel in April and is available on Disney Plus, Hulu, and the ABC News YouTube channel.

## How it works
- Pulls real‑time data from top search engines and social‑media platforms to surface the most talked‑about topics.  
- Recent episode topics included an Illinois Powerball winner, NBA star Russell Westbrook’s retirement, and Tropical Storm Lala.

## Format and Hosts
- Half‑hour, weekday‑only program.  
- Hosted by ABC News correspondents Gio Benitez and Ashan Singh.

## Strategic context
- Launched as broadcast‑TV viewing declines and more people turn to social media for news.  
- Part of a broader lineup of new streaming‑only shows on ABC News Live:
  - **All Access with Linsey Davis** – interviews with celebrities, politicians, athletes, and influencers.  
  - **Burden of Proof** – focuses on the most talked‑about trials.  
  - **Doctor’s Note** – highlights medical facts and health trends.  
- Reflects a wider industry move toward live news and sports content on streaming platforms; Disney Plus is also exploring a free streaming tier.

## Availability
- Episodes can be accessed in the backlog on the ABC News YouTube channel.  
- The show runs alongside the other new ABC News Live programs listed above.
