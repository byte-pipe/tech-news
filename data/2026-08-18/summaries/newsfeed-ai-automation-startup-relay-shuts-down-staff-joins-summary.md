# AI automation startup Relay shuts down, staff joins Google's Chrome team

## Key Events
- Relay, an AI‑powered workflow automation tool launched in 2021 as a potential Zapier competitor, will cease operations.
- Paying customers lose access on September 14; free users already lost access on August 15.
- The shutdown was first announced in July.

## Leadership Move
- Jacob Bank, Relay’s founder and CEO, is returning to Google as Vice President of Product for Chrome.
- In this role, he will lead Chrome’s product and developer‑relations teams.
- Bank highlighted his career focus on building AI tools that enhance productivity without limiting creativity.

## Founder Background
- Previously spent over six years at Google after his earlier startup, Timeful, was acquired in 2015.
- At Google, he contributed to Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Chat before leaving to start Relay.
- His experience spans product leadership across multiple Google services.

## Relay’s Offering
- Provided workflow automation for businesses, automating tasks such as document drafting, copyediting, and project management.
- Aimed to boost productivity by handling repetitive processes through AI.

## Future AI Integration in Chrome
- Bank described Chrome as “a perfect place to collaborate with agents,” hinting at deeper AI features.
- Google continues to embed its Gemini model across products, including an optional in‑browser assistant in Chrome.
- Gemini recently surpassed 1 billion users, indicating strong user adoption of Google’s AI initiatives.

## Context
- The transition reflects Google’s broader strategy of integrating generative AI into its user experience, especially within Chrome and search.
