description: 'A 200k LI creator asked me to audit his email funnel. 90 minutes later, here''s what I found: But first, some context: This creator has ~10k email subs, sends 3-5 emails/week pretty consistently, and runs a paid community with a few hundred paying members. He''s definitely got a solid foundation. But after digging into his funnel, I found 4 upgrades that could help him add at least $10k-$30k in new revenue per month (2 of them are actually urgent): Upgrade 1: Fix the opt-in flow I actually found 2 diff problems here, and both are costing him subscribers AND paid members. a) there''s no thank you page. You opt in for his lead magnet, you get a little success message, and that''s it. But this is a huge missed opportunity. The moment right after someone subscribes is when they''re most engaged & the likelihood of them taking another action (e.g. signing up for a free trial) is exponentially higher. A simple thank you page that gently pitches a free trial of the community would
  convert a chunk of new subscribers on day one. b) he had double opt-in enabled. We''ve had clients lose 1/3 of their subscribers because of this one setting. People opt in, never see the confirmation email, and - boom - they''re gone. So I''d just turn it off and then purge his cold subscribers every 3-4 months instead. That way you keep the list clean without killing growth. Upgrade 2: Run a monthly-to-yearly upgrade campaign Most of our guy''s paying members are on monthly plans. When I asked him if he''d ever tried to get some of those people to upgrade to annual, he said nope. So right after fixing his opt-in flow, I''d run a short campaign giving them a reason to switch. 2 things happen when you do this: > You collect a big chunk of cash upfront > Your retention jumps (annual members can''t just cancel next month anymore) We ran this exact campaign for a client with a similar community and generated over $20,000 in 3 days. Now, once these 2 upgrades have been implemented, here''s
  what I would do next: Upgrade 3: Start building evergreen sequences Some examples: - An onboarding sequence for new trialers (trial-to-paid is the KPI that matters most in this business) - A cancellation sequence to save people on their way out. What''s nice about these: You build them once, they run forever. Upgrade 4: Run monthly promos. Rotate the theme every month so you don''t fatigue the list. This supplements the signups already coming in from the daily emails + the sign-up flow. And that''s it. None of this stuff is complicated. But the order matters. Plug the leaks first. Get the fast cash second. Build the systems that run on autopilot. Hope it''s helpful! - If you run a paid community or newsletter doing at least $30k/mo, I''ll be happy to do this same audit for you 100% free. DM me "AUDIT" and we''ll get it done. | 17 comments on LinkedIn'
