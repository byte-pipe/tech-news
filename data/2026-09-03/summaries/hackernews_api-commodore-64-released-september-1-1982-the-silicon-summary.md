- **Sound:** SID chip (designed by Bob Yannes) with three flexible voices, more expressive than rival chips.  
- **Compromises:** Slower disk drive, limited modem support, but these did not hinder popularity.  
- Creative programmers extracted impressive audio‑visual effects, extending the machine’s relevance into the late 1980s.  
- Artists could develop assets on more powerful PCs and port them to the C‑64.
