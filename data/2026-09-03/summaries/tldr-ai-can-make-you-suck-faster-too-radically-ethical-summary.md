## Today’s Sad Reality
- Traditional research (Google, libraries) has been replaced by quick answers from LLMs that often cite low‑quality sources (e.g., Reddit outranking financial experts 176 % of the time).
- People rely on “apps for that” instead of critical thinking; these apps cannot reason and pull from unverified opinions.
- The lowered barrier to entry in skilled domains (e.g., DIY electrical work) carries real risks; similarly, amateur software built with AI can expose users to data breaches, financial loss, and legal liabilities.
- The ease of using AI lowers the cost for malicious actors (hackers) as well as for well‑intentioned developers.

## The Wizard of Oz Effect
- Trust in GenAI stems from its ability to quickly surface “good enough” information, creating an illusion of competence.
- The conversational interface masks the underlying uncertainty and lack of rigorous validation that traditional development processes enforce.

## Takeaway
- While AI can automate repetitive coding tasks, it does not remove the fundamental challenges of software design, security, and reliability.
- Overreliance on LLMs without proper expertise can accelerate not only productivity but also the propagation of low‑quality, insecure, or outright dangerous software.
