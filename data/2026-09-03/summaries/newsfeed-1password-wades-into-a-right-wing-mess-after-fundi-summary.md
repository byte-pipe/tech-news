# 1Password wades into a right‑wing mess after funding a Linux project

## Background
- The controversy centers on David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH), a Danish entrepreneur known for Ruby on Rails, Basecamp, and the Hey email client.  
- DHH has repeatedly posted anti‑vaccine, anti‑immigrant, and overtly racist comments, including calls for deportation of ethnic minorities in Europe.

## Donation details
- 1Password pledged **$300,000** to the Omacom Foundation, which oversees the Linux distribution **Omarchy**—an “opinionated” version of Arch Linux created by DHH.  
- The contribution is made to the foundation, not directly to DHH, and is presented as supporting a major customer environment (Omarchy is the second‑most used Linux distro among 1Password users).

## Public backlash
- Social media users accused 1Password of “supporting ethnic cleansing of Europe” because of the donation.  
- Customers asked for alternative password‑manager recommendations to avoid funding DHH‑linked projects.  
- The Verge’s outreach to 1Password for comment received no response before publication.

## Internal response
- Employees expressed disappointment and reacted with emoji responses in internal Slack channels.  
- Co‑founder Roustem Karimov downplayed DHH’s racist remarks, emphasizing differing personal opinions and warning against ostracizing dissenting staff.  
- CEO David Faugno issued an internal message stating that 1Password does **not** endorse hateful or exclusionary views and that the donation is not an endorsement of DHH’s personal beliefs. He also noted the donation’s focus on the Omacom Foundation and the significance of Omarchy’s usage among customers.

## Other patrons of the Omacom Foundation
- The foundation has 12 founding patrons, each contributing $1 million, including:
  - Patrick Collison (Stripe CEO)  
  - Michael Dell  
  - Jack Dorsey  
  - Tobias Lütke (Shopify) – recently endorsed a proposal giving wealthy Canadians greater voting power.  
- DHH himself also contributed $1 million to the project.

## Current status
- 1Password plans to discuss the issue further at an upcoming all‑hands meeting.  
- The incident highlights tension between corporate sponsorship decisions and employee/customer expectations regarding political and ethical stances.
