# Summary of “Stani's big vision for Aave”

## 1. V4: Restructuring the infrastructure  
- Launched on Ethereum 30 Mar 2026 after two years of development, audits, and a public security contest.

- Enables new desks without fragmenting the deposit base, solving the institutional vs. retail split.  
- Initial spokes: Lido, EtherFi, Kelp, Ethena, Lombard; assets include USDT, XAUt, USDC, EURC, cbBTC, frxUSD, USDG.  
- V4 runs alongside V3 (no forced migration), preserving liquidity.  
- aTokens switch to ERC‑4626 share accounting, removing rebasing and simplifying audit, tax, and integration.

## 2. Acquisitions: Building the consumer layer  
- **Los Feliz Engineering / Family (Nov 2023)** – acquired the team behind the “Family” self‑custody wallet and ConnectKit. Result: Family Accounts, a white‑label embedded wallet used across Aave App, Aave Pro, and Aave Kit.

## 3. The Aave App: Owning the consumer experience  
- Early‑access iOS launch Nov 2025; offers up to 9 % APY on deposits, $1 M balance protection, funding via bank, debit card, or stablecoin, plus automated savings and visualizer.  
- Targets users who have never used a crypto wallet; marketed as a “trojan horse” aiming for 1 M users.  
- Differs from neobanks (Chime, Revolut, Monzo) by sitting directly on Aave’s DeFi credit book, eliminating middle‑bank spreads.

## 4. Stable Vaults: B2B “correspondent bank” service  
- Introduced July 2026; a fixed‑rate yield engine that any business can embed.  
- Operators choose stablecoin, strategy, and promised rate; Chainlink and CCIP handle pricing and cross‑chain moves.  
- Excess earnings above the promised rate go to the operator, allowing fintechs to offer boosted rates without building yield infrastructure.  
- Turns wallets, exchanges, and payment platforms into distribution channels that pay Aave for wholesale funding.

## 5. Horizon and GHO: Institutional credit and liability side  
- **Horizon** – permissioned RWA market launched Aug 2025; institutions borrow stablecoins against tokenized treasuries/credit assets. Reached $50 M deposits in days, $570 M by year‑end, target >$1 B in 2026 with partners like Circle, Ripple, Franklin Templeton, VanEck.  
- Regulatory milestone: SEC closed a four‑year investigation in Dec 2025 without charges, enabling institutional partnerships.  
- **GHO** – Aave’s native stablecoin; ~$600 M in circulation, generates fee income. Post‑V4, minted GHO is a liability backed by Aave’s credit book, not a side project.

## 6. Funding abundance & end game  
- Stani Kulechov’s long‑term focus (post‑2026) is on abundant funding and tokenization of credit, positioning Aave as the ultimate bank‑like balance sheet in DeFi.
