## Buying Guide Highlights
- Prioritize chairs with extensive adjustability (seat height, depth, armrest movement, lumbar support).  
- Look for breathable materials (mesh, mixed foam) to reduce heat buildup.  
- Consider weight capacity and warranty length as indicators of durability.  
- For tall users (>6 ft), verify seat depth and height extensions are available.  
- Headrests are optional but can improve neck comfort for some users.
