Activists plaster missing posters around Mexico stadiums ahead of World Cup

Activists have put up missing persons posters around Mexico’s football stadiums to raise awareness of the disappearance crisis ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup. According to official government data, there are currently 133,960 people missing in Mexico.
