# Cancel it, Trump says after artists drop out of US Freedom 250 festival

## Key developments
- President Donald Trump posted on Truth Social that the musical performances for the 250th‑birthday celebration should be “canceled,” calling the scheduled acts “overpriced” and “boring.”
- Of the nine artists originally announced, only three remain: Vanilla Ice, Milli Vanilli (one member), and Flo Rida. Martina McBride, The Commodores, Young MC, and Bret Michaels have withdrawn.
- Trump suggested replacing the concert series with a “Make America Great Again” rally, even proposing to appear himself at the same time and location.

## Freedom 250 and the event’s background
- Freedom 250, created by the Trump administration, runs the Great American State Fair concert series and claims to be non‑partisan.
- The White House is partnering with Freedom 250 on a “once‑in‑a‑generation” series of events marking America’s 250th anniversary, including an opening ceremony on June 24 where Trump is slated to headline.
- Congress established America250 a decade ago to oversee the broader anniversary celebrations; the group includes bipartisan appointees and organizes July 4 events and block parties nationwide.

## Artists’ reactions
- Young MC said he was not informed of any political involvement and would consider performing at a non‑political event in the future.
- Martina McBride described the invitation as “misleading” after learning the event was tied to the White House.
- Vanilla Ice emphasized that the concert is “not a political platform” and is simply celebrating the nation’s birthday.
- Fab Morvan of Milli Vanilli confirmed his participation, while the other vocalists behind the group said they would not perform.

## Trump’s follow‑up statements
- Trump claimed artists were experiencing “the yips,” a golf term for performance anxiety, and ordered his representatives to explore an “AMERICA IS BACK” rally on the same day and venue.
- He later reiterated the idea of a giant “MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN” rally, dismissing the remaining singers as “overpriced” and “boring.”

## Additional anniversary activities
- The White House backs several Freedom 250 events: a UFC fight on the South Lawn, the Great American State Fair in June–July, a Grand Prix race in August, and the release of commemorative passports featuring Trump’s portrait.
