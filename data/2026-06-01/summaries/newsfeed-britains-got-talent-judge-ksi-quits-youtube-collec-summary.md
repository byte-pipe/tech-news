# Britain's Got Talent judge KSI quits Sidemen collective after 13 years  

## Announcement  
- KSI (Olajide Olatunji) confirmed via a YouTube video that his final Sidemen appearance will be on 31 May, ending a 13‑year involvement.  
- His spokesperson told the BBC the decision was genuine, despite some fans initially suspecting a stunt.  

## Reasons given by KSI  
- Described the video announcing his departure as “the hardest video I’ve ever had to make.”  
- Said he had contemplated the choice for months and felt pulled in many directions, running “at 100 mph.”  
- Emphasised the decision was entirely his own and that nothing negative occurred between him and the group.  

## Reactions from Sidemen and fans  
- Sidemen posted a statement expressing sadness, wishing KSI well, and noting the news was a surprise.  
- The group thanked each member—Simon Minter, Josh Bradley, Vikram Barn, Tobi Brown, Ethan Payne, and Harry Lewis—for their contributions.  
- Fans posted tributes, with many initially doubting the authenticity before accepting it.  

## Sidemen collective overview  
- YouTube channel launched in 2013; now holds 23 million subscribers, with the entire group exceeding 140 million subscribers across channels.  
- Notable videos: a $20,000 vs $200 budget holiday challenge (100 million+ views), “Sidemen Tinder,” eating challenges, and comedy roasts.  
- Activities include charity football matches raising millions, and ventures such as a fried‑chicken chain, clothing line, alcohol brand, and cereal.  

## KSI’s broader career  
- Began creating videos in 2008; personal YouTube channel has over 18 million subscribers.  
- Also known as a musician, former boxer, and recent minority owner of Dagenham & Redbridge Football Club.  
- Served as a judge on ITV’s Britain’s Got Talent final shortly before the announcement.
