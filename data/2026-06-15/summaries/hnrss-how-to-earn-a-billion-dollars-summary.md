## What Drives Sustainable Growth
- Consistent high growth requires a product that users love and share organically.  
- The growth rate is the primary indicator that founders have built the “right thing.”  
- Market size limits how long high growth can continue; larger markets allow longer exponential phases.

## Finding a Product People Will Share
- Successful founders often create something they personally need.  
- Young founders are positioned to spot emerging needs because their own preferences forecast future demand.  
- The market quickly fills obvious gaps, so the key is to discover unmet needs that are not yet obvious to others.  
- Building for oneself and one’s peer group provides a reliable signal for product‑market fit.

## Implications for Politicians and the Public
- The perception that billion‑dollar wealth must involve wrongdoing is a mathematical illusion, not a moral one.  
- Understanding the two variables—growth rate and duration—shows that cheating is unnecessary to achieve massive wealth.  
- Policymakers should recognize that fostering environments where startups can achieve high growth benefits the broader economy.
