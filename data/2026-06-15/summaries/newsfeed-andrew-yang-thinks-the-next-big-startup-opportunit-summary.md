# Summary of “Andrew Yang thinks the next big startup opportunity is lowering the cost of living | TechCrunch”

## Main ideas
- Yang argues that the next wave of startups should focus on giving money back to customers rather than extracting it, targeting essential expenses such as housing, education, food, fuel, transportation, media, and wireless.
- Inspired by Mark Cuban’s Cost‑Plus Drugs, Yang launched Noble Mobile, a low‑cost mobile virtual network operator that refunds customers who use less data.
- He sees AI‑driven wage compression and job displacement as a catalyst for demand for cheaper basic‑need services, creating a “rich vein of opportunity.”
- Yang continues to champion Universal Basic Income (UBI) as a way to redistribute AI‑generated wealth, but believes market solutions can also fill gaps where policy falls short.
- Noble Mobile has attracted thousands of users and generates millions in revenue, sharing profits with subscribers to boost loyalty and word‑of‑mouth growth.
- The business model’s appeal includes long‑term financial benefits for customers (e.g., $50 monthly savings compounding to $24,000 over 40 years).
- Funding challenges persist because capital is heavily directed toward AI, and investors often request AI integration even for non‑AI ventures.
- Yang urges founders and investors to pursue socially‑impactful problems, avoid groupthink, and build profitable enterprises that lower living costs.

## Notable quotes
- “AI is going to suck up a lot of the value and the jobs… meeting people’s needs ‘less expensively’ is a very rich vein of opportunity.”
- “There is room for a direct connection between the money and the people.”
- “Think bigger and more broadly about trying to tackle problems and don’t subscribe so much to groupthink.”
