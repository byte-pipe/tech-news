# Anthropic CEO Amodei to have dinner with Trump at White House

## Main event
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei will dine one‑on‑one with U.S. President Donald Trump at the White House on Sunday evening.  
- The meeting was confirmed by Trump and first reported by Axios.

## Context of the meeting
- Relations between Anthropic and the Trump administration have been strained for months.  
- Trump reiterated his opposition to slowing AI development, arguing that new regulations could let China overtake the United States.  
- He claimed the U.S. is about a year and a half ahead of China in AI and emphasized the need to preserve that lead.

## Recent legal and policy actions
- A federal appeals court upheld the Pentagon’s designation of Anthropic as a “supply chain risk,” prohibiting the U.S. military from using its models.  
- The Trump administration sanctioned Anthropic after the company refused in February to allow its tools for fully autonomous weapons or domestic mass surveillance.  
- Trump criticized Amodei for calling for a pause in AI development, describing him as “a perfect little angel.”

## Industry landscape
- Amodei was absent from the state dinner for Chinese President Xi Jinping, which was attended by AI leaders including OpenAI’s Sam Altman.  
- The U.S. government maintains that major AI firms (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta) cannot afford to slow down for fear of a Chinese advantage.  
- Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson are scheduled to meet with other leading AI executives on Tuesday.
