# An expert guide to finding the perfect pillow for your sleeping position – BBC News  

## Main advice from Dr Chris McCarthy  

- **Identify your sleeping position** – most people are side, back or front sleepers.  
  - Front sleeping is discouraged because it keeps the neck twisted for long periods, increasing the risk of muscle or ligament irritation.  
- **Match pillow thickness to your spine curvature and shoulder width**  
  - Back sleepers: consider the natural flexion of the upper spine; a larger curve needs a slightly thicker pillow.  
  - Side sleepers: the wider the shoulders, the thicker the pillow required.  
- **Take mattress firmness into account**  
  - On a firm mattress, broad‑shouldered side sleepers need a higher pillow because the shoulder does not sink.  
  - On a softer mattress, the shoulder sinks more, so a thinner pillow is appropriate.  
  - Back sleepers are less affected by mattress softness.  
- **Adjust pillow height with a simple towel trick**  
  - Fold a towel and place it under the pillow; add or remove folds until the pillow feels the right height.  
  - Use this method to test whether a higher front (for side sleepers) or a dip (for back sleepers) improves comfort.  
- **Consider pillow shape**  
  - Contoured or ergonomic pillows have limited evidence; a modestly raised front can help side sleepers.  
  - Some back‑sleep pillows have a central dip and raised sides, which may feel “claustrophobic” for some users.  
- **Choose filling material wisely**  
  - **Feather/down**: cool and breathable but may shift and create a wedge if you move a lot.  
  - **Hollow‑fibre/microfibre**: retain shape better but are less effective at heat wicking.  
  - Some foam pillows combine firmer lower sections with ventilation channels to keep shape and temperature stable.  
- **Personalise and trial**  
  - No single pillow design fits everyone; treat the choice as “horses for courses.”  
  - Take advantage of manufacturers’ trial periods to test comfort before committing.  

## Practical tips  

- Avoid sleeping on your stomach whenever possible.  
- If a pillow feels too flat, use a folded towel underneath to raise it temporarily and gauge the needed height.  
- Larger, heavier heads generally benefit from a thicker, firmer pillow.  

## Bottom line  

Selecting the right pillow depends on a combination of sleeping position, shoulder width, mattress firmness, and personal comfort preferences. Simple adjustments like the towel trick and trying out different materials during a trial period can help you find a pillow that supports proper spinal alignment and improves sleep quality.
