# Project rebrand: Nura

## Overview
- The operating system formerly known as **postmarketOS** is now called **Nura** (announced 27 September 2026).  
- “Nura” shortens **Nuraghe**, ancient granite towers in Sardinia, symbolizing long‑term stability and durability.  
- The name was contributed by community member Davide Depau.

## Why rename the project?
- **Pronunciation & memorability:** “postmarketOS” is long, hard to pronounce, and varies in capitalization, causing confusion.  
- **Scope limitation:** The descriptive name tied the project to a specific market niche, restricting perception of broader goals.  
- **Trademark issues:** The old name cannot be trademarked; a trademark for “Nura” has already been filed to protect the brand.

## Logo
- The existing logo will remain because it aligns with the mission.  
- Minor adjustments were made (spacing, mathematical construction) to improve legibility and reproducibility.

## Timeline
- Initial rename announcement made in March 2025.  
- The process took over a year due to the need for community consensus, trademark legal work, and timing the reveal for the conference.

## Domain choice
- **Nura.eco** is used because **Nura.org** is owned by another party and unavailable for purchase.  
- The “.eco” TLD emphasizes the project’s ecological focus and includes safeguards against greenwashing.

## Naming process
- A selection team (Pablo, Pan, Ranny, Sriram Ramkrishna) reviewed >300 community suggestions.  
- Four finalists were evaluated for linguistic issues across continents, then voted on using range voting.  
- “Nura” received the highest average score (0.4 on a –2 to 2 scale) and was selected.

## Additional branding updates
- Small logo refinements (arrow spacing) improve visibility from a distance.  
- The logo is now mathematically defined, allowing anyone to recreate it.

## Ongoing transition
- Some user‑facing strings still show “postmarketOS.”  
- Contributions via merge requests or issue reports are welcomed to complete the rename.
