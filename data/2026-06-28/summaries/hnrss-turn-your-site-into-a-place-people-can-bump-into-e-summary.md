# Turn your site into a place people can bump into each other

## Overview
- Added a tiny “Town Square” strip to the bottom of every page as a playful experiment.  
- The strip shows stick‑figure avatars representing other visitors who are currently on the site.  
- Users can see what page each person is reading, walk around, and send short messages in real time.  

## Core Philosophy
- Not intended to become another social network.  
- Aims to revive the feeling that real people are behind the screen, making the web feel more like a shared place.  

## Design Characteristics
- Minimalist and transient: no accounts, profiles, follower counts, or permanent chat history.  
- Messages exist only while participants are present.  

## Open‑Source Release
- After interest from others, the project was open‑sourced and a public server was provided.  
- Repository: https://github.com/cauenapier/TownSquare/  
- Site owners can integrate Town Square without self‑hosting by registering their website on the public server.  

## Future Directions
- Adding more interactive props for the stick‑figure characters.  
- Improving overall chat user experience.  
- Enabling connections between different Town Squares, allowing users to “walk” to neighboring sites—essentially a modern Webring.
