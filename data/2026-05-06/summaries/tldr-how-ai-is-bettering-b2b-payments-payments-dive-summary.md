# How AI is bettering B2B payments

## Overview
- Billtrust, an accounts‑receivable (AR) software provider, is leveraging artificial intelligence to address what its CEO calls a “data science challenge” in AR.
- The company aims to expand in Europe while embedding AI tools that help customers with limited IT resources automate and optimize payment processes.

## AI initiatives at Billtrust
- **Agentic AI and automation**: Offering AI‑driven features that guide users toward trustworthy AI adoption.
- **Communication automation**: Automating interactions between buyers and suppliers in AR workflows.
- **Voice and chat interfaces**: Introducing conversational AI for easier customer engagement.
- **Future autonomous operations**: Planning fully autonomous AI functions that operate within preset parameters.
- **Customer sentiment**: Clients are described as “somewhere between highly interested and enthusiastic” about AI adoption.

## Market positioning and growth
- CEO Grant Halloran joined Billtrust in December 2023 to lead growth; the firm is owned by BTRS Holdings, acquired by EQT for $1.7 billion in 2022.
- Headquarters in Nashville, Tennessee, with ~1,000 employees and 2,600 customers, primarily medium to large enterprises ($50 M–tens of billions in revenue).
- Processes roughly $1 trillion in annual invoice volume.
- Focus on expanding U.S. multinational customers into European markets.

## Competitive landscape
- Main competitors: HighRadius and Versapay.
- Other payment firms (e.g., Bill Holdings) target smaller companies, differentiating Billtrust’s focus on larger organizations.

## B2B payment dynamics and AI optimization
- Relationship between buyers and suppliers is often “adversarial”:  
  - Buyers prefer flexible payment methods (e.g., credit cards) for rebates, rewards, and float periods.  
  - Suppliers balance lower transaction costs with the need to satisfy buyer preferences.
- This tension creates a complex data‑science problem involving many buyers, suppliers, and banks.
- Billtrust’s AI continuously optimizes payment acceptance policies and buyer experiences, using algorithmic and increasingly autonomous approaches.
- The goal for large finance departments is to drive optimal economics across the end‑to‑end AR process.
