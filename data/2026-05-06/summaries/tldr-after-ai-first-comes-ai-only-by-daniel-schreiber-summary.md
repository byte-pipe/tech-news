# After “AI-First” Comes “AI-Only”

## Main Argument
- The author, co‑founder of Lemonade, predicts that “AI‑first” will soon look outdated, like the horseless carriage.
- The next evolutionary step is “AI‑only”: workflows run entirely on machines, with humans only setting goals, values, constraints, and escalation conditions.
- This shift moves humans from active participants to stewards of autonomous systems.

## Why “AI‑only” Matters
- Removing the human “pilot” eliminates the need for human‑centric design (cockpits, ergonomics, safety envelopes) and unlocks the full potential of expensive technology.
- Current AI‑generated code (≈98% at Lemonade) yields 30–50% gains, but human bottlenecks (decision‑making, environment setup, review) limit further improvement.
- The “dam” holding back intelligence is human involvement; breaking it could produce multiplicative productivity gains, not just percentage increases.

## Rethinking Organizational Architecture
- Many CEOs now enforce “no new headcount until AI can’t do the task,” but this still assumes a human‑shaped job structure.
- “AI‑only” questions the existence of the “chair” (the role) itself, not just who sits in it.
- Traditional corporate elements (queues, handoffs, managers, meetings) exist because of human limits; removing the final human removes these constraints.

## Economic and Social Implications
- Companies that achieve faster, cheaper, more reliable service by eliminating humans will gain a competitive edge, forcing industry‑wide adoption.
- The transition will be gradual, workflow by workflow, as trust in AI systems grows.
- While economically compelling, the shift poses significant unemployment risks and broader social costs that will not self‑balance.
- The author urges acknowledgment of these forces and preparation (see mosaicmodel.org).

## Conceptual Shift: From “Artificial Intelligence” to “Alien Intelligence”
- Early AI was framed as a “better human” (the “horseless carriage” metaphor).
- As technology outpaces the metaphor, AI should be seen as a fundamentally different form of intelligence.
- Embracing an “alien intelligence” mindset helps break the anthropomorphic view that AI must mimic humans.

## Closing Thought
- Quoting the android Ada from *After Yang*: “We always assume that other beings would want to be human. What’s so great about being human?” This encapsulates the move from AI‑first to AI‑only.
