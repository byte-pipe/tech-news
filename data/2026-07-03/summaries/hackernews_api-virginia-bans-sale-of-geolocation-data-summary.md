# Virginia Bans Sale of Geolocation Data

**Key Points:**

* Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger signed into law S.B. 388, amending the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act ("VCDPA"), to prohibit the sale of geolocation data.
* The VCDPA defines "sale" as the exchange of personal data for monetary consideration by the controller to a third party.

**Implementation:**

* Implementation of the ban on geolocation sales goes into effect on July 1, 2026.
* This marks several other US states that have introduced similar legislation, including California, Massachusetts, Vermont, and Washington State.
