# Apple's Hide My Email feature has a bug that's been exposing real email addresses, researcher claims | TechCrunch

**Apple's Hide My Email Feature Exposed: Bug Allows Real Email Addresses to be Unmasked**

Apple's "Hide My Email" feature uses disposable addresses to hide users' real email for online anonymity, but a new research reveals that a bug has been allowing the feature to expose users' actual email addresses. The bug was reported by 404 Media and led by researcher Tyler Murphy. 

**Key Findings:**

* A bug in Apple's "Hide My Email" feature allows users to be unmasked using their real email address.
* Researchers tested the vulnerability and exploited it, with 100% of Hide My Email addresses being accessible.
* The researchers warn that this flaw makes publicly accessed people-search sites vulnerable to linking an email address to other personal details.

**Implications:**

* Publicly accessible people-search sites may expose users' email addresses if they are linked to others through these databases.
* The vulnerability applies to private and secure online services, such as those built around Hide My Email.

**Apple's Response:**

* Apple has no immediate comment on the issue or a response due its potential consequences.
* This incident is just another example of how flawed privacy features can negatively impact user security.

**Related Concerns:**

* Given Apple's reputation for strong adherence to user privacy, this issue raises questions about trust and reliability with their products.
* The discovery may prompt Apple to update the feature or implement patches to address these issues.
