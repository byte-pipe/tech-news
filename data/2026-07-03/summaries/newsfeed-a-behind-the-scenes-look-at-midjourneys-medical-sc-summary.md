# The Verge's Midjourney Medical Scanner Video Leaves Many Questions Unanswered

Unfortunately, the video only provides glimpses into the technical challenges of building this device. Here are some key takeaways:

* The device consists of hacked-apart ultrasound probes connected to off-the-shelf computers and Raspberry Pis.
* The scanner is supposed to launch as a wellness product focused on body composition, rather than as a diagnostic medical device.

Despite promising an "ultracomforting" experience, the video still glosses over the limitations and challenges of working with ultrasound technology. Experts have raised concerns about the feasibility of generating detailed images at the scale and speed claimed by Midjourney:

* The company has failed to show evidence that its scanner can overcome key physical barriers in ultrasound imaging.
* Early attempts at scaling up the technology may require significant investment and tweaking.

The media commentary on this project reveals a general lack of understanding about the technical challenges involved. Experts emphasize the need for more rigorous testing and regulatory approvals before a device approved for medical use can be considered:

* Physicians would need access to regular scans on high-quality hardware.
* The device must demonstrate safety and efficacy in clinical settings.

CEO David Holz downplays these concerns, saying that his company is "freedomed" to pursue the project without industry support. However, this lack of accountability leaves many questions unanswered about Midjourney's plans for medical imaging.
