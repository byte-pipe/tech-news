# A hot summer trend in the sharing economy? Rental swimming pools : NPR

**A Hot Summer Trend in the Sharing Economy: Rental Swimming Pools**

* The Airbnb-style company Swimply has reported a significant surge in private pool reservations, with over 275,000 so far this year.
* Companies like Swimply have become popular for hosting pool parties and gatherings, offering guests access to private spaces on rented properties.
* Rentals can range from cars to guest bedrooms and come with amenities such as pools, air conditioning rooms, and private bathrooms.

**Trends Driving the Trend**

* The growth has led to a near-frictionless experience for renters, allowing them to invite guests and enjoy other amenities without the hassle of managing their own properties.
* With prices ranging from $381 to over $1,000 per guest, rentals offer an attractive option for those looking for unique experiences or parties.

**Key Features of Rental Swimming Pools**

* Guests receive access to private pool space, often with additional amenities such as floats and water toys
* Pool floats are available in different sizes, including a 50th edition size Unicorn inflatable that can be customized with water spray features
* Renters typically provide their own food, drinks, and decorations
* Some rentals come equipped with poolside areas or loungers for guests to relax and socialize.
