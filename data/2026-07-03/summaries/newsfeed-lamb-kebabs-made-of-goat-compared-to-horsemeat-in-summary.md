# Lamb kebabs made of goat compared to horsemeat in lasagne scandal - BBC News

## Lamb Kebabs Controversy in the UK

* **Main idea:** A controversy has erupted in the UK regarding lamb kebabs sold by Kismet Kebabs, which were found to contain goat meat, contrary to its advertised 70% lamb content.
* **Key points:**
	1. Kismet Kebabs was one of the largest doner kebab makers in the UK and estimated to have made £6m from a fraud that dates back to 2021.
	2. The firm was fined £500,000 for admitting to a massive meat-based scam over five years ago.
	3. DNA testing revealed the kebabs actually contained less than 10% sheep fat, not lamb as advertised.
* **Context:** Investigative agencies began randomly testing doner meat from takeaways in Swansea and found unexpected amounts of skin, fat, goat fat, and trim containing high levels of lipids before discovering the underlying truth.
*"It's almost the same as the horsemeat scandal," said a Swansea trading standards officer.
