# PipePipe – Open‑source Android app for YouTube and other services

## Overview
- Fork of NewPipe started in early 2022; developed independently and does not sync with NewPipe updates.  
- Aims to provide a faster, more stable experience with many additional features.

## Core Features
- **YouTube Enhancements**
  - SponsorBlock integration for skipping sponsors (YouTube & BiliBili).  
  - ReturnYouTubeDislike support to restore dislike counts.  
  - Option to show original (non‑localized) video titles.  
  - Login capability to access restricted or premium content.  

- **Media Capabilities**
  - Live‑chat overlay in danmaku style.  
  - AV1 and VP9 codec support for high‑quality, efficient playback.  
  - Music‑player mode with background playback.  

- **Filtering & Feed Control**
  - Advanced search filters for improved discovery.  
  - Keyword/channel‑based exclusion of unwanted items.  
  - Ability to block Shorts and paid videos.  

- **Playback Controls**
  - Swipe‑to‑seek and fullscreen gestures.  
  - Long‑press to accelerate playback speed.  
  - Sleep timer for bedtime listening.  

- **Playlist Management**
  - Bulk download of entire playlists.  
  - Search and sort within local playlists and watch history.  

## Sign‑in & Privacy
- Login cookies are used only for user‑specified scenarios, configurable under “Cookie Functions.”  
- For YouTube, cookies are employed solely when retrieving playback streams.

## Contribution
- Issues and pull requests are welcomed; service‑request tickets are not accepted.  
- Developers are encouraged to fork the repo to create new services.
