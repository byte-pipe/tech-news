# Anthropic to pay Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years in cloud deal | TechCrunch

# Anthropic‑Akamai Cloud Deal Summary

## Deal Overview
- Anthropic will spend **$11.6 billion** over seven years on Akamai’s cloud infrastructure.  
- The amount is more than six times larger than the $1.8 billion deal reported in May.  
- The commitment is conditional on Akamai meeting delivery and service‑availability requirements; either party may terminate under specified conditions.  

## Financial Impact for Akamai
- Largest contract in Akamai’s history.  
- Expected revenue: $150 million‑$300 million in 2027 (starting H2), rising to an annual pace of about **$1.7 billion** by the end of 2028.  
- Akamai plans to invest roughly **$5.5 billion** to build the required capacity and an additional **$1.7 billion** this year for advance purchases of components such as memory.  

## AI Infrastructure Focus
- The deal emphasizes CPUs, the general‑purpose chips increasingly needed as AI agents take on broader tasks.  
- Akamai did not disclose the specific workloads Anthropic will run on these CPUs.  

## Equity Component – Warrants
- Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for non‑voting preferred stock convertible into **7.7 million common shares** (about **5 %** of Anthropic’s outstanding stock) at **$111.33 per share**.  
- Approximately **2 %** of the warrant vests after Anthropic’s first payment; the remainder vests as Anthropic’s spending increases.  
- Every additional **$3 billion** spent unlocks roughly another **1 %** of the warrant, allowing the total deal value to potentially grow to **$20 billion**.  
- This is the first Akamai cloud contract to include a warrant; a similar structure was used by AMD with OpenAI.  

## Industry Context
- Other major cloud and chip providers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, AMD) have invested in Anthropic while supplying chips or cloud capacity.  
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei noted that the company’s participation in such deals is smaller in scale compared to some peers.  

## Market Reaction
- Akamai’s share price rose as much as **17 %** in after‑hours trading following the announcement.
