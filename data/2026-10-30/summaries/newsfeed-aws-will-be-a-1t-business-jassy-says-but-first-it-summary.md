# AWS will be a $1T business, Jassy says. But first, it will spend big.

## Main Announcement
- Andy Jassy reiterated that AWS aims to become a trillion‑dollar annual revenue business.
- He noted that no business has ever achieved a trillion dollars of yearly revenue.

## Capital Expenditure Plans
- Amazon expects cash capex of approximately $220 billion in 2026, with the majority allocated to AWS.
- The company plans to spend “meaningfully more” in 2027, citing a clear line of sight to financial returns.

## Investor Concerns & AI Spending
- Wall Street analysts questioned the size of AWS’s AI infrastructure investment and its impact on margins.
- Jassy defended the spending as essential to capture a historic surge in global cloud demand driven by AI.

## Growth Metrics & Forecasts
- AWS revenue rose 39 % year‑over‑year in the latest quarter, marking six consecutive quarters of growth.
- Jassy forecasted a 40 % revenue increase for both 2027 and 2028.
- Current annualized revenue run‑rate stands at $184 billion; the goal is >40 % growth in 2027‑28 to reach a trillion‑dollar revenue business within five to ten years.

## AI Strategy Outlook
- Jassy described enterprise AI adoption as being in its “very early days.”
- He labeled the AI boom a “once in a lifetime opportunity,” justifying the substantial financial commitment.

## Market Reaction
- Despite the optimistic outlook, Amazon’s shares fell about 6 % in after‑hours trading following the earnings call.

## Industry Context
- The AI boom is a mixed blessing for cloud providers: it drives demand for cloud infrastructure while prompting large capital expenditures.
- Competitors such as Microsoft are also reporting record cloud revenue growth and significant spending.
