# DoorDash to Acquire Instacart to Help Local Stores Win

## Overview
- DoorDash announced a definitive agreement to acquire Instacart (Maplebear Inc.) for $65 per share, valuing Instacart at roughly $17 billion.  
- The deal is structured with at least 75 % cash; Instacart shareholders may elect cash, DoorDash stock, or a mix, subject to a cap that DoorDash stock does not exceed 25 % of total consideration.  
- Both companies share a mission to empower local businesses and plan to keep Instacart’s brand, technology products, and app post‑closing.

## Transaction Details
- **Purchase price:** $65.00 per Instacart share, a 26 % premium to the October 22, 2026 closing price.  
- **Consideration options per share:**  
  - $65 cash, or  
  - 0.3372 DoorDash shares, or  
  - $48.75 cash + 0.0843 DoorDash shares (subject to proration).  
- **Funding:** Cash on hand and a senior unsecured bridge loan from JPMorgan Chase; DoorDash aims to maintain an investment‑grade capital structure.  
- **Closing timeline:** Expected end of 2027 or first half of 2028, pending shareholder approval, antitrust review (Hart‑Scott‑Rodino), and other regulatory clearances (including Canada and Israel).  
- **Shareholder support:** Approximately 19 % of Instacart’s outstanding shares have already pledged to vote in favor.

## Strategic Rationale
- Combine DoorDash’s delivery network with Instacart’s grocery‑fulfillment technology to give stores of all sizes access to capabilities typically built by the largest online retailers.  
- Enable local grocers to run their own websites, retail media networks, and AI‑driven shopping tools, increasing sales and advertising revenue.  
- Provide consumers with more store choices, savings, and faster delivery; expand earning opportunities for Dashers and Instacart Shoppers.

## Benefits for Stakeholders
- **Consumers:** Access to over 50,000 additional stores (on top of Instacart’s ~100,000), unified membership (DashPass + Instacart+) saving an estimated $100 million annually.  
- **Dashers & Shoppers:** More order volume across grocery and restaurant categories, greater flexibility in when and how they work.  
- **Local Grocers:** Ability to deploy Instacart technology on their own sites, tap DoorDash’s DashMart fulfillment for faster, more accurate deliveries, and retain freedom to partner with any delivery platform.  
- **Brands:** Scaled alternative for reaching online customers through combined retail media and advertising tools.

## Post‑Close Commitments
- **Open Platform:** No exclusivity; local grocers may continue using any delivery service.  
- **Shopper Experience:** Instacart Shoppers retain existing features, flexibility, and the option to accept DoorDash orders or remain solely on Instacart.  
- **Operational Independence:** Until closing, DoorDash and Instacart will operate as separate entities.

## Executive Statements
- **Tony Xu, CEO, DoorDash:** Highlights Instacart’s expertise in grocery fulfillment and DoorDash’s delivery network as a combined solution to keep dollars local.  
- **Chris Rogers, CEO, Instacart:** Emphasizes that the partnership expands innovation, improves experiences for retailers, brands, customers, and shareholders, and makes online grocery more seamless and affordable.
