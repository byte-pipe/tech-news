Everyone nods. Blah, blah, blah. Nobody changes anything. A few years ago, I discovered Liberating Structures. It''s a collection of 43 facilitation methods created by Henri Lipmanowicz and Keith McCandless. The idea is simple: Change the format and you change what a group can accomplish. Since adopting them, I''ve seen better ideas surface faster, shyer voices contribute more often, and teams leave with clearer decisions. A few examples: ✅ 25/10 Crowdsourcing: surface a group''s best ideas in 10 minutes flat ✅ Nine Whys: make your project''s real purpose crystal clear ✅ Min Specs: find the minimum rules needed to do great work These aren''t icebreakers (and I do love icebreakers!).
