Colo and BaaS provider DaSTOR is usingMyota’sShard and Spread technology to create sharded backup data fragments. When attackers get through perimeter defenses, the encrypted, sharded and distributed data fragments captured are meaningless and computationally impossible to reassemble, yet they remain immediately accessible to authorized users. Advanced mathematical techniques make it computationally impossible for attackers to reconstitute the sharded data while at the same time allowing data owners to instantly and completely reconstruct their data even if fragments are lost or unavailable.

Storage system supplierStorONEis usingMyota’sShard and Spread technology to reduce SSD data occupancy. StorONE’s Real-Time Tiering software automatically keepsonly active data on flash while transparently placing inactive data on lower-cost media, enabling up to 9x more effective use of flash. Myota’s patented Shard and Spread tech encrypts data at write time and distributes cryptographic shards across independent storage locations. This ensures individual data fragments are independently meaningless and resistant to reconstruction, while still enabling seamless, real-time access for authorized users. Myota’s software delivers an extremely high level of protection against ransomware, data breaches and accidental data loss.

Gal Naor, CEO of StorONE, said: “Our architecture was designed to minimize flash usage while maintaining enterprise performance. By combining StorONEwith Myota’s Cyberstorage technology, organizations can achieve both infrastructure efficiency and the highest level of data security while maintaining full control over their storage infrastructure.”
