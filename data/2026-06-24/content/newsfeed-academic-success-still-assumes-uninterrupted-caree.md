Your News article on the academic motherhood penalty highlights a reality that many women in academic medicine experience at first hand: maternity leave remains misaligned with how academic success is measured (Nature652, 549–551; 2026). As a clinical academic general practitioner, I have found my job intellectually rewarding and flexible. Yet, on becoming a mother, I noticed an assumption embedded in many academic systems: that careers progress continuously.
