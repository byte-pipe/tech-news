# AI coding will soon get pricier than human developers

## Dive Brief
- Gartner predicts that rising token prices will make AI‑generated code costlier than the average developer’s salary by 2028 as consumption‑based pricing overtakes subscription models.  
- Variable AI costs and opaque token‑billing practices hinder enterprise leaders from forecasting and controlling spend.  
- Enterprises still lack maturity in AI projects and struggle to measure business impact, leading software‑engineering budgets to be depleted faster than expected.

## Dive Insight
- AI integration into software workflows is now the default, shifting employee time from writing code to managing AI outputs.  
- Token overspending is linked to ungoverned autonomous agents used in engineering departments.  
- As more developers adopt AI tools, light users will become mainstream, driving higher token consumption and overall spend.  
- Only about one‑quarter of enterprise C‑suite leaders have real‑time visibility into AI operating costs (KPMG).  
- Many AI agents run in the background for days without audit, leaving CFOs and CIOs unaware of accumulating expenses.  

## Recommendations for CIOs
- Consolidate and closely monitor AI usage across cloud platforms, copilots, agent frameworks, coding tools, business workflows, and team‑level experiments.  
- Adopt a “tokenomics” semantic model that links usage to cost, ownership, business value, workload, behavior patterns, and risk.  
- Identify and throttle agents that run discretionary loops on premium models, focusing on mission‑critical workflows.  
- Establish instrumentation and a triage plan for the AI agent fleet to maintain visibility into cost spikes or capability degradations.
