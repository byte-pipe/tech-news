# Addendum: Transmission of MPXV from fire‑footed rope squirrels to sooty mangabeys

## Key points of the addendum
- The original Nature paper reported monkeypox virus (MPXV) transmission from a fire‑footed rope squirrel (*Funisciurus pyrropus*) to sooty mangabeys.  
- Recent research (Curaudeau et al., 2025) proposes that West African populations of this squirrel constitute a distinct species, *F. leucostigma*.  
- A mitogenomic phylogeny performed by the authors confirms that the squirrel involved in their study belongs to the newly recognized taxon *F. leucostigma*.  
- Because major reference databases have not yet adopted this taxonomic change, the authors retained the original species name (*F. pyrropus*) in the published article.

## Reference supporting the taxonomic change
- Curaudeau, M. et al. “Squirrel reservoirs of monkeypox virus are sister species separated by the Sanaga River (Cameroon), as are the two main viral clades.” *One Health* 21, 101157 (2025).
