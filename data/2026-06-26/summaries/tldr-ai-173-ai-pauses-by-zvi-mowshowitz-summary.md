# AI #173: AI Pauses – Summary

## Main Event: Pause of Frontier AI
- The White House imposed export controls at 5:23 pm on Friday, shutting down Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5.  
- The Trump administration blamed a “jailbreak” reported by Amazon and summoned Dario Amodei, who argued that a shutdown was unnecessary.  
- The “jailbreak” consisted of asking the model to fix code, which exposed vulnerabilities; the model would refuse direct hacking prompts but could still help identify bugs.  
- The administration now demands Anthropic “fix” the jailbreak, but the author argues this is impossible: an AI is either capable of secure coding or it is not; the distinction cannot be patched away.  
- This has led to a seven‑day pause in the deployment of frontier AI models, with uncertainty about ending by July 1.
