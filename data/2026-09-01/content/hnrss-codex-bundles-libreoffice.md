I was poking around in my~/.cache/folder usingOmniDiskSweeperwhen I spotted something interesting. The OpenAI Codex desktop app (sincerebrandedto just ChatGPT) has 1.7GB of stuff in there in a folder calledcodex-primary-runtime, including a full Python installation, a full Node.js installation, and native binaries forPoppler, git, and theLibreOfficeopen source office suite (which forked from OpenOffice.org in 2010):

The~/.cache/codex-runtimes/codex-primary-runtime/plugins/openai-primary-runtime/plugins/documentsfolder includes skills which tell Codex how to find and use those binaries.
