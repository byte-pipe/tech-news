# Apple's top App Store exec, Phil Schiller, follows wave of exits as CEO Tim Cook steps down | TechCrunch

## Key developments
- Tim Cook is leaving his role as Apple’s CEO, prompting a series of senior departures.
- Phil Schiller, head of the App Store since 2015, stepped down from that position but remains at Apple as an Apple Fellow, a role that often precedes full retirement.
- Day‑to‑day App Store responsibilities will shift to Carson Oliver, a 14‑year Apple veteran, and the App Store will move from the marketing department to the services division led by Eddy Cue.

## Recent executive exits
- COO Jeff Williams retired in December after 27 years.
- Lisa Jackson, VP for Environment, Policy, and Social Initiatives, also retired in December.
- General counsel Kate Adams announced her retirement.
- Apple Pay head Jennifer Bailey plans to retire in October after more than 20 years.
- Vision Pro VP Paul Meade left in June to join OpenAI; the Vision Pro team has since been reduced by about 100 employees.

## Legal context
- Schiller oversaw Apple during the Epic Games antitrust lawsuit (filed 2020). The court largely ruled in Apple’s favor but required the company to allow developers to link to external payment options; the case remains ongoing.

## Outlook for new leadership
- John Ternus, the incoming CEO, will need to rebuild Apple’s leadership team while addressing ongoing challenges such as the Vision Pro restructuring and the Epic Games litigation.
