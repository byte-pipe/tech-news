# China’s top DRAM maker CXMT sues Pentagon over its blacklisting — argues chips are standard civilian JEDEC spec, not defense hardware

## Background
- CXMT (ChangXin Memory Technologies) is China’s leading DRAM producer.  
- The U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) placed CXMT on a list of companies “linked to the Chinese military” in January 2025, a designation retained by the Trump administration and reaffirmed in June 2025.  
- CXMT contends it has no ties to the People’s Liberation Army (PLA) and that the DoD’s decision lacks evidence.

## CXMT’s Claims
- The company spent over a year providing information to the DoD to overturn the classification.  
- A February notice indicating removal from the list was withdrawn the same day without explanation.  
- The DoD’s June classification cites indirect affiliation with China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) and the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission (SASAC).  
- CXMT argues that the “military‑grade” label stems from an advertisement by an unauthorized distributor, which later admitted the claim was erroneous.  
- Its DRAM products conform to JEDEC commercial standards and do not meet the stricter requirements for Chinese military‑grade semiconductors (e.g., temperature, packaging, testing, oversight).  
- CXMT holds no licenses to manufacture military‑use products and says its chips would be classified as “purely civilian” if produced in the U.S.

## Impact of the Blacklist
- Inclusion on the DoD list can restrict eligibility for U.S. government contracts, though CXMT seeks few such contracts.  
- More critically, the designation harms commercial relationships and reputation, as many firms avoid partners labeled as linked to the Chinese military.  
- CXMT reports continuous commercial and reputational damage since the January 2025 designation.

## Legal Action
- CXMT has filed a lawsuit seeking removal from the DoD list, arguing due‑process violations and lack of supporting evidence.  
- The lawsuit aims to protect the company’s business interests and restore its market standing.
