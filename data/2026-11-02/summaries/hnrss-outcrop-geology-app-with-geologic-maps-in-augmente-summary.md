# Outcrop — Geology app with geologic maps in augmented reality

## Earth’s history  
- Displays a rock’s age within Earth’s 4.5 billion‑year timeline.  
- Periods shown:  
  - Cenozoic 66–0 Ma  
  - Mesozoic 252–66 Ma  
  - Paleozoic 539–252 Ma  
  - Precambrian 4540–539 Ma  
- Tap or hover a line to read details about a period or event.

## Geologic maps in augmented reality  
- Point the phone at a canyon wall, road cut, or ridge.  
- The app overlays the geologic map on the landscape, naming the central rock and labeling visible peaks and lakes.  
- Selecting a rock keeps it colored while other features turn gray.

## Know what’s under your feet  
- The map centers on the rock you stand on, showing its name, age, composition, and formation.  
- Tap any other spot to view information for that location.

## Read the rock below  
- When Macrostrat provides a column for a spot, the app shows the subsurface units from surface down.  
- Each unit displays its age, any time gaps, and associated fossils.  
- Tap a unit for a detailed description.

## See where it formed  
- Convert the map into a globe representing the rock’s age, using Christopher Scotese’s PALEOMAP data.  
- Visualize past positions, movement speed, and plate trajectories.  
- Press play to animate the plate’s journey to the present.

## Find any rock  
- Search by unit, age, rock type, or fault.  
- The map highlights all mapped occurrences and indicates the distance to the nearest one, even off‑screen.

## From bedrock to topsoil  
- Access detailed bedrock and fault information from Macrostrat’s geologic maps.  
- Switch to Quaternary geology for recent glacial, river deposits, active faults, soils, and vegetation.  
- View data both on the map and through the camera.  
- Coverage: worldwide bedrock and faults; US‑focused Quaternary, active faults, and soils.

## Save it before you go  
- Outline an area to store offline on the phone.  
- Offline mode retains map and camera view; fossil finds and active faults still require an internet connection.

## Availability  
- Released on Google Play; beta version available for iPhone via TestFlight.
