# A fully playable version of Portal prequel F‑Stop leaked

## Key points
- Leakers have assembled a near‑complete, playable build of the cancelled Portal prequel **F‑Stop**.
- Most assets originated from a 12‑terabyte leak released in August; the missing source code and map data were later shared on the Valve Cut Content Discord, enabling the reconstruction.
- F‑Stop features a unique camera mechanic: players photograph objects and later place them using Polaroid‑style photos, similar to the game *Viewfinder*.
- The full campaign runs under an hour, reflecting its original design as a short experimental title.
- Developed in 2007 during Valve’s “Directed Design Experiments,” a three‑month program where five teams created prototype games.
- Some dialogue and elements from F‑Stop were later incorporated into *Portal 2*.
