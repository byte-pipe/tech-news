# The Kobeissi Letter on X: "AI spending is rapidly accelerating in the US.

US private fixed investment in information processing equipment and software grew at an annualized rate of +7.3% in Q3 2026, to a record $1.77 trillion.

This marks the largest quarterly increase since Q4 1983.

By comparison, at the height of the 2000 Dot-Com Bubble, this metric grew +6.2%.

Since Q4 2022, private fixed investment in information processing equipment and software has surged +61%, or +$671 billion.

As a % of GDP, this metric is up to a record 5.4%, significantly surpassing the previous high of 4.5% recorded in Q4 2000.

The scale of US AI investment is unlike anything seen before."
