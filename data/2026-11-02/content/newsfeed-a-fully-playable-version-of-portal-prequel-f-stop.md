# A fully playable version of Portal prequel F-Stop leaked

Nearly 20 years after it was canceled, the camera-based Portal prequel F-Stop is no longer lost media.

Leakers have pieced together a playable version of thecanceledPortalprequelF-Stop. Most of the assets came from the massive12-terabyte leakthat dropped this August. That didn’t include the game’s source code or map data, but those final pieces apparently hit the Valve Cut Content Discord this week, allowing a nearly complete version to be pieced together.

Some dialog and elements ofF-Stopeventually found their way intoPortal 2. But it differs dramatically from the mainlinePortalgames in that it’s built around a camera mechanic that lets you capture an item by taking its picture and place it elsewhere using the resulting Polaroid-like photos. It’s not unlikeViewfinderin that way.

The game is short, with videos showing off its full campaign all clocking in at under an hour. That’s not terribly surprising, though.F-Stopwas developed as part of the “Directed Design Experiments,” a three-month period in 2007 when Valve broke into five teams to build new video game experiments.
