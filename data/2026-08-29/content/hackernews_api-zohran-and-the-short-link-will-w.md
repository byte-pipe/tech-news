If you live in NYC and are anywhere on social media, you’ll likely have
 seen Zohran's videos.

 

Oftentimes, his videos announce a new project or initiative or event.

 

If the announcement is something that you can
 actively participate in, the videos also always end with a short, top-level link:nyc.gov/{initiative}.

 
 
 
nyc.gov/
 
* ├──click-to-cancel
* ├──combat-antisemitism
* ├──EndStreetHarassment
* ├──jingle
* ├──knicks
* ├──knicksgame3
* ├──rental-ripoff
* └──Summer
 
 
 

I love that. I think it:

 
1. Makes government easy to engage with. It’s a human-readable
 URL that I can easily follow through on. It also funnels social media distribution into one place, owned and operated by NYC.
2. Sets up a pattern of interaction.nyc.govis where I can
 expect to interact with city government online.
3. Follows up announcements with actual experiences.
