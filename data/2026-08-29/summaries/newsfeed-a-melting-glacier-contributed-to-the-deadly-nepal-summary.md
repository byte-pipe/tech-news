# A melting glacier contributed to the deadly Nepal floods. We can expect more like this

## Overview
- Flash floods on 28 August 2026 struck Nepal and Tibet after a large ice chunk broke off a glacier and plunged into the valley.
- More than 1,000 people are missing and at least 400 have been confirmed dead.
- The disaster destroyed homes, bridges, and riverside settlements along the Trishuli River in Nepal’s Nuwakot district.

## Primary Causes
- **Glacier collapse:** A massive piece of ice detached from a glacier, sending rock, ice, and mud down the mountain for miles.
- **Monsoon rains:** Heavy seasonal rains had already saturated the soil and swollen the rivers, amplifying the flood’s severity.
- **Climate change:** Human‑caused warming is accelerating glacier melt and destabilizing permafrost, making such events more likely.
