## This Week's Opportunities

### Razorpay AI Buildathon
- Target: Students in AI/software engineering who can attend in Bangalore.

- Tracks: AI Growth & Agentic Commerce, AI Risk Manager, AI Revenue Recovery, AI Finance Controller, Open Track.  
- Outcome: Project evaluated for a 6‑ or 12‑month AI Builder Internship with ₹75,000 monthly stipend.  
- Application: Resume, project details, public repo, pitch video; no aptitude test or group discussion.  

### Agents for Humans Hackathon
- Target: Developers, students, builders worldwide (excluding certain regions).  
- Format: Six‑week online hackathon using Strands Agents SDK.  
- Tracks: Everyday Agents (home/errands), Professional Agents (work tasks), Good Neighbor Agents (community orgs).  
- Goal: Build an AI agent that automates a repetitive real‑world task.
