Correction to:Naturehttps://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10250-yPublished online 18 March 2026

In the version of this article initially published, there were errors in the first paragraph of the “Haem directs T cell fate through BACH2” section, where citations to Fig. 3b,c, Fig. 3e, Fig. 3f,g, Fig. 3g and Fig. 3h originally cited Fig. 4b,c, Fig. 4e, Fig. 4f,g, Fig. 4g and Fig. 4h, respectively. In Fig. 3d,f–h, the current colour keys (Bach2WTin purple,Bach2MUTin blue) were originally reversed owing to removal of a figure from the last revised version.  In addition, the Source Data file associated with Fig. 5j contained a copy-and-paste error. The text, figure and source data are now amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
