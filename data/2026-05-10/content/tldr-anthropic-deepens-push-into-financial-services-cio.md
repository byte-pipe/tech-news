### Dive Insight:

As AI providers Anthropic and OpenAI target enterprise customers ahead oftheir upcoming IPOs, Anthropic is taking a vertical-focused approach in developing tools to change workflows in specific industries and shoring up its infrastructure needs through new partnerships.

Like its release for healthcare andlegal, Claude’s finance agents will tackle tasks specific to the industry, such as creating pitchbooks for client meetings, reviewing transcripts and filings, creating financial models and analyses and reviewing valuations. The agents will work in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Work and Outlook with plugins,Anthropicsaid.

AI providers are facing bottlenecks with the infrastructure, compute and investment resources needed to keep up with demand. Most enterprise companies reported in a recentDeloittereport that they plan totriple their AI infrastructure budgetsby 2028.Last month,Anthropic signed new agreementswithGoogle and Broadcomto add multiple gigawatts of tensor processing units capacitystarting in 2027as customer demand accelerates.

Anthropic’s formation of an AI services company with private equity investors more closely ties the relationship between Wall Street and AI. The new company is also backed by a collection of asset managers.

The newly formed and yet-to-be-named venture will pair Claude models with a network of mid-sized, investor-owned companies, embedding engineers into various aspects of their workflows or building custom tools. The company is positioning itself to gain an edge in middle-market adoption of the technology, as it aims to become a go-to model in private and public sectors.

“Our partnerships with the world's leading systems integrators are central to how Claude reaches large enterprises,”Raosaid in a statement.
