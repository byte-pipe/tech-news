# Best Live‑Captioning Smart Glasses (2026) – WIRED Test Summary  

## Overview  
- Live‑captioning glasses turn spoken words into real‑time subtitles displayed on a tiny, single‑color lens (usually green).  
- They are a niche subset of smart glasses focused on transcription, translation, and basic AI assistance rather than typical features like camera recording or music playback.  
- WIRED tested several models and found the **Even Realities G2** to be the clear winner.  

## Core Capabilities  

- **Transcription & Caption Archive** – Captures speech in real time and saves the text to a paired smartphone for later reference.  
- **Language Translation** – Provides on‑the‑fly subtitles in up to 35 languages; most apps support a basic two‑way translation loop.  
- **AI Enhancements (optional)** – Some models include voice‑assistant queries, automatic summarisation of transcripts, and other AI‑driven utilities.  

## Subscription & Pricing  

- All glasses work out of the box with a free tier that covers basic transcription and translation.  
- Most vendors offer paid plans (minute, monthly, or yearly) that unlock higher‑quality captions, additional languages, and premium AI features.  
- The Even Realities G2 includes all features without any subscription.  

## Offline Functionality  

- Offline support varies; devices that claim offline mode often perform worse, especially for translation.  
- Vendors generally recommend staying connected via phone or Wi‑Fi for optimal accuracy.  

## Medical & Vision Considerations  

- Classified as hearing‑support and vision‑support devices, making them eligible for vision‑insurance, FSA, or HSA reimbursement.  
- Prescription lenses can be ordered directly (starting at $159) or added via clip‑on/ magnetic adapters, though the latter can add bulk.  
- The caption overlay can interfere with close‑up tasks, so users may need to switch to regular glasses for reading or computer work.  

## Drawbacks (The Catch)  

- Weight: Up to three times heavier than standard glasses, often sitting high on the nose bridge with limited adjustability.  
- Battery life: Measured in hours (≈10 h for the G2) rather than days, limiting continuous wear.  
- Aesthetics: Many models look “ridiculous” to style‑conscious users.  

## The Only Captioning Glasses WIRED Would Buy  

### Even Realities G2  
- **Price:** $599 (prescription lenses extra).  
- **Design:** Two frame styles, three colors; lighter (≈43 g) and slimmer than competitors; prescription lenses integrated at order.  
- **Features:** Real‑time transcription, 35‑language translation, note summarisation, HUD navigation, customizable dashboard (news, stocks, calendar), app ecosystem via Even Hub, optional smart ring for discreet controls and health tracking.  
- **Battery:** ~10 h per charge; case provides seven full recharges.  
- **Subscription:** None required; all functionality included.  
- **Limitation:** Relies on internet connection for most features; offline capabilities are minimal.  

## Other Tested Models (Brief)  

- Several alternative captioning glasses were evaluated; they shared similar form factors and basic transcription/translation functions but lacked the G2’s polish, battery life, and extensibility.  
- Most required clip‑on lenses, were heavier, and offered subscription‑based upgrades.
