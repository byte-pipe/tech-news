# Apple is increasing my cortisol levels

## Summary of the article
- I built a simple Go‑based utility to manage Claude Code profiles and can compile it as a static executable for any platform.  
- Distribution works smoothly on Windows (`.exe`) and Linux (executable after `chmod +x`).  
- On macOS the binary is blocked by Gatekeeper quarantine, showing the “unidentified developer” warning.  
- Users can bypass the warning manually in Terminal, but that is a poor experience for a public release.  

## Apple Developer Program hurdles
- Signing the binary requires enrollment in the Apple Developer Program, which costs a yearly fee.  
- My planned pricing model (pay‑what‑you‑want on itch.io, many users choosing $0) would not cover the subscription after Apple’s VAT and itch.io’s cut.  
- The cost feels like a barrier that pushes hobbyist developers out of the ecosystem.  

## Verification process frustrations
- Apple requires a webcam photo of my ID for account activation.  
- The built‑in MacBook webcam produced poor images; external webcams lacked autofocus, leading to repeated failures.  
- After many attempts I succeeded only by using the iPhone app, but the desktop portal still showed the enrollment as pending, forcing me to start over.  

## Broader grievances with Apple’s ecosystem
- I appreciate Apple hardware quality (M‑series chips, build) and enjoy the iPhone SE 2022.  
- However, recent product decisions (removal of budget models, loss of silent‑mode toggle, Touch ID, 3.5 mm jack) make me question future purchases.  
- macOS imposes limitations that require third‑party tools (AutoRaise, Rectangle, DiscreteScroll) to achieve a workflow comparable to Linux.  
- Other annoyances include unconventional keyboard shortcuts, Finder lacking a “Cut” command, and apps that appear closed but remain in memory.  

## Core complaint
- Apple forces developers into a costly, closed ecosystem: you must pay to sign binaries, struggle with ID verification that depends on expensive hardware, and endure delays and unclear status updates.  
- This combination of financial and technical barriers leads to strong frustration, expressed in the article’s concluding profanity.  

## A glimpse of alternatives
- Other countries offer fast digital ID verification (e.g., SmartID) and electronic signing solutions (eParaksts) that work on PCs and phones without Apple’s restrictions.  
- These services demonstrate that secure, user‑friendly verification can exist outside Apple’s walled garden.
