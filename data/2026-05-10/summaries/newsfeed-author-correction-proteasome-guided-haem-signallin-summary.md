# Author Correction: Proteasome‑guided haem signalling axis contributes to T cell exhaustion

## Summary of Corrections
- The first paragraph of the “Haem directs T cell fate through BACH2” section contained incorrect citations: references to Fig. 3b,c, Fig. 3e, Fig. 3f,g, Fig. 3g and Fig. 3h were mistakenly linked to Fig. 4b,c, Fig. 4e, Fig. 4f,g, Fig. 4g and Fig. 4h. These citations have been corrected to the appropriate Fig. 3 panels.  
- In Fig. 3d and Fig. 3f–h the colour keys were reversed (Bach2WT shown in purple, Bach2MUT in blue) due to the removal of a figure in the last revised version. The colour keys have been restored to their correct assignment.  
- The Source Data file associated with Fig. 5j contained a copy‑and‑paste error. The file has been replaced with the correct data.  
- All amended text, figures and source‑data files are now reflected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
