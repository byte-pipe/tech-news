# June Solstice Game Jam Overview

## What is the jam
- Runs from June 3 through June 21, ending on the solstice.
- Open to participants worldwide; celebrates the solstice, Pride month, Juneteenth, and other June events.

## Prompt
- Build a game inspired by the solstice or any June celebration (light and darkness, passage of time, Pride, Juneteenth, World Cup, etc.).
- The theme is intentionally broad; any creative interpretation is welcome.

## Additional prize categories
- **Best Ode to Alan Turing** – incorporate algorithms, code‑breaking, or AI that honors the father of modern computing.
- **Best Google AI Usage** – showcase how Google AI tools (Antigravity, Gemini, Cloud Run, etc.) were used in the game.

## Judging criteria
- Relevance to the theme.
- Creativity of the concept.
- Technical execution and playability.
- Quality of the submission write‑up.
- (Optional) Effective use of an additional prize category.

## Prizes
- Three overall winners plus one winner for each additional prize category (five winners total).
- Each winner receives:
  - $200 USD
  - DEV++ Membership
  - Exclusive DEV badge
- All participants with a valid submission earn a completion badge.

## How to participate
- Build a game and publish a post on DEV using the provided submission template.
- Include a demo video and an overview of how the game was built.
- Review the judging criteria, rules, guidelines, and FAQ before submitting.

## Important dates
- **June 3** – Jam begins.
- **June 21, 11:59 PM PDT** – Submissions due.
- **July 9** – Winners announced.
