# Apple’s Messages app on iPhone now has a third-party AI agent

## Overview
- Poke has become the first third‑party AI agent approved for use inside Apple’s Messages app on iPhone.  
- Approval was granted through Apple’s “Messages for Business” framework, allowing users to chat with the AI directly in iMessage and have it perform actions on their requests.  

## How it works
- Users can start a conversation with Poke by following the setup instructions on Poke’s website.  
- The service offers various examples of tasks it can handle, including integrations with other third‑party services (details provided on Poke’s site).  

## Current status
- Some users, including the author, have reported delayed or missing replies from Poke shortly after launch.  
- The issue appears to be related to high demand rather than a withdrawal of Apple’s approval, though the situation remains under observation.
