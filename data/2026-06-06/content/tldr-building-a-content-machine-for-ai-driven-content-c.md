description: 'I built a content machine. It turned me into a one-person media company, has driven tens of millions in pipeline for Tenex, and is allergic to AI-slop. I may opensource the whole thing, but for now, I''m going to share how I built it & how it works. Step 1: Map out the process Content has been my bread & butter for the last decade, so I started by pulling out an 8.5x11 sheet of printer paper and drawing the traditional process. 1) Look for inspiration 2) Pick a 10x content idea 3) Research the idea 4) Brain dump all of my thoughts about the idea 5) Decide the post format I want to create 6) Create a draft of the post 7) Edit the post 8) Create derivative versions of the post 9) Go live 10) Track performance Step 2: Where am I needed vs. not needed? First mile: picking the idea/direction & providing necessary context Final mile: fine tooth comb & giving final sign-off. AI can handle the rest. Step 3: Build the Content Machine A directory of skills that mimic the new AI-native
  content process. 1) The Oracle [AI] Mines my Slack, Notion, and Gmail for spikes, moments I naturally said something worth expanding, while the Internet Reader curates an external feed of X accounts & websites I''ve selected. Qualifying ideas (≥6/10) are written to The Vault (a notion database of content ideas). 2) Select the idea [Human] 3) The Researcher [AI] Build a sourced research-report.md: TL;DR, key facts, current developments, contrarian angles, and open questions. Claims are adversarially checked; fact is separated from opinion. 4) Interview Panel [AI + Human] Six world-class interviewers ask 15 questions, one at a time, each pushing a different dimension...never satisfied with vague answers. Won''t advance without 2–3 specific stories, real numbers, and emotional specificity. 5) Production [AI] The interview becomes a raw .md file: transcript, key stories, core insights, quotable moments, emotional anchor. This raw file is sacred: my exact words, never paraphrased away. 6) Refinement
  [AI + Human] I tell the machine what content type I want to create. It reads my custom style guide + past feedback lessons + content-type spec, then drafts in my voice...pulling real stories and quotes from the raw file. 7) Writer''s Council [AI] Six expert reviewers score the draft through their own lens, splitting fixes into editorial (the machine can rewrite) and information gaps (these route back to the interview panel). 8) Revision Loop [AI] Iterate until council scores 9/10. 9) Repurposing Engine [AI] One anchor → 10+ natively-formatted derivatives, each re-hooked for its platform and each held to the same full Council → revision bar of 9/10. This is how two people produce like a hundred. 10) Final revision [Human] 11) Learning Loop [AI] After approval, the machine compares first draft vs. final, extracts confirmed lessons, and saves them to that creator''s content-lessons.md. Feel free to steal the machine & ask me any questions about how it works! | 48 comments on LinkedIn'
