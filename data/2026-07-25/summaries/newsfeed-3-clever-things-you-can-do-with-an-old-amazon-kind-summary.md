# 3 Clever Things You Can Do With an Old Amazon Kindle | WIRED

**3 Clever Things You Can Do With an Old Amazon Kindle**

You can repurpose your old Amazon Kindle in three clever ways:

## Turn Your Kindle into a Spotify Controller
* Install the **Spotify Connect software** on your computer, from which you can select music and podcasts to play on your Kindle.
* Use the same Wi-Fi network as other devices connected to Spotify, but no app is required to control playback.

## Turn Your Kindle into a Smart Home Display
* Set up a custom website using a site builder or online tool, linking weather forecasts, calendar information, and todo lists collected from other services (e.g., Google Calendar).
* Use the Kindle's e-Ink screen to display the gathered data, creating a smart home display that provides a centralized view of available information.

### Challenges and Notes:

* All three projects require some technical expertise, including setting up new software or websites, so be prepared for some hands-on work.
* Be sure to have an old Kindle with E Ink screen (5th generation or later) before attempting this project.
* As with any hack, consider using alternative methods for storing and managing music files on your device.
