### Meta Pauses Smart Glasses Subscription Fee Plan due to Backlash

Following backlash from its announcement on July 24, 2026, regarding a planned subscription fee for the smart glasses within the Meta device that enhances hearing clarity, the company has paused this particular plan.

The initial idea proposed charging a $20 monthly subscription fee for a feature called Conversation Focus — an accessibility tool inside Smart Glasses. Although this feature can run locally on your glasses without requiring a cloud service, Meta planned to charge users a recurring fee.

Meta representative Tyler Yee confirms that even though "Conversation Focus will remain available for free through our Early Access Program for early testers," certain premium features including the subscription-based 'rate limits' will make their way to paid services over time and they're actually still exploring options for this feature. 

The key points of the conversation include:

*   Meta is currently pausing its "rate limit" test with Conversation Focus
*   Not all premium features will be subscription-based
*   This pause is in response to feedback received, indicating that the company still aims to expand Accessible Features through paid services
