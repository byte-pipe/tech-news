Everyday, we’re seeing creators thrive and grow on LinkedIn, and we’re committed to helping them unlock even more success on the platform in new ways. One of the biggest opportunities lies in making it easier for creators and brands to find and partner with one another. Our new Creator Marketplace is designed to streamline and support just that.

Rolling out this month, the Creator Marketplace helps creators become more discoverable to brands who are looking to connect and partner with credible voices on LinkedIn. This can include different types of brand partnership opportunities, from Thought Leader Ads, to speaking gigs to branded partnerships and more.

Sounds cool, right? But how does it work? Here’s what you need to know:

Eligibility:Access to the Creator Marketplace is currently invite-only, and we will expand access over time. Eligibility is based on factors like expertise, content quality, platform presence, and topic alignment with advertiser demand. Those who are invited to the experience allow LinkedIn to make select information about them available in Campaign Manager (like their preferred email for brands to reach out to).

How to Opt-In: Creators who have been invited to opt-in will have access to the new Monetization tab in the coming weeks. This tab serves as a homebase for eligible creators to learn about programs, opt in, manage participation, and see monetization opportunities that may become available to them.

So essentially: You get the invite -> you opt in -> brands can find you within the marketplace -> magic happens from there.

 

Brand Connection:We are making it easier for brands to identify the right creators and reach out to them directly. From there, brands and creators establish partnership details, including any fees and payments, directly with each other.

Across the board, creators on LinkedIn are finding widespread opportunities, whether it’s through paid deals, brand building, or expanding their professional influence and connections, and we expect the marketplace to accelerate that growth. Two stories that particularly stand out include:

"I'm excited to be part of LinkedIn's Creator Marketplace. As professionals, we have long been multidimensional and multifaceted, and I love that being publicly celebrated. LinkedIn is the ideal platform for these partnerships because brands are no longer solely concerned with reach alone. They're looking for credibility, context, and genuine audience connection." -Melissa Cohen

“LinkedIn has genuinely transformed my career in ways I never expected. What started as a casual thought dump online turned into speaking opportunities, brand partnerships, podcast guests I once looked up to, and an incredible community of ambitious people around the world. As someone building at the intersection of tech, storytelling, and entrepreneurship, I’m excited to see LinkedIn continue investing in creators and making it easier for trusted voices to connect with brands and opportunities in a meaningful way.” -Aashna Doshi

In addition to our Creator Marketplace, we’re continuing to add additional ways for creators to turn their expertise into opportunities. These include:

BrandLink: Creators earn a share of ad revenue from pre-roll ads that run alongside their content.Top Voices 360: An integrated brand partnership that includes an exclusive editorial show, co-branded assets, in-feed videos, and live event appearances.Advice Sessions: We are starting to roll out the ability for entrepreneurs and experts to offer paid 1:1 consultations directly from their LinkedIn Profile, sharing insights and getting paid for what they already know.LinkedIn Learning: LinkedIn Learning allows experts to earn revenue by creating courses, with options for royalties, licensing, or affiliate commissions.

We are excited to expand the possibilities with our Creator Marketplace this month. For more information, check out more here:Link!
