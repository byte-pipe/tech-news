# In the Age of AI, You Need a Point of View

## Market uncertainty and what buyers need
- Big market shifts create uncertainty that makes long‑term purchase decisions hard for technology buyers.  
- Buyers are not immersed in tech news; they must educate themselves amid a flood of AI information.  
- Research shows buyers consult about seven sources; when AI appears, 69 % still rely on sales reps to validate insights.  
- Reps remain the primary source for understanding business problems, selecting suppliers, gaining internal support, and closing deals.  
- Prospects want a clear, confident point of view on the future to feel safe buying today for use in five years.

## Point of view is not product vision
- Product vision describes the future shape of the product for the team and investors.  
- Customers care about today’s product and will delay purchases if today’s offering feels weak, regardless of a compelling long‑term vision.  
- A point of view (POV) explains how you see the market evolving and the assumptions guiding your roadmap; it is distinct from the internal product vision.

## Building a POV rooted in distinct strengths
- A strong POV should be based on what your company can do better than anyone else.  
- **OpenAI** (Sam Altman): AI as a utility like electricity or water—fits a consumer‑focused model.  
- **Anthropic** (Dario Amodei): AI will write 100 % of code within a year—targets B2B coding markets.  
- **Microsoft** (Satya Nadella): Models are commoditized; “context engineering” is the differentiator, leveraging Microsoft’s ownership of business documents and data.  
- **ServiceNow** (Bill McDermott): Workflows and governance, not models, drive safe AI adoption—aligns with ServiceNow’s existing workflow platform.

## Do you have a POV?
- Ask yourself: When prospects inquire about the future, do you have a clear POV?  
- Is your roadmap built on principles you expect to hold true?  
- Are you communicating that POV so customers can judge whether they want to travel with you?  
- Comparing your POV with competitors’ helps you see where you stand and where you can differentiate.

## Closing thoughts
- A well‑articulated POV helps prospects understand the rationale behind product choices and roadmap direction, influencing their buying decision.
