# A tech worker‑backed PAC is bringing a $5M knife to Big Tech’s $100M gunfight

## Overview
- The Guardrails Alliance, a new super PAC, was launched by Democratic operatives Shaunna Thomas and Leah Hunt‑Hendrix.  
- Backed by tech employees, labor unions, and other groups, it positions itself as a grassroots, populist movement demanding responsible AI development and deployment.  
- The PAC aims to support AI‑focused legislation and give a political voice to workers on the “front lines” of the AI boom.

## Funding and Strategy
- Guardrails currently controls about **$5 million** and plans to raise **$15 million** in the current election cycle.  
- This is modest compared to rival super PAC **Leading the Future**, which has amassed **over $100 million** from tech leaders such as OpenAI President Greg Brockman.  
- The strategy emphasizes small donations from everyday tech workers rather than trying to match the opponent’s spending dollar for dollar.

## Political Activity
- Guardrails will purchase advertising to back **Alex Bores**, a New York congressional candidate who has become the first target of Leading the Future.  
- Bores aired an ad featuring the parents of Adam Raine, the teenager who died by suicide after prolonged interactions with ChatGPT.  
- Bores also receives support from **Public First Action**, another pro‑legislation super PAC backed by Anthropic.

## Broader Context and Issues
- Tech workers this year have mobilized to demand that their companies end contracts with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).  
- They are also urging the Pentagon to withdraw its “supply‑chain‑risk” designation of Anthropic, a label critics say was imposed without due process as retaliation for Anthropic’s limits on surveillance and autonomous‑warfare uses of its technology.  
- Guardrails frames its mission as providing a political home for those concerned about an anti‑regulation AI sector attempting to manipulate elections, rather than simply matching financial firepower.

## Key Quotes
- Thomas: “Our fundamental belief here is that people still do have the power to stop this autocratic takeover of the Trump administration and the tech sector.”  
- Thomas: “This vehicle is meant to be a political home for people who are concerned about the way the anti‑regulation AI tech sector is trying to manipulate elections.”
