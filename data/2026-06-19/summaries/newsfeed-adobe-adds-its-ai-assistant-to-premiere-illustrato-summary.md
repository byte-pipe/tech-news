# Adobe adds its AI assistant to Premiere, Illustrator and InDesign

## Key updates
- Adobe expands its Firefly AI assistant to Premiere, Illustrator, InDesign and Frame.io.  
- New capabilities include generating brand kits, product videos, and storyboards.  
- The Firefly app now lets users save generated characters, objects, or locations as reusable “Elements.”

## New features in specific apps
- **Premiere**: AI can sort assets into bins, batch‑rename clips, detect interview questions, and add markers.  
- **Illustrator**: AI can reorganize layers across a document and check for missing fonts.  

## Firefly platform enhancements
- **Elements**: Saves AI‑generated assets for reuse across projects (private beta).  
- **Projects**: Centralizes existing assets and shared context for team‑based video series or brand campaigns (private beta).  
- Users can describe a brand or upload collateral to generate a full brand kit (logos, identity, color palettes) and create product videos from photos.  

## Integration and support ecosystem
- Firefly already works with Express, Photoshop, and Acrobat.  
- Supported AI models include ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot; Adobe plans to add Google Gemini and Slack soon.  

## Outlook
- Adobe is progressively embedding AI throughout its Creative Cloud apps, aiming for an assistant that automates multi‑step workflows across the suite.
