# Adobe’s redesigned AI studio remembers what your creations look like

## Overview
- Adobe launched a private‑beta redesign of its Firefly AI studio, aiming to provide “persistent context, reusable assets, and organized workflows” so creators can move from idea to production without hopping between apps.  
- The update is the latest overhaul since Firefly’s initial release in September 2023.

## Key New Features
- **Elements** – lets users name and save characters, locations, and objects (e.g., “Charlie’s bedroom”) for easy reuse across Firefly and Firefly Boards, reducing the need to re‑type detailed prompts.  
- **Projects** – a workspace that groups assets, generations, and creative context, enabling users to pick up exactly where they left off.  
- **Brand kit generation** – the Firefly AI assistant can create logos, color palettes, and full brand kits from simple textual descriptions of a company’s name and style.  
- **Video tools** –  
  - *Quick Cut*: automatically assembles video clips into a polished first draft.  
  - Storyboard generation and conversion of images into short‑form video, allowing image‑based storyboards to become moving content.  

## Workflow Impact
- The conversational editing capabilities are designed to cut tedious tasks while preserving creative control; users can start a project with AI prompts and then fine‑tune results in Firefly or other Creative Cloud apps.  
- Adobe positions Firefly as a “co‑working partner” rather than a replacement for human designers, adapting to varied user preferences for interaction.

## Quote from Adobe
- Forest Key, Adobe’s VP of agentic AI for creativity and productivity, said the goal is for Firefly to meet creators “however they want to work with the agent,” acknowledging that some will rely heavily on natural‑language commands while others will not.

## Context
- The redesign follows earlier UI updates and adds functionality that streamlines asset management, brand creation, and video editing within a single integrated interface.
