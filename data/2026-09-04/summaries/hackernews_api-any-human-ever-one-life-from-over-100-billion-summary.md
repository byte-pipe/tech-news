# Any Human Ever: one life from over 100 billion

## Overview
- The project visualizes a single, randomly selected human life from the more than 100 billion people who have ever existed.  
- It uses real demographic data to choose a birth year, location, and a brief life narrative.

## How it works
- **When**: A birth year is drawn from a distribution that reflects exponential population growth; most random births fall in recent centuries.  
- **Where**: A location is selected based on historical population density, with brighter clusters indicating regions that held more people at the chosen time.  
- **Life**: After year and place are set, a concise story of that individual’s life is generated from the underlying data.

## User interaction
- Click “Redraw year” or “Redraw location” to generate new random selections.  
- Press “Continue →” after each step to move to the next stage.  
- Once the life is drawn, you can read the story or start over with “Draw another life”.

## Key observations
- Randomly chosen births are heavily weighted toward the modern era because of exponential growth.  
- Geographic selection mirrors historical population concentrations, highlighting where most people lived at any given time.
