# King and Queen lead tributes for David Attenborough's 100th birthday  

## Royal tributes  
- King Charles III and Queen Camilla posted birthday wishes and shared historic photos of Sir David with Prince Charles and Princess Anne from 1958, when he introduced them to “Cocky” the cockatoo.  
- The royal couple wrote: “Enjoy your special celebration this evening!”  

## Messages from the royal family and other public figures  
- Prince William (Prince of Wales) sent a video for the Earthshot Prize, calling the milestone “Happy 100th David” and thanking Attenborough for his inspiring work.  
- Prince Harry, the Duke of Sussex, described Sir David as a “secular saint” in Time, noting his role in making climate issues feel immediate.  
- Sir David Beckham called the broadcaster “our National Treasure.”  
- Actress Joanna Lumley sent a video greeting with the people of Stroud.  
- Naturalist Chris Packham wrote that no one has contributed more to public love of life on Earth.  
- WWF released a tribute video voiced by Dame Judi Dench, Morgan Freeman, Miranda Richardson, Asa Butterfield, Sam Heughan, Iwan Rheon, Geri Halliwell‑Horner and Liz Bonnin, set to “What a Wonderful World.”  
- Composer Hans Zimmer said working for Attenborough matters more than his film career.  
- Sir Ian McKellen praised Attenborough as the best of the BBC’s serious programmes for a popular audience.  

## 100th‑birthday concert at the Royal Albert Hall  
- A 90‑minute live concert, hosted by Kirsty Young, aired on BBC One and iPlayer (20:30 BST).  
- Guests included Sir Michael Palin, Steve Backshall, Liz Bonnin and Chris Packham, who reflected on Attenborough’s legacy.  
- The BBC Concert Orchestra performed music associated with his series (e.g., “Planet Earth II” snake chase, “Frozen Planet II” orca sequence).  
- Musical contributions: Dan Smith (Bastille) with the orchestra on “Pompeii,” Sigur Rós on “Hoppípolla,” singer Sienna Spiro and harpist Francisco Yglesia.  

## BBC programming and other celebrations  
- A week of special broadcasts marked the centenary, including a documentary on the making of the 1979 series *Life on Earth*.  
- The new series *Secret Garden* featured Attenborough exploring British garden wildlife.  
- Many of his classic programmes are available as a dedicated collection on iPlayer.  
- BBC chief content officer Kate Philips called the birthday an “extraordinary” moment and highlighted Attenborough as a “truly remarkable individual.”  

## Additional honours  
- The Natural History Museum named a newly identified Patagonian parasitic wasp *Attenboroughnculus tau* after him.  
- Previously, a wildflower, butterfly, grasshopper, dinosaur and ghost shrimp have also borne his name.  

## Personal background (brief)  
- Born 8 May 1926 in west London; joined the BBC in 1952.  
- Married to Jane (deceased 1997) with two children.  
- Brother Richard Attenborough, Oscar‑winning actor/director, died in 2014.
