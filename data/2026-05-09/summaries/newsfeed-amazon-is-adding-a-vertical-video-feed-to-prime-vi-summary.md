# Amazon is adding a vertical video feed to Prime Video

## Overview
- Prime Video introduces a new vertical “Clips” feed that shows short‑form snippets from shows and movies.
- The feed appears as a carousel on the app’s homepage and updates each visit based on the user’s viewing history.

## Key Features
- **Short‑form content**: Clips are brief excerpts that can be tapped to:
  - Jump directly into the full title
  - Rent or purchase the content
- **Watchlist integration**: Users can add titles seen in Clips to their watchlist.
- **Sharing**: Clips can be shared via text, email, or social media; recipients need the Prime Video app to view them.
- **Personalization**: The feed tailors suggestions according to each user’s past viewing behavior.

## Rollout Details
- Initially rolling out to “select” customers in the United States.
- Available on iOS, Android, and Fire tablets.
- A broader rollout is planned for the summer of 2026.

## Context
- Prime Video previously offered a TikTok‑style feed featuring NBA game highlights.
- The new Clips feed aligns Amazon with competitors Netflix and Disney+ that already provide vertical short‑form video experiences.
