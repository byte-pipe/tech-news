# No leap second will be introduced at the end of December 2026

## Key information
- The International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service (IERS) confirms that no leap second will be added to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) at the end of December 2026.  
- The current offset between UTC and International Atomic Time (TAI) remains **UTC − TAI = –37 seconds**, a value that has been in effect since 1 January 2017.  
- Leap seconds are only inserted at the end of June or December, based on the evolution of UT1‑TAI; Bulletin C is issued semi‑annually to announce a leap second or to confirm its absence.  
- The notice is addressed to authorities responsible for the measurement and distribution of time.  

*Christian Bizouard, Director, Earth Orientation Center of IERS, Observatoire de Paris*
