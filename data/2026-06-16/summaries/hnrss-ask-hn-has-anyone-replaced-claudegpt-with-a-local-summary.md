# Ask HN: Has anyone replaced Claude/GPT with a local model for daily coding? | Hacker News

**Replacing GPT with Local Models for Daily Coding**

*   **Who has replaced GPT with local models**: The authors have fully swapped GPT for local models in their daily coding, despite concerns about data privacy and the potential risks associated with large language models.
*   **Local model setup**:
    *   The authors use Qwen 3.6 (35B) or 3.5 (122B) models in isolation to avoid dependencies on external services like Wi-Fi or an internet connection.
    *   They run these models on their devices with limited resources, such as Mac Studio for 128GB RAM, MacBook for 36GB RAM, and Strix Halo laptops for 128GB unified memory chips.
*   **Comparison with GPT**:
    *   The authors report a significant speedup using local models over large language models like GPT (Claude Opus).
    *   They describe the local models as more suitable for tasks that require precision, clarity, and direct control, whereas large models like GPT tend to rely on assumptions and often get stuck in looped thinking.
*   **Additional insights**:
    +   The authors discuss a potential challenge when using proprietary tools or services, but opt not to do so due to their preference for open-source solutions.
    +   They prioritize testing and experimentation with local models to ensure they can handle specific use cases and tasks.
