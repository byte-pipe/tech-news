# Commodore Revives Flip Phone with Retro Aim
by David Pierce


Commodore, the iconic PC brand, has officially dabbled into flip phones once again.


* The Callback 8020 is a retro-style flip phone with modern features and aesthetics.
* It runs on Sailfish OS and has access to Android apps through an emulated interface.



It seems that Commodore is taking inspiration from early '80s-era flip phones and creating something similar, albeit not trying to make the same level of impact as Apple's iPhone.


However, when asked about its design philosophy, the creator stated it aims to provide users with all necessary features minus unnecessary distractions like social media connectivity:


## Design Philosophy
* Block social media and web browsing entirely

While others might criticize this decision for being restrictive or limited, Commodore claims that it effectively strikes a balance between providing what's needed for usability while preventing distractions.


Given its limitations in processing power and storage capacity compared to modern smartphones, the Callback 8020 is an intriguing footnote in the history of mobile technology.
 
The specs indicate modest hardware capabilities but promising app support on Sailfish OS. The appeal might not be for everyone due to its limited functionality.
### Specifications

* Screen size: 3.25 inches
* Resolution: 480 x 640 pixels
* Processor: MediaTek Helio G81
* RAM and storage: 4GB and 64GB respectively
* Audio features: Headphone jack, FM radio antenna
* Operating System: Sailfish
