# 'Hero' Abergele girl rescues friend from drowning at beach

Little Hero: 12-Year-Old Girl Rescues Drowning Friend by Hair

*   14-year-old Tamika had been enjoying hot weather at Pensarn beach, Abergele, when the tide changed direction and swept them off their feet.
*   The duo lost their footing and were pulled under water. One of them suffered from a seizure while struggling to breathe.
*   An ambulance was called, but before the paramedics could arrive, Tamika grabbed her friend's long hair with one hand and helped drag them out of the water with the other.
*   After being rescued and taken to hospital for treatment, both girls recovered well. One girl has now had more seizures than expected while being transported, which is still under investigation.

Summary:

*   A 14-year-old girl saved her 12-year-old friend by grabbing her hair while they struggled underwater due to a sudden change in the tide.
