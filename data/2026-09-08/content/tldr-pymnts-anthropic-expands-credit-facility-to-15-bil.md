Anthropicis preparing to finalize an expansion of its revolving credit facility to $15 billion, up from the $2.5 billion facility it secured last year, BloombergreportedFriday (Sept. 4), citing unnamed sources.

Companies typically finalize their revolving credit facility before notifying banks of their roles in the firm’s initial public offering, according to the report.

Anthropic is getting set to launch an IPO in which it aims to raise an amount equal to or higher than that raised bySpaceX, the report said.

It was reported Aug. 20 that SpaceX achieved the biggest-everIPOwhen it raised $75 billion at that outset, before increasing that figure to $86.2 billion with an overallotment option, and that Anthropic expects to equal or surpass that amount.

SpaceX increased its revolving credit facility from $1.5 billion to $5 billion in May, a month before its record-breaking IPO, per the Friday Bloomberg report.

Morgan Stanleyis leading Anthropic’s expansion of its revolving credit facility, withGoldman Sachs,JPMorgan ChaseandCitigroupalso having prominent roles. These four lenders are also leading Anthropic’s IPO, according to the report.

The details of the facility could change, the report said.

Anthropic and the banks declined to comment, per the report.

Anthropic announced June 1 that it confidentially filed for anIPOby submitting a draft registration statement to theSecurities and Exchange Commission.

“This gives us the option to go public after the SEC completes its review,” Anthropic said in the announcement. “The proposed initial public offering will depend on market conditions and other factors.”

Days earlier, on May 28, Anthropicbecamethe world’s most valuable artificial intelligence startup in May when it raised $65 billion in a Series H funding round that valued the company at $965 billion post-money. That figure was up from the $380 billionvaluationit achieved in a February Series G funding round in which it raised $30 billion.

It was reported Aug. 19 that rival AI startupOpenAIwill make itspublic debutby 2027. OpenAI Chief Financial OfficerSarah Friartold the company’s employees that there is no reason to worry if Anthropic goes public first because “we are running our own race,” per the report.
