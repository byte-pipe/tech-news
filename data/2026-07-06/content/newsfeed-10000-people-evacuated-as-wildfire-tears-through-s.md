10,000 people evacuated as wildfire tears through southern France

A fast-moving wildfire has forced around 10,000 people to evacuate after burning more than 1,500 hectares in southern France near the Spanish border. 
Firefighters are battling the blaze from the ground and air as officials assess possible disruption to the Tour de France.
