# Dropbox CEO Drew Houston to step down after 19 years

## Leadership transition
- Houston will move from CEO to executive chairman after an interim period as co‑CEO with Ashraf Alkarmi.  
- Alkarmi, formerly product chief, is promoted to co‑CEO and will eventually assume the sole CEO role.

## Company background and performance
- Founded by Houston at age 24; he is now 43.  
- Dropbox’s market cap is just over $6 billion, roughly half of its 2018 IPO peak and below the $10 billion private‑market valuation in 2014.  
- Annual revenue topped $1 billion in 2017 and $2 billion in 2021, but has been flat for two years and slipped slightly in 2025.  
- Over 18 million paying users, with strong adoption among media professionals, designers, architects, etc.  
- Faces intense competition from Google, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft, and rival Box (valued ~ $3.5 billion).

## AI initiatives and product updates
- Launched AI‑powered “Dash” feature that lets users query and manipulate text, video, and audio across apps.  
- Analysts cite the AI opportunity and current valuation as reasons value investors may be attracted to Dropbox.  
- Houston plans to pursue AI entrepreneurship outside Dropbox, noting the technology’s transformative potential.

## Market and analyst outlook
- Dropbox shares have fallen less than 5 % over the past year, outperforming many SaaS peers that lost over 60 %.  
- Gartner analyst John Lovelock compares the AI era to the early cloud era, calling the ultimate revenue impact “the unanswerable question.”  
- Monness, Crespi, Hardt & Co. maintain a hold rating, highlighting progress and AI‑driven growth prospects.

## Additional executive moves
- Mike Torres will join from Google as chief product officer in July, bringing experience as Google Chrome’s VP of product.

## Houston’s perspective
- He acknowledges there is “never a perfect time” for the transition and sees AI as an exciting new frontier for entrepreneurship.  
- Confident that Alkarmi’s leadership and the company’s direction are right for the future.
