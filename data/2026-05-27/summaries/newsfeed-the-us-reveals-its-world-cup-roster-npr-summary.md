# The U.S. reveals its World Cup roster

## Overview
- The 26‑player roster for the 2026 FIFA World Cup was announced in New York City and televised live on Fox.  
- Players were notified by text and email before traveling to the public reveal.  
- The tournament will be co‑hosted by the United States, Mexico and Canada, with matches in 16 cities.

## Veteran presence
- Returning World Cup veterans include forward Christian Pulisic (AC Milan), midfielder Weston McKennie (Juventus) and midfielder Tyler Adams (Bournemouth).  
- Half of the squad also played in Qatar 2022, including the three goal‑scorers from that tournament: Pulisic, Tim Weah (Marseille) and Haji Wright (Coventry City).  
- Tim Ream (Charlotte FC) highlighted the pressure and privilege of playing a home World Cup.

## Newcomers
- Thirteen players make their first World Cup appearance.  
- Notable debuts: striker Ricardo Pepi (PSV), defenders Chris Richards (Crystal Palace) and Miles Robinson (FC Cincinnati).  
- Other first‑time participants include defenders Max Arfsten, Alex Freeman and midfielders Sebastian Berhalter.

## Notable inclusions & snubs
- Surprising inclusion: forward Alejandro Zendejas (Club América), who had limited minutes under coach Mauricio Pochettino.  
- Prominent omissions: Lyon midfielder Tanner Tessmann and Real Salt Lake midfielder Diego Luna, both dealing with injuries despite expectations of availability.

## Coach Mauricio Pochettino’s comments
- Emphasized a year‑and‑a‑half of work to reach this moment and the difficulty of pleasing everyone.  
- Stated the goal is to be competitive and aim for the best finish in a generation, noting the U.S. last reached the quarter‑finals in 2002.

## Tournament context
- This will be the first World Cup hosted in the U.S. since 1994, a tournament that spurred the growth of MLS and youth soccer.  
- The U.S. hopes to improve on recent performances, having not advanced past the Round of 16 since 2002.
