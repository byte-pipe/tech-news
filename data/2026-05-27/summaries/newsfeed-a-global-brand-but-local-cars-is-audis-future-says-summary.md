# A global brand but local cars is Audi's future, says CEO – Ars Technica

## Main shift in strategy
- Post‑COVID, trade tensions and geopolitical conflicts have made the “global car” model less viable.  
- Audi CEO Gernot Döllner stresses the need for flexibility: develop cars with regional priorities first, then launch globally.

## US‑focused development – the Q9
- The upcoming Q9 is being designed around U.S. requirements (interior size, cup‑holder dimensions, JD Power expectations).  
- Launch sequence: **U.S. first**, followed by a worldwide rollout.  
- Interior updates include re‑engineered door panels, dedicated switches, improved cooling, and a revised roof concept.

## China‑specific models
- Audi is creating “local‑for‑local” vehicles for China, exemplified by the **AUDI E7X** developed with SAIC.  
- Döllner notes the need for China‑specific supply‑chain, production, and ecosystem solutions, separate from European and North‑American platforms.

## Potential new R8 and V8 interest
- Döllner is a “big fan of V8” engines, especially for full‑size SUVs.  
- When asked about a third‑generation R8, he highlighted cross‑brand technology sharing (e.g., Lamborghini’s V8) and suggested possibilities, but gave no definitive commitment.

## Wagons and RS models in the U.S.
- Audi dealers have expressed interest in bringing RS‑type wagons (e.g., **RS5 Avant**) to the U.S. market.  
- Döllner indicated surprise at dealer demand and said plans are not set, but the idea is being considered.
