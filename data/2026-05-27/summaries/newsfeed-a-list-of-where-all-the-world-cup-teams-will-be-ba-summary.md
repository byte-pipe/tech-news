# Summary of World Cup 2026 Team Base Camps in the United States  

## Overview  
- FIFA confirmed the training‑site list for the 2026 World Cup (June 11 – July 19).  
- 39 of the 48 qualified national teams will be based in the United States.  
- More than 20 cities across 19 states will host these base camps, separate from the 11 match‑host cities.  
- The tournament will be co‑hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico, marking the first World Cup in the U.S. since 1994 and the largest edition ever staged.

## Teams Based Outside the United States  
- **Canada:** Canada and Panama will train in Canadian venues.  
- **Mexico:** Colombia, South Korea, Mexico, South Africa, Uruguay and Tunisia will train in Mexican venues.  

## Additional Notes  
- The Iranian team was originally assigned a base in Tucson, Arizona, but after the outbreak of the U.S.–Israel conflict with Iran, it successfully moved its camp to Mexico.  
- The World Cup final will be held at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, with a Super Bowl‑style halftime show featuring BTS, Shakira and Madonna.  
- Base camps are expected to generate economic activity and give fans additional ways to engage with the tournament.
