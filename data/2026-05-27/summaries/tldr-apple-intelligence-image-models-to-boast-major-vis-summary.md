# Apple Intelligence image models to receive major visual upgrades in iOS 27

## Overview
- The Power On newsletter reports that Apple’s on‑device image generation models, used in Genmoji and Image Playground, will get a “big boost” in visual quality with iOS 27.  
- The improvements address the low‑fidelity results seen since the features debuted in iOS 18.2.

## Visual quality improvements
- Current outputs are described as “far from anything to write home about,” especially for Image Playground.  
- Apple’s updated models aim to raise image fidelity dramatically, though it is not yet confirmed whether they will remain on‑device.

## Expanded AI model support
- iOS 27’s redesigned Image Playground will support third‑party image generation models beyond OpenAI’s ChatGPT.  
- Expected additions include Google’s Nano Banana models and other competitors.  
- This expands the ecosystem while still offering Apple’s own enhanced models.

## On‑device vs. cloud processing
- The original poor quality is partly attributed to the constraints of on‑device inference.  
- The upcoming upgrade may involve a shift to cloud‑based processing, but Apple has not clarified this point.
