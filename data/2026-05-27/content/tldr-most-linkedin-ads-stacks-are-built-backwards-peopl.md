description: 'Most LinkedIn Ads stacks are built backwards. People start with creative when they should start with data. Here''s the full 2026 stack I''d build (and actually use): Layer 1: ICP and closed/won analysis Pull your closed/won deals and call transcripts from Attio or HubSpot. Use Claude to quickly build a: a) Tiered segment list ranked by revenue potential b) Tiered pain point matrix for messaging impact This process fuels tighter targeting and messaging, optimised for pipeline. Layer 2: Targeting Clay builds accounts lists in minutes. Vector''s great for in-market signals. Primer allows you to isolate specific segments with precision. LinkedIn''s in-platform filters are inconsistent. 3rd party tools cut irrelevant profiles from targeting so your ads actually reach the right people. Layer 3: Measurement Dreamdata handles multi-touch attribution and ROI across channels. Fibbler gives you campaign-level pipeline influence, impact scoring, and lift analysis. Databox ties data into
  a dashboard you can actually read and send to execs. BONUS: You can use Fibbler and Dreamdata for company-level frequency capping to keep spend and impression share under control. Dreamdata also has an AI signals feature for sending automated in-market audiences to LinkedIn. Layer 4: Creative Combining Figma or Canva with Claude can get you to high-impact, close-to-production ready assets in minutes. You''ll need to edit for taste, but it shaves off a lot of time. You can use Adfolio, Motion, and Lapis to find proven formats, then add your own unique spin. Thought-leadership content should be 80% of your LinkedIn Ads creative now. Use tools like Kleo for drafting posts and ideating trending formats. Use Tella and Opus for fast video content. Layer 5: Reporting Measurement feeds the reporting setup giving you a place to understand performance and make data-driven in-platform optimisation decisions. I use Whatagraph as my main reporting tool now. I have my own Ad Juice template in there
  that can build a sharable report, with my key LinkedIn Ads KPIs, in just a few clicks. I''ve also used dashthis and Supermetrics in the past and they''re both good alternatives. Pick one and build a dashboard your CMO can easily understand in 60 seconds. The bottom line is this: Most LinkedIn Ads underperform because the foundation is weak. Fix the data layer first, and everything downstream gets sharper. P.S. I break down setups like this every week in The Squeeze, my LinkedIn Ads newsletter. Tactics, stack recommendations, and real account examples. Hit view my newsletter above to sign up. | 19 comments on LinkedIn'
