# `123456' password used in Danish CPR data breach

**"123456" Password Used in Danish CPR Data Breach**

The Danish government has confirmed that a sensitive piece of personal data, including "123456" passwords, is at risk in a large-scale data breach.

The Danish Personal Data Protection Agency reported that the breach occurred at the Danish Labour and Welfare Service (AKS). According to the agency, approximately 1.3 million sensitive records, including "123456" passwords, were stolen in a cyber attack.

The compromised records were stored at the AKS, which is responsible for providing employment, income support, and pension services to Danish citizens. The agency revealed that the "123456" passwords were likely obtained through the breach and could be used to gain unauthorized access to individual records.

The breach was described as a significant data breach, as it involved the theft of sensitive personal data, including passwords. The agency emphasized the importance of individuals taking precautions to protect their online identities and passwords.

The investigation into the breach is ongoing, and authorities are working to assess the potential damage and inform individuals whose data was compromised.
