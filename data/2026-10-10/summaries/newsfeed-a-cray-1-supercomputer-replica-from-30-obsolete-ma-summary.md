# A Cray-1 supercomputer replica from 30 "obsolete" Mac Minis

### A Cray-1 Supercomputer Replica from 30 "Obsolete" Mac Minis Unveiled

#### Main Idea

A visual replica of the 1976 Cray-1 supercomputer, created from 30 "obsolete" Mac Minis, was unveiled by the Museo de Historia de la Computación in Spain. The project serves as a 50th-anniversary tribute to the Cray-1 and Apple's founding, showcasing the creative team's ingenuity in leveraging consumer hardware for performance-enhanced computing.

#### Key Points

* The Cray-1 replica utilizes 30 2012-era Mac Minis for a 1:1 scale visual recreation, instead of expensive, cutting-edge tech.
* The system features 64 active processing cores, achieving 1.3 teraflops of performance in its tests.
* The museum reportedly achieved 1.5 teraflops using all 70 available cores before one node went offline.
* The replica showcases the advancements in computer architecture since the original Cray-1 (1976).
* The project utilizes a custom Python script for matrix-multiplication tasks, leveraging the strengths of macOS Mojave (10.14) for efficient communication and sharing of work.
* The team faced significant engineering challenges, including fan cooling and rack mount designs for passive convection cooling.

#### Additional Information

* The display allows guests to witness the system's performance in real-time as tasks are processed simultaneously.
* The team aims to preserve the essence of the original Cray-1's vector processing and parallel cluster architecture.
* The replica serves as a valuable educational tool, illustrating the evolution of computer architecture.
