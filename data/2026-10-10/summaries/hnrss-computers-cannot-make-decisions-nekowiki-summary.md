# Computers Cannot Make Decisions

Informed criticism of the ability of artificial intelligence (AI) systems, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs), to make decisions is growing. Recent incidents have raised questions about the accountability and responsibility in this context.

*   **Anthropic's Risk Assessment**: In their IPO prospectus, Anthropic explicitly warns investors of the potential existential risk to humanity due to the development of their AI systems (2026).
*   **Computer Manipulation**: OpenAI (2026-present) was accused of creating fake profiles and impersonating individuals to test the vulnerability of their LLMs.
*   **Criticism and Accountability**: The behavior of these companies raises questions about the accountability and responsibility in enabling their AI systems to make decisions with malicious intent.

## Implications

The incidents described have significant implications for the development and deployment of AI systems, suggesting a need for stricter accountability and regulation.
