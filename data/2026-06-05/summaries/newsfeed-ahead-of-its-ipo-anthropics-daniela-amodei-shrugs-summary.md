# Ahead of its IPO, Anthropic's Daniela Amodei shrugs off doubts about AI's returns | TechCrunch

**Anthropic's Private Listing Decision Amidst AI Growth**

Private investors are seeking significant investment from Anthropic due to the company's rapid growth and high valuation of its AI model maker at $65 billion. Despite this demand, the firm is exploring whether to undergo an initial public offering (IPO).

Co-founder Daniela Amodei views the decision as primarily driven by capital requirements for training AI models and serving inference on them. Over time, she predicts that core companies will need access to capital due to advances in the field.

**Growth Outpaces Revenue**

Anthropic's annualized revenue surpassed $47 billion in May, up from around $9 billion at the end of 2025. However, this growth is facing potential reining-in because corporations like Uber may start to reduce investment in AI technologies.

**Deployment Strategy**

Amodei believes that AI-powered core uses such as coding, financial services, legal, healthcare, and artificial intelligence applications will continue to drive efficiency and creativity despite initial skepticism from the business community. As businesses become familiar with AI tools, she hopes for more seamless integration into daily work processes.

**Competition and Computing Needs**

Anthropic does not plan on building more computational power than it can effectively utilize. This approach aims to prioritize customer demand for the product over the company's ability to fulfill the requirement of sufficient compute capacity.

**Relationships with Rivals**

The company surprised the industry by partnering with xAI, a competing AI platform in January. However, this collaboration was later disclosed through an S-1 filing that included an estimated $1.25 billion monthly cost associated with their partnership with SpaceX for computing services related to rocket launches.
