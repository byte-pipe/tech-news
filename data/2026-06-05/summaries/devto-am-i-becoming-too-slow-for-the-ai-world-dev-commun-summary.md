# Am I Becoming Too Slow for the AI World? - DEV Community

**Verification as the New Engineering Bottleneck**

The article highlights the challenge of feeling slow as an engineer when working with artificial intelligence (AI) tools. Despite having access to rapid prototyping capabilities, automated tests, and workflows, the author finds themselves struggling with deeper decision-making processes.

*   **The Rise of AI**: The author notes that many engineers feel overwhelmed by the sheer speed at which they can generate code and automate tasks, creating the illusion that everything quickly compiles.
*   **Verification Takes a Backseat**: In contrast, verification, which involves ensuring the accuracy of generated outputs and the validity of assumptions, is often neglected in favor of rapid development and deployment.
*   **Judgment Overwhelm**: The author mentions feeling slow when making crucial decisions, such as determining whether a generated concept is trustworthy enough to integrate into an existing system. This judgmental process requires careful consideration and expertise.

**Implications of the bottleneck**

The issue of speed vs. deliberation in AI development has several implications:

*   **Noisy Field**: The rapid development environment adopted by AI tools may lead to decisions that do not necessarily result in trustworthy or maintainable products.
*   **Lack of Judgment**: In a fast-paced industry with a focus on quick deployment and iteration, judgment takes a backseat, making it challenging to develop robust systems.

**Key Takeaways**

To overcome this bottleneck, engineers must prioritize deliberation over speed:
-   Focus on the end goals, rather than the technology.
-   Develop expertise in areas such as design, integration, and validation.
-   Foster a culture that values high-quality, reliable software over efficiency.
