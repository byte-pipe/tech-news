# Project Glasswing Expands to 150 More Companies with Focus on Critical Infrastructure

Anthropic, a leading AI-based vulnerability hunting platform, has expanded its Project Glasswing initiative by adding 150 more companies, with a focus on critical infrastructure sectors such as power, water, healthcare, communications, and hardware.

This expansion marks a significant milestone in the project's journey to make software more secure. The current participant base of 30 companies includes major AI vendors like AWS, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, JPMorganChase, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Palo Alto Networks. Additional participants have joined, including Okta.

The introduction of Project Glasswing reflects Anthropic's commitment to addressing a critical bottleneck in software patch development: an inability for vendors to keep pace with identifying vulnerabilities and patches them in a timely manner. This mismatch is causing concerns among analysts and security vendors, as it can lead to catastrophic consequences if successful attacks targeting codebases are possible.

**Key Insights**

* With 150 additional companies participating in Project Glasswing, the number of vulnerable systems to identify will be doubled.
* The expansion aims to increase the project's effectiveness by improving adaptation for vulnerability validation, prioritization, and fixing by defenders with limited resources.
* This push expands Anthropic's goal towards making AI more accessible to various industries.

**Challenges Ahead**

The success of Project Glasswing depends on several factors. Key considerations include trust in automated patch generation tools (CISOs) and adapting vendors to keep pace with the large volume of patches generated, which can be difficult for some organizations due to limited resources and lack of familiarity with AI-based vulnerability detection.

Anthropic and its companions recognize these challenges but believe that the expansion to 150 more companies is a crucial step toward addressing security concerns through improved software integrity.

The platform will continue to prioritize and expand its participant base as it works towards becoming a go-to tool for identifying vulnerabilities across critical infrastructure companies.

### Future Development

With this new milestone accomplished, Anthropic can now focus on improving the project's underlying tools and strategies. This includes refining the automated vulnerability detection process and enhancing collaboration between vendors.

Key areas of development will likely involve scaling up AI-based patch generation capabilities while maintaining security controls that safeguard system vulnerabilities. With time, Project Glasswing will play a vital role in shielding computer networks from threats associated with inadequate software configuration.
