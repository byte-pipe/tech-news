# The battle for digital dollars is moving onchain

# America’s largest banks are building a new digital currency network to stop a massive deposit drain

## What the banks plan
- JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citigroup and other major lenders will launch a shared tokenized‑deposit network through The Clearing House by the first half of 2027.  
- The network will enable round‑the‑clock, blockchain‑based settlement of bank deposits, giving them near‑instant, 24/7 transfer capability.  

## Why it matters
- The initiative is a direct response to the growth of stablecoins such as USDC and USDT, which currently dominate on‑chain cash usage.  
- By tokenizing deposits, banks keep customer funds inside the regulated banking system while offering speed and efficiency comparable to stablecoins.  
- Analysts warn stablecoins could cause a 3‑5 % runoff of core deposits over the next five years, potentially cutting average bank earnings by about 3 %.  

## How tokenized deposits differ from stablecoins
- A tokenized deposit is a digital representation of a traditional bank deposit; the underlying funds remain under bank control and within existing compliance frameworks.  
- Stablecoins are issued by non‑bank entities and circulate on public blockchains, giving users full ownership but exposing banks to deposit migration.  

## Expected benefits
- Near‑instant, cross‑border payments with lower costs and reduced settlement friction compared with traditional wire transfers that take one to two business days.  
- A new on‑chain cash instrument that could compete with stablecoins and tokenized money‑market funds for corporate payments and treasury operations.  

## Industry perspective
- TD Securities’ Reid Noch sees a competition emerging among stablecoins, tokenized deposits, and tokenized money‑market funds for the preferred on‑chain cash instrument.  
- Digital Chamber CEO Cody Carbone notes that the participation of America’s biggest banks signals blockchain’s move into the financial mainstream.  
- Noelle Acheson highlights that banks are extending private‑blockchain experiments to a multi‑bank network, maintaining strict control unlike the open‑network model of crypto.  

## Potential impact
- If successful, the Clearing House network could become a major rival to stablecoins for corporate finance, reshaping how money moves on blockchain networks.  
- The effort underscores a broader trend of traditional finance adopting blockchain technology while still competing with crypto‑native alternatives.
