# Announcing the Schema.org Usage Statistics Dataset

## Overview
- Google and the Schema.org community released a new dataset showing aggregate usage statistics for Schema.org terms on the public web.
- The dataset is updated monthly and aggregates counts at the domain level, presented in popularity range buckets to reduce noise and protect privacy.

## Content and Access
- Available in CSV and JSON formats on the official Schema.org GitHub repository.
- Statistics are also displayed directly on individual schema term pages for quick reference.

## Purpose and Benefits
- Provides transparency on how Types and Properties are adopted by developers and publishers worldwide.
- Serves researchers, toolmakers, and the broader community for analysis and experimentation.

## Collaboration Invitation
- While Google contributed the initial data, other crawlers and indexers are encouraged to submit their own statistics using the same open format.
- Collective contributions aim to create a more comprehensive and resilient semantic web.

## Additional Information
- For more details, see the “About Usage Statistics” section on the Schema.org site.
