# Say hi to “Siri AI” – Apple’s new conversational voice assistant

## Announcement and overall vision
- Apple unveiled “Apple Intelligence,” a Siri update dubbed **Siri AI**, scheduled for rollout this fall.
- The update pairs a Google‑powered foundation model with tighter AI integration across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, and visionOS.
- SVP Craig Federighi emphasized that Apple’s AI is meant to serve users’ needs rather than pursue AI for its own sake.

## Conversational capabilities demonstrated
- **Multi‑step scenario 1:**  
  - User asks about the World Cup schedule.  
  - Requests Brazil vs Morocco‑inspired recipes.  
  - Retrieves a dessert mention from a recent message with “Maria.”  
  - Combines everything into a watch‑party menu and sends it to a group chat.  
- **Multi‑step scenario 2:**  
  - User queries the location of a photo of an arch.  
  - Looks up a friend Jeff’s new address.  
  - Siri provides directions to the arch with a stop at Jeff’s via Apple Maps, all without manual data juggling.

## Personal context and privacy‑first design
- Siri AI can search a user’s messages, email, and other on‑device data to answer questions and tailor email tone to past correspondence.
- “World knowledge” is fetched via private‑cloud compute, while on‑device processing uses personal context to decide which app actions to invoke.
- All conversations are stored locally and in iCloud, accessible only through a dedicated Siri app; Apple asserts they are not readable by the company or third parties.

## New visual and writing features
- **Visual intelligence:** answers directly from the Camera app, extracts multiple calendar events from a concert‑schedule image, and, on visionOS, provides information about physical objects in view.
- **Write with Siri:** generates text anywhere the user types.
- **AI‑powered proofreading:** system‑wide spell‑check and style suggestions.
- macOS integration places Siri AI into Spotlight, enables Ctrl‑click queries on files, photos, or text, and introduces a floating Siri Orb in visionOS.

## Two‑tier AI model architecture
- Apple’s partnership with Google’s Gemini model underpins Siri AI, but a fully local version proved challenging.
- **Most capable model** runs on devices meeting specific hardware thresholds:  
  - iPhone Air and iPhone 17 Pro  
  - iPads with M4 (or later) CPUs and ≥12 GB RAM  
  - Macs with M3 (or later) chips and ≥12 GB RAM  
- **Less capable model** runs on older hardware, lacking features such as the expressive‑voice slider and some advanced dictation improvements.

## Cloud reliance, usage limits, and iCloud+ benefits
- Certain functions (e.g., image generation) still depend on private‑cloud servers and are subject to daily usage caps.
- iCloud+ subscribers receive higher limits for these cloud‑based features.

## Language rollout and future availability
- Siri AI launches initially in English, with plans to expand to all iOS‑supported languages over time.
