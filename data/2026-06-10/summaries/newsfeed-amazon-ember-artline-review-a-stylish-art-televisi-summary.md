# Amazon Ember Artline Review: A Stylish Art Television | WIRED

# Amazon Ember Artline Review Summary

## Overview
- Stylish, wall‑mountable art television priced at $1,099 (65‑inch) and $899 (55‑inch).  
- Integrated Alexa+ and AI‑generated artwork features.  
- Positioned as a more affordable alternative to Samsung Frame Pro.

## Design & Mounting
- Weight: 60.2 lb (65‑inch) and 42.5 lb (55‑inch); may require assistance for installation.  
- Mounts flush against wall using two magnetic plates; slight protrusion compared to competitors.  
- Includes a free bezel frame (10 color options); extra bezels $85 ($75 for 55‑inch).  
- Installation takes ~20 minutes with basic tools (stud finder, drill, bits).

## Features & AI
- Over 2,000 free art images; no subscription required.  
- “Moving artwork” adds subtle motion to static pieces.  
- **Match the Room**: AI generates art that matches room’s color palette.  
- **Omnisense**: Motion sensor powers art display off when room is empty and on when occupied (≈90 % reliability).  
- Alexa+ handles volume, app launch, weather, music, sports, and complex content searches; limited for classic art searches.  
- Voice‑generated AI art available but tends to be generic.

## Performance & Picture Quality
- Uses existing QLED panel (same as prior Fire/Ember TVs); contrast ratio not disclosed and appears average.  
- Anti‑glare matte finish improves realism for artwork and video.  
- Benchmark testing shows washed‑out skin tones, muted greens, and overall lower contrast compared to Samsung Frame Pro.  
- Refresh rate low; contrast ratio considered a drawback.

## Pros & Cons
- **Pros**  
  - Attractive design and easy wall mounting.  
  - Affordable price, especially versus Samsung Frame Pro.  
  - Free extensive art library and moving artwork.  
  - Useful AI features (Match the Room, Omnisense).  
  - Simple setup with Amazon account; Wi‑Fi 6 support.  

- **Cons**  
  - Average contrast and lack of texture in artwork.  
  - Alexa+ less powerful than Google Gemini on competing models.  
  - Low refresh rate.  
  - Motion sensor occasionally misses presence detection.

## Verdict
- The Ember Artline offers a sleek, budget‑friendly art TV with unique AI‑driven features that make it a strong contender for users who prioritize style and integrated Alexa over premium picture fidelity.  
- Best suited for displaying artwork throughout the day and casual media consumption; not ideal for viewers demanding high‑contrast, ultra‑realistic visuals.
