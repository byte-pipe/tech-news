@soumyadeepdeyset out to catch a limp that a dog can't tell you about, using ten million rows of collar data from a wearable sensor. The trick is that a limp isn't one number going up, it's a pattern over time: walk, stop, walk a little, stop again, and averaging the day away erases exactly that pattern. He used Snowflake to search for that exact sequence directly in the data and pull out the specific seconds that gave it away, so every result comes with evidence attached.

@kakerothbuilt ChainPaws to fix a genuinely rotten problem: when your dog goes missing, most of the "I found him!" messages you get are scammers trying to extort a reward. ChainPaws puts your dog's ID and the reward money on the Solana blockchain instead, so the payout only goes through once someone actually proves they found your dog, no wire transfers to strangers required. It's a real working smart contract with tests to back it up, not just a mockup of the idea.

@vikram003built Fetch'd, a swipe app for shelter adoptions with a twist: the dog gets a say too, not just the human. Gemini does two jobs behind the scenes, reading a scanned vet intake form into usable info, then comparing that against what an adopter says about their home to write one honest sentence about whether it's a good fit. No score, no percentage match, just a real observation, or a question back to you if there isn't enough info yet to say anything useful.
