# Apple planning to sell AI servers powered by M8 Ultra chips, says report

## Key points
- Apple is developing enterprise AI servers that will use its upcoming M8 Ultra chips.  
- Two configurations are being considered: a smaller model with two M8 Ultra chips and a larger model with four chips.  
- The design may incorporate Nvidia’s NVLink Fusion technology (switches, chiplets, and software) to enable high‑speed communication between the chips.  
- The servers are intended for external sale to AI developers, businesses, and governments, not just for internal use.  
- Project work has been ongoing for about a year, with CEO John Ternus supporting the effort.  
- The Information reports that the plans are not yet finalized and the first units are not expected to ship until 2029, though the project could be cancelled before then.  

## Commentary from 9to5Mac
- The timeline is long, but the concept aligns with existing use of Mac Studio clusters for local AI model training, suggesting a potential market.  
- The main uncertainty is whether Apple will prioritize the launch enough to bring the product to market.
