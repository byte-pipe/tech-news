# ByteDance’s AI drug unit Anew Labs raises $290M at $1.5B valuation, Reuters reports

# Summary of ByteDance’s AI drug unit Anew Labs funding round

## Funding round details
- Anew Labs, the AI drug discovery spin‑off of ByteDance, closed a $290 million financing round.  
- The round valued the company at $1.5 billion.  
- ByteDance retained a 56 % ownership stake after the round.

## Lead and participating investors
- Lead investors: HSG (formerly Sequoia China), IDG Capital, and Hillhouse Investment.  
- Co‑lead: 5Y Capital.  
- Additional participants: Gaorong Ventures, Primavera Venture Partners, Boyu Capital, Shanghai Future Industries Fund (state‑backed), and SBP Group (strategic investor).

## Spin‑off and team composition
- Approximately 50 core staff members moved to Anew Labs, bringing algorithms, technology platform, and pipeline assets.  
- Volcano Engine, ByteDance’s cloud division, will continue supplying computing power.  
- The unit is led by Liu Kai, former venture‑capital professional at IDG Capital and Fire Stone Investment; IDG Capital is a major investor in the round.

## Technology and drug candidates
- Anew Labs has developed structure‑prediction and protein‑design models such as Protenix and PXDesign.  
- It has generated a small portfolio of drug candidates; one highlighted candidate is an AI‑designed IL‑17 inhibitor presented at the American Association of Immunologists meeting in Boston.  
- The unit currently has four drug candidates in its pipeline, none yet approved.

## Geographic presence and team size
- Operations are located in Shanghai, Singapore, and San Jose, California.  
- The company listed 36 core team members on its website as of May.

## Strategic significance
- The spin‑off aims to allow the AI‑driven drug discovery business to follow a distinct industry logic and management approach separate from ByteDance’s core media products.  
- Holding a majority stake in a drug development venture with early‑stage candidates represents an atypical diversification for ByteDance’s portfolio.
