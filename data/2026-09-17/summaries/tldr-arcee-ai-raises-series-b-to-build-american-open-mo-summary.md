# Arcee AI Raises Series B to Build American Open Models | Arcee AI | Building Open Intelligence

# Summary of “Arcee AI Raises Series B to Build the Future of American Open Models”

## Funding announcement
- Arcee AI closed a Series B round led by Vista Equity Partners, Cambium Capital, and Emergence Capital.  
- Participating investors include AI10 Ventures, Hitachi, IAG, M12, P7, and Wipro.  
- The round values the company at **over $1 billion**.

## Vision and conviction
- The company’s core belief: organizations building with AI should be able to understand, adapt, deploy, and own the models they use.  
- To achieve this, Arcee focuses on creating open‑weight foundation models from the ground up and providing the products and infrastructure that let developers, enterprises, researchers, and public institutions use those models on their own terms.

## Recent achievements
- Decided less than a year ago to build its own frontier open‑weight model family, resulting in the **Trinity** series.  
- Scaled from a 4.5 B dense model to **Trinity Large**, a 400 B Mixture‑of‑Experts model, within six months.  
- Trinity Large is the first highly capable, permissively licensed U.S.‑built model released since Meta stopped the Llama series in early 2025.  
- The entire 2025 model lineup, including Trinity Large, was built for roughly **$20 million**, covering salaries, compute, data, infrastructure, and operations.  
- Emphasized efficient training pipelines, extreme sparsity, focused post‑training, and cost‑effective inference.

## Planned use of Series B resources
1. **Scale the Trinity model program** – continue training the next generation of models across a spectrum from phone‑ and laptop‑friendly sizes to frontier systems for complex scientific and developer workloads.  
2. **Expand collaboration with the U.S. Department of Energy and national laboratories** – launch initiatives such as **Genesis‑Science‑1** to demonstrate how open models can support research institutions tackling the world’s hardest problems.  
3. **Grow the product suite around open models** – develop tools for customization, evaluation, deployment, and reliable operation, making the full AI stack—from pre‑training to production serving—simpler, faster, and more accessible.

## Outlook
- Arcee views open models as emerging critical infrastructure for enterprises and institutions worldwide.  
- The next phase focuses on building the best open models globally, building on the team’s proven ability to deliver frontier‑scale AI efficiently in the United States.
