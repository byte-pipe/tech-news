Writing therapy and a new book trilogy

Hi, I'm Cesar, a Senior Software Engineer with 10+ years of experience.

In 2023, I got sick and burned out.In 2024, I was laid off and even rejected from FAANG.

I used the time between jobs as a mini-sabbatical to recover.Writing became part of my therapy, and dev.to was a big part of the journey.Hitting the Top7 and encouraging comments pushed me to keep writing.

Since then, I've been turning my lessons into posts and books.My goal is to complete a trilogy before I retire (hopefully before I'm 60).I already published the second one,Street-Smart Coding, and I'm working ona prequel.

This week, I'm celebrating my 7-year anniversary on dev.to.Ask me anything about coding, burnout, or writing.I don't have all answers, tho.Or simply stop by to say Hi.
