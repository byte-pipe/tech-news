# I Stopped Debugging at My Desk. Here's What Changed - DEV Community

**Patience and Perspective from the Garden**

I typically write about Next.js, caching, and bugs that disrupt your sleep. However, this post is different as it explores what happens before writing code.

**What Changes How You Think About Problems**

Some developers get stuck by walking, making another cup of coffee, or staring at a file until they find an answer hidden between lines of code. My approach changed when I started spending time in my garden. It's not to escape the problem, but because patience and perspective become essential tools.

**The Role of Gardening**

Every day before opening my laptop, I'd dedicate 10-15 minutes to gardening. While most problems seem straightforward, they are often more complex than they appear. By stopping and taking a break, I clear my mind: gardening prepares me for the next task as much as it does for the work I'll do.

**Stuck Again**

Yesterday evening, I was stuck due to caching issues that seemed obvious but not immediately solvable. Most of the application behaved as expected, with one minor detail throwing me off (stale data on a page). To resolve the issue, I needed a different perspective: instead of trying to force my way through, I closed my laptop and took some time for reflection.

**A Quiet Understanding**

I arrived at the garden after lunch and found it still damp from the night. The soil has acquired a unique scent that's often understated until morning. This new quietness helped me approach my plants with renewed focus.
