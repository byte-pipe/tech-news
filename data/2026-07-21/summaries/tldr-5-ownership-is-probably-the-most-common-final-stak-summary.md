# 5% ownership is probably the most common final stake for VC funded startup founders — @levelsio (Pieter Levels)

## 5% Ownership and Startup Acquisition Outcomes

This analysis provides insights into the potential final stake in VC-funded startups. Key points include:

*   The median ownership stake for initial founders (via Seed funding) ranges from ~56% to Series D, with a median of ~10%.
*   The expected acquisition outcome varies based on the number of co-founders:

    *   One founder: $25.6 million
    *   Two or more co-founders: $12.8 million-$6.4 million

## Acquired Stock Ownership Distribution Across Different Numbers of Co-Founders

The distribution of remaining stock ownership when a startup is acquired shows significant variation, depending on the number of co-founders and their ownership stakes:

*   One founder: $280 million
*   Two or more co-founders: $6.4 million-$90 million
