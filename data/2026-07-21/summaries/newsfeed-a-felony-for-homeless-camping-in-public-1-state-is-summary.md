### A Charge for Camping in Public: Tennessee to Become the Latest State to Crack Down on Homeless People


Homelessness is a growing concern in American society, with thousands of people experiencing homelessness across the country. This includes individuals who have chosen not to leave public areas such as parks and streets due to fear, lack of access to services, or other conditions.

### Current Situation: Joseph Williams Arrested for Camping on Tennessee State Property

Joseph Lamont Williams, a 49-year-old homeless individual, was charged with two felony counts of camping on state property after police found him living in a public park in East Nashville. He spent nine days in jail before the charges were dismissed by a judge.

### Rise in Homeless Camping Bans Across the Country

Eleven states have passed homeless camping bans over the last four years, according to the National Homelessness Law Center. This increase can be attributed to growing national attention on homelessness and efforts from individual cities to tackle the problem.
