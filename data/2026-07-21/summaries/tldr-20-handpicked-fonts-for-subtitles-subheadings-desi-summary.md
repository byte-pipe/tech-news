### Top 20 Fonts For Subtitles & Subheadings

These fonts offer a mix of modern and classic styles, with clean sans-sans pairable textures suitable for various content.

*   **Playfair Display**: An elegant serif font perfect for subheadlines, ideal for corporate branding or luxury design.
*   **Merriweather**: A classic serif font that pairs well with bold headings and body text, great for publications centered around formal or technical content.
*   **Lora**: A handwritten script font that adds a touch of charm to titles and headlines. It provides modern elegance and sophistication in presentations or marketing materials.
*   **Montserrat**: A sleek and minimalist sans-serif font suitable for modern designs with clean lines and simple typography. It offers great versatility and can handle various content styles, from news websites to business branding.
