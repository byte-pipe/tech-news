## Incident overview
- A female driver stopped her red Nissan Micra and offered PC Kerswell a lift while he was chasing suspect Jacob Roberts.  
- Roberts had reverse‑rammed a police car twice, crashed into parked cars and a building, and fled on foot after abandoning his van in Abercwmboi, Rhondda Cynon Taf.

## Chase details
- Police began pursuit after detecting the smell of cannabis from Roberts’s van; he drove erratically, ignored multiple junctions, mounted pavements and put pedestrians at risk.  
- Body‑cam footage shows the officer asking the driver for a lift, the driver replying “Get in,” and asking for directions before the officer jumps out, draws his taser and arrests the suspect.

## Arrest and evidence
- Roberts was apprehended on foot; a search found two cannabis cigarettes.  
- He was sentenced at Merthyr Tydfil Crown Court to one year and two months in prison and a three‑year driving disqualification.  
- Convictions included dangerous driving, driving while disqualified, driving without insurance, failure to stop after an accident, criminal damage to property, and possession of Class B controlled drugs.

## Police comment
- PC Kerswell said Roberts was already banned from driving and showed complete disregard for other road users.
