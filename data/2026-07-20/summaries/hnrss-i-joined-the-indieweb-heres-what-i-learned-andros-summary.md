# I joined the IndieWeb, here's what I learned | Andros Fenollosa

## What is the IndieWeb?

The IndieWeb is an ideology that advocates for a people-focused alternative to the corporate web, rather than a project-focused approach. It is defined as "a people-focused alternative to the corporate web", embracing plurality of approaches, projects, and not just software/frameworks.

### Key Concepts and Pillars

*   **Your content is yours**: Share your own content without any restrictions.
*   **You are better connected**: Distribute your articles across multiple platforms, and respond and like comments from other services to have a comprehensive online presence.
*   **You are in control**: Post anything you want, with readable permanent URLs that will always work.

## The IndieWeb's Opponents: Silo

The IndieWeb is opposed by the concept of a "silo", which refers to centralized websites owned and controlled by for-profit corporations, restricting content access. The wiki defines the silo as a walled garden.

### Summary

*   The IndieWeb is an alternative ideology that promotes personal, independent websites sharing commitments.
*   It emphasizes openness, connection, and control over online interactions.
*   The IndieWeb aims to challenge the corporate web's dominance by supporting for-profit owners and providing access through distributed platforms.
*   To adapt to this movement, individuals should consider its key principles: your content is yours, you are better connected, and you are in control.
