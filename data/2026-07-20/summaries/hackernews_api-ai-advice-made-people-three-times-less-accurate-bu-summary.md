# AI advice made people three times less accurate but twice as confident, researchers found

# Study Reveals Impact of AI Advice on Human Judgment and Confidence
Researchers found that access to AI advice significantly suppressed human judgment, accuracy, and confidence, while increasing these measures. The study suggests that relying on AI advice can lead to "cognitive surrender," where people become more confident in incorrect answers.

## Key Findings:
* Accuracy from 27% to 9%
* Confidence rose from 30% to 76%
* Percentage of incorrect AI answers increased significantly
* People trusted AI responses, even when they incorrectly stated the answer

## Methodology:
The study was conducted using questions where AI models are typically inaccurate. Monetary incentives were not effective in increasing trust in AI advice.

## Main Implications:
1. **Human Judgment Suppressed by AI Advice**: The study shows that humans become less cautious and more confident when relying on AI advice.
2. **Cognitive Surrender Phenomenon**: The researchers coined the term "cognitive surrender" to describe this phenomenon, where people trust AI responses despite being incorrect.
3. **AI Advantages in Certain Situations**: In some cases, AI can be effective or even superior to human reasoning.

## Further Research Suggested:
The study's results and the emergence of cognitive surrender suggest a need for more nuanced exploration of the role of AI in decision-making processes, particularly in critical situations requiring nuance and depth of understanding.
