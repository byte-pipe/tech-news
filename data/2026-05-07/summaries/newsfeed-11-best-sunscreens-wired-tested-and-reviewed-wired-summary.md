## Key Points:
- Sunscreen is important to protect against UV rays that cause sunburn, premature aging, and skin cancer
- Choosing a sunscreen depends on various factors such as sensitivity, weather conditions, and activity level
- Mineral sunscreens create a physical barrier that provides protection while leaving a white cast on the skin
- Chemical sunscreens contain ingredients that absorb UV rays before converting them to heat for release

## Structured Organization
* Types of Sunscreens: Physical (Mineral) vs. Chemical
	+ Mineral Sunscreens: physically protect by creating a barrier, tend to be gentler and cause less irritation
	+ Chemical Sunscreens: absorbed into the skin for conversion to heat before being released by the body
