# Anthropic, Goldman and others launch $1.5 billion AI venture

**Anthropic Signs $1.5 Billion Deal with Goldman Sachs and Blackstone**

* Anthropic has partnered with private equity giants Goldman Sachs and Blackstone to deploy AI firm Claude across hundreds of businesses.
* The new venture will embed engineers inside mid-sized companies to redesign workflows around artificial intelligence (AI) agents.
* The goal is to address a key talent bottleneck in the AI boom, where experts are scarce for implementing technology into real-world operations.

**Key Facts:**

• **New Deal:** €1.5 billion investment
• **Partnerships:** Goldman Sachs and Blackstone; Hellman & Friedman
• **Companies:** Targeted mid-sized businesses with a focus on healthcare, manufacturing, financial services, retail, and real estate sectors.
• ** Claude AI Model:** Deployed directly inside businesses to transform operations.

**Impact:**

• Tackles key talent bottleneck in the AI boom
• Helps companies apply AI tools effectively and implement changes
• Prepares for massive IPOs as early as this year

**Key Players:**

• Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei (no longer at the helm due to a change of leadership)
• Michael J. Novolinski, CEO of Goldman Sachs
• Peter Goldston, Head of Technology Investment Banking at Blackstone
