**OpenReel Video: A Client-Side Video Editor**
=====================================================

### Overview

OpenReel Video is an open source CapCut alternative that offers professional-grade video editing capabilities in a fully browser-based environment. It eliminates the need for uploads, installations, and cloud processing, providing complete privacy.

### Key Features

* **100% Client-Side**: Videos remain on your device, never leaving you exposed to online platforms.
* **No Installation Required**: Works in Chrome/Edge, making it accessible on any web-enabled device.
* **Professional Features**: Multi-track timeline with keyframe animations and GPU-accelerated rendering for smooth 4K editing.
* **GPU Accelerated**: WebGPU and WebCodecs ensure fast exports, reducing overall edit time.
* **Free Forever**: MIT licensed, no subscriptions or watermarks required.

### Key Capabilities

#### Video Editing
* Multi-track timeline with unlimited video, audio, image, text, and graphics tracks
* Real-time preview with smooth playback
* Precision editing for frame-accurate scrubbing and cuts
* Integrated video effects, including brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, and more
* Blend modes with 20+ options

#### Graphics & Text
* Professional text editor with rich styling and animations
* 20+ text animations, including typewriter, fade, slide, bounce, pop, and glitch
* Karaoke-style subtitles synced to audio
* Shape tools for rectangle, circle, arrow, polygon, star with fill/stroke options
* SVG support for importing graphics with color tinting and animations
