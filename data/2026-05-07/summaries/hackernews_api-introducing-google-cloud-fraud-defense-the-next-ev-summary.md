# Introducing Google Cloud Fraud Defense: The Next Evolution of reCAPTCHA

April 22, 2023

## Key Features of Google Cloud Fraud Defense

The introduction of Google Cloud Fraud Defense marks a significant shift towards ensuring security and authenticity in the digital landscape. This platform builds upon the capabilities of reCAPTCHA, providing businesses with advanced tools to measure and control agentic activity on their websites.

### Core Capabilities

#### Agentic Activity Measurement

* A new dashboard for measuring and understanding agentic activities
* Integration with industry standards such as Web Bot Auth and SPIFEE
* Analysis and classification of agentic traffic using traditional methods

#### Agentic Policy Engine

* Granular control over agent and human identities at different stages of the end-user interaction
* Allowance and blocking based on risk scores, automation types, and agent identity

### New Features

* **AI-Resistant Challenge**: Requesting users to verify their presence through a new QR code-based challenge, making automated fraud economically unviable.

#### Existing reCAPTCHA Customers

Existing reCAPTCHA customers will automatically become part of the Fraud Defense platform without any change in pricing or action required.
