# Higher Usage Limits for Claude and Compute Deal with SpaceX

**Summary**

In response to growing demand for high-performance computing capabilities, **Claude**, an AI development platform, has announced significant compute capacity increases via partnerships with **SpaceX**. This agreement allows users like those on the company's Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans to experience more powerful processing times.

**Key Points:**

* Higher usage limits are now in effect for Claude Code in five areas:
	1. Redoubled Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans' five-hour rates
	2. Removal of peak hours limit reduction on all accounts
	3. Significant increase in API rate limits (up to 10x)
* Additional compute capacity is now available at **SpaceX**'s Colossus 1 data center, providing access to:
	+ Over 300 megawatts of new capacity (over one GW) within the month

**Partnership with SpaceX**

In addition to its compute deals with Amazon and Google/Broadcom, **Claude**, has entered a strategic partnership with Microsoft and NVIDIA for up to $30 billion of Azure computing capacity. Additionally, it has committed to covering any increases in consumer electricity prices from its US data centers.
