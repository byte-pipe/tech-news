# Anthropic working on Orbit, its upcoming proactive assistant

**Anthropic Announces Proactive Assistant Orbit**

Anthropic is planning to launch a proactive assistant called Orbit, which will provide personalized insights and briefings from various connected tools.

### Key Features:

- **Opt-in**: The setup for Orbit will be opt-in, with an empty setting representing "Orbit" in Cowork.
- **Personalized Insight Creation**: Orbit will generate proactively based on users' inputs through Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Calendar, Drive, and Figma connected work tools.
- **Inclusive App List**: Orbit apps include Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Calendar, Drive, and Figma alongside Claude Code.

### Context:

This marks Anthropic's expansion into proactive briefing and insights systems. Similar proactive briefing layers are being developed by other companies like Google with Gemini.

### Timing:

Anthropic has planned key events for the launch, including its developer conference Claude Cowork which kicks off on May 6 in San Francisco, followed by London and Tokyo on May 19, and June 10. The build activity suggests Orbit is already under late-stage development preparation.
