description: 'Personal content consistently gets the highest reach on LinkedIn. But most founders are either doing too much of it, doing it wrong, or not doing it at all. Here are the 3 most common mistakes I see with top of funnel content: (1) Too much TOF, not enough everything else If your entire feed is personal stories, relatable moments, and founder life content, you''re building a fan base, not a customer base. Your audience likes you but they have no idea what you''re an expert in or why they should buy from you. Personal content should be 20-50% of your mix depending on your goals. Not 90%. (2) TOF that''s too generic Even your personal posts need a point of view or a specific moment attached to them. The bar isn''t lower just because it''s top of funnel. A post about a hard decision you made this week is personal content. A post about working from Morocco and how you''re more productive when you change your environment is personal content. You need specifics and real stories for
  this content to land. (3) Avoiding TOF entirely because it feels "fluffy" A lot of founders think personal content is a waste of time because it doesn''t directly sell their product. But the data is really clear on this - TOF posts get the most reach, every time. And that reach is what feeds the rest of your funnel. You can''t convert people who don''t know you exist. If nobody''s engaging with your educational posts, it''s probably because your audience doesn''t feel connected to you as a person yet. Personal content fixes that. Be honest - are you guilty of any of these? | 25 comments on LinkedIn'
