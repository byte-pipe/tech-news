# A New Trick Reveals AI Models’ Inner Thoughts | WIRED

**Computer Scientists Discover Hidden "Thinking" Process of FrontierAI Models**

A team of computer scientists has developed a way to extract and analyze the hidden reasoning process of frontierAI models, which they believe may have been used previously without proper attribution. The finding provides evidence that certain Chinese open-weight Chinese model providers may actually be distilling reasoning information from their US counterparts.

**Key Findings:**

* Researchers have identified flaws in several major AI providers' methods and demonstrated how they can be used to recover personal information, such as passwords and API keys.
* The method appears to work for both closed and open-weight models, including China's Kimi K3 and the US company Thinking Machines' DeepSeek.
* Distillation of reasoning information from existing models has become a widely used technique in AI development.

**Potential Implications:**

* The discovery may lead to new areas of research and investigation into the ethics and safety of AI development.
* It also raises concerns about data security and the potential for large-scale hacking attacks using flawed methods.
* Researchers believe that their method could provide a more complete understanding of why some Chinese models seem to be mimicking US models, but this requires further confirmation.

**Context:**

* The technique is described as "distillation," which is often used in AI development to quickly copy the capabilities of existing models over to new ones.
* The researchers note that distillation has become a contentious topic due to claims that Chinese companies are using it to allegedly copy US models.
