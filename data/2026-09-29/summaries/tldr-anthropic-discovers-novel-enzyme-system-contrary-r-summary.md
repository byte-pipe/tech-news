## nChroma Bio announcement
- Boston‑based nChroma Bio reported the elimination of hepatitis B virus (HBV) from human liver cells using an epigenetic therapy.  
- The therapy disables viral DNA without cutting it, offering an alternative to CRISPR‑based DNA slicing.  
- By avoiding DNA breaks, the approach aims to reduce the elevated cancer risk associated with traditional CRISPR editing.
