# 300-plus local data center bans have only delayed just three US projects — bans 'much more bark than bite,' only 2.3 gigawatts of data centers are hel...

**300+ Local Data Center Bans Delayed US Projects**

*   Local data center construction moratoriums have delayed approximately 3 US projects, with 2.3 GW of planned data centers under moratorium in moratorium areas expected to be delayed by as little as 21 GW.
*   A report by SemiAnalysis estimates that only 1.5 GW of moratoriums have been delayed, with a total of 38 GW of planned data center capacity in 2027, expected to come on line.
*   Analysts attribute the ineffectiveness of moratoriums to the fact that they do not revoke existing approvals. Instead, short moratoriums may expire before they take effect, and litigation or equipment lead times may also set the schedule.
*   The report notes that moratoriums do not necessarily delay construction, as they are more of a freeze on accepting new applications. Capacity already under construction or fully permitted is also out of reach, making it difficult to delay delivery dates.
*   The report concludes that while moratoriums may appear to inhibit growth, they have only delayed a small fraction of projects, with a total of 16 GW of proposed capacity under moratorium expected to come online in 2026 instead of 2027.
*   The report notes that moratoriums can be implemented in several ways, including:
    *   A temporary pause in permitting new applications
    *   A freeze on accepting new bids or permits
    *   A delay in completing existing permits
    *   A requirement that projects must be completed before a certain date
    *   A requirement that projects must meet specific technical or security standards
