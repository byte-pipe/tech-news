# ‘Incredible’: bleached corals thrive within days when touching healthy neighbour | Nature

Here is a concise and informative summary of the article:

* Bleached coral can rapidly recover from bleaching through contact with healthy coral fragments
* A laboratory study showed that coral fragments can become healthy again within weeks after being touched by a healthy fragment
* The study involved 10 pairs of coral fragments from the same animal, with bleached and healthy fragments touching each other or being separated by millimeters
* In the touching pairs, the bleached coral recovered within 8 weeks, and the symbionts transferred from the healthy fragment reviving the coral in just a few days
* The study found that coral fragments can fuse together to facilitate the transfer of symbionts, allowing them to recover from bleaching quickly
* This breakthrough has the potential to inspire scalable solutions for reversing widespread coral decline caused by rising sea temperatures.
