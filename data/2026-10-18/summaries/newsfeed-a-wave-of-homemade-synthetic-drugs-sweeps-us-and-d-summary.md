# A wave of homemade, synthetic drugs sweeps US and deepens crisis - BBC News

## America's War on Drugs: The Synthetic Wave

*   A recent wave of synthetic opioids, particularly fentanyl, has swept the US, deepening the country's opioid crisis.
*   Homemade, synthetic drugs are more powerful and readily available, transforming the landscape of addiction in the country.
*   Fentanyl, a 50 to 100 times more potent synthetic opioid than heroin, is now often cut with tranquillisers, making reviving overdose victims more difficult.
*   The opioid crisis has reached an all-time high, with 110,000 Americans dying from overdoses in 2022. The number has recently dropped to around 70,000.
*   The widespread adoption of naloxone (brand name Narcan) has helped reverse opioid overdoses, but the use of fentanyl and other synthetic drugs continues to pose significant risks.
*   The opioid crisis is unlikely to be cured, as the primary users of fentanyl and other synthetic drugs are often not struggling with addiction, but rather trying to treat overdoses.
