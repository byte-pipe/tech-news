# Apple is reportedly cutting hundreds of jobs from Siri, Vision Pro teams

## Key Points
- Apple is eliminating over 200 positions, with roughly 100 cuts from the Vision Pro team and another 100 from Siri and the Intelligent Systems Experience group that integrates AI into devices.  
- Bloomberg reports the layoffs are part of a reshuffle to prioritize newer AI initiatives and upcoming products.  
- Apple confirmed the reductions, stating the move is to “evolve our business to deliver the best experiences for our users,” and noted that new roles will be created while some existing roles are impacted.  
- The cuts occur amid a broader transition period for Apple, including efforts to strengthen its AI position, rising memory costs tied to AI development, and higher production expenses for flagship hardware.  
- Earlier in the summer, Apple raised prices on Macs and iPads and launched a leasing program for major hardware to attract more customers and offset increased costs.  
- Apple is also pursuing legal action against OpenAI, accusing the company of trade theft.
