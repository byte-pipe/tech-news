# Advocacy groups file complaint against Ghana over Trump deportations | Migration News | Al Jazeera

**Advocacy groups accuse Ghana of violating human rights**

* The ECOWAS Court of Justice has been criticized for its role in a complaint filed by 27 deportation victims from the United States who are seeking asylum in Ghana, according to reports.
* A senior partner at Ghanaian law firm Merton & Everett LLP says that individuals granted "protections" but sent back within hours or days could face persecution and torture, prompting the lawsuit.
* The complaint alleges that Ghana is helping deportees from the US find safe haven, rather than ensuring their basic human rights are respected.

**Background**

The US has implemented a "third-country" removal policy targeting individuals who cannot be sent directly to their home countries. However, this process often puts victims at risk of serious harm in those nations.
* The lawsuit was filed alongside Cornell Law School's Transnational Disputes Clinic and the Global Strategic Litigation Council, a coalition of NGOs.

**Key findings**

* US judges have ruled that individuals cannot be sent directly back to their home countries due to concerns over torture or persecution.
* Ghana is accused of violating domestic and regional law by "facilitating removals to unsafe countries".
* The US has reversed visa curbs on Ghana in response to this agreement.
