# Binance says some Europe clients may be affected as license bid pulled

**Summary**

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, has withdrawn an application for European Union (EU) regulation via Greece. The company plans to pursue an authorization in another EU member state, where it currently holds a local license. Some Binance customers in Poland, Italy, Spain, and France have received emails advising them on how to withdraw their money from the exchange due to the changed regulations.

**Main Points:**

* Binance has withdrawn its application for EU regulation via Greece
* The company plans to pursue an authorization in another EU member state (France)
* Some customers in Poland, Italy, Spain, and France may be affected by the regulations
* Binance is communicating with customers on how to withdraw their money from the exchange
* The company will comply with applicable requirements before July 1 and aims to secure a license "in the coming months"
* EU-licensed rivals like Bitpanda and OKX have been tweeting praise for Greece's regulatory approach

**Additional Information:**

* Binance's recent involvement in investigations and criminal charges, including money laundering
* The company's ban in the UK since 2021 and its plea guilty to financial sanctions in 2023
* Binance founder Changpeng Zhao is currently awaiting sentencing for money laundering violations after being pardoned by U.S. President Donald Trump
