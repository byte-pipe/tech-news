# Why the Bronze Age collapsed

**Why the Bronze Age Collapsed**

**Key Points**

* The Bronze Age collapse is a topic of ongoing debate among historians
* Historians suggest various factors contributed to the collapse, including drought, famines, invasions, and internal revolts
* However, the question remains why the empires that once thrived in the Bronze Age didn't re-emerge centuries later

**The Battle of Kadesh**

* Held in 1274 BC, the Battle of Kadesh was a massive conflict between Egypt and the Hittite Empire
* Estimated 30,000-50,000 soldiers and 5,000 chariots took part in the battle
* Despite a massive and well-equipped force, the outcome of the battle remains uncertain

**Why the Bronze Age Collapse**

* historians argue that the collapse was not due to sudden catastrophes such as droughts or invasions, but rather a combination of internal and external factors
* Key factors contributing to the collapse include:
	+ **Influence and Trade Disruption**: The loss of sea routes and trade connections may have weakened the economies of the major powers and created internal conflicts.
	+ **Drought and Famine**: Prolonged droughts could have weakened the agricultural systems of the Bronze Age civilizations, making them more vulnerable to external pressures.
	+ **Internal Revolts and Revolutions**: Power struggles and internal conflicts within the empires may have weakened their ability to cooperate and respond to external threats.

**The Implications of the Collapse**

* The Bronze Age collapse raises questions about the nature and fate of the societies that once thrived during this period
* The sudden disappearance of regional powers may have contributed to the instability and decay of many other Bronze Age civilizations
* A lack of cooperation and inter dependence among the major powers of the time may have made it difficult for them to respond effectively to external threats, leading to their collapse.
