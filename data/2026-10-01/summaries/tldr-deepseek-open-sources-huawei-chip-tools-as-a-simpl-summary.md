# DeepSeek open-sources Huawei chip tools as a simpler alternative to CUDA

**DeepSeek Opens Source Huawei Chip Tools as Alternative to CUDA**

DeepSeek, a Chinese AI lab, has open-sourced its programming tools for Huawei's Ascend AI chips. The tools, dubbed "TileLang," are designed as a simpler alternative to Nvidia's CUDA. This development marks a significant shift in the landscape of Huawei's chip offerings.

**Key Points:**

* DeepSeek has released six software modules for Huawei's chips, including TileLang, a programming language that offers more ease of use than CUDA.
* The tools have been developed with Huawei in mind, with six modules already available for existing customers.
* A "supernode" system has been built from 128 Ascend 950 chips, demonstrating the potential of the technology.
* DeepSeek plans to work together with Nvidia to develop new technologies.
* The company has also received support from Huawei during development, and will deploy at least 160,000 Huawei accelerators in a data center.

**Background:**

* Huawei's AI chips have been struggling to meet demand due to export control restrictions and limited access to Nvidia's advanced chips.
* Huawei has announced plans to restrict sales outside of China, citing a desire to maintain control over its fate.
* DeepSeek's decision to open-source its tools is seen as a significant alternative to Nvidia's CUDA.

**Trends:**

* The development of independent, self-controlled GPU software ecosystems is a key priority for DeepSeek.
* The company's goal is to establish a universal, high-level language that is easy to use and can reach the full performance potential of the hardware.
* The involvement of both DeepSeek and Nvidia suggests a strategic partnership to address the growing demand for AI chips.

**Financials:**

* DeepSeek is raising funds at a valuation close to $74 billion, with plans to deploy at least 500 billion yuan ($74 billion) worth of technology.
* Huawei's chairman, Eric Xu, has stated that the company must "build AI fast" to avoid risks associated with US export controls.
