# Two Driverless Cars Crash at Abu Dhabi Racing League Event

At the Abu Dhabi Autonomous Racing League (A2RL) event, two driverless cars crashed during a Formula 1-inspired endurance race at the Imola, Italy track. The event, which included Formula 1 champions, attempted to test the boundaries of autonomous vehicle capabilities.

*   The two surviving cars were completed by UAE's Kinetiz and Germany's Constructor Racing. 
*   Only two drivers completed the entire course, showcasing the gap between autonomous vehicle response and human reaction.
*   The event was an international competition with special testing conditions, including rain and hail.
*   PoliMove's entry, a driverless car, was damaged in the crash and did not qualify for the podium ceremony.
*   Although self-driving cars do not intend to compete with human racing, this event supports the development of autonomous racing for testing purposes.
*   Autonomous racing is focused on simulation and testing, rather than direct competition with human drivers.
*   This allows for the evaluation and improvement of driverless vehicle performance in critical scenarios.
