# The Space Review: Stars in the sky: The top secret URSALA, RAQUEL, and FARRAH satellites from the 1970s to the 21st century

### 1. Introduction to HEXAGON Program

* The HEXAGON program, launched from California between 1971 and 1986, consisted of 20 HEXAGONS, with one failure.
* HEXAGON was a photo-reconnaissance satellite, primarily engaged in gathering electronic intelligence (ELINT) data.

### 2. Early Features and Submissivity

* The program began with "hitchhiker" satellites, which soared into orbit and gathered signals before landing.
* These early satellites were quickly replaced, demonstrating that their payloads were expendable and designed to be easily replenished.
* By the 1970s, the National Reconnaissance Office (NRO) standardized the HEXAGONS, shifting their operational mission from national leadership to tactical support for military forces.

### 3. Subsats and Program 989

* By the 1970s, sub-satellites were deployed off the sides of larger HEXAGON satellites, making it possible to carry multiple programs within a single satellite.
* Program 989, a subsatellite of HEXAGON-2, was the first of these smaller satellites (credit: Peter Hunter).

### 4. Program 989 and Mission 7300

* The first "hitchhiker" satellite, which also carried electronic intelligence payloads, was called Program 11 (P-11) and launched in 1965/1966.
* In 1967, Program 11 was identified among the top ten electronic intelligence missions and was eventually renamed Program 989, indicating that it operated as a subsatellite.
* In 1970, it was reidentified as Program 989-A, reflecting its continued operational mission.
* The 21st century introduced significant information about Program 989, including previously secret space shuttle payloads.

### 5. Legacy and Significance

* HEXAGON and its associated subsatellites introduced the concept of small, expendable satellites carrying electronic intelligence data for tactical support.
* This shift marked a significant change in the way that intelligence data was collected, from national leadership to supporting tactical forces.
* The data received by these smaller satellites provided a profound increase in real-time intelligence about potential threats to national security.
