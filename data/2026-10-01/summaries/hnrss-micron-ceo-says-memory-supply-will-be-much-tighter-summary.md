# Micron CEO Claims Memory Supply Will Tighten in 2027 and 2028

Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said memory and storage supply will be much tighter in the next two years than in the current year. Mehrotra made these remarks during Micron's fiscal Q4 2026 earnings call on September 30.

## Key Points and Analysis

* Mehrotra expects supply and demand to remain tight in 2027 and 2028, with no clear balance in site yet.
* Industry demand has strengthened, and AI is driving up demand for memory and storage.
* Overcoming supply challenges will take additional time, with ID2 fab starting wafers output in late 2028.
* More than 75% of Micron's 2027 output is already committed to customers.
* DRAM prices rose in the high-teens percent range last quarter, while NAND prices increased by about 30%.
