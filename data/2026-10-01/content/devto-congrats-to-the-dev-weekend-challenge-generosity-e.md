@yashksainibuilt Cairn, which changes who issues the receipt for a donation. Funds unlock only when the recipient signs, and that signature carries the hash of a recording where they say what they received. The separation of authorization from testimony is a careful piece of design, and keeping one implementation of the hash function compiled for both chain and client is the sort of thing you only notice when it is missing.
