# 'Superworms’ help scientists with a vexing task: Cleaning animal specimens : NPR

**Using Superworms to Clean Animal Specimens**

* Scientists are using larvae of a beetle native to South and Central America as an alternative to prepare animal skeletons for scientific study.
* These larvae, called superworms, can strip animal flesh from skeletons and are considered a safer and more efficient way to clean specimens than current methods.
* A team at Mashhad's natural history museum uses superworms to help with the cleaning process, which also has benefits for conservation efforts.
* The use of superworms is time-efficient and gentle on delicate bones, making it an attractive alternative to other methods.
