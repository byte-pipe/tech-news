# A $10K Bounty Aims to Make Sony’s PlayStation 5 a Computer Again | WIRED

**Fuluwants to Make Sony's PlayStation 5 a Computer Again**

**A One-Time Bounty of $10K**

*   Fulu seeks individuals who can fix or bypass Sony's proprietary software locks on its PlayStation 5 consoles.
*   The organization will pay $10,000 for successful bounties and offer up to an additional $10K in matching donations.
*   The goal is to prove that consoles like the PS5 have significant computing power that can be repurposed for non-entertainment purposes.

**Breaking through Digital Locks**

*   Individuals must demonstrate a fix or bypass of Sony's software restrictions, which can be complex given the company's strict digital rights management (DRM) protections.
*   The Fulu bounty aims to show that gaming consoles can function as general-purpose computers by repurposing their internal hardware for non-entertainment purposes.

**The Risks Involved**

*   Breaking through a company's software restrictions may be punishable under Section 1201 of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).
*   Fulu wants to emphasize that only individuals who are aware of and comply with applicable laws can potentially use their Sony consoles for non-entertainment purposes.
