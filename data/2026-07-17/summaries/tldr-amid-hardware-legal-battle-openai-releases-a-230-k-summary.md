# Amid hardware legal battle, OpenAI releases a $230 keyboard for Codex | TechCrunch

**OpenAI Enters Hardware Market with $230 Keyboard**

• OpenAI has launched a custom-built keyboard designed for its AI coding assistant, Codex.
• The device is called the Codex Micro and comes equipped with features such as "Agent Keys," customizable Command keys, and a joystick for launching workflows.
• The company emphasizes that it's more of a novelty item than a product intended for mass appeal.

**Long-Haul Hardware News**

• OpenAI has also revealed plans for a portable, screenless smart speaker called the Codex SoundWave.
• The device is anticipated to be designed with mechanically moveable elements and will integrate with ChatGPT and involve "mechanical elements that can move on their own."
• However, details are still sketchy and are subject to change, according to Bloomberg.

**Closely ties with Apple**

• OpenAI's attempt to enter the hardware market has drawn attention from its former engineers from Apple, which is currently suing OpenAI.
• The company alleges wrongdoing under Section 3332 of the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), stating that OpenAI used confidential information developed by Apple for its own product development.
