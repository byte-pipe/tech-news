# A Humanoid Company Backed by Eric Trump Is Preparing Its Robots for War | WIRED

**Foundation Future Industries: Building the Robot Supersoldier**

The company Foundation Future Industries aims to create an all-American humanoid robot capable of lethal capabilities, with a potential focus on military applications. CEO Sankaet Pathak revealed that his startup plans to unveil enhanced versions in the next couple of months.

Foundation's robots are designed for combat, logistics, reconnaissance, and inspection tasks, catering specifically to the US military market. With backing from Eric Trump, the president's son, who is an engineer at heart, the company has secured government contracts worth millions and a $24 million contract with the Pentagon.

Unlike other companies focused on military applications, Foundation prioritizes human-robot collaboration and autonomy. The Phantom MK1 humanoid robot has been tested for Ukrainian forces during the war in Ukraine, demonstrating its versatility and potential uses.

* Foundation Future Industries aims to develop an all-American humanoid robot with lethal capabilities
* Company CEO Sankaet Pathak plans to unveil more advanced versions in 2024
* Background: US military interest in humanoids (Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency) and development of technologies relevant for "militarized humanoid capabilities"

* Unique approach: Focus on collaboration between humans and robots, prioritizing autonomy and versatility
