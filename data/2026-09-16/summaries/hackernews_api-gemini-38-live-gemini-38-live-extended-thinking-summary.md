# Gemini 3.8 Live & Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking – Summary

## Overview
- Google introduced two new voice‑enabled AI models: **Gemini 3.8 Live** and **Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking**.  
- Aim: make voice interactions more natural, fluid, and capable of complex, real‑time reasoning.

## Core Capabilities
- **Real‑time visual grounding** – processes images and video frames while conversing.  
- **Multi‑language support** – detects and switches among 97 languages mid‑dialogue.  
- **Background tool & API execution** – acknowledges requests, continues chatting, and completes tasks asynchronously.  
- **Extended Thinking** – simultaneous reasoning and speech for multi‑step workflows, with verbal cues like “Let me check that…”.
