# Marburg Virus Disease Case Identified in Uganda Amidst Ebola Outbreak

• A one-and-a-half-year-old child was identified as a case of Marburg virus disease, which is related to Ebola viruses and causes similar hemorrhagic diseases.
• Despite the identification of this new case, Ugandan health authorities appear reluctant to publicly disclose information about its context.
• There are no current active cases in Uganda beyond the deceased toddler, with Africa CDC reporting 0 out of 4 people in the country tested positive for Marburg virus.
