# Giant trees have no trouble pumping water to top branches - University of Exeter News

**Tall Trees Can Pump Water Efficiently to Top Branches**

*   A study published in Science found that tall Dipterocarp trees can pump water efficiently to their top branches, despite conventional scientific theory suggesting it would be difficult.
*   The research, led by the University of Exeter and Cardiff University, used 71-meter-tall Dipterocarp trees from Malaysian Borneo with varying branch heights to assess drought resilience.
*   Key findings:
    -   Taller trees compensate for their height through various adaptations, including wider water-carrying vessels near the ground and leaves that withstand greater water stress before wilting.
    -   The hydraulic system of tall Dipterocarp trees is perfectly evolved to support their height, with a weaker focus on growth under drought conditions.
*   This study challenges existing predictions about the vulnerability of tall trees to drought and highlights the importance of further research into these species.

The findings suggest that researchers need more information to fully understand how other tall tree species cope with drought conditions.
