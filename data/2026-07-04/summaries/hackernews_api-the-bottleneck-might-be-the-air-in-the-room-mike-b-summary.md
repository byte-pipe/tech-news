# The bottleneck might be the air in the room | Mike Bowler

### The Importance of Room Air Quality for Team Performance

*   An analysis of studies using a portable carbon dioxide monitor found significant drops in decision-making performance at 1,000 ppm and 2,500 ppm.
*   Research indicates that air quality plays a crucial role in team productivity, even when all other factors are controlled.
*   Lack of fresh air can lead to decreased cognitive function, increased fatigue, and impaired focus.

### Potential Causes of Poor Team Performance

*   As mentioned earlier, the room's air is not just a matter of CO2 levels; it also relates to humidity, temperature, and exposure time.
*   A combination of poor ventilation, inadequate air circulation, and confined spaces such as meeting rooms can hinder team performance.

### Considerations for Remote Teams

*   When deciding whether to bring remote teams back into the office or continue with hybrid models, consider the specific characteristics of your team's work environment and the individuals involved.
*   Factors such as indoor air quality, lighting, sound levels, and noise pollution should also be taken into account when evaluating the suitability of different working spaces.
