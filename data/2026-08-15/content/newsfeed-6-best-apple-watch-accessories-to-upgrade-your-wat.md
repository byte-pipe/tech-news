If you haveaniPhone, you've probably considered buyingan Apple Watch—and with good reason. It's thebest smartwatchandbest fitness trackerfor iPhone owners, and it allows you to make calls, send texts, pay for purchases, and more without reaching for your phone. That said, I’m a firm believer that your Apple Watch is only as good as the accessories that support it.

The right band can make it comfortable enough to wear all day and night for sleep tracking. A charging stand will remind you to keep it topped up, and a protective case could save you from a shattered screen. To help upgrade your Apple Watch setup, I, along with a few of my WIRED colleagues, tested chargers, bands, cases, and more to find the accessories worth the money. I’d also recommend getting AppleCare, which is the company'sextended warranty plan, given thatservice pricingfor watch repairs is exorbitant.

For more smart accessories, check out our guides to theBest Apple 3-in-1 ChargersandBest Apple Watch Bands.

Nike

### Sport Loop

$49
 

Apple

When you buy your Apple Watch, you get to choosea bandat checkout. I find textile and metal (stainless steel or titanium) bands to be more comfortable and fashionable than fluoroelastomer bands, which tend to be rigid and can irritate your skin. I love the Nike Sport Loop so much that it's been my everyday band for six years, and I ended up buying it in two colors: navy blue and black. Made from recycled polyester, Spandex, and nylon, it's soft, breathable, and easy to hand-wash. The Velcro pull tab makes it as adjustable as a band can get, and it's easy to fasten with one hand. Apple doesn't advertise any water resistance, but I've swum with it multiple times; after rinsing it off and letting it air dry, it looked brand new. The yarn is reflective if you're running at night, though it is subtle.

The band comes in five colors and three case sizes, which makes it compatible with most versions of the Apple Watch. If you're shopping for a leather band, Konsu NYC makes agorgeous strap, too. For more recommendations, check out ourBest Apple Watch Bandsguide.

### Stand One Max

Nomad

The Nomad Stand One Max is a splurge, costing even more than reviewerSimon Hill’stop pick(theBelkin 3-in-1 Qi2 Charging Stand), but its minimalist design is arguably worth the extra spend. It’s compact enough that it doesn’t overcrowd a nightstand, and with the cable tucked away, it looks sleek. It’s also one of our favorites for fast Apple Watch charging, along with Qi2 25-watt charging for iPhones and MagSafe devices. The angled charging pad supports your phone in either portrait or landscape orientation, and the Apple Watch puck allows for Nightstand mode. It comes in three colors and includes a durable, braided 6.6-foot USB-C cable. The only downside is that there’s no included power adapter.Nomad’s 40W Slim Power Adapter ($25)is an easy add-on, but it does increase the cost of an already pricey charger.

Hontao

### Ultra Thin Case

$8
 

Amazon

Unlikea phone, a watch is worn on your wrist, so every millimeter of real estate counts. If all you want is a thin, affordable, and attractive bumper that will protect your watch from bangs and scratches throughout the day, this is my favorite. It's made from a matte thermoplastic urethane (TPU) that is extremely thin and fits a 41-mm watch well. I have and like the dark green color, but reviewers say the lighter colors show dirt very easily, so I would stick with darker shades.

Runner-Up:TheSpigen Thin Fit ($15)is also a very nice, minimal case. But it's a bit pricier, and the plastic is not as soft to the touch.—Adrienne So
