# 3 AI Prompts for Google Ads

## Stand out from competitors
- Prompt asks AI to compare the client site (e.g., www.example.com) with listed competitors and highlight differentiators.  
- Generates theme‑based ad headlines (≤30 characters) and descriptions (≤90 characters) for three themes:  
  - Benefits and outcomes  
  - Product and technical features  
  - General features and industry‑specific language  
- Requires at least 10 headlines and 10 descriptions per theme.  
- Can be extended to include sitelinks, callouts, and structured snippets, or to focus on a specific webpage.

## Create landing page content
- Prompt produces landing‑page copy centered on a chosen keyword (or variation) with the goal of collecting email sign‑ups.  
- Constraints: headline includes the keyword, body ≤200 words, one paragraph plus bullet points, keyword appears at least twice.  
- Generates three versions targeting different psychological triggers:  
  - Urgency – why the form must be filled now  
  - Benefits and outcomes – what the user gains  
  - Comforting – calm, reassuring tone  
- Allows mixing of triggers based on advertiser needs.

## Define the audience
- Prompt reviews the client site and crafts an ideal customer profile aligned with Google’s audience options.  
- Produces two custom segments:  
  1. Interests or purchase intentions (list of URLs/apps).  
  2. Keyword‑based searches across Google properties (including YouTube).  
- Maps these segments to relevant in‑market and affinity audiences, noting where exact matches are unavailable and suggesting conservative vs. aggressive targeting strategies.
