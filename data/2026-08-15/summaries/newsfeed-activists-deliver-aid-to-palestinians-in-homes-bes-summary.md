# Activists deliver aid to Palestinians in homes besieged by Israeli settlers

## Key events
- Activists left water and food outside a house in Qusra, where two Palestinian families have been trapped since Sunday after settlers blocked access and cut power and water.
- Israeli forces said they prevented activists from entering the “closed military zone,” but did not stop the aid from being left outside.
- On Friday settlers set up a tent near the site; Israeli troops later removed it while “protecting the local residents.”
- About a dozen Palestinian households were ordered to leave their homes during a Thursday operation to disperse settlers; some vacated houses were reportedly used by the Israeli military as barracks.

## Israeli military and government response
- The IDF described Qusra as a closed military zone, restricting civilian entry.
- Soldiers were instructed that residents should remain in their homes; the army said its aim was to protect them.
- A spokesperson condemned the settlers’ actions as “deplorable” and “unacceptable,” promising investigations and arrests.

## Settler actions and motivations
- Settlers blocked the road to the families’ gates, cut utilities, and prayed with soldiers who first arrived on the scene.
- An attempt to remove settlers on Wednesday led to clashes involving tear gas and stun grenades.
- The broader aim of the settlers appears to be to empty the area of Palestinian residents, a pattern seen in previous forced evictions.

## Humanitarian impact
- The trapped families have been without power and water for several days.
- Activists’ delivery of water and food represents the only recent aid reaching the besieged homes.
- Displacement figures: since January 2023, 127 Palestinian communities in the occupied West Bank have faced full or partial displacement, affecting over 6,390 people; in 2026, about 3,800 Palestinians were displaced due to settler violence, demolitions, and evictions, nearly half of them children.

## Wider context
- Israeli settlements are considered illegal under international law, yet the Israeli government continues rapid expansion.
- The Qusra incident highlights ongoing challenges for Israeli forces in curbing settler violence and the relative impunity settlers enjoy.
