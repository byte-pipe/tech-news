# A10 Networks introduces AI Gateway to secure and manage enterprise AI

## Overview
- A10 Networks announced the general availability of the **A10 AI Gateway**, a centralized, intelligent control plane that provides unified routing, cost management, and governance for every AI agent, application, and large language model (LLM) used by an organization.  
- The solution addresses the visibility gap that emerges as AI adoption accelerates and developers embed AI across business systems.

## Why the gateway is needed
- Without a central control point, teams have limited insight into which models are used, their performance, associated costs, and workload efficiency.  
- Enterprises must balance rapid AI deployment with governance, security, and cost control.

## Key capabilities
- **Identity‑based AI access** – Access and routing policies are applied per user or group (e.g., engineering vs. finance) and synchronized with existing directory services.  
- **Smart routing** – The gateway evaluates the complexity and type of each request, routing simple tasks to cost‑efficient models and complex, reasoning‑intensive tasks to more capable models.  
- **Centralized traffic and cost management** – Real‑time per‑request cost tracking in dollars, per‑model and per‑team token budgets with hard limits and soft‑limit alerts, and business‑layer rate limiting (TPM/RPM) enforced per key or team, all with unified visibility.

## Benefits
- Provides a governed environment for all AI interactions, enabling performance optimization, spend control, centralized observability, and enforced security.  
- Gives developers and users reliable access to the appropriate models while maintaining organizational oversight.

## Integration with A10’s AI security portfolio
- Complements existing TrojAI and ThreatX solutions, securing AI across its full lifecycle: tested before deployment, defended at runtime, and enforced at the network and API layers.

## Deployment options and data sovereignty
- Available as software or integrated hardware, capable of running entirely within the customer’s environment—on‑premises, in a private cloud, or air‑gapped.  
- Ensures models, data, and policies remain under the organization’s control, offering full data sovereignty not achievable with cloud‑hosted or edge‑bound gateways.

## Leadership perspective
- Dhrupad Trivedi, President and CEO of A10 Networks, states that the AI Gateway gives enterprises a single intelligent control plane to see AI usage, govern access, and match each request to the right model at the right cost, allowing fast AI adoption without sacrificing control.
