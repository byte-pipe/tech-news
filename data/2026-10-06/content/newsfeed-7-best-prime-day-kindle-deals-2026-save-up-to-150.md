There’s no bettertime to shop for Amazon’s e-readers than during one of its own sale events. Prime Day Kindle deals are here, but they aren’t as impactful as they used to be.

That’s because Amazon raised prices on almost allKindle e-readersback in August, along withother products it makes. Buying during Amazon’s fall Prime Big Deals Day is one of your only chances to avoid paying the new price tag. Not all the deals are huge discounts, but if you’re set on a new Kindle, this is your moment.

It’s also worth noting thatAmazon announced new Kindles last week. There’s a new 2026 version of the Kindle, Kindle Paperwhite, and Kindle Colorsoft, plus Kids versions of all three. Each has an aluminum version, something Amazon has never offered before. So far, only the Kindle is available now, with the rest of the lineup to come. It’s up to you if you want to wait to see how the new models compare to the old before buying, or snag one of these on-sale models before they eventually disappear.

Check out all the Prime Day Kindle deals below, from the best deals to my favorite models, regardless of price. Also, browse our roundup of theAbsolute Best Prime Big Deal Days Deals, plus my full guide toall of Amazon’s on-sale devices and whether the sales are any good.
