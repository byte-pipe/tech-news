# Hacktoberfest Open‑Source AI Challenge – Week 1: Touch Grass

## Overview
- Runs from October 5 to October 11, 2026.  
- Build with open‑weight or open‑source AI to get people off screens and into the outdoors (e.g., bird‑call identifier, garden planner, run‑club route builder).  
- Emphasize that the open components are essential to the project’s functionality.

## How to Participate
- Publish a DEV post using the provided “Touch Grass Submission Template.”  
- Describe what you built, show a demo, and explain why open innovation matters for your solution.  
- Optional: highlight any partner technology used.  
- Review judging criteria, rules, guidelines, and the FAQ before submitting.

## Prize Structure
- **Overall winner** – $250 USD, DEV++ membership, exclusive DEV badge.  
- **Featured category winners** (6) – $200 USD + exclusive badge each.  
- **Partner category winners** (10) – $100 USD + exclusive badge each.  
- Every valid submission receives a completion badge.

## Judging Criteria
- **Writing Quality** (heaviest weight): clarity, engagement, explanation of the project, target audience, and open‑innovation impact.  
- **Relevance to Prompt & Theme:** core use of open‑source AI/open‑weight models and encouragement of outdoor activity.  
- **Creativity:** originality or fresh take on a known problem.  
- **Technical Execution:** functionality and sound engineering.  
- **Use of Partner Technology** (optional): meaningful integration of the selected partner’s tool.
