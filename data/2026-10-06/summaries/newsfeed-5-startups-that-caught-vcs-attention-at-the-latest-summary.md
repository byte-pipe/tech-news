# Summary of “5 startups that caught VCs’ attention at the latest PearX demo day | TechCrunch”

## PearX accelerator overview
- PearX is a 12‑week accelerator run by Pear VC, limited to 20 startups per cohort.  
- Demo days are biannual, attract top venture capitalists, and are kept secret until the event.  
- Investments can reach up to $2 million per company; the program does not use standard terms like Y Combinator.  
- Recent alumni include Known (voice‑AI dating platform, funded by Forerunner Ventures) and Andera (audit‑automation startup, $37 M Series A from Lightspeed).  

## Startups that generated the most buzz

- **Speridlabs**  
  - *What it does*: Provides spatial foundational models for robotics, gaming, and visual effects.  
  - *Why it stood out*: Claims its “Mundus” 3D model (a “3D Midjourney”) allows persistent geometry editing, unlike competitors such as Runway, Odyssey, and Google’s Genie, which cannot be queried or modified.

- **Saia**  
  - *What it does*: Builds a fast, cost‑efficient chip that runs AI inference directly on device.  
  - *Why it stood out*: Bypasses traditional memory by running AI from flash storage, delivering higher speed, eight‑times the capacity, and four‑times lower power consumption compared with Nvidia’s Jetson.  
  - *Progress*: In talks with Samsung for memory integration; test chips slated for next year; mass production targeted for 2028. Founder Ayaan Govil secured backing from Pear VC co‑founder Mar Hershenson.

- **Ren**  
  - *What it does*: Secure AI personal assistant.  
  - *Why it stood out*: Emphasizes privacy by keeping data on‑device or in a private cloud and enforcing user‑defined guardrails. Uses a fully automated voice system, eliminating human operators from the call‑making process.

- **Veros**  
  - *What it does*: AI‑native trust and estate planning platform.  
  - *Why it stood out*: Automates tasks traditionally handled by attorneys and wealth managers, recommending structures and managing assets over time. Already oversees $250 M in assets under management and is pursuing a trust charter to become a regulated trust company.

- **Datum**  
  - *What it does*: AI engineer for industrial design.  
  - *Why it stood out*: Indexes a company’s 3D design library and uses proprietary “Geometric Fingerprint” technology to identify parts by shape, accelerating physical product development and automating engineering workflows.
