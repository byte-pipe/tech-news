# At 19, founder raises $11M for Ghost, maker of a $3,499 computer for personal AI

## Funding and Team
- Seed round of $11 million led by Andreessen Horowitz; other investors include Abstract, Audacious Ventures, SV Angel, and Nova.  
- Founding team: Zain Javaid (19, CEO), Nicholas Chua (19), Yifei Chen (24), and Gautam Sharda (23).

## Product: Core
- Personal AI computer designed to run AI agents continuously.  
- Price: $3,499; pre‑orders open Monday, shipping scheduled for the last week of October.  
- Hardware: Nvidia RTX Pro 4000 SFF Blackwell GPU (included), custom software layer, browser, and filing system.  
- Form factor: “brain in a box” without a screen; accessed via phone or app; voice mode available through the app.  
- Pre‑installed AI models: Qwen‑3.8‑Next, Qwen‑3.8‑27B, and Gemma‑4‑31B; users can add models from Hugging Face or load their own.

## Differentiation from General‑Purpose PCs
- Built specifically for AI agents, with models pre‑installed, unlike general‑purpose devices such as the Mac Mini.  
- All processing, memory, and model weights run locally; personal data is encrypted and not accessible to cloud providers or Ghost itself.  
- Over‑the‑air updates similar to Tesla; optional auto‑updates for the latest model generations.  
- Integrated firewall monitors outgoing network requests, blocking unauthorized actions and flagging them for user review.

## Privacy and User Control
- Source code, logic, and model weights reside on the device, not on external services.  
- Users own the hardware, data, and control the autonomy granted to AI agents.  
- Device is designed to continue functioning even if Ghost’s cloud services shut down.

## Example Use Case
- Core can proactively notify a user of a potential illness by analyzing elevated resting heart rate, late‑night work patterns, and seasonal flu data for San Francisco.

## Market Outlook
- Founder is not overly concerned about competition from established tech companies; emphasizes that Core’s advantage lies in its software layer—how the model reasons about a user’s life, remembers information, and decides when to act.

## Production
- Units are manufactured at Ghost’s San Francisco facility.
