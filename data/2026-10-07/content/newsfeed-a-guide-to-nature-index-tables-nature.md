The tables for the 2026Nature Spotlight: Infectious diseasesare based on theNature Index databaseand cover infectious-disease articles published in natural-science and health-science journals between 2021 and 2025.

Nature Spotlight: Infectious diseases

A keyword search on abstracts and titles was conducted to extract infectious-disease articles in Digital Science’s Dimensions database (Digital Science is part of Holtzbrinck, the majority shareholder ofNature’s publisher, Springer Nature). The search covered 83 infectious diseases and included 5 subcategories to cover severe coronaviruses and influenza subtypes. Thediseases and their Dimensions search stringswere the same as those forNature Index 2021 Infectious disease. The Dimensions articles identified that were also in the Nature Index database formed the data set for the tables in this Spotlight.
