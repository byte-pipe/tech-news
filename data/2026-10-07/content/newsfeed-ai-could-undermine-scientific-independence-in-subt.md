Confidence in scientific findings is boosted when independent researchers reach the same result by different routes. But the use of artificial intelligence might create a common source of error, with Lisa Messeri and Molly Crockett warning that AI could produce scientific monocultures in which methods and questions converge (L. Messeri and M. J. CrockettNature653, 675–676; 2026).
