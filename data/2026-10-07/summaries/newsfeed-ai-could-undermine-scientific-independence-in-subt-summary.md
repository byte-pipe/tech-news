# AI could undermine scientific independence in subtle ways

## Main Argument
- Confidence in scientific findings grows when independent researchers arrive at the same result through different methods.  
- The authors warn that artificial intelligence may create a common source of error across studies.  
- Reliance on AI could foster scientific monocultures, causing convergence of methods and research questions.  
- This convergence threatens the methodological diversity and independence that underpin robust science.
