# Hacktoberfest Open‑Source AI Challenge: Week 1 – “Touch Grass” Summary

## Overview
- Week 1 of the Hacktoberfest 2026 DEV Challenges runs from Oct 5 to Oct 11.  
- Participants build open‑weight or open‑source AI projects that encourage people to leave their screens and go outside.  
- One overall winner and winners in 16 partner categories will be selected.

## Theme & Project Ideas
- **Theme:** “Touch Grass” – create tools that get users outdoors.  
- Acceptable approaches include running open‑weight models locally, using open‑source agent frameworks, or combining both.  
- Example ideas:  
  - Bird‑call identifier that works offline on a trail.  
  - Garden planner that suggests planting based on local frost dates.  
  - Run‑club route builder highlighting nearby fall foliage.  
- Submissions should explain why the open‑source approach matters (e.g., offline operation, data privacy, cost‑free execution).

## Prize Categories
- **Overall winner** (1) – $250 USD, DEV++ membership, exclusive badge.  
- **Featured categories** (6) – $200 USD + badge each (e.g., Best Use of Render, TabPFN, Tinker, Arduino, DigitalOcean, Gemma).  
- **Partner categories** (10) – $100 USD + badge each (e.g., Backboard, ElevenLabs, Entire, GitHub Copilot, Mastra, MongoDB Atlas, Sentry Agent Tracing, SerpApi, Temporal, Tiger Data).  
- Projects may enter multiple categories but can win only once per challenge.  
- Partners provide free credits and promo codes (Tinker, Render, Backboard, ElevenLabs).

## Judging Criteria
- **Writing Quality** (most weight): clarity, engagement, explanation of the build, target audience, and open‑innovation rationale.  
- **Relevance to Prompt & Theme:** core use of open‑source AI/open‑weight models and encouragement of outdoor activity.  
- **Creativity:** originality or fresh take on a problem.  
- **Technical Execution:** functionality and build quality.  
- **Use of Partner Technology** (optional): meaningful integration for partner category entries.  
- Optional: embed DevRelay session or provide demo links to illustrate the process.

## Prizes & Recognition
- Overall winner: $250 USD, DEV++ membership, exclusive badge.  
- Featured category winners: $200 USD + badge each.  
- Partner category winners: $100 USD + badge each.  
- All valid submissions receive a completion badge.

## How to Participate
1. Publish a DEV post using the provided “Touch Grass Submission Template.”  
2. Describe the project, demo it, and explain the open‑innovation benefits.  
3. Review judging criteria, rules, and FAQ before submitting.  
4. Include any partner technology details if entering a partner category.

## Important Dates
- **Oct 5:** Week 1 challenge opens.  
- **Oct 11, 11:59 PM PDT:** Submission deadline.  
- **Week of Oct 12:** Winners announced.  
- **Oct 12:** Week 2 launches with a new theme.
