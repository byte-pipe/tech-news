# Author Correction: Autophagic cell death restricts chromosomal instability during replicative crisis

## Main correction details
- In the Methods “Antibodies” section, the P16 antibody was incorrectly listed as “Abcam ab51243”. The correct reagent is BD Pharmingen Purified Mouse Anti‑Human p16 antibody (catalog no. 550834).  
- In the “Plasmids and transductions” section, the wild‑type mCherry‑EGFP‑LC3B plasmid is plasmid #22418, not #26477.  
- Because the original article is dated, the text cannot be directly updated; this notice serves as the official correction.
