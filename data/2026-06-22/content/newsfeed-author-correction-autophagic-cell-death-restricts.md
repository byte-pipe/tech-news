Correction to:Naturehttps://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-0885-0Published online 23 January 2019

In the version of this article initially published online, there was a mistake in the Methods “Antibodies” section, where the P16 antibody listed as “(Abcam ab51243)” was actually BD Pharmingen Purified Mouse Anti-Human p16 antibody (cat. no. 550834), while in the “Plasmids and transductions” section, the wild-type mCherry–EGFP–LC3B plasmid was in fact plasmid number 22418 (and not 26477). Due to the age of the article, the text cannot be updated directly; this notice serves to correct the article.
