# ShootClip — Video Editor for macOS

## What is ShootClip
- A macOS video editing app that lets you import footage, cut and arrange clips on a multi‑track timeline, add captions, text animations, color grades, and blur effects.  
- Export finished videos locally or publish directly to your own YouTube channel using a connected Google account (only for sign‑in and publishing).  
- Your footage stays on your Mac; no other Google data is accessed.  

## Core Features
- **Metal‑native engine** – Real‑time playback of 8K ProRes and RAW on Apple Silicon, no mandatory proxies.  
- **Magnetic timeline with pro trims** – Ripple, roll, slip, slide using frame‑accurate keyboard shortcuts and instant audio waveforms.  
- **Built‑in MCP server** – Exposes the project to Claude or any MCP client; every edit is scriptable, reviewable, and undoable.  
- **Standalone text tracks** – 19 Canva‑style animations (typewriter, burst, skate, roll, merge, etc.).  
- **Blur & censor layers** – Add Gaussian blur, mosaic, or black‑box overlays for any duration.  
- **Flexible export options** – H.264, HEVC, ProRes, and social‑aspect presets; background rendering while you keep editing.  

## AI‑Powered Highlights

- **Text behind subject** – Automatic segmentation lets titles slide behind people or objects without manual masking or rotoscoping.  
- **Auto captions** – One‑click speech‑to‑text with styled, frame‑accurate captions; Pro includes 300 minutes of captioning per month, no API key required.
