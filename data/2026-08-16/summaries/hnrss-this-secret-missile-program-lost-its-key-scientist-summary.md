- Egypt, led by President Gamal Abdel Nasser, pursued missile capability to counter Israel after the 1956 Suez Crisis.

- West German intelligence chief Reinhard Gehlen linked Nasser with Otto Skorzeny and other German scientists.

## The German Rocket Team and Their Work
- Key figures included Otto Skorzeny (commando trainer), Wolfgang Pilz, Eugen Sänger, Hans Kleinwachter, and Hans Goercke.

## International Reaction
- The launch sparked an arms race with Israel, which had already tested its own sub‑orbital missile.  
- Israel pressured West Germany to stop the German scientists’ involvement; German authorities forced Sänger to resign in November 1961.  
- Mossad initiated Operation Damocles to intimidate and eliminate the Egyptian missile program’s German personnel.

## Disappearance of Heinz Krug
- On 11 September 1962 Krug met a man calling himself “Mr. Saleh,” left his Munich office, and never returned.  
- His car was found abandoned in the Munich suburb of Solln; an anonymous call claimed he was dead.  
- Police investigations and public appeals produced no definitive answer; historians largely attribute the disappearance to a Mossad‑linked assassination.

## Legacy
- Krug’s disappearance crippled Egypt’s missile effort and remains unresolved.
