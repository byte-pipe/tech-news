# Apple proposes 15% cut of purchases made outside the App Store

## Key points of the filing
- Apple submitted a proposal in U.S. District Court (Northern California) outlining new commission rates for purchases made via external links in iOS apps.  
- Base commission: **15%** for standard apps.  
- Discounted rates:
  - **5%** for small‑business developers.  
  - **10%** for developers in the Video Partner, News Partner, and Mini Apps Partner programs.  
  - Subscription renewals reduced to **10%**.  

## Legal context
- The proposal follows Apple’s attempt to delay the issue while the Supreme Court considered a separate contempt question about a 27% commission on link‑out purchases.  
- The Supreme Court rejected Apple’s request to pause the lower‑court proceedings, compelling Apple to disclose its commission structure.  

## Apple’s justification
- Apple argues the fees are needed to recoup investments in the tools, technology, and services that support the App Store and its ecosystem.  
- The company compares its link‑out fees to Google Play’s rates (20% standard, 15% for special programs, 10% for subscription renewals), noting that Epic Games had accepted those terms.
