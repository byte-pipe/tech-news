# AI-driven memory crunch jolts India's smartphone market | TechCrunch

**India's Smartphone Market Shows Devastating Impact of AI-Driven Memory Demand**

Months after analyst warnings about the impending disruption in consumer electronics due to AI-driven demand for memory chips, India is witnessing a major price squeeze on smartphones as manufacturers like Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron shift production capacity towards high-bandwidth memory used in AI accelerators. This shift has left less capacity, increased costs, and reshaped smartphone markets worldwide.

**Key Findings:**

* Smartphone shipments fell 10% year-over-year in April-June quarter in India, marking the steepest decline in six years.
* Higher memory costs have pushed up handset prices, with higher-end smartphones experiencing a 45% decline from a year earlier.
* Indian smartphone market is concentrated among sub-₹20,000 (under $210) segments, where higher memory costs have had the greatest impact on prices.

**Industry Perspective:**

* India has been a prominent market for global smartphone brands but faces challenges due to lower average transaction value (ATV).
* Consumer demand in price-sensitive markets is expected to grow, though consumers are unlikely to abandon smartphones altogether.
* Competing with premium brands like Apple and Samsung remains an advantage for mid-range brands.

**Market Impact:**

* Samsung was the only major smartphone brand to post shipment growth in India this quarter.
* Apple's shipments fell 3% year-over-year from a year earlier, largely due to supply constraints and inventory shortages.
* Financing has made expensive devices more affordable among consumers in higher-end segments.
