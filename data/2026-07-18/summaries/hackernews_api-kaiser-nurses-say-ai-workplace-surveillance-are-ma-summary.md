# Kaiser nurses say AI, workplace surveillance are making their jobs and patient care worse - Local News Matters

**Kaiser Permanente Nurses Express Concerns About Workload and Patient Care**

In recent months, Kaiser Permanente nurses have raised concerns about the growing use of artificial intelligence (AI) in monitoring work tasks ahead of contract negotiations. Their main points include:

*   **Increased workload**: Eighteen current and former nurses said they face criticism from management or performance evaluation meetings after spending more than 15 minutes handling patient calls.
*   **Efficiency metrics**: Kaiser Permanente uses software to track call length, predicting whether nurses are being unproductive or failing to answer quickly. AI systems also rate empathy and tone of voice during calls.
*   **Autonomy concerns**: Some nurses are picketed against the use of AI in their workplace last fall due to safety and efficiency concerns.

    California lawmakers are considering legislation that could protect nursing caregivers from retaliation for overriding automated care recommendations, which could have implications for patient care with AI.
