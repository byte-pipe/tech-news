# Quality of Evidence - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Summary of Key Points**

* Product teams are drowning in data from various channels, but still making uninformed decisions due to poor evidence quality.
* The goal is not perfection, but moving in the right direction. Coaches should focus on increasing value rather than eliminating low-quality signals.

**Main Ideas and Essential Details**

* Product teams collect a wide range of data, including behavioral analytics, support tickets, sales call notes, and feedback inboxes.
* Not all product evidence created equal: low-effort signals (support tickets) are abundant but carry weak signal strength, while high-effort signals (story-based interviews) are harder to collect but carry far more context for decision-making.
* The "ladder of evidence" framework helps teams prioritize evidence quality by increasing value up the steps, from low-effort signals to high effort signals that provide rich narrative insights.

**Why Low-Quality Signals Carry Little Value**
```markdown
We're inundated with these low-value signals all around us:
* Regular user reports and reviews (support tickets, app store reviews)
* Appstore analytics
* Sales call notes

The risk? Teams fill in gaps with their own assumptions. This limits the insights teams can derive from customer interactions.
```

```markdown
