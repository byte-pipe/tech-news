# Anthropic, Blackstone bet the next trillion-dollar AI business is implementation, not just models | TechCrunch

**Ode: Preparing for Peak Enterprise Sales in $1.5 Billion-$2 Trillion Market**

As enterprise adoption of ai models accelerates, two companies, Anthropic and OpenAI, are taking the next giant step by creating a business dedicated to deploying AI engineers to customers' offices.

The venture is taking its name from **Ode**, which has already made its mark in this space. Founded with a $1.5 billion investment from entities like Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, Goldman Sachs, and others, Ode aims to make enterprise customers the primary focus.

**Key Components:**

* Ode employs 100 AI engineers
* Partnered with Anthropic's applied AI team for customized solutions tailored to each business
* Works closely with companies before implementation
* Employees will be guided by a "Claude-first" principle, implementing selected technologies

As Ode grows, it plans to open its doors to external customers, fueling further demand in the market. With potential sales tied directly to enterprise adoption rates, two high-profile stakes (OpenAI and Blackstone) now participate as clients-in-residence.

**Breakdown:**

* Ode's internal team focuses on strategic deployments with a mission-driven approach
* Partner companies can choose from Anthropic's offerings, such as Claude Tag in Slack, while using rival products if necessary

With significant growth potential, **Ode is poised to become one of the most high-profile AI implementation services businesses.**

* Chris Taylor, CEO/Ode; co-founder/Fractional AI startup
