I went to San Francisco for Muni Heritage Weekend 2026!
I stood by the tracks for three hours with myline scan camera, scanning trams.

Tram pics are freely licensed under CC-By-SA 4.0 (I’ve also uploaded a bunch to Wikimedia Commons).
Eight of these photos are also available as large metal prints inmy shop.

# 1No. 1: Muni Streetcar 1 (1912)

Muni No. 1is the first publicly owned big city streetcar in the US.
Before then, all streetcars were privately owned.
It’s pretty cool!

# 2No. 162: Muni (1914-1950s)

Muni No. 162started out from 1914 and served until 1958.
It’s making its first Muni Heritage Weekend appearance since a 2014 collision.
Sorry, there was a car in the background and someone bumped my camera, causing some vibrations.

# 5No. 578: Dinky (1896)

Muni No. 578is probably the oldest one.
It looks like a cable car but it is electrically powered rather than being powered by a cable.

Note that up until 1909, Divisadero Street used to be calledDevisadero Street, so it’s a period-correct spelling, not a typo.
