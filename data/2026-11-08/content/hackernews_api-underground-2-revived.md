The PC game's own code, recompiled for Chrome and Firefox: up to 120 fps in widescreen, free roam and Online races with friends, and controllers, wheels and touch controls.

My brother and I played this game together about 20 years ago. Because of that I tried porting it to the browser so we could play together again. It worked surprisingly well, and it got a bit out of hand. It's great to see so many people playing it.

To help with hosting and development costs, you canbuy me a coffee. Everyone gets the same game, with no microtransactions or paid perks. Supporters just get a Discord role.
