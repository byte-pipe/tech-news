# Summary of “Please take the job!” – Trump asks UFC boss Dana White to be White House press secretary

## Key event
- President Donald Trump posted on Truth Social asking UFC president Dana White to become White House press secretary, writing “Dana, please take the job!”
- The offer’s seriousness is uncertain; the BBC has sought comments from the White House, UFC, and parent company TKO Group Holdings.

## Context
- The press‑secretary position has been vacant since mid‑October after Katie Zacharia’s brief tenure.
- Trump’s post followed the Republican defeat in the U.S. mid‑term elections, which ended their congressional majorities.
- In the same post, Trump described White as a “violent Fighter and Warrior” and suggested White bring several high‑profile UFC fighters (including non‑U.S. athletes) to help “defend us against the Lunatic Left.”

## Reactions
- Dana White shared an Instagram photo of himself behind the White House press briefing lectern with the caption “On to the next chapter,” leaving it unclear whether he was serious or joking.
- White was in London that day, ringside at a UFC‑styled boxing match at the Copper Box Arena.
- The BBC attempted to contact White for comment but received no response.
