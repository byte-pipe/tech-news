# How AI created a cybersecurity ‘dark age’

## Overview
- The conversation with Alex Stamos, CSO of Cognition and Stanford lecturer, explains how generative AI has dramatically accelerated cyber‑attacks, pushing the industry into a “dark age.”
- AI models now automate most of the traditional cyber kill chain: discovering vulnerabilities, writing exploits, and deploying attacks in real time.
- Open‑source tools such as the Chinese project ARTEX, powered by large language models, can be downloaded and used to breach thousands of organizations, including major banks and AI service providers.

## Recent incidents
- A cascade of breaches hit AI‑related companies: Fireworks was compromised, its API keys were used to infiltrate Cursor, which then pushed a malicious plugin to about 5,000 downstream firms.
- The Binance hack is described as the largest financial heist in history.
- Multiple high‑profile bugs in PHP, Node, and other platforms have been exploited in rapid succession throughout October and November.

## Threat actors
- Both criminal groups and nation‑state actors (e.g., Russia, North Korea, China) are leveraging AI to scale attacks.
- The accessibility of AI‑driven hacking tools means that even small, loosely organized groups can launch sophisticated, automated campaigns.

## Impact on the industry
- Traditional security practices (password changes, awareness campaigns) are insufficient against AI‑powered, continuous breaches.
- The cybersecurity sector, once a multibillion‑dollar industry, is now scrambling to adapt to a landscape where attacks can be generated and executed at scale without human intervention.
- Stamos likens the current situation to the late‑1990s “wild‑west” era of Windows security, but with the entire global economy now online, the stakes are far higher.

## Outlook
- Stamos does not view the situation as hopeless; he expects a period of intense adjustment as defenders develop new tools and strategies.
- The discussion also touches on related challenges such as AI‑generated misinformation and content moderation on social platforms.
