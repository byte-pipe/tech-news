# Alphabet plans to raise $80B to pay for AI buildout

## Key Points
- Alphabet announced a plan to raise **$80 billion** by issuing new stock, earmarked for “general corporate purposes, including capital expenditures to scale AI infrastructure and global compute.”
- **$10 billion** of the offering will be sold to Berkshire Hathaway.
- The company cites “strong demand for its AI solutions and services” that exceeds current supply, prompting the need to expand foundational infrastructure.
- Alphabet says the stock issuance will allow it to fund investments while maintaining a healthy balance sheet.
- CEO Sundar Pichai previously indicated Google expects to spend **$180‑190 billion** on capex this year; industry analysts project total AI‑related capex across tech giants could reach **$700 billion** in 2026.
