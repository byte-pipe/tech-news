A free webinar will explore the pros and cons of using AI to recruit scientists.Credit: Catherine McQueen/Getty

Artificial intelligence can help in drafting job advertisements, processing applications and shortlisting candidates. Scientist jobseekers can use it to polish CVs, write cover letters and complete application forms.

But what are the strengths, limitations and risks associated with using the technology?

This free 60-minute webinar and Q&A session fromNature’s careers team aims to help researchers and hiring managers to decide how and when to use AI as a recruitment tool.
