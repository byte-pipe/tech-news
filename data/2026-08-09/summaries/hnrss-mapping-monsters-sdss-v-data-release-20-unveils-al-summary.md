# Mapping Monsters: SDSS‑V Data Release 20 Unveils All‑Sky Views of Supermassive Black Holes

## Overview
- DR20 marks the first southern‑hemisphere optical spectra from Las Campanas (Chile) and expanded observations from Apache Point (NM).  
- Over 3.3 million optical spectra released: ~500 k galaxies, 1.5 million stars, and extensive quasar/AGN data.  
- Part of SDSS‑V’s Black Hole Mapper (BHM) program, delivering new insights into SMBH masses, growth, and physics across cosmic time.

## eROSITA + SDSS Synergy (SPIDERS)
- SPIDERS links SDSS optical spectroscopy with eROSITA X‑ray maps, providing redshifts for ~200 k X‑ray sources.  
- Largest uniform spectroscopic follow‑up of X‑ray objects to date.  
- Enables measurement of the X‑ray luminosity function, tracing SMBH growth back to z ≈ 6.  
- Finds more luminous quasars and higher space densities at early epochs than previously expected.  
- Reveals that 70‑90 % of SMBH mass growth occurred behind heavy dust/gas obscuration or in X‑ray‑quiet phases.

## Time‑Domain Quasar Studies
- **Reverberation Mapping (RM):** Repeated spectra over days‑years give time delays between accretion‑disk and broad‑line region light, yielding geometric SMBH mass estimates across redshifts.  
- **All‑Quasar Multi‑Epoch Spectroscopy (AQMES):** Monitors tens of thousands of quasars for dynamical changes, outflows, binary SMBH candidates, and “changing‑look” transitions.  
- Visual inspection of unusual spectra was required; these inspected data are included in DR20.

## Robotic Focal Plane System
- Identical robotic fiber positioning systems installed on the 2.5 m Sloan Foundation telescope (APO) and the du Pont telescope (LCO).  
- Rapid fiber configuration accelerates multi‑object observations and supports Target‑of‑Opportunity modes.

## Open Data Access
- All DR20 products are publicly available via the Science Archive Server (SAS) and Catalog Archive Server (CAS).  
- Access tools include SciServer Compute, SQL queries, SDSS Zora & Valis web interfaces, and Python APIs.  
- Value‑added catalogs (AGN fitting, visual inspection) and Jupyter Notebook tutorials accompany the release.  
- Data portal: www.sdss.org/dr20.
