# Genesis Open Models Initiative – Summary

## Overview
- The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) launches the Genesis Open Models program to create open‑weight foundation models for scientific research.  
- The first model, **Genesis‑Science‑1**, is co‑developed with industry partner **Arcee AI**.  
- Open‑weight models are intended to accelerate discovery across domains such as materials, energy systems, earth modeling, fusion, biology, and high‑energy physics.  
- The effort follows open‑science, reproducibility, and responsible‑AI principles, aiming to lower barriers to advanced AI for the public good.

## Contribution Opportunities
- **Open‑weight model contributions** – Provide base models with transparent provenance for downstream fine‑tuning or immediate use.  
- **Pre‑training contributions** – Supply high‑quality, domain‑specific data, curated datasets, benchmarks, or specialized corpora for future training cycles.  
- **Fine‑tuning efforts** – Develop downstream, domain‑adapted versions of Genesis‑Science‑1 (e.g., lab assistants, simulation surrogates, scientific copilots).  
- **Post‑training assets** – Offer expert demonstrations, annotated task examples, research software, workflow environments, reinforcement‑learning tasks, held‑out evaluations, scoring rubrics, tests, and verifiers.  
- **Expert review & infrastructure** – Scientists, engineers, and providers can assist with task review, model behavior assessment, and additional training/evaluation capacity.

## Application Process & Timeline
- **Contribution portal:** https://genesisopenmodels.anl.gov/ (open as of today).  
- **First‑round deadline:** August 14 2026 for initial applications; selected contributors receive later delivery deadlines.  
- **Tracks & deadlines:**  
  - *Foundation‑stage data* – Apply by Aug 14 2026; delivery deadline Aug 28 2026 (recurring every 3 months).  
  - *Post‑training data & environments* – Apply by Aug 25 2026; delivery deadline Sep 14 2026 (recurring every 3 months).  
- Application requires description of scientific work, readiness of material, supporting experts, and proposed terms of use. No scientific material is transferred during the application.  
- Review follows a five‑gate DOE‑governed process: scientific fit, rights/handling, expert readiness, technical integration, and final selection.  
- Selected contributors gain early evaluation access, credit in technical reports, and collaboration with model developers.

## Genesis Mission Background
- Established by an executive order in November 2025 to double the productivity and impact of U.S. science and engineering within ten years.  
- Connects DOE’s 17 national labs, leadership‑class computing facilities, scientific data resources, and industry/academic partners.  
- Directed by DOE Under Secretary for Science **Darío Gil**. More information: energy.gov/genesis-mission.

## Partners
