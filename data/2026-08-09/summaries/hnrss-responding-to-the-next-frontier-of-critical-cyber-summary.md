# Responding to the next frontier of critical cyber capabilities

## Overview
- OpenAI reports rapid changes in cybersecurity as AI models become more capable, offering both defensive strengths and new attack vectors.
- The company is being transparent about potential shifts in capabilities, especially concerning the upcoming model Astra.

## Preparedness Framework & Critical Threshold
- The Preparedness Framework, introduced in December 2023, guides identification of emerging capabilities and corresponding company actions.
- A model reaches the **Critical** cybersecurity threshold if it can autonomously create functional zero‑day exploits for hardened systems or devise end‑to‑end novel attack strategies from high‑level goals.
- Previous models, such as GPT‑5.6‑Sol, were assessed at the **High** threshold, not Critical.

## Preliminary Findings on Astra
- Internal evaluations over recent days show significant advances in agentic coding and cybersecurity for Astra.
- Preliminary results suggest Astra may meet the Critical threshold, though final benchmarking is ongoing.
- Astra was not involved in the Hugging Face exploitation incident.

## Actions Taken
- **Enhanced safeguards:** isolated testing environments, restricted network/tool access, stronger model‑weight protection and encryption, expanded monitoring, and sandboxed execution.
- **Activity pause:** internal projects involving Astra that do not meet the new security requirements are on hold.
- **Universal monitoring:** real‑time oversight of risky actions and misalignment across all agentic applications, with automatic security responses to high‑risk activity.
- **Collaboration:** plans to work with government agencies and selected AI safety organizations to test Astra’s capabilities.
- **Third‑party guidance:** providing recommended security controls for external partners conducting high‑risk evaluations.

## Future Plans & Collaboration
- OpenAI will continue to apply the same principled approach used during the 2025 biology capability transition.
- The company aims to help defenders identify and remediate vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them.
- Commitment to cooperate with governments, safety institutes, and civil society to ensure responsible deployment of frontier AI capabilities.
