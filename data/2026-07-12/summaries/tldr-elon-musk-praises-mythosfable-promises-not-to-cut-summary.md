# Summary of “Elon Musk praises Mythos/Fable, promises not to ‘cut off’ Anthropic”

## Key developments
- After speculation that Musk could shut down Anthropic’s AI workloads on SpaceX servers, Musk publicly denied such intentions, calling that “not my style.”
- As of July 2026, Anthropic is one of SpaceX’s largest customers, using SpaceX‑owned compute for its models.
- In May 2026 Anthropic signed a deal to purchase 300 MW of compute (the full output of xAI’s Colossus 1 data center near Memphis, TN) for $1.25 billion per month through May 2029 – roughly $40 billion in revenue for SpaceX’s xAI unit.
- Google also signed a separate agreement to rent SpaceX infrastructure through June 2029 for $920 million per month.

## Musk’s statements
- Musk admitted he was “clearly wrong about Anthropic” after a September 2025 post that predicted Anthropic could not win.
- He praised Anthropic’s leadership, saying no company has released a model as good as Mythos/Fable and that Mythos 2 is forthcoming.
- He reiterated he would never “cut them off in a way that hurt them badly,” citing past Tesla policies (no patent lawsuits, open Supercharger network) and SpaceX’s practice of launching competing satellite systems without unfair terms.

- Hosting Anthropic’s compute gives SpaceX deep visibility into Anthropic’s operations, which could be leveraged for competitive intelligence.

## Benefits for SpaceX and Anthropic

- SpaceX engineers gain experience building and supporting large‑scale AI workloads, similar to Amazon’s internal AI infrastructure teams.
- Continued partnership aligns incentives: Anthropic benefits from reliable, high‑performance compute, while SpaceX gains technical expertise and financial returns.
