# Apple sues OpenAI over alleged trade secret theft

## Lawsuit overview
- Apple filed a lawsuit on Friday in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California accusing OpenAI of trade secret theft and breach of contract.  
- The complaint claims a systematic effort by OpenAI to extract confidential Apple information through former Apple employees now working at OpenAI.

## Allegations against OpenAI leadership
- Chief Hardware Officer Tang Tan, a former Apple VP of product design for iPhone and Apple Watch, is accused of:
  - Using Apple project code names during OpenAI recruiting.  
  - Requesting interview candidates to bring Apple hardware components to meetings.  
  - Coaching departing Apple staff on evading Apple’s security procedures.  
  - Seeking details about Apple’s unannounced products.  

## Specific employee claims
- **Tang Tan** – alleged to have directed the overall information‑gathering strategy.  
- **Chang Liu** – former senior systems electrical engineer at Apple, accused of:
  - Failing to return an Apple‑issued laptop after moving to OpenAI in 2026.  
  - Downloading confidential Apple technical documents on that laptop.  
  - Sharing Apple secrets with other Apple employees applying to OpenAI and advising them on interview preparation.  

## Context and potential impact
- The lawsuit coincides with rumors that OpenAI is developing its first hardware product, possibly a smartphone that relies on AI agents, which could directly compete with the iPhone.  
- OpenAI acquired Jony Ive’s design startup, iow, in a $6.5 billion deal last year; iow is named in the filing, though Ive himself is not.  
- Apple alleges OpenAI used a proprietary metal‑finishing technique derived from Apple’s confidential data, misleading a partner into believing it had Apple’s permission.

## Apple’s requested relief
- An injunction barring OpenAI from using or disclosing Apple’s trade secrets.  
- Return of any Apple confidential materials in OpenAI’s possession.  
- Preservation of evidence related to the alleged theft.

## Statements from the parties
- Apple’s statement emphasizes the seriousness of protecting its innovations and cites “significant evidence” of wrongful acquisition of secret information.  
- OpenAI, in a post‑publication X statement, denied interest in other companies’ trade secrets and reiterated its focus on building empowering technology.

## Additional notes
- Apple sent a letter to OpenAI in February raising its concerns; the complaint says OpenAI did not respond.  
- The filing is publicly available for review.
