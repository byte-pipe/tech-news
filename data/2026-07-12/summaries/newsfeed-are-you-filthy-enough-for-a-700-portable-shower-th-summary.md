# Are you filthy enough for a $700 portable shower?

## Overview
- The article reviews the **Hottap Go**, a portable hot‑water shower made by Australian company Joolca.  
- Tested by Thomas Ricker while van‑lifing, using it after surfing and cooking.  
- Priced at US $554 (about $719 with optional power bank) and marketed as an all‑in‑one solution for hot showers on the road.

## How it works
- Water is heated with a standard 1 lb propane canister; electricity (12 V) powers the pump and display.  
- The unit contains a 12 L (3.2 gal) integrated tank that recirculates water until the set temperature is reached, eliminating the initial cold‑water blast.  
- Requires an external 12 V power source; Joolca sells a magnetic 12 V/5 A power bank for $165, or users can supply their own (≈ $100 in Europe).

## Key Features
- Integrated 12 L water tank, hoses, showerhead, gas canister, and optional battery all store inside the unit.  
- Magnetic holder for the showerhead; on/off button on the head to conserve water while lathering.  
- Flow‑adjustable showerhead with a two‑handed dial.  
- Two‑stage filter allows use of creek water.  
- 12 V DC cigarette‑socket power cable (5 m).  
- Pre‑heats water to the selected temperature in about 4–5 minutes; temperature display shows up to 60 °C (140 °F).  

## Pros
- Fully self‑contained hot‑water system; no external tank or long hose needed.  
- No water waste thanks to recirculation heating.  
- Temperature stays steady during use.  
- Convenient storage of all accessories inside the unit.  
- Magnetic showerhead holder is strong and versatile.  

## Cons
- High price point; $554 without the optional power bank, $719 with it.  
- Battery is an add‑on that attaches externally.  
- Heating takes several minutes before showering can begin.  
- Water pressure is modest (1.5–3.5 L/min); adequate for hair washing but not for heavy cleaning.  
- Sensitive to wind; may reignite and show an E3 error if placed in a breezy spot.  

## Performance Test
- Reached 47 °C (117 °F) in exactly four minutes using tap water.  
- Provided two satisfactory showers from a full tank; water flow was “just okay” even at max setting.  
- On a windy beach day the unit needed repositioning to keep the flame lit.  
- The on/off switch on the head helped conserve water; the seal prevented water from spilling onto the power bank.  

## Specifications
- Tank capacity: 12 L (≈ 2 showers)  
- Water flow rate: 1.5–3.5 L/min (0.4–0.9 gal/min)  
- Shower hose length: 3 m (9.8 ft)  
- Power draw: 45 W, 12 V DC  
- Max temperature: 60 °C (140 °F), pre‑heat time ~5 min  
- Propane canister: 0.45 kg (1 lb), ~15 showers per canister  
- Gas flow rate: 20 MJ/hr (≈ 19 000 BTU/hr)  
- Weight (empty): 9.5 kg (20.9 lb)  
- Dimensions: 495 × 359 × 180 mm (19.5 × 14.1 × 7.1 in)  

## Verdict
- The Hottap Go offers a convenient, recirculating hot‑water system that solves many shortcomings of other portable propane showers.  
- It is best suited for van‑lifers or frequent outdoor users who need reliable hot showers multiple times a year.  
- For occasional use, cheaper tankless alternatives may be more sensible, but for regular, on‑the‑go hot‑water needs the Hottap Go can be justified despite its premium price.
