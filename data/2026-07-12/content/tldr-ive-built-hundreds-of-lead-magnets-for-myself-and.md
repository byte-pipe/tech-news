description: 'I''ve built hundreds of lead magnets (for myself and for clients). This is the most effective format I''ve found both for engagement AND for conversions: Most people build a lead magnet the same way. They create a PDF, put it behind an opt-in, and call it a day. Someone downloads it... And then it disappears into their downloads folder, never to be seen again. That''s the problem. A PDF only does ONE of the two jobs a lead magnet is supposed to do. Job #1: deliver value. Job #2: train people to open your emails. A forgotten PDF does neither. So here''s what you should do instead: You deliver the entire lead magnet inside an email sequence. Every lesson, every framework, every "aha" - broken across 5 days of emails. This is what we call an email course. Why this works so much better: - Everyone checks their inbox (almost nobody re-opens a downloaded PDF) - People get trained to associate your emails with real value - By day 5, they actually know you, trust you, and want more
  And that last part is the key. Because right after those 5 days of pure value, you add a short pitch sequence. 3-5 emails where you say: "You''ve got the whole roadmap now. If you''d rather we just do it for you, here''s the next step." Now your lead magnet isn''t a dead PDF. It''s an evergreen machine that nurtures and sells 24/7. And that''s it! Hope it''s helpful! PS - I built a prompt that generates your entire email course value prop for you. Comment "value prop" and I''ll send it your way. | 224 comments on LinkedIn'
