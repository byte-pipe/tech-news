Residents near Middlesbrough have told the BBC of their concern after a spate of violent incidents in their area, including a crashthat killed seven peopleincluding two police officers and a house fire thatkilled a woman and a seven-year-old girl.

Police have said the two incidents are not connected. They have made aseries of arrests in relation to the crashand two arrests in relation to the house fire.

On Friday Redcar MP Anna Turley said disorderat a balloon releasefor one of the young men who died in the crash was "unacceptable".
