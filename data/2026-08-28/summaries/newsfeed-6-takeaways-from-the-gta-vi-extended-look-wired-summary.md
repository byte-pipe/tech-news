# 6 Takeaways From the GTA VI Extended Look | WIRED

## It Sure Is Pretty  
- Vice City’s cityscapes, nightclubs and natural parks are vibrant and colorful.  
- Cutscenes look more polished than gameplay, which appears slightly flatter and more desaturated.  
- Early lighting in a dark basement scene washes out details on several Black characters.

## You Play as 2 Characters Simultaneously  
- Players control Jason and Lucia, a Bonnie‑and‑Clyde‑style couple whose relationship evolves based on player choices.  
- Voice acting appears tightly written; actions can affect the story’s ending.  
- Appearance is reactive: eating, dieting, and working out change weight and physique; extensive clothing options include collaborations with real‑world designers, some locked to the $100 Ultimate Edition.

## Maybe Don’t Hit and Run  
- NPCs show far more personality and responsiveness; Rockstar has a dedicated LA studio for pedestrian behavior.  
- Driving includes a realistic GPS‑style minimap; players can switch characters, drive, shoot, and cause environmental damage (e.g., tire‑shooting, car flips).  
- Early gameplay avoids running over pedestrians, hinting at more nuanced crowd interactions.

## So Much Room for Activities  
- The map covers Vice City and surrounding Florida, featuring Everglades fan‑boating, river kayaking, scuba diving and jet‑skiing.  
- Satirical social‑media elements appear (e.g., kidnapping a livestreaming influencer).  
- Characters frequently use phones and text, suggesting commentary on modern digital culture.

## You Can Pet the Dog  
- A dog appears in at least one scene, and players can interact with it by petting.

## User‑Generated Content  
- Rockstar acquired the Cfx mod marketplace in 2023 and integrated it as an official hub for buying and sharing mods.  
- GTA Online’s Rockstar Mission Creator already enables custom levels and modes.  
- The Extended Look did not showcase new creation tools, likely because the initial release focuses on single‑player content before multiplayer features arrive.
