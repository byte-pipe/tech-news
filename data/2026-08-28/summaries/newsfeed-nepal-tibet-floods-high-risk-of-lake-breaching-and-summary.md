# Nepal‑Tibet floods: ‘High risk’ of lake breaching and causing second flood, warns China – BBC News  

## Main points  
- China warned that the barrier lake created after a landslide near the Nepal‑Tibet border faces a “high risk” of breaching, which could trigger a second downstream flood.  
- About 2.5 million m³ of water had accumulated in the lake by Friday morning; an additional ~3 million m³ is expected to flow in between Thursday and the weekend, raising breach concerns.  
- The lake formed at the confluence of the Chhochen Khola and Purepu Tsangpo rivers, upstream of Gyirong Port, and is already overflowing amid heavy rain.  
- Engineering teams have been sent to monitor water levels and atmospheric conditions; most Chinese emergency services are positioned 7 km (4 mi) from Gyirong Port.  
- Rescue operations near the Nepal‑Tibet border were suspended because of the overflow risk.  
- Nepali authorities warned of possible blockages causing further floods along the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli rivers and urged people to stay away from the rivers.  
- Scientists say the deadly flash flood on Wednesday was likely caused by a glacier collapse near Langtang Lirung, which sent a massive surge of water and debris downstream.  
- Casualties: several hundred dead and missing in Nepal; more than 550 missing on the Tibet side, many of them foreigners.
