# Apple rescues Hide My Email feature from the privacy scrap heap

## Key developments
- Apple reversed its earlier plan to move Hide My Email addresses from `@icloud.com` to `@private.icloud.com`.  
- The feature will continue to use the `@icloud.com` domain, preserving its compatibility with website sign‑ups.  
- No official reason was provided; John Gruber noted that Apple employees had opposed the change from the start.

## Feature background
- Hide My Email is an iCloud+ service that generates unique, disposable email aliases for users, forwarding messages to their regular inboxes.  
- The aliases look like standard `@icloud.com` addresses, making them indistinguishable from regular Apple emails, though Apple can still reveal the owner to law‑enforcement when required.

## User reaction and prior issues
- Users criticized the proposed shift to `@private.icloud.com`, warning that many sites would reject the new domain and defeat the privacy purpose.  
- Earlier in the year, a bug caused masked addresses to leak users’ real email addresses when messages were marked as spam; Apple fixed the issue after more than a year.
