Efforts toreform research assessment are on the rise. Beyond publications and citations, research should be evaluated by its quality, integrity and societal contribution. Yet one dimension remains relatively under-recognized: the importance of sustained community partnerships. Universities are often asked to demonstrate societal impact, but assessment systems emphasize scholarly outputs.
