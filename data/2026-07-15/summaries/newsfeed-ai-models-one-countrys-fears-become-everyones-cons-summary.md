# Summary of “AI models: one country’s fears become everyone’s constraint”

## Key event
- On 12 June, the United States halted access to two “frontier” artificial‑intelligence models for every foreign national worldwide, citing national‑security concerns.

## Developer response
- The models were created by Anthropic.
- Anthropic reported that it had discovered a small number of previously known, minor vulnerabilities.
- Within three days of the models’ release, Anthropic complied with the U.S. directive and disabled the models for all users.

## Context and implications
- The suspension reflects growing governmental scrutiny of advanced AI systems and the potential for security‑related risks.
- By applying the restriction to all foreign nationals, the U.S. set a precedent that national‑security fears can translate into global access constraints for cutting‑edge AI technology.
