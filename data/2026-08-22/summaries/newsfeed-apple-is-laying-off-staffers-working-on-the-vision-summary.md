# Apple is laying off staffers working on the Vision Pro and Siri

## Key Points
- Apple is cutting more than 200 jobs, targeting staff on the Siri and Vision Pro projects.  
- The layoffs include largely shutting down the Vision Pro gaming team and reducing the team that creates immersive Vision Pro content.  
- Apple said the changes are to “evolve our business to deliver the best experiences for our users,” and noted that new roles will be created.  
- The Vision Pro headset, launched in early 2024 at $3,499, has underperformed, prompting Apple to work on a slimmer, lighter successor and to suspend a Samsung Display partnership for an entry‑level XR device.  
- Bloomberg reports Apple plans to announce its own smart glasses at WWDC 2027 and launch them later that year.  
- A revamped, AI‑powered Siri was unveiled at this year’s WWDC and will enter public beta later in 2026.  

## Context
- The layoffs were reported by Bloomberg; Apple did not immediately comment to The Verge.  
- The move follows mixed market reception to the Vision Pro and follows industry trends such as Meta’s smart‑glasses rollout.  

## Outlook
- Apple aims to continue investing in new hardware (smart glasses) and software (AI‑enhanced Siri) while restructuring existing teams to better align with its strategic priorities.
