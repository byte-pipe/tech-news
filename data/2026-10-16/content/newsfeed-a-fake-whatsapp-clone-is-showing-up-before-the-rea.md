# A fake WhatsApp clone is showing up before the real version on the Play Store

The ‘WhatsApp Messenger’ dupe reportedly popped up as a sponsored result above the real version.﻿

After aChrome clonewas spotted on the Play Store earlier this week,a user on Redditsays they’re seeing a fake version of WhatsApp in a “sponsored” slot above thereal version. As reported earlier byAndroid Authority, the WhatsApp dupe has racked up over 500,000 downloads and, at the time of writing, its Play Store listing isstill live.

Google did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

The WhatsApp clone is from a developer going by “Agment Assets sallu” who doesn’t currently have anything else listed on the Play Store. Their listing for the WhatsApp impostor has the same icon, screenshots, and text as the real version, but the real WhatsApp has over 10 billion downloads, an “editor’s choice” badge, and over 246 million reviews.Android Authoritypoints out that the WhatsApp clone may be using Google Play’s Early Access program to disable public user reviews, which could have helped users warn people against downloading the app.
