### 📌 MATS Residency: AI Safety Research

Who it's for:Researchers, academics, engineers, and professionals from AI, machine learning, policy, governance, and related fields who want to work on AI safety research.

What it's about:

The MATS Residency is a full-time research program focused on reducing catastrophic risks from advanced AI systems. Residents pursue their own research agendas over6 to 24 months, with access to research advisors, computing resources, and the wider MATS research community.

The program has three tracks for different research goals:

* Bridge:For experienced researchers transitioning into AI safety who want to develop a body of relevant research and receive career development and placement support.
* Anchor:For researchers looking for a longer-term role to pursue an independent AI safety research agenda.
* Incubation:For researchers exploring new research directions or developing AI safety research organizations.

Residents receive$155,000 to $285,000 per year (gross), depending on factors such as experience, circumstances, and office location, along with benefits and research computing resources. The program is based in London, Berkeley, and Washington, DC, with a hybrid arrangement requiring residents to work in person at one of the offices for at least a third of their residency.

The program is open to researchers from a range of backgrounds. Applicants are assessed on the quality and seriousness of their proposed work, with track-specific requirements applying. The residency is full-time, although certain academics on sabbatical may be considered for part-time participation.

### 📌 Bolt Builders Week

Who it's for:Builders, founders, entrepreneurs, and anyone interested in creating or growing a business using Bolt.

What it's about:

Bolt Builders Week runs from October 17 to 24, 2026, and includes a free building weekend, live sessions, and a business-building contest.

During the building weekend on October 17 and 18, all Bolt users can access GLM 5.3 Flash through the model picker without changing their plan or redeeming a code. Usage is provided in six-hour blocks, with paid plans receiving five times the free allowance per block. Standard rate limits apply, and unused allowance does not carry over.

Live sessions run from October 20 to 23, covering topics such as SEO, customer onboarding and retention, design, and branding. The schedule also includes a Q&A with Bolt co-founders Eric Simons and Albert Pai.

The contest invites participants to build a business idea into a product or use Bolt to develop an existing business. Projects must be built primarily in Bolt, and submissions must include a publicly accessible live link and a Bolt project link. A demo video is optional.

The prizes are $10,000 for first place, $5,000 for second place, and $2,500 for third place. The first-place prize also includes a one-on-one mentoring session with Bolt co-founder and CEO Eric Simons. Entries are judged equally on use of Bolt, the idea, implementation and design, and potential impact.

Format:Online building weekend, live sessions, and contestDates:October 17–24, 2026Deadline:October 20, 2026Prizes:$10,000, $5,000, and $2,500Eligibility:Contest entrants must be at least 18 and meet the official rules

### 📌 Y Combinator Winter 2027 Batch

Who it's for:Founders who are building a startup or have an idea they want to develop into a business.

What it's about:Y Combinator (YC) is accepting applications for its Winter 2027 startup funding batch. The program runs from January to March 2027 and takes place in person in San Francisco.

YC invests $500,000 in each accepted company. During the three-month program, founders work with a dedicated YC partner, attend group sessions and meetups, and get support with developing their products and raising further investment.

Startups at different stages can apply, including those that are still at the idea stage. Applications are open to startups from anywhere in the world. However, founders should be prepared to work full-time on their company during the batch, which takes place in San Francisco.

If the upcoming batch doesn't fit your timeline, YC also accepts applications for future batches through its Early Decision option.

### roadmap.sh: Developer Roadmaps and Learning Resources

Who it's for:Developers who want to learn a new technology, prepare for a role, or figure out what skills to learn next.

What it's about:roadmap.sh is a community-driven platform with roadmaps, study plans, and learning resources for different developer roles and technologies.

The roadmaps are interactive, so you can click on individual topics to explore them and find resources to learn more. There are paths for frontend, backend, DevOps, cybersecurity, AI engineering, data science, cloud, and many other areas.

Beyond roadmaps, the platform also has best practices for topics like API security and code review, along with questions to test your knowledge of technologies such as JavaScript, React, and Node.js.

You can browse the roadmaps and use them to plan your learning. The project also accepts community contributions to its roadmaps.

Format:Online learning resourceCost:Free to access the roadmaps and resources
