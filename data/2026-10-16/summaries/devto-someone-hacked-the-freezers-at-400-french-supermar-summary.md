# Someone hacked the freezers at 400 French supermarkets - DEV Community

## Physical Chaos from Cyberattacks: A New Example

The recent cyberattack on refrigeration systems at over 400 French supermarkets is a prime example of how outdated software can lead to physical chaos. This incident is more than just a matter of the Terminators waving their hands, but a demonstration of the impact of sophisticated cyberattacks.

*   **Over 300 shops affected**: The attack compromised the remote control systems used to manage the refrigeration facilities, causing widespread damage to stock and services.
*   **High cost**: The attackers targeted the older and less secure version of the systems, which can cost tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of dollars, especially for supermarket chains.
*   **Software vulnerabilities**: Danfoss, the manufacturer of these systems, acknowledged that the compromised systems were often outdated and did not receive security updates.
*   **Global precedent**: This is not the first such incident. Examples include the destruction of nuclear reactors (Stuxnet) and the disruption of Ukrainian power grids (December 2015 and June 2022). The use of AI in cyberattacks is considered high-level (i.e., not a simple botnet, but an advanced system).
