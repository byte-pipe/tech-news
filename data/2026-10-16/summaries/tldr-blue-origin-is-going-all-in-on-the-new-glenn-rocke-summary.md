# Blue Origin is going all in on the New Glenn rocket - Ars Technica

**Blue Origin Expands New Glenn Rocket Facilities in Florida**

Blue Origin, a company founded by Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, announced a major expansion of its launch facilities in Florida, with the goal of launching more than 100 rockets a year. The company added two new launch pads for the New Glenn rocket, in addition to two existing ones at Cape Canaveral, and invested heavily in a new manufacturing facility that will produce up to 150 upper stages for the larger variant of the New Glenn rocket.

Blue Origin's expansion is centered on building the capacity to support multiple missions per week by the end of the decade. The company has invested approximately $3 billion in facilities and infrastructure in Florida since its inception and now has over 5,000 employees working in the state and 500 suppliers.

Blue Origin plans to procure two additional landing ships and build a new customer integration facility, located in conjunction with the US Space Force, which will feature four payload bays to process multiple missions at the same time. The company's new infrastructure is designed to support multiple flights per week, increasing the company's ability to support more missions reliably and efficiently.
