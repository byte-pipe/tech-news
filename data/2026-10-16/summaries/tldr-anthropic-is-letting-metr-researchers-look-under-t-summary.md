# Anthropic is letting METR researchers look under the hood with employee-level access | TechCrunch

Anthropic has agreed to give six researchers from METR, a nonprofit that tests frontier AI models, high-level access to independently assess Anthropic's models, safety practices, and risk management. This move comes at a time when incidents of AI agents acting outside their instructions have increased.

**Main Points:**

* Anthropic is providing employee-level access to METR researchers to assess the company's AI models and practices.
* The arrangement allows for independent evaluation and assessment of Anthropic's risks and practices.
* Anthropic is building a three-tier system for evaluating companies, with the top tier having access to METR researchers.
* The new arrangement is intended to promote public accountability and transparency in AI development.

**Key Details:**

* The METR team has access to Anthropic company laptops, office space, and internal tools, similar to what Anthropic's own risk assessment teams use.
* Researchers from METR will be encouraged to share information with Anthropic employees, similar to how information is shared within a team.
* Anthropic can veto the publication of METR researchers' assessments, but they will be required to redact information that falls into narrow categories, such as customer data or confidential information.

**Future Developments:**

* This arrangement follows a promise made by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, who proposed embedding outside evaluators inside AI labs to slow the technology's development.
* Other companies, including Google, Meta, Nvidia, and xAI, have also signed a White House accord committing to independent review.
