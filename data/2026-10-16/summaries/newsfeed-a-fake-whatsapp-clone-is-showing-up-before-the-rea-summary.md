# A fake WhatsApp clone is showing up before the real version on the Play Store | The Verge

# Tech News: WhatsApp Fake App on Google Play Store

A fake WhatsApp clone has appeared on the Google Play Store, deceiving users into downloading it as the real app. The app, labeled as "WhatsApp Messenger," is actually a spoof that has been live on the platform for over 500,000 downloads.

## Why the Fake App?

According to Android Authority, a developer has been using Google's Early Access program, which allows developers to create an app before public release, to promote the fake WhatsApp app. This program, known as the "Program to Allow Review and Test" (PARS), is intended to prevent users from reporting suspicious apps.

## Similarities with the Real WhatsApp App

The fake app has the same icon, screenshots, and text as the real WhatsApp app, but the app's metadata, such as its download count and reviews, are actually fake. Android Authority notes that the fake app might be using Google Play's Early Access program to disable public user reviews, potentially helping to avoid widespread warnings among users.

## How to Avoid Downloading the Fake App

To avoid downloading the fake WhatsApp app, users can follow these steps:

* Verify that the app is from a trusted developer (in this case, "Agment Assets sallu")
* Download the app from the Google Play Store website
* Look for reviews, ratings, and comments to ensure the app is genuine
* Check the app's permissions and verify that they match the real WhatsApp app's permissions
