# Automakers routinely share personally identifiable connected-car data with third parties, report says | The Record from Recorded Future News

**Automakers Routinely Share Personally Identifiable Connected-Car Data with Third Parties, Report Says**

* Automakers are said to routinely share personally identifiable connected-car data with third parties.
* The data is connected to vehicle identification numbers (VINs) and may include information such as vehicle make, model, and user identification numbers.
* This practice is part of a larger industry trend where automakers share data to improve vehicle performance, safety, and features.
* The report cited sources stating that several major automakers, including Ferrari and Porsche, have publicly acknowledged sharing connected-car data with third parties.
* The practice raises concerns about data privacy and security for vehicle owners, who may unknowingly contribute to the collection and analysis of their data.
* The industry's reliance on connected-car data has also sparked debate about the potential for data misuse and exploitation.
