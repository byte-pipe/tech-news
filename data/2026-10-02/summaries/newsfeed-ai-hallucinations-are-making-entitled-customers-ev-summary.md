# AI hallucinations are making entitled customers even worse | The Verge

* AI Hallucinations Worsen Entitled Customer Behavior
* Technology
* Industry Reports

The adoption of generative AI tools has transformed the customer service experience in restaurants and retail, but it also poses a challenge for workers. The introduction of AI-powered chatbots has led to a phenomenon known as "AI hallucinations," where customers expect AI to provide accurate information, but AI-generated responses often have unintended consequences.

* Main Issues: 
  + AI-generated responses ignore customer warnings about allergens
  + Patrons ask for items that are not available or do not exist
  + AI hallucinations exacerbate anxiety about sommeliers and wine
* Key Findings: 
  + Customer requests for specific wines increase, driven by AI-generated information
  + AI hallucinations are a common issue in customer service jobs, particularly with the launch of Meta's Muse AI agent
* Contributing Factors: 
  + Limited understanding of AI capabilities by customers
  + Lack of training for customer service staff on AI integration
* Consequences: 
  + Increased reliance on AI, leading to unintended consequences at restaurants and retail
  + Potential backlash from customers who may perceive AI-generated information as incorrect or dishonest

* Solutions:
  + Providing clear explanations for AI-generated responses
  + Training customer service staff to handle AI hallucinations
  + Encouraging customers to understand the limitations of AI in providing accurate information
